# This Finger Exercise SOLVED My Biggest Technique Problem!

## Synopsis

A 3-level fretting-hand finger-independence exercise built on a harmonic minor shape, targeting the classic "flying pinky" problem — fingers feeling mechanically linked rather than independently controllable.

## Techniques

- **"Hover, don't fly away" pinky cue.** When the pinky isn't fretting, it should hover close over the strings rather than bending away/upward — same economy-of-motion principle as [[Fretting and Picking Hand Mistakes Checklist]] point 7, but with a specific worked shape (harmonic minor) and a concrete failure image (pinky "bending away," feeling glued to the other fingers) that makes the fix easier to self-check.
- **Resist the instinct to widen motion as tempo rises.** Named directly as the hardest part — the natural reaction to pushing tempo is bigger motion, but staying small/relaxed is what actually produces clean results at speed.
- **3-level progression**, deliberately not about raw speed — the goal is control and independent finger feel first, with a faster take only after the slow control is solid.
