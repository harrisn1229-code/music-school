# Ian Kirkpatrick — FFF (Zara Larsson) Breakdown

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ge9PAxBdOg8)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ge9PAxBdOg8)

## Synopsis

An edited livestream teardown of Zara Larsson's "FFF" — deliberately "underwhelming" drums where the whole personality is one hi-hat, a two-bass layering trick, and a vocal chain built around **Soothe** for a shrill voice and **MIDI-triggered LFO Tool** for rhythmic gating. Also a candid look at his **print-with-abandon** habit (he forgets what half the synths are), his **swing-percentage-then-by-ear** groove workflow, and how he **delegates extra production to two collaborators** and cherry-picks the best parts.

## Techniques

- **The drums' whole personality is the hi-hat (Addictive Drums)** — kick/claps are simple; "the coolest thing about these drums is the hi-hat," from Addictive Drums. Let one element carry the character and keep the rest "tasty — they don't hog the stage." → [[1 Drums Hat Track]], [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Groove: saved swing percentages → then by ear / off-grid** — starts from saved Cubase swing values, then nudges by ear; e.g. **lag the hi-hat ~5 ms behind the kick**. MIDI **note lengths** are "a majority of the feel." → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[Automation]]
- **Oxide (tape) on the drum bus** — tape saturation for glue/character. → [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[3 Drums Bus]]
- **Two basses, one perceived bass** — high-pass one bass above **~109 Hz** (dynamic EQ too) and let **Reaktor** cover the low end, but the low synth only plays the **root notes** (not the walking "up" notes) — so the listener hears one bass. → [[Layering and Stacking]], [[Bassline Writing]], [[SUB Slot]], [[2 Bass Group|4 Bass Group]]
- **Bass grit chain: Trilian patch → Saturn → Trash (convolve) → Q3 (cut lows + highs past 14k) → Soothe** — Saturn + Trash do the dirt; Q3 carves; Soothe removes more low end (a little redundant but fine). Saturn on the sub bass too. → [[Saturn 2]], [[Soothe2]], [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[Bassline Writing]]
- **OTT as a "brightening shortcut" — used honestly** — light OTT on his master bus to brighten; he agrees with the haters that **abusing OTT wrecks a mix**, and notes **Steinberg Squasher** is "a better OTT." → [[OTT]], [[Multiband Compression]], [[Master Chain|8 Master]]
- **Vocal FX *before* the compressor, on purpose** — echo/reverb placed **before OTT** so that when the lead stops, the compressor "relaxes" and **brings the delay tail up loud** — a way to make the delay bloom only in the gaps. (Mixers hate the ordering; he likes the behavior.) → [[EchoBoy Jr]], [[Delay]], [[Compression]], [[Compression Staging]]
- **Soothe is the centerpiece for a shrill voice** — Zara's voice has intense shrill frequencies (the "er" of "friend" ~2.6 kHz, plus a harsher band); **Soothe** tames them dynamically better than he could by hand. Pre-Soothe method: FabFilter **Q3 with peak-hold** to find the peak, then a **dynamic EQ** node on it. → [[Soothe2]], [[De-essing]], [[EQ]], [[Pro-Q 4]]
- **MIDI-triggered LFO Tool for rhythmic vocal gating** — a "Spark" track sounds pitched but is actually the vocal with **LFO Tool gated by MIDI notes**, letting only slivers through on a rhythm. You can put LFO Tool on the master/a synth group and **gate a whole group live from your MIDI keyboard**. → [[LFO Tool]], [[Sidechaining]], [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]]
- **Manual delay from time-compressed word chops** — cut up words and time-compress them to build a delay-like effect by hand. → [[Delay]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Pre-echo transition: "forever" morphs into "is" via Little AlterBoy** — a pitched-down transition using **Little AlterBoy with the formant raised** (instead of a plain pitch-down) so it sounds intentional, not chipmunked. → [[Little AlterBoy]], [[Pitch and Formant]]
- **Print-with-abandon (Skrillex/Noisia habit)** — build an idea on a CPU-heavy synth/chain, love it, **print immediately and move on** — keeps momentum and forces commitment (downside: he forgets what the synth was). → [[Layering and Stacking]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **PadShop + tiny tuned delay for a ticking-clock texture** — a clock-tick candy made from PadShop and a super-short delay with feedback up, tuned. → [[PadShop]], [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]], [[TEXTURE Slot]]
- **Delegate extra production, then curate** — gave two collaborators (Vic, Juan) stems with "add every little part you can think of," then **peppered in the best bits** — a way to multiply ideas and cherry-pick. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]

## Philosophy

- **Print for CPU and for commitment** — recurring theme; the same habit that keeps sessions moving makes him forget his own moves a year later.
- **The pre-chorus melody follows the riff** — a collaborator's idea (Julia/Justin) to have the pre-chorus melody trace the instrumental riff; "the listener doesn't know which came first," but it ties the song together. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
