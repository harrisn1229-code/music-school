# ARR-C08 — Carry one element unchanged across the boundary

**Q.** Two sections either side of a transition sound very different. What stops it reading as an abrupt cut?

> [!question]- Answer
> Keep **one recognisable element** — a melodic motif, a signature sound — playing continuously and unchanged straight through the boundary. It gives the listener something to hold while everything else changes underneath. The complementary device on the same axis: a theme restated later **in a new key** reads as development rather than repetition, so a returning idea can feel like it's moved without a full modulation plan.

## Why it matters

Connective tissue is a separate job from contrast, and the two are easy to confuse. Maximum contrast plus one constant thread beats moderate contrast everywhere.

## Drill

Find your most abrupt section change. Pick one element and let it run unchanged across the boundary. A/B.
