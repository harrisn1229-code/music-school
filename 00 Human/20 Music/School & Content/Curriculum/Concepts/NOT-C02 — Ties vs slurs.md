# NOT-C02 — Ties vs slurs

**Q.** A curved line joins two noteheads. What's it called when the pitches are the same, and when they differ?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Same pitch = a tie** — attack only the first, hold through the second. **Different pitches = a slur** — play legato; on guitar that's a hammer-on or pull-off.

## Why it matters

The guitar-specific half matters: a slur is an instruction about *technique*, not just phrasing.

## Drill

Find both in a chart. Play a tied pair and a slurred pair and confirm you're doing different things.
