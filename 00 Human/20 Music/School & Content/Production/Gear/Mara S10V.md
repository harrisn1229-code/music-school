# Mara S10V

**Category:** Tube Amp (1×6L6 combo)
**Vendor:** Mara Amps
**Launch price:** $799

## What it is

A newly-launched (2026) US-built boutique tube combo positioned as a direct, better-built alternative to the [[Fender Blues Junior]] at the same street price — hand-stuffed, hand-built, and hand-wired in the USA with American parts. Not currently owned — referenced here purely as a comparison point to the owned Blues Junior.

## What it's used for

Not in the studio — this is a comparison/wishlist reference only, not an owned signal path.

## Comparison to the owned [[Fender Blues Junior]]

Per the source video, a head-to-head pitch aimed squarely at Blues Junior buyers:
- **Power tube / voicing** — single 6L6 (classic American clean-to-breakup tone) vs. the Blues Junior's EL84 pair (British voice, earlier breakup, more compression).
- **Transformers** — North American-made transformers with a Canadian Hammond brand, pitched as higher quality than the Blues Junior's stock imported transformers.
- **Cabinet** — solid pine wood cab vs. particle board on the Blues Junior — claimed to matter for cab resonance/tone.
- **Build** — hand-stuffed, hand-built, hand-wired in the USA with American parts, vs. the Blues Junior's overseas mass production.
- **Price** — $799 launch price, positioned as matching the Blues Junior's street price while claiming better construction and tone.
- **Colors** — natural or blue finish.
- Video is presented by an affiliate (Daniel Seriff) with a disclosed commission link — treat the "better sounding" claim as a sales pitch, not a verified A/B; no actual audio comparison or settings were given in the video.
