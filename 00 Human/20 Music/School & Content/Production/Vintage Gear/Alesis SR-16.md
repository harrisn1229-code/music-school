# Alesis SR-16

## What it is
The best-selling drum machine of all time — introduced 1990 and *still in production*, three decades later. Not a scene-founder like the TRs; a species of infrastructure: the default beat under a million demos, buskers, worship services, and bar-band gigs.

## What makes the sound
- **16-bit samples recorded with ambience baked in** — the headline feature: dry/room/reverb versions of the kits, so it sounded "mixed" through a PA with zero effort.
- **Dynamic articulation**: velocity switches between sample layers (a hat hit soft vs. hard is a different recording) — an early stab at realism budget rivals skipped.
- Serviceable stock patterns with fills (the infamous preset grooves half the world has jammed over) + A/B/fill song structure.
- Polite, mid-focused, unfashionably *pleasant* — its anonymity is the identity.

## Digital versions
- **Owned:** [[Drum Rack]] with SR-16 samples; honestly any decent acoustic-kit sample set + [[Reverb]] room layer replicates the concept.
- **Paid:** Alesis still sells the hardware new (~$150); UVI Beat Box Anthology 2 covers the era.
- **Free:** SR-16 sample dumps are everywhere.

## Used on
- Genre/era: thirty years of demos, singer-songwriter records, worship, blues/bar bands, and lo-fi bedroom productions — deliberately genre-level; the SR-16's story is ubiquity, not credits.
