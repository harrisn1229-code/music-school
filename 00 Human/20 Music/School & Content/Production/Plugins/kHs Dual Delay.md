# kHs Dual Delay

![[Images/kHs Dual Delay.png]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A two-line delay snap-in allowing independent timing/feedback per delay line.

## What it's used for

Complex, layered rhythmic delay effects (e.g. different subdivisions per channel).

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain.

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Time | Base delay time — the ms readout; free or synced via the clock button | Sync to 1/8 or dotted for rhythmic echoes |
| ② | Ratio | Multiplier (× 1.62) setting the second delay time against the first | Offset the two for a wide, ping-pong feel |
| ③ | Tone | Filter/tone of the repeats | Darken so echoes sit behind the dry |
| ④ | Feedback | Repeats/regeneration | Higher for longer trails |
| ⑤ | Spread | Stereo spread of the two taps | Widen for a big stereo delay |
| ⑥ | Duck | Ducks the delay under the dry signal | Keeps echoes out of the way until phrase ends |
| ⑦ | Crosstalk | Feedback bleed between L and R channels | Add for a ping-pong that trades sides |
| ⑧ | Mix | Dry/wet blend | Low as an insert; high on a send |
