# DS Clap

![[Images/DS Clap.jpg]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Ableton (Drum Synths pack, Max for Live)

## What it is

One voice from Ableton's Drum Synths pack — a synthesized hand-clap generator built from layered noise bursts with adjustable spread.

## What it's used for

Programmed claps for house/pop beats, or layering with a sampled clap to add width and a synthetic edge.

## FX chain notes

Used as a drum-voice instrument in a Drum Rack; typically followed by a short transient-shaping compressor and a drum-bus saturation stage.

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Sloppy | Timing looseness/spread of the individual clap layers | More for a wider 'group' clap; low for a tight single hit |
| ② | Tail | Length/density of the noisy tail after the initial claps | More for a roomy, reverberant clap |
| ③ | Spread | Stereo spread of the clap layers | Widen for a big stereo clap |
| ④ | Tone | Brightness / filter of the clap | Brighten to cut; darken to sit back |
| ⑤ | Tune | Pitch of the clap | Tune to taste; higher for a snappier crack |
| ⑥ | Decay | Overall tail length | Short for tight; long for roomy |
| ⑦ | Volume | Output level | Balance against the rest of the kit |
