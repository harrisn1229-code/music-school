# HAR-C01 — The three functions: T, SD, D

**Q.** What are the three harmonic functions, which chords carry each in a major key, and what's the normal cycle?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Tonic (T)** — I, vi, iii — home, at rest. **Subdominant (SD)** — IV, ii — departure. **Dominant (D)** — V, vii° — tension wanting to resolve. The normal cycle is **T → SD → D → T**.

## Why it matters

The idea the whole course rests on. It is almost never tested directly, which is why its absence shows up everywhere else instead. Once chords are *functions* rather than letters, a progression becomes a shape, and substitution becomes obvious: chords sharing a function can stand in for each other.

## Drill

Label every chord in four of your own progressions T, SD or D. Find the ones that break T→SD→D→T.
