# Lophiile — Fixing an Old Beat (Cookups Stream)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJJZHz5D3C4)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJJZHz5D3C4)

## Synopsis

Stream segment where lophiile reopens an old beat that "isn't quite there" and shows his diagnostic process live: instead of a vague "it doesn't sound good yet," he forces himself to isolate the exact element he hates (a too-obviously-Omnisphere pluck in the intro), swaps sounds before rewriting parts, and re-evaluates old sections with fresh ears. Also a sermon on affordable plugins and a reminder that Pro Tools stock loops are secretly great.

## Techniques

- **Isolate what you hate — name it before you fix it.** "It's good to really isolate the sh* you don't like... let's figure out what the f* it means." He drills from "the intro kills me" to "this picky sound, it sounds so Omnisphere" — then acts on THAT. Diagnosis beats vibes. → [[Referencing and Translation]], /mix-check
- **Change the SOUND before changing the PART.** "Before we f* with the part, let's just change the sound and see how far we get." Half the time a part you hate is a patch you hate. → [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Don't hate your past self** — the weird bit-reduction he found on a layer annoyed him, but "you get cool sh* by experimenting... you can't hate yourself for that." Salvage the experiments (he kept the weird chord-morph part *for a later section*). → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Re-audition old parts in new roles** — the part he hated as an intro worked as a breakdown later in the song; sections aren't fixed to their original slot.
- **Real played percussion loops to humanize a groove** — went hunting for an actually-played percussion/shaker loop (not programmed) to push the groove; warped it into the pocket ("I like to warp myself" — manual warp markers, not auto). → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[2 Drums Hats Group|Drums Hats Group]]
- **Pro Tools stock loops slap** — "the ones that come with Pro Tools are [great] — don't even sleep on them"; one was a whole song. Stock content is unmined gold. → [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Affordable-plugin doctrine** — he deliberately tweets/uses cheap and free tools ("you can get wavy sh* for $30") and pushes back on $500-plugin snobbery; matches his free-chorus/free-Melda picks elsewhere in this batch. → [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **Too-high/too-low key check** — flagged the beat's key as awkward ("up here is really high, down here is really low") — a key where both registers strain is a rewrite signal. → /transpose-song
- **When there's already a lot going on, simplify the new part** — his added idea only worked once he stripped it ("there's already a lot of sh* going on — that's better"). → [[Masking and Carving]]
