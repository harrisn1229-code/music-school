# Silly Love Songs — Wings

> McCartney's pointed response to critics — especially Lennon — who dismissed his sentimental writing. Multi-layered counterpoint with multiple vocal lines running simultaneously. The funk bass groove is the backbone of the entire track.

## Production & Arrangement
1976 — McCartney's disco-inflected pop, famous for its **interlocking melodic bass line** (a counter-melody hook in its own right) and the **stacked multi-melody outro** where three separate vocal lines sing different words at once in counterpoint.
