# Mixing Vocals from 'Dancing With A Stranger' with Kevin Davis

## Synopsis

A short Mix with the Masters breakdown of Kevin Davis's vocal FX chain on Sam Smith & Normani's "Dancing With A Stranger." Davis solos the wet vocal FX against dry to demonstrate each layer: a chorus (Valhalla Space Modulator) to thicken the lead before reverb goes on at all, a two-plate reverb blend (a longer/darker plate plus a shorter plate run together), a delay throw (EchoBoy) paired with a hall reverb on specific verse lines, and a subtle "Repeater" 40s-style tape delay run as a barely-audible thickening insert. He then contrasts the two lead vocalists' chorus treatments — Sam Smith gets an octave-up vocal doubled and drenched in a large room reverb, Normani gets a vocoder — both aimed at the same job (uplift the chorus) but landing as a different color per voice, which he frames as *why* the two vocalists' contrasting tones (one warm, one sharper) work together rather than clash. He also touches on a background harmony part added in the song's vamp, a touch of reverb on ad-libs for space, and closes on a background "effects vocal" part (a re-purposed Sam vocal that runs throughout the song) chained through a sidechain "pumper," a little Scheps (channel-strip character), SSL-style bus compression, and reverb — all A/B'd against bypass. Everything here is demonstrated by ear (mutes/solos), not read off numeric plugin values, so most of what's captured below is qualitative technique rather than exact settings — still highly actionable as a recipe/ordering guide.
