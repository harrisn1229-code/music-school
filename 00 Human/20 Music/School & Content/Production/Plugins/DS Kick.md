# DS Kick

![[Images/DS Kick.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Ableton (Drum Synths pack, Max for Live)

## What it is

One voice from Ableton's official Drum Synths Max for Live pack — a synthesized kick drum generator with pitch envelope, click/punch, and decay controls (no samples involved).

## What it's used for

Building fully synthesized kick drums for electronic genres without loading samples — quick to tune and layer under sampled kicks.

## FX chain notes

Used as a drum-voice instrument in a Drum Rack; typically followed by a short transient-shaping compressor and a drum-bus saturation stage.
