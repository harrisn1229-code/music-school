# EchoBoy Jr

![[Images/EchoBoy Jr.png]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Soundtoys

## What it is

A simplified, CPU-light version of EchoBoy with a reduced control set for quick delay duty.

## What it's used for

Fast, simple delay effects when EchoBoy's full depth isn't needed.

## FX chain notes

Same send/insert placement as EchoBoy.
