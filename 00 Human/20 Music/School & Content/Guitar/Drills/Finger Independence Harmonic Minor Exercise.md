# Finger Independence Harmonic Minor Exercise

A 3-level fretting-hand exercise on a harmonic minor shape, targeting finger independence and the "flying pinky" problem.

## How to run it

- When the pinky isn't fretting, hover it close over the strings rather than letting it bend away — the concrete self-check this drill trains.
- Resist widening the motion as tempo increases; stay small and relaxed even under speed pressure.
- Prioritize control and independent finger feel over raw speed — work through the 3 levels slowly before attempting a faster pass.

Pure fretting-hand dexterity/economy-of-motion — no scale/mode/chord-tone decision-making applied (the harmonic minor shape is a vehicle, not the point).
