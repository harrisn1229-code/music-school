# Ozone 9 Vintage Limiter

![[Images/Ozone 9 Vintage Limiter.png]]

**Category:** Compressor/Limiter
**Vendor:** iZotope

## What it is

Ozone 9's vintage-modeled limiter, emulating classic analog limiter/compressor behavior for a more colored, less transparent loudness stage.

## What it's used for

Adding vintage character while maximizing loudness, as an alternative to the transparent Maximizer module.

## FX chain notes

Used as the final or near-final stage in a vintage-flavored mastering chain.
