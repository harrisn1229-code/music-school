# FND-C02 — Order of sharps and flats

**Q.** Give the order of sharps as they appear in key signatures, and the order of flats.

> [!question]- Answer
> **Sharps: F C G D A E B.** **Flats: B E A D G C F** — the sharp order reversed.

## Why it matters

Both sequences run all seven, and stopping short is the usual mistake. It matters because the key-reading rules are indexed off the *end* of the list — the last sharp, the second-to-last flat — so a truncated list can't be read backwards.

## Drill

Write both orders from memory. Then: which sharps does E major take? Which flats does A♭ major take?
