# Honeysuckle Rose — Fats Waller

> The flagship [[The Montgomery-Ward Bridge|Montgomery-Ward]] tune — Wikipedia names it "particularly" as the example. Its A section is a ii–V vamp that refuses to resolve for four full bars, which makes it a genuinely odd tune to walk over.

## Chords

**Key:** F major. 32-bar AABA, up-tempo swing.

### A section (bars 1–8)

Two published charts, differing only in harmonic rhythm and one connector:

| Source | bars 1–4 | bars 5–8 |
|---|---|---|
| Learn Jazz Standards | `Gm7 \| C7 \| Gm7 \| C7` | `Fmaj7 A♭°7 \| Gm7 C7 \| Fmaj7 B♭7 \| Am7 D7` |
| iGigBook / realbook.us | `Gm7 C7 \| Gm7 C7 \| Gm7 C7 \| Gm7 C7` | `F6 F7/A \| B♭6 C7 \| F6 B♭7 \| Am7 D7` |

The whole first half is **a ii–V on Gm7–C7 that never lands** — four bars of it — before bar 5 finally reaches the I. Both charts agree exactly on `F6/Fmaj7 – B♭7 – Am7 – D7` to close. → [[The ii-V-I]]

### Bridge (bars 17–24) — the Montgomery-Ward

- **Bare (iGigBook):** `F7 | F7 | B♭6 | B♭6 | G7 | G7 | C7 | C7`
- **ii–V substituted (LJS):** `Cm7 | F7 | B♭maj7 | B♭maj7 | G7 | G7 | C7 | C7`

In F: **I7 – IV – II7 – V7**, two bars each. Learn Jazz Standards states outright that this is *not* a rhythm-changes release (III7–VI7–II7–V7) — it's the commercial bridge. → [[The Montgomery-Ward Bridge]]

## For bass

- **The A section is the hard part, not the bridge.** Only two roots — G and C — across four bars. There is no root motion to lean on, so the line has to generate its own interest through approach notes and chromatics. Good tune for exactly that problem. → [[Chromatic Bass Lines]]
- **Bridge root motion breaks once:** `F → B♭` (down a 5th), then **`B♭ → G` (down a m3 — the hinge)**, then `G → C → F` (fifths home). That third-drop is the fingerprint of this bridge, and the thing that distinguishes it by ear from the rhythm-changes release, which is an unbroken fifths chain.
- **Oscar Pettiford** on Thelonious Monk's version is the cited bass reference — his line mirrors Monk's left-hand Gm7/C7 and transposes it up a minor third. *(Single-sourced, Reverb "Bass Walk of the Week.")*

## Related repertoire

Charlie Parker's **"Scrapple from the Apple"** (also in F) is built on this tune's A section, with a rhythm-changes bridge swapped in for the Montgomery-Ward. → [[Rhythm Changes]]
