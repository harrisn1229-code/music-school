# ARR-C02 — How to actually write the B section

**Q.** Give the three-step method, and the test that tells you it worked.

> [!question]- Answer
> **1.** Duplicate the drums, strip everything else — bass, keys, melody out. **2.** Write a **complementary** part over them: different chords, opposite density, different mood (a sad/loose/simple B under a full/happy/soulful A). **3.** Test **the return** — playing out of B back into A should feel like *oh my god, we're home*. That coming-home lift is the whole point: **the right B section makes the A section better.**

## Why it matters

The test is the part people skip. A B section that's merely different isn't working; it has to make the return land. Extend the same move and you get A-B-C form — verse chords, different pre-hook chords, different hook chords.

## Drill

Take one loop. Duplicate the drums, strip everything, write a B. Then loop B→A four times and rate the return honestly.
