# LWT - Auto Track Colors

![[Images/LWT - Auto Track Colors.png]]

**Category:** Utility
**Vendor:** Max for Live

## What it is

A Max for Live workflow utility that automatically assigns/organizes track colors in a Live set based on rules (e.g. by type or group).

## What it's used for

Keeping large sessions visually organized automatically instead of manually coloring tracks.

## FX chain notes

Runs inline in the signal/MIDI chain at the point it's inserted; combines with whatever device follows.
