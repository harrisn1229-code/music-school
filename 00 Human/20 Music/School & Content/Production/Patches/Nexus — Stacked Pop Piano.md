# Nexus — Stacked Pop Piano

**Instrument:** [[Nexus]] · **Slot:** [[BED Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Acoustic Piano Track|Acoustic Piano Track]]

## What it's for
Instant pop "stacked piano" — a piano already layered/produced (piano + pad, piano + strings, or a doubled unison piano) that needs zero further processing. Reach for it when the part just needs to be *there* and polished, not sound-designed.

## Settings
- Browse the `Keys`/`Piano` category for stacked/layered patches specifically (single-sample piano patches belong to the acoustic lane, not this one) — audition ≤5, commit
- Bypass most of the chain below this patch — it arrives already EQ'd, compressed, and often reverbed; re-processing tends to fight the built-in mix
- Use the 4 macro knobs only for brightness/level trim, not reshaping — the patch's internal balance is the point
- Gain-stage into the track rather than adding a compressor first; check level against the mix before touching anything else

## In context
- The speed option against [[Keyscape — LA Custom C7 Grand]] / [[Keyscape — Wing Upright & Tack Piano]] when there's no time to build or mic-select a real piano patch.
