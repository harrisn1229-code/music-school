# kHs Flanger

![[Images/kHs Flanger.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A classic flanger modulation snap-in using short modulated delay with feedback.

## What it's used for

Classic sweeping flange effects on guitars, drums, and synths.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain, post-EQ.

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Delay | Base delay time of the flange | Short for tight metallic sweeps |
| ② | Depth | Sweep range of the short delay | More for an obvious flange; subtle for movement |
| ③ | Rate | Speed of the sweep LFO | Slow for a jet-sweep; fast for warble |
| ④ | Scroll | Barber-pole / scrolling flange mode (toggle) | On for an endlessly-rising sweep |
| ⑤ | Offset | L/R phase offset of the sweep LFO | Offset for a stereo-animated flange |
| ⑥ | Motion | Motion pattern of the sweep | Change the shape of the movement |
| ⑦ | Spread | Stereo spread of the effect | Widen for a big stereo whoosh |
| ⑧ | Feedback | Resonance/intensity of the flange | High for the metallic whoosh; low for gentle motion |
| ⑨ | Mix | Dry/wet blend | ~50% for the classic comb-notch flange sound |
