# Disclosure — Making a Song for You to Remix (Stems Stream)

![](https://youtu.be/IOSPhXcXT_8)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/IOSPhXcXT_8)

## Synopsis

The ENERGY-release-week stream where Guy and Howard build a housey-garage tune live specifically to give the stems away for a remix contest — which means they narrate the whole build and finish it properly, ending with Guy singing and stacking a full topline. Standout material: the **second drum bus** trick (mix through heavy crush but spare the hi-hat), velocity→pitch percussion movement, Howard's one-slap-delay signature, bassline-first-then-harmonize writing, and the complete **group vocal-stack bus chain**.

## Techniques

### Drums & groove
- **Audition loops with one question: "why isn't this a finished song yet?"** — every sample pack pass is a yes/no against that bar, not "is this cool." → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Mix THROUGH the drum crush** — MPC-style bit-crush/distortion plugin on the drum bus early ("it's like an interesting EQ — really nicely tames the shaker"), then build the rest of the beat through it; "it needs noise, needs wrongness" (tape + vinyl layers from [[RC-20 Retro Color]]-style tools). → [[3 Drums Bus|Drums Bus]], [[Saturation and Distortion]]
- **The second drum bus (post-distortion)** — loved what the crush/compression did to everything *except* the hi-hat, so: drums → distorted bus → route THAT into a clean "drum bus post distortion," and put the sends/next effects there. Staged buses let one element escape a group treatment without unwinding it. → [[3 Drums Bus|Drums Bus]], [[Compression Staging]]
- **Velocity→pitch on percussion** (sampler mod matrix, small range) — each hit lands at a slightly different pitch; instant humanization for one-shot perc. → [[1 Drums Hat Track|Drums Hat Track]], [[Automation]]
- **Deliberately-late cowbell, messy shaker left messy** — off-grid ornaments stay; "the sidechain will bring it into rhythm." Fix feel with the pump, not the grid. → [[Sidechaining]], [[2 Drums Hats Group|Drums Hats Group]]
- **LinnDrum kick click, low** — "I'll use it for the click of the kick but not very loud": [[LinnDrum]] top layered under the main kick for attack only. → [[Kick Design]], [[1 Drums Kick Track|Drums Kick Track]]
- **[[Hand Clap Studio]] "underneath claps"** — layered under the main clap, plus a dry clap placed *ahead of the beat* in the step sequencer. → [[1 Drums Snare-Clap Track|Drums Snare-Clap Track]]

### Howard's signature + bass
- **One-slap, extremely short delay on almost anything** — vocals, drums, instruments, super low in the mix: "it just makes it sound like you're in a small club — that's where things sound good." → [[Delay Send Slap]], [[Depth and Space]]
- **Bassline first, then harmonize the bassline** — write the bass on a raw sine/organ patch, then build the chords/pads by harmonizing it with other synths ("we make a bassline and then just harmonize it with other sounds so it follows it"). Same philosophy as Kenny's bass-implied reharm. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]]
- **Dirty bass excuses a kick clash** — the overdriven bass "is clashing with the kick but it's good because it's so dirty — I don't think it matters"; sidechain it anyway. Distortion buys masking forgiveness. → [[Sidechaining]], [[Masking and Carving]]
- **Record synth chords one at a time as audio, then chop** — when the patch can't fade/retrigger in time, print each chord separately and edit the audio; also "restart the note halfway through so it hits twice — keeps the groove going." → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]

### Vocals (Guy's topline session)
- **Tracking chain: Neve 1081 preamp (high boost, low cut — "not a 1073, a 1081, and it's wonderful") → LA-2A ("the one-LA") → Maag EQ4 air band.** The house tracking chain, confirmed again. → [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]]
- **The stack bus:** every stack track gets Tube-Tech EQ (lows out) + de-esser → the GROUP bus gets **overdrive (saturate on the way in) → stereo 1176 (auditioned LA-3A — "good for group vocals" but passed) → EQ → [[Decapitator]] (considered Culture Vulture) → de-ess AGAIN** ("it's a lot of S's as a group"). De-ess before AND after the group color. → [[2 Backup Vocals Group|Backup Vocals Group]], [[1 Double Vocal Track|Double Vocal Track]]
- **Remix-stem delivery format:** drums / bass / pads separated; vocals delivered **wet AND dry**. → bounce discipline
