# MEL-C08 — Fixing a boring melody

**Q.** A melody you've written feels boring. Name three things you'd change, and which usually matters most.

> [!question]- Answer
> **Change the rhythm first** — most boring melodies are boring *rhythmically*, circling even note values on the beat. Then **widen it** (add a leap, push the top note higher). Then **move where it sits against the chord** — target 9ths and 7ths instead of roots and 5ths. Also: add space, displace it off the downbeat, or reharmonize underneath it.

## Why it matters

**The ranking is the answer**, not the list — rhythm is the highest-leverage fix by a wide margin, and it's the one people reach for last because pitch feels like where melody lives.

## Drill

Take a melody that isn't working. Apply the triage in order and stop at the first fix that solves it.
