# RX Breath Control

![[Images/RX Breath Control.png]]

**Category:** Pitch/Vocal
**Vendor:** iZotope

## What it is

An RX module that detects and automatically attenuates breath noises in vocal recordings. Owned in both the RX 10 and RX 11 suites.

## What it's used for

Reducing distracting breath sounds in vocal takes without manual gain automation.

## FX chain notes

Used early in a vocal cleanup pass, before creative EQ/compression.

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Mode (Gain / Target) | Reduce breaths by a fixed amount, or duck them down to a target level | Target mode for consistent breath level across a take |
| ② | Target level / Gain [dBFS] | The level breaths are ducked to (Target) or reduced by (Gain) | Duck, don't fully remove, for a natural result |
| ③ | Sensitivity | How aggressively breaths are detected | Tune so only breaths, not soft words, are caught |
| ④ | Output breaths only | Solos the detected breaths to check detection | Toggle on to confirm you're only catching breaths |
