# VocAlign Ultra AU

![[Images/VocAlign Ultra AU.png]]

**Category:** Pitch/Vocal
**Vendor:** Synchro Arts

## What it is

The higher-tier version of VocAlign with additional algorithms and finer alignment control over Project 5.

## What it's used for

Same automatic timing-alignment role as VocAlign Project 5, for more demanding alignment tasks.

## FX chain notes

Same placement as VocAlign Project 5 — a timing-correction stage before further vocal processing.

## Used in my chains

The higher-end Synchro Arts aligner — same align-a-dub-to-a-guide job as [[VocAlign Project 5 AU]] with more advanced control:

> [!warning] **Hangs Live on automated load** in my sessions — so [[VocAlign Project 5 AU]] is the working substitute across the vocal stacks. If Project 5 also stalls, fall back to manual: warp each take's transients to the reference, or use [[Melodyne]]'s time tool.
