# Keyscape — Pianet

**Instrument:** [[Keyscape]] · **Slot:** [[BED Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Electric Piano Track|Electric Piano Track]]

## What it's for
The Beatles-adjacent "I Am the Walrus" tine sound — a reedy, slightly nasal electric piano distinct from Rhodes/Wurli warmth. Reach for it specifically for that vintage-British-pop character.

## Settings
- ⑥ Patch: Pianet (Hohner-modeled tine EP)
- ⑤ Drive/EQ knob: light touch only — the Pianet's nasal midrange is the point, don't smooth it out with drive
- ③ Tremolo: OFF if [[PanMan]] is doing the movement downstream (Pianet patches often ship with tremolo baked in — check before layering PanMan)
- ⑧ Internal FX: reverb OFF always
- ① Velocity curve: standard per-controller default — the Pianet's dynamic range is narrower than a Rhodes, don't overcompress the curve trying to fix that

## In context
- Same BED-slot role as [[Keyscape — Rhodes LA Custom E & Mark I]] and [[Keyscape — Wurlitzer 140B & 200A]]; pick it specifically for a vintage/psych-pop reference rather than neo-soul or funk-rock.
