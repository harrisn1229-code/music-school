# PhaseMistress

![[Images/PhaseMistress.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Soundtoys

## What it is

Soundtoys' analog-modeled phaser effect with multiple classic phaser circuit emulations.

## What it's used for

Classic sweeping phaser effects on guitars, keys, and synths.

## FX chain notes

Used as an insert effect, post-EQ.
