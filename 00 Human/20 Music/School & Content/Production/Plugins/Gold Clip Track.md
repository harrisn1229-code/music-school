# Gold Clip Track

![[Images/Gold Clip Track.jpg]]

**Category:** Compressor/Limiter
**Vendor:** Schwabe Digital

## What it is

The track/channel-optimized variant of Schwabe Digital's Gold Clip, tuned for individual track use rather than bus/master clipping.

## What it's used for

Adding clipping-based loudness/character to individual tracks (drums, bass) rather than a full mix bus.

## FX chain notes

Placed near the end of an individual track's chain, before any bus-level processing.
