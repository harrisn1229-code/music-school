# BLU-C05 — Call and response is the form

**Q.** How is call and response built into the 12-bar form, and how does it show up in performance?

> [!question]- Answer
> The form **is** call and response: **AAB**. Bars 1–4 state a line (**call**), 5–8 **repeat** it, 9–12 **answer** it. In performance the vocal states a phrase and the guitar or horn answers in the gaps — **the space in the form is where the response lives**.

## Why it matters

The repeat in bars 5–8 is the structural point — it gives the listener the line twice before resolving, and gives the singer time to land the answer. It's also why blues arrangements leave so much room.

## Drill

Take any blues lyric and mark its A / A / B sections against the twelve bars.
