# UAD Cooper Time Cube Mk II

![[Images/UAD Cooper Time Cube Mk II.png]]

**Category:** Delay
**Vendor:** Universal Audio — owned (confirmed 2026-07-17).

## What it is

Emulation of the Cooper Time Cube — the famous mechanical (garden-hose) acoustic delay, giving short, dark, characterful slap and doubling delays.

## What it's used for

Vintage slapback and thickening delays on vocals and guitars; its band-limited tone sits behind a source without cluttering the top end.
