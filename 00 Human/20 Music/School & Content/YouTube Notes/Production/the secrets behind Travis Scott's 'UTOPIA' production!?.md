## Techniques

### God's Country (eerie R&B lane)
- **Simple one-note repeating synth pad**, doubled by a **distorted synth playing the exact same melody as the vocal** — the distorted-synth double is what reads as "R&B" per the video, not a separate hook part. → [[Melody Writing]], [[BED Slot]]
- **Clone the vocal into two differently-processed layers, not one chain doing two jobs:** Layer 1 = the "any vocal" default chain — [[Auto-Tune Pro]] → Reverb → EQ. Layer 2 = built specifically to cut through the mix — a **guitar amp** (maps to owned [[Morgan Amps Suite]]) into an EQ, run on the vocal itself rather than a guitar. → [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]], [[Saturation and Distortion]]
- **Synth accent as a finishing layer** — added last, after the vocal and pad layers are set, described as the catchy signature touch.
- **Hi-hats are "the most important part of the beat"** — frantic rolls followed by deliberate empty space, leaving room for a separate "Travis hat" texture layer to sit in the gaps rather than filling continuously. → [[Rhythm and Groove]], [[Genre Drum Patterns]]
- Drum build order: clap first, then hi-hats, then the 808 last.

### MY EYES / TELEKINESIS (monologic/introspective lane)
- **Gentle synthy electric piano, all major chords**, for the soft opening character. → [[BED Slot]]
- **"Bursting through the mix" arrival technique — EQ low-pass + automation, not a new part.** Grab a second, more synth-y key sound, copy the SAME chords onto it, then put an EQ with a low-pass filter on it and automate the filter open — carefully choosing WHERE the highs re-enter rather than one continuous ramp — so the harsh top-end "bursts through" at chosen moments instead of gradually fading in. A hand-placed variant of the filter-reveal move already on [[EQ]]/[[Automation]] (usually drawn as one long ramp across a section) — here the reopening is deliberately punctuated, not smooth. → [[EQ]], [[Automation]], [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- Simple filtered drums under the gentle opening: a snare and a perc layer, together.
- **Tempo switch-up between beats: automate tempo 120 → 155 BPM, leave the last chord ringing through the transition** rather than cutting it — the sustained chord bridges the tempo change instead of a hard cut. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]

### The fast-paced trap switch-up (glitchy melody, inspired by a Wallace Lane sample on TELEKINESIS — name uncertain from captions)
- **Thick filtered synth brass** plays the melodic chords; the bounce in the progression comes from note movement, not the sound design — described explicitly as "the key to this progression."
- **A "shaper box" processing stage layered under the chords** — reads as [[Plugin Wishlist]]'s already-tracked **Cableguys ShaperBox 3** (medium-impact wishlist item), used here for rhythmic movement/bounce on the brass chords, not just the sidechain-ducking use case already logged. → [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- A subtle synth bass under the chords, EQ'd to remove excess low end so it doesn't compete with the 808.
- **Sample-flip as the whole part's identity: bounce to audio → chop → reverse → double the speed.** Four sequential audio-domain moves (not MIDI edits) turn one source into the lead/melodic hook. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- Drums: two-step hi-hats with **alternating velocities** plus a few fill rolls, a short punchy snare, a "classic Wonder Girl [likely **Wondagurl**, Travis Scott's frequent 808/drum collaborator] style kick," and a sub 808.

### HYENA / SIRENS (the two "craziest" beats — sample-flip from a 1970s Zambian record)
- **Frantic, deliberately offbeat guitar melody** for a humanized, non-gridded feel — stated as the point of the part (contrast with the hi-hat two-step precision above). → [[Pocket and Microtiming]]
- **Processing chain on the guitar melody: Compressor → guitar amp → harmonizer → half-time effect → EQ.** The half-time stage is explicitly NOT meant to be heard directly on SIRENS — its purpose is to free up space for a separate layer stacked on top of it, i.e. used as a texture-generator/spacing device rather than a heard effect. Maps to [[Plugin Wishlist]]'s already-tracked **Cableguys HalfTime** (previously logged from the Bryson Tiller breakdown at a partial ~70% mix; here used as a full/mostly-full effect specifically to carve room, a new use case for the same device). → [[Compression]], [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **HYENA's main instrument: a thin mandolin/ukulele-like string sound**, sourced from a free ukulele instrument plugin (unowned, name not given) — melody copied from the guitar part with a few notes removed for a sparser read. → [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Breakdown section: acoustic drums from a loaded Kontakt bank/pattern, plus a bass part** — maps to owned [[Kontakt 7]]. → [[Kontakt 7]]
- **HYENA's main drums are "dark and grimy" — MPC-driven feel** (per the video, Travis made much of *UTOPIA* on an MPC). Build: a couple of snares layered WITH a vocal sample, a shaker, a low dark [tom-type element], and **three separate kicks layered together** specifically for extra swing/"air"/character (not one kick — three, deliberately layered for groove). 808 last. → [[Layering and Stacking]], [[Snare Design]], [[Rhythm and Groove]]
- **The switch-up: Mike Dean adds a synth pad with glitchy vocal chops**, then the earlier breakdown-section sample material is brought back in with a different 808 pattern to close the beat out. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
