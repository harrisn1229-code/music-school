# Twin 3

![[Images/Twin 3.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** FabFilter

## What it is

FabFilter's current synthesizer, combining subtractive, FM, and wavetable-style synthesis with an extensive modulation matrix.

## What it's used for

Flexible synth duty spanning classic subtractive tones to modern wavetable-style textures.

## FX chain notes

Used as the instrument source; often paired with FabFilter Saturn/Pro-Q downstream for shaping.

## Techniques

- **A "super vibey" Twin pad as one of only four founding elements of Billie Eilish's "Birds of a Feather" — laid down within the song's first five to ten minutes alongside a chopped drum loop, a chopped tambourine loop, and the instrumental "underlick" motif.** Finneas, on the instrument directly: "we've got a Kontakt plug-in going on here. This is a Twin II. Couldn't love the Twin more." (The video's Twin 2 is the predecessor to the owned Twin 3; the plugin is a standalone FabFilter synth, not actually Kontakt-hosted — likely a loose in-the-moment description rather than the literal host.) Used as a core harmonic pad from the very start of the arrangement, not a late texture add. *(qualitative only — no patch/preset settings given in the transcript)* → [[Chord Progressions]], [[Motivic Development]], [[Sound Selection Guide]] — Finneas, [[Behind the Track - 'BIRDS OF A FEATHER' by Billie Eilish]]
