## Synopsis

A rapid-fire FAQ format answering Google's autocomplete questions about music theory ("is music theory hard," "is it necessary," "is it the same for every instrument," etc.) — general meta-commentary rather than instructional content.

## What's taught

General framing rather than technique: theory feels dense from the outside but clicks once applied to real songs you already know, similar to how driving theory only makes sense alongside actual driving practice. Theory's necessity is genre/role-dependent — most valuable for communicating with other musicians (band settings, learning cover songs) and least necessary for a solo singer-songwriter working entirely alone. Theory itself doesn't change between instruments (a C major chord is a C major chord regardless of who's playing it), but how much theory a given instrument's learning culture emphasizes varies — guitarists are cited as often skipping formal theory longer than other instrumentalists, partly because tab notation doesn't require it.
