# ARR-C10 — Too busy is a relative term, and the artist sets it

**Q.** Give the simplicity referee: the number, and the principle behind it.

> [!question]- Answer
> **Five to seven sounds — the extra perc layer always matters less than it feels like it does while you're producing.** Lil Baby cut Kenny Beats off at that count on "Toes" and "Smack the Baby". The principle is that **"too busy" is relative**, so it needs a referee outside your own ears: let the performer or the vocal decide, because the artist is the best common denominator for what the record actually needs.

## Why it matters

The bias only runs one way — nobody over-strips while producing, everyone over-adds, and each layer feels essential at the moment you place it. Naming a number gives you something to check against when the loop is still fun to build on. It's also the counterweight to the rest of this lesson: scheduled absence only works if there's a limit on what got scheduled in.

## Drill

Count the sounds in the beat you're on. Over seven, mute the one you added most recently and play it back.
