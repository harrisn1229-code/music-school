# dnksaus - The Underrated Spectral Stock Plugins in Ableton

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoYHYsrnjYo)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoYHYsrnjYo)

## Synopsis

A dedicated tour of the spectral-processing devices Live 11 Suite quietly added — **Spectral Time** (a frequency-bin delay with Tilt/Mask/Spray controls and a built-in freezer), **Spectral Resonator** (a MIDI-drivable resonant texture device dnksaus calls "a robotic vocoder that isn't a vocoder"), and **Spectral Blur** (a clearer freeze/smear effect that ships free via Ableton's Creative Extensions pack, making it "half stock"). This is the dedicated full-treatment video that "Scale-Aware Abuse" (earlier in this queue) deliberately deferred Spectral Resonator's coverage to. dnksaus demos each device building a track section live — Spectral Time laser-delaying a lead with Tilt/Spray/Mask, Spectral Resonator taking MIDI chords from a Wavetable track to resonate a vocal, and Spectral Blur freezing a vocal into instant atmosphere — then closes with a rundown of his own paid Max for Live spectral devices on Patreon/Gumroad (Hologram, Liquid Mask, Spectral Shift), noting Hologram gets its own dedicated video next in this queue.

## Techniques

- **Spectral Time — Tilt orders which frequencies delay first** — a linear frequency-bin sweep: positive Tilt (e.g. +340 ms) delays the lowest frequency first and the highest last, up to the Tilt time; negative Tilt (e.g. -240 ms) reverses the order. Used in his own tracks "Vitamin D" (Tilt forward on an octave-up switch, for a wet/watery transition) and "Bread" (as a section-to-section transition device). → [[Spectral Time]]
- **Spectral Time — Freeze with Re-trigger (note-aware or time-synced)** — Freeze locks the current harmonic content; Re-trigger can fire on new incoming notes, or sync by time with a Rate knob refreshing the freeze on an interval, for a hands-off continuously-refreshing texture. → [[Spectral Time]]
- **Spectral Time — Spray (airy scramble) and Mask (transient vs. laser split)** — Spray randomly scrambles each bin's delay timing for an airy, scattered "laser" texture; Mask shifted left limits the delay to lower frequencies (more transient-like), shifted right limits it to higher frequencies (more laser-feedback). Plus a built-in Shifter section usable as an automatable switch-up effect. → [[Spectral Time]], [[Automation]]
- **Spectral Resonator — tuned resonance + MIDI-driven chords** — resonates at a tunable frequency/note with stretch/pitch-shift on top; turning on MIDI input from another track (Poly mode) lets an external chord progression (demoed from a [[Wavetable]] track) drive what pitches it resonates, applied to a vocal for a robotic, non-vocoder character. Built-in Chorus (random harmonic modulation) and Granular modes add further movement; turning Harmonics down on a familiar sample (Beastie Boys "Intergalactic") gets extremely wet. → [[Spectral Resonator]], [[Wavetable]]
- **Spectral Blur — a clearer freeze for instant vocal atmosphere** — free via Ableton's Creative Extensions pack; functions like Spectral Time's Freeze but reads noticeably clearer — freeze a vocal anywhere and it becomes a standalone ambient bed on its own. → [[Spectral Blur]]
- **His own paid spectral M4L devices (Patreon/Gumroad)** — **Hologram** (a spectral gate — full dedicated coverage next in this queue), **Liquid Mask** (draws custom wet-filter shapes, Shape control, squeaky at high FFT settings especially stacked with several [[OTT]]s), and **Spectral Shift** (a "cloudy" pitch-shifter; a second shifter-type device was mentioned too quickly/garbled to name reliably). → [[Plugin Wishlist]]
