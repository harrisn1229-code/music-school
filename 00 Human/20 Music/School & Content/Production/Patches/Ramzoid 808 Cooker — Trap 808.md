# Ramzoid 808 Cooker — Trap 808

**Instrument:** [[Ramzoid 808 Cooker]] · **Slot:** [[SUB Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]]

## What it's for
Purpose-built trap 808 design — pick a base tone from the plugin's 99 categorized presets, dial drive/character in-instrument, done. The fast alternative to sample-based 808s ([[Simpler — 808 Sample (Choke + Glide)]]) or building one from scratch ([[Operator — Clean FM Sub]]).

## Settings
- Start from a preset in the 99-patch browser closest to the target length/character (long & sustained vs short & bouncy), then adjust rather than building from a blank slate
- ADSR: attack instant, sustain full for a held sub; shorten decay/release for a bouncier, more rhythmic 808
- Glide: on, for pitch-bend slides between notes (the plugin's dedicated glide knob — same role as Simpler's glide or Operator's portamento)
- Distortion: multiband waveshaper, light-to-moderate — pushes harmonic content up into the BASS-slot overtone range so the 808 still reads on small speakers, without losing SUB-slot weight
- Pre/post-FX EQ: use the pre-distortion EQ to shape what gets driven (scoop what shouldn't distort), the post-FX EQ for the final tonal trim
- Stereo Width: keep at or near mono — subs stay centered ([[Stereo Field]] mono rule)

## In context
- 1 voice, mono only — never stack two subs in the same section ([[SUB Slot]] rule).
- Followed by saturation and a limiter in the chain to control sub-bass level, per [[Ramzoid 808 Cooker]]'s FX chain notes.
