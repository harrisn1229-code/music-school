# 5 Beginner Guitar Habits You're Guilty Of

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y4cVULtdSL0)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y4cVULtdSL0)

## Synopsis

Five common beginner habits: downpicking everything instead of alternate picking, squeezing the neck too hard, double notes from hand-sync issues, watching the picking hand too much, and avoiding pick use based on fingerstyle outlier examples (Matteo Mancuso, Mark Knopfler). Three of the five (downpick-only, grip squeeze, double notes) duplicate ground already covered by [[14 Technical Mistakes Every Intermediate Guitarist Makes]] — only the two new points were routed further.

## Techniques

- **Habit 1 — downpicking only.** Fix: alternate picking, drilled via 3 minutes of continuous alternate-picked playing daily at a comfortable tempo. *(Already covered generally by [[Directional Alternate Picking System]] and [[Fretting and Picking Hand Mistakes Checklist]].)*
- **Habit 2 — squeezing the neck too hard.** Already on [[Fretting and Picking Hand Mistakes Checklist]] as the minimum-pressure test.
- **Habit 3 — double notes from hand-sync issues.** Already on [[Fretting and Picking Hand Mistakes Checklist]].
- **Habit 4 — watching the picking hand too much (new).** A normal beginner reflex, but one worth deliberately breaking so muscle memory replaces the need for visual confirmation.
- **Habit 5 — using fingerstyle outliers as an excuse to avoid the pick (new).** Citing Matteo Mancuso or Mark Knopfler (who don't use picks) as justification for skipping pick technique doesn't hold up unless the goal is specifically chasing their fingerstyle sound. A pick slipping out of the hand early on is normal and self-resolves with reps — not a reason to abandon it.
