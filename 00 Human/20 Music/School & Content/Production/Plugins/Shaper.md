# Shaper

![[Images/Shaper.jpg]]

**Category:** Utility
**Vendor:** Max for Live

## What it is

A Max for Live modulation device that generates a drawn/custom modulation shape to control mapped parameters over time.

## What it's used for

Custom modulation curves for parameters that need a shape LFO/step modulators can't produce.

## FX chain notes

Runs inline in the signal/MIDI chain at the point it's inserted; combines with whatever device follows.

- **How Shaper's mapping modes actually work (Live 12)** — press **Map**, then click almost any Ableton parameter to modulate it. **Mod** mode (new in Live 12) lets you keep hand-turning the mapped knob while the modulation is still running; the **+/−** toggle sets **bipolar** (moves both directions from the knob's center) vs **unipolar** (one direction only — more expressive for automation-style moves, though not everyone likes the feel). Pressing Mod again switches to **Remote** mode, which drives the parameter across its full defined range instead. **Manual** mode (also new in Live 12) turns the drawn curve into something you scrub through non-linearly by hand rather than something that plays back on a clock — useful for storing a mix of exponential and linear curves as a *performable* shape inside a rack, distinct from a fixed automation clip. Turn off **Snap** to draw freely, hold **Option** while drawing for curves rather than straight segments. → [[Randomized Rack Design]] — dnksaus, [I fixed ableton's Shaper](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=45l00IhqVd4)
- **Why dnksaus built his own "Multi-Shaper" fix** — stacking several stock Shapers in Manual mode (each mapped to a macro, then mouse-mashed for random curves) to get one expressive "wamp"/bass automation patch works, but is slow to hand-build one parameter at a time; see [[Plugin Wishlist]] for his free Max for Live replacement that randomizes eight Shaper-style curves at once. — dnksaus, [I fixed ableton's Shaper](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=45l00IhqVd4)
- **Manual-mode probability biasing** — instead of letting a Random-mode [[LFO]] make a straight 50/50 coin-flip decision (e.g. whether a resurfaced loop plays or not), route Shaper in **Manual** mode (a simple hand-drawn shape, Mode = Manual) into the signal path and map a Random-mode LFO to Shaper's manual-value knob; skewing the LFO's max value past 50% biases the odds so the triggered event happens less (or more) often than a plain coin flip — smooth the result to avoid clicks. A finer-grained alternative to a raw Binary/Random LFO wherever a probability needs tuning rather than a hard 50/50. → [[Randomized Rack Design]], [[Looper]] — dnksaus, [looper is actually a SOUND BLENDER](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fDTkW7H4FaE)
