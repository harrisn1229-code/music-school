# Ramzoid 808 Cooker

![[Images/Ramzoid 808 Cooker.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Ramzoid

## What it is

A trap/hip hop 808 bass sound-design plugin by producer/YouTuber Ramzoid, offering quick synthesis and processing of 808 sub-bass sounds.

## What it's used for

Fast 808 bass sound design for trap and hip hop production.

## FX chain notes

Used as the 808/bass instrument source, typically followed by saturation and a limiter to control sub-bass level.

## Used in my chains

An **808 instrument** (Ramzoid — instrument source, not FX slot) — a sampler of 808 sub-bass with a powerful multiband distortion section built in, so a lot of the [[1 Digital Bass Track|808 make-or-break]] processing happens at the source:

- A **bass source** on [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]] for trap/hip hop 808s — its multiband distortion does the same "drive the heard band, keep the sub clean" job as the [[Saturn 2]] 808 move, in the instrument. Glide for the classic 808 pitch slides.
