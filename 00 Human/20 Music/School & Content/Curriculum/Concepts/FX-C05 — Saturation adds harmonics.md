# FX-C05 — Saturation adds harmonics

**Q.** Beyond "warmth" — what does saturation do to a signal, in terms of harmonics?

> [!question]- Answer
> It **generates new harmonic content that wasn't in the source**. **Even-order (2nd)** sit at octaves and read warm and full; **odd-order (3rd)** add edge and grit. It also rounds transient peaks, so a saturated signal reads louder and denser at the same meter reading.

## Why it matters

'Adds harmonics and distortion' is the core of it. The even/odd split is the half that turns it into a *choice* rather than a texture you either like or don't.

## Drill

On one source, find a saturator with a tone or bias control. Push it toward even, then odd. Name the difference.
