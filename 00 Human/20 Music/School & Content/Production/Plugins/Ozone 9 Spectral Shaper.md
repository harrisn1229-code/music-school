# Ozone 9 Spectral Shaper

![[Images/Ozone 9 Spectral Shaper.png]]

**Category:** EQ
**Vendor:** iZotope

## What it is

Ozone 9's dynamic, frequency-specific shaping module that combines EQ and dynamics in narrow adjustable bands.

## What it's used for

Targeted dynamic EQ moves on a master — taming resonances or harshness that change over time.

## FX chain notes

Used alongside/after the main EQ module for problem-frequency dynamic control.
