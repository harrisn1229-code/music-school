# kHs Ladder Filter

![[Images/kHs Ladder Filter.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A Moog-style ladder filter emulation snap-in known for its warm resonant character.

## What it's used for

Classic analog-style resonant filtering and filter-sweep sound design.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain, often paired with distortion for a classic synth-filter drive sound.

|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Cutoff | Filter cutoff frequency | Sweep/automate for classic analog filter movement |

| ④ | Saturate | Enables saturation in the filter path (toggle) | On to let Drive add analog grit |
| ⑤ | Drive | Saturation amount into the ladder | Push for analog grit as the filter self-drives |
| ⑥ | Bias | DC bias into the saturation stage (asymmetry) | Add for even-harmonic, asymmetric warmth |
