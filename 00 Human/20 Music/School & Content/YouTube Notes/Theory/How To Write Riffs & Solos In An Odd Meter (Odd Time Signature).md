# How To Write Riffs & Solos In An Odd Meter (Odd Time Signature)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LA8Ucl_oDbI)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LA8Ucl_oDbI)

## Synopsis

A third BERNTH odd-meter video — the core 2-2-3-in-7/8 grouping trick is already well-documented on [[Time Signatures & Note Values]] from two other sources; this video's genuinely new content is the drum-arrangement perception trick, writing odd-meter leads with their own phrase arc, and layering harmonic-minor/diminished color into an odd-meter line.

## What's taught

- **Odd-meter grouping (2-2-3 in 7/8):** duplicate of already-routed content — see [[Time Signatures & Note Values]].
- **Drum arrangement changes whether a riff reads as odd-meter or syncopated 4/4.** Demoed replaying an identical 7/8 guitar riff with a straighter drum beat underneath — the listener's perception shifts toward "syncopated 4/4" even though the guitar part is unchanged, since it's the drums' accent placement (on the riff's own note-groups vs. elsewhere) that determines the felt meter.
- **Write odd-meter leads with their own internal phrase arc**, not as a familiar-length phrase simply cut one note short — a stated composition goal on top of the grouping mechanics.
- **Layer harmonic-minor/diminished color into an odd-meter line** — the raised 7th (harmonic minor) and the ♭5 (diminished) as added melodic interest alongside the metric device.
- **Names konnakol** (Carnatic Indian rhythm-solfège) as a recommended alternative to numeric counting, without teaching it in this video.
