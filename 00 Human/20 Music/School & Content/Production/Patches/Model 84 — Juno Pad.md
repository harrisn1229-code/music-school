# Model 84 — Juno Pad

**Instrument:** [[Model 84 Polyphonic Synthesizer]] · **Slot:** [[BED Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Synth Bed Track|Synth Bed Track]]

## What it's for
The Juno pad lane — saw + sub, chorus on, slow filter: the 80s/indie warm bed in 30 seconds. A middle-ground standard bed, not as bright as [[Model 84 Polyphonic Synthesizer#Patch Recipes|the 80s polysynth/Synthwave variant]] and not as dark as its bedroom-pop soft-bloom recipe — those two are the bright and dark extremes of this same DCO setup.

## Settings
- ① DCO: saw full + sub ~50%, pulse off
- ③ HPF position 2 — thins the pad so the mix's low end stays clean (contrast with [[Model 84 — Juno Bass]]'s position-0 bump)
- ④ VCF ~50–60% open, res ~25–30%, slow manual filter rides across the section rather than LFO automation
- ⑤ Env → VCF: light amount, slow attack for a gentle swell-in
- ⑥ **Chorus II ON** — non-negotiable, this is the sound
- ⑧ Poly, no unison — pads use voice count for the chord, not fattening
- Attack 0.5–2 s, release 1–3 s (Step 1a universal pad rule)

## In context
- Swappable with [[Omnisphere — Layered Dark+Airy Bed]] or a Keyscape Rhodes/Wurli — same slot.
- No sub octave beyond the DCO's own sub mix — the chain HPFs the track anyway.
