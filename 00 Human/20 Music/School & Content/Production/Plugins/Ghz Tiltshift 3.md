# Ghz Tiltshift 3

![[Images/Ghz Tiltshift 3.png]]

**Category:** EQ
**Vendor:** Goodhertz

## What it is

Goodhertz's Tiltshift, a single-knob tilt EQ that simultaneously boosts one end of the spectrum while cutting the other around a pivot point.

## What it's used for

Fast overall tonal balance adjustments (brighter/darker) without multi-band EQ complexity.

## FX chain notes

A broad tonal-balance pass, often on a mix bus, before more surgical EQ.
