# Synthet - The Vocal Trick to Like Yourself

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dulq8IJR9nI)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dulq8IJR9nI)

## Synopsis

A personal, practical take on vocal self-consciousness aimed at non-professional-singer producers: stacking multiple takes of your own voice makes it easier to accept than a single exposed take, syllable/consonant sync across the stack is what separates "professional" from "sloppy" sounding doubles, harmonies help further by making it feel less like "just you" singing, and pitch/formant shifting (demoed via an AI voice-conversion sponsor tool, but the underlying idea is general) can transform a take into a different vocal character entirely. Ends on a grounded note: the goal isn't to sound like a "fantastic vocalist," it's finding a register and treatment you're comfortable with.

## Techniques

- **Stacking a vocal makes an imperfect take easier to accept than a single exposed take** — recording the same phrase multiple times and layering them reads as more "tolerable"/professional to the singer's own ear than any one take alone (a psychological effect as much as a technical one). → [[1 Backup Vocal Track]]
- **Syllable/consonant sync is what separates professional from sloppy stacks** — a stacked phrase where one take's consonant (e.g. the "f" in "phone") lands a fraction off from the others reads as unprofessional; sync the syllable timing across takes as you record (or manually adjust afterward) rather than leaving it to chance.
- **Harmonies help mask self-consciousness, not just add musical value** — adding a harmony line makes a stack feel less like "just you" singing alone, both musically and psychologically for the performer.
- **Try singing in different "voices" within a stack** — deliberately varying vocal character/register take-to-take on a stack can read as a small choir of different people rather than one voice multiplied. → [[Layering and Stacking]]
- **Pitch + formant shifting builds a different vocal character from the same take** — adjust pitch and formant together (not just pitch alone) to create a distinct character — pushed one way for a deeper/darker EDM-adjacent voice, the other way for a lighter children's-choir-adjacent voice. Demoed via an AI voice-conversion sponsor tool, but the underlying pitch+formant move is available in any formant-capable pitch plugin. → [[Pitch and Formant]]
- **The goal is a register/treatment you're comfortable with, not "fixing" your voice** — heavy effects/auto-tune used purely to mask a voice can also mask what's actually good about a take; the framing here is finding what you like rather than erasing the original character entirely.
