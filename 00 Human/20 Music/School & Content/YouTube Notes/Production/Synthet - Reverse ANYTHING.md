# Synthet - Reverse ANYTHING

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tvErmRA0jAk)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tvErmRA0jAk)

## Synopsis

A survey of reverse tricks across several source types: reversing chopped-up guitar chords for a transition-friendly effect, playing the reverse of an *upcoming* chord just before it lands (a pre-echo that primes the listener for the change), a fast reverse-reverb recipe via Edison, reversing whole samples (especially effective on toms), reversing a MIDI melody for fresh idea generation, and reversing recorded vocal ad-libs to flip an exclamation into something closer to a question.

## Techniques

- **Reverse the *upcoming* chord and place it just before the change** — chop each guitar chord into its own clip (Shift+drag to duplicate, "make unique"), reverse the copy, and place that reversed copy immediately before the chord it previews — primes the listener for an incoming change; a transition device, not just a texture. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Fast reverse-reverb recipe** — select the sample, Ctrl+E to open it in Edison, Ctrl+R to add reverb, Alt+Left-Arrow to reverse it, drag it back into the track. Works well building up to a vocal entrance; try cutting the reversed tail just before the bar ends. → [[Reverb]]
- **Reverse whole samples for texture — toms called out as especially good candidates.** Small reverses ("really small ones") add energy; works on essentially any sample, not just percussion.
- **Reverse a MIDI melody (Alt+drag/select-all-and-reverse in the piano roll) to generate new ideas when stuck** — export the reversed version and reverse it again and you're back to the original melody, but *played with a different feel* than the original take (a way to break out of a rut without changing a single note choice).
- **Reverse vocal ad-libs/exclamations** — flips an exclamation-mark delivery into something that reads closer to a question mark; a character/emotion trick, not just a sound-design one.
