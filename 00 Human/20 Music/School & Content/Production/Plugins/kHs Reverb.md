# kHs Reverb

![[Images/kHs Reverb.png]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A general-purpose algorithmic reverb snap-in within the Kilohearts ecosystem.

## What it's used for

Adding space and ambience within a modular effects chain.

## FX chain notes

Used as a send or insert reverb stage in a Snap Heap chain.

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Decay | Tail length | Short for body; long for space |
| ② | Dampen | High-frequency absorption of the tail | More damping to darken and sit the reverb behind the mix |
| ③ | Size | Perceived room size | Small for ambience/glue; large for a wash |
| ④ | Width | Stereo width of the reverb | Narrow to keep it centered; wide for a big space |
| ⑤ | Early | Level of the early reflections | More for a closer, roomier sense of space |
| ⑥ | Mix | Dry/wet blend | Low on inserts; 100% on a send |
