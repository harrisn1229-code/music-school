# MIX-C07 — What a mastering engineer needs

**Q.** What does a mastering engineer need from you, and what makes their job harder?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Needs:** a full-resolution WAV at native rate, 24-bit, real headroom, nothing on the 2-bus, plus a reference and a note on intent — and the running order and gaps if it's a release. **Harder:** an already-limited mix, sample-rate conversion, an MP3 anywhere in the lineage, or a problem buried in the mix that mastering can only make louder rather than fix.

## Why it matters

The summary concept — it's every other MIX concept applied at once.

## Drill

Write the two-line email you'd send with a mix. What's attached, and what does the note say?
