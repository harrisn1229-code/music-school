# BAS-C04 — Busy basslines only work under static harmony

**Q.** When can the bass show off, and when can it only play roots?

> [!question]- Answer
> Showcase basslines — Chris Brown "Fine China", Thundercat — work **over static held chords**. When the harmony above is busy, **only root notes work**. And with a vocal or a synth hook on top, the bass supports; it doesn't compete. The corollary is the useful half: **two chords means the riffs ARE the line** — static harmony moves all the interest into the fills between roots, and one perfectly placed fill can be the entire event.

## Why it matters

It reframes "how busy should the bass be" as a question about what else is moving, rather than a taste call. Most overwritten basslines are written against harmony that was already doing the work.

## Drill

Take a busy bassline. Freeze the harmony to two chords and hear it come alive. Then put a vocal on top and hear it stop working.
