# RHY-C01 — Compound meter is felt in dotted beats

**Q.** In 6/8, how many beats are in a bar and what note value gets the beat?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Two beats, and the dotted quarter gets the beat.** Six eighth notes are *written*; they group into **two dotted-quarter pulses**.

## Why it matters

Compound time signatures lie: the top number is the *division* count, not the beat count. Divide by 3 for the real beat count, and the beat unit is always a dotted note. The written half (six eighths) is the easy one; the felt half is what matters when you're playing it.

## Drill

Count a 6/8 bar out loud in six, then in two. Then do 9/8 and 12/8.
