# FND-C03 — Reading a key signature (sharps)

**Q.** How do you work out the major key from a key signature with sharps?

> [!question]- Answer
> Take the **last sharp** and go **up a half step**. Last sharp G♯ → **A major**.

## Why it matters

This is half of FND-18, where you knew the count mattered but not the method. It works because the last sharp is always the leading tone — scale degree 7 — which sits a semitone below the tonic.

## Drill

Name the key: 2 sharps · 3 sharps · 4 sharps · 5 sharps. Then work backwards — how many sharps in B major?
