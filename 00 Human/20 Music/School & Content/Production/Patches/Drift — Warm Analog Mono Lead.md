# Drift — Warm Analog Mono Lead

**Instrument:** [[Drift]] · **Slot:** [[LEAD Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Synth Lead Track|Synth Lead Track]]

## What it's for
The warm, analog, mono synthwave/indie lead — a single played line with real glide and a bit of circuit imperfection, not a stacked wall of unison.

## Settings
- From [[Drift#Patch Recipes|Drift — Lead / pluck]]: Voice Mode Mono
- Osc 1: Pulse or Saw, ② Env1 → Shape (fast decay) for a brightening pluck attack
- ⑥ Optional Cycling Envelope → cutoff for added rhythmic movement
- ④ Drift: light — analog glue on the pitch, not audible wobble
- ⑩ Glide ~60–100 ms, legato on
- Kill content below ~100 Hz; vibrato via mod-wheel LFO, not baked in
- FX: tempo-synced delay send

## In context
- This is the "warm analog mono" default for synthwave/indie leads; [[Model 84 — Juno Lead]] is the same lane on the real Juno-circuit model when you want the chorus-driven version instead.
- Mono source — width, if any, comes from the chain, not this patch ([[Stereo Field]]).
