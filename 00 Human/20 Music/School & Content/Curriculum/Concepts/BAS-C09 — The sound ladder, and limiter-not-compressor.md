# BAS-C09 — The sound ladder, and limiter-not-compressor

**Q.** The bass part is right but the tone is wrong. What's the escalation, and how do you control it?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Walk the ladder:** synth patch → a different synth → a real or sampled bass guitar (Scarbee Rickenbacker, NI Motown, Trilian) → DI plus amp sim. Then control the level with a **limiter, not a compressor**, saturate for body, and **sidechain last**. Don't skip rungs by processing a patch that was never going to get there.

## Why it matters

Same lesson as swapping a kick sample rather than stacking chains — the fix is upstream. The limiter choice matters because bass needs a hard ceiling with its dynamics intact, not gain-riding that pumps the low end.

## Drill

Take a bass part whose tone you dislike. Walk one full rung up the ladder before touching a device. Then add limiter → saturation → sidechain, in that order.
