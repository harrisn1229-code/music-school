## Techniques

- **Named source for Jake One's "bright, unorthodox" synth sound on Travis Scott's *A Man*.** The actual unit cited is the **Oberheim SEM**, not the OB-Xa — no owned SEM emulation exists, so this OB-Xa note is the best-match Oberheim-family stand-in (family character, not a literal SEM substitute). The technique itself: chords voiced deliberately bright/unusual so they stand out from the mix rather than blend, then **attack time automated per-chord** so some notes play as short stabs and others sustain longer — a reusable automation-lane move independent of the specific synth. → [[Automation]], [[Guitar Rig 7 FX]] (wah reused on this same synth part), [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[Vintage Gear Worklist]] — loner, [the secret samplemaker behind Southside's best beats!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WLOHe9CJChY)
