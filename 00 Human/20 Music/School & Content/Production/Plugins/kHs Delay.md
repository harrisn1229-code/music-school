# kHs Delay

![[Images/kHs Delay.png]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A tempo-syncable delay snap-in for rhythmic echo effects.

## What it's used for

Rhythmic delay effects on vocals, synths, and drums within a modular chain.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain, often paired with a filter snap-in to shape the repeats.

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Time | Delay time — the ms readout; free or tempo-synced via the clock button | Sync to 1/8 or dotted for rhythmic echoes |
| ② | Tone | Filter/tone of the repeats | Darken so echoes sit behind the dry |
| ③ | Feedback | Number of repeats | Higher for longer trails |
| ④ | Pan | Stereo position / offset of the repeats | Offset for a stereo bounce feel |
| ⑤ | Duck | Ducks the delay under the dry signal | Keeps echoes out of the way until phrase ends |
| ⑥ | Bounce | Ping-pong toggle — bounces repeats L↔R | On for a stereo ping-pong delay |
| ⑦ | Mix | Dry/wet blend | Low as an insert; high on a send |
