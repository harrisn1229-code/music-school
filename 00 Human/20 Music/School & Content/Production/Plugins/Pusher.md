# Pusher

![[Images/Pusher.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Kush Audio

## What it is

Kush Audio's console-emulation/punch plugin, adding analog-console-style harmonic density and transient punch.

## What it's used for

Adding console-style 'glue' and punch to individual tracks or a mix bus.

## FX chain notes

Used late in a channel or bus chain for final character, often just before a limiter.

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Flux Drive | Magnetizes **all four cores** at once — the main drive control. | This is the master saturation knob. Everything else shapes what the drive does; start here. | — |
| ② | Bias | Stresses the cores, shifting how they break up. | Same idea as tape bias — nudge it for a different flavour of distortion at the same Flux Drive setting. | — |
| ③ | Magnetic EQ (Steel / Cobalt / Iron / Nickel) | Four selectable core materials. **Each rounds transients and distorts differently.** | The real character control. Audition all four on the source before touching Flux Drive — they're more different from each other than any amount of drive. | — |
| ④ | Blend — Magnetics | Dry vs. magnetized balance for the saturation stage. | Parallel saturation built in: drive hard, then blend back for weight without losing definition. | — |
| ⑤ | Threshold | Leveler threshold, with **Smacky / Diode / Limiter** modes. | Smacky for character, Limiter when you actually need control. The mode changes the whole feel of the dynamics stage. | — |
| ⑥ | Attack | Fast, Medium or Slow. | Slow lets transients through before the leveler grabs — the usual choice on drums. | — |
| ⑦ | Release | 50–1200 ms. | Time it to the track so any pumping is musical. | — |
| ⑧ | Sidechain Hi-Pass | 20–300 Hz high-pass on the detector. | Push to ~100 Hz on a full mix so the kick stops triggering the leveler on everything else. | — |
| ⑨ | Blend — Dynamics | Dry vs. processed balance for the leveler stage, with LEDs marking the blend points. | Separate from the magnetics blend — you can run heavy saturation with light dynamics, or the reverse. | — |
| ⑩ | Output | Output level. | — | — |
