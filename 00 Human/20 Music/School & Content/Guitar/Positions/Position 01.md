# Position 1 — Open Position (open–fret 4)

## Overview
- Fret range: open strings + frets 1–4
- Fingers: 1 → fret 1, 2 → fret 2, 3 → fret 3, 4 → fret 4 (open strings played with no finger).
- Lowest note: **E** (string 6, open) · Highest note: **G#** (string 1, fret 4)
- Interval span (lowest to highest note): **2 octaves + a major 3rd**

## Stretches
- **Pinky stretch (fret 5, one above the pinky):** 1 (E): A · 2 (B): E · 3 (G): C · 4 (D): G · 5 (A): D · 6 (E): A
- **Index stretch:** none below the nut — the open strings (already shown in the Open column) are as low as this position goes.

## Chord shapes in this position
- **Open ('cowboy') chords:** E, Em, E7 · A, Am, A7 · D, Dm, D7 · G, G7 · C, C7 · Am7, Em7, Dsus.
- **Barre shapes anchored at fret 1:** **F** (E-shape barre) · **B♭** (A-shape barre) — the first movable barre positions.
- These are the foundation chords; everything higher up is a *movable* version of the E/A/C/G/D (CAGED) shapes seen here. See [[Chords]] · [[Chords by Key]] · [[Seventh Chords]].

## Scales in this position
- **Open-position major scales:** C, G, D, A, E all lie naturally in open position (the Berklee Book 1 reading zone).
- **Pentatonic:** E minor pentatonic and A minor pentatonic sit in open position (roots on the open 6th/5th strings).
- Read melodies here first, then move the *same shapes* up the neck. See [[Scales]] · [[Scale Modes Reference]] · [[Circle of Fifths]].

## Arpeggios in this position
- **Open triad arpeggios:** E, A, D, G, C major · Em, Am, Dm minor — the chord tones of the open chords above.
- Practice arpeggiating each open chord to hear its 1–3–5. See [[Chords]] · [[Chord Inversions]] · [[Seventh Chords]].
- **Soloing use:** this zone is the first of three E major triad "positions" (open, 5th fret, 9th fret) used as a fretboard map for transposing blues lines — see [[Triads as a Fretboard Map]] — Jack Ruch, [[Triads! The Secret To Learning The Fretboard]].
