# Portal

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** "iZotope"

## What it is

A glitch/transition texture effect (buffer-freeze and granular-style processing) for building drops and creative transitions.

## What it's used for

Building drops/transitions, and generating "pedal-lock"-style evolving textures by browsing presets while recording continuously — capturing a moment worth keeping rather than programming a result upfront.

- **"Echoes" preset + a half-time device (1-bar setting) on a synth melody line, so the notes play back delayed rather than where they're triggered — a reversed/echoey accent effect rather than a straight melodic delay.** On a Brent Faiyaz-style ambient beat, a simple synth melody run through Portal's "Echoes" preset followed by a half-time device reads as delayed/reversed — the melody "doesn't actually play here, it plays a bit later," so the processed result lands almost like an accent or hit on beat one instead of a normal in-time phrase. → [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[Brent Faiyaz Ambient Synth Chain]] — loner, [how to make ambient synth beats for brent faiyaz (r&b music theory trick!?)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ugup5iVzGYY)
- **"Dreamy Music Box" preset as the closing stage of a 4-effect flute chain, dry/wet brought down to ~50%.** On a chill/ambient Frank Ocean-style beat, this device runs last on a Kontakt Ventus Ethnic Winds ocarina flute — after a reversed-rhythm reverb, a medium vintage verb, and a half-time Cassette Crushed stage — with mix pulled back to roughly half so the preset colors rather than replaces the flute's own tone. → [[Kontakt 7]], [[ValhallaVintageVerb]], [[Cassette]], [[EQ]], [[Frank Ocean Chill Ambient Chain]] — loner, [how to make chill ambient beats for frank ocean (sad/melodic chord technique)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=whI75NJcom8)

- **The wash-out chain generalizes into a full "one or two knob pedal" macro rack: tape-echo → octave-up pitch shift → Portal → chorus → half-time → low-pass EQ.** A fuller, fully-wet version of the pluck wash-out pattern documented below: a tape-flavored "space echo" reverb/delay preset → an octave-up pitch shift ([[kHs Pitch Shifter]]/Waves SoundShifter) → **Portal** loaded with a "pitch" preset category for texture → a chorus on its "vocal chorus" preset → a half-time device → a low-pass EQ, saved as a reusable preset ("Sparkle") and dragged onto new tracks/projects. Only two macros exposed to the end user across the whole 6-stage chain: Half Time mix and the low-pass cutoff frequency — everything upstream stays baked-in, not performable. Full build: [[Sparkle Texture Rack]]. → [[EQ Eight]], [[Delay]], [[Pitch and Formant]], [[TAL-Chorus-LX]], [[EffectRack]] — loner, [this secret plugin will change the way you process your melodies!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xwuodd40bug)
- **Stage 2 of a pluck wash-out chain: spring reverb → Portal (preset-loaded) → half-time → EQ.** Recreating Dez Wright's *Wants and Needs* (Drake) sample: a dissonant, semitone-apart pluck counter-melody gets washed out by running it through a spring reverb first, then this device loaded with one of a synth-kit's own presets for glitch/granular texture, then a half-time device, finished with EQ. Portal sits specifically after the reverb rather than before it — it's shaping an already-washed signal, not the dry pluck. → [[Ghz DC19 3]] (spring reverb), [[EQ Eight]], [[Reverb]], [[Melody Writing]] — loner, [dez wright's synth secrets will elevate your melodies!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_8vOz54DTiY)
- **Record Portal's output live to catch happy accidents ("pedal-lock" workflow)** — loaded Portal on a sample specifically for its glitch/buffer-freeze textural sounds, hit record into an audio editor (Edison, FL Studio's — the technique is DAW-agnostic: record continuously into any audio track/editor), then browsed presets while it ran rather than dialing in a target sound first, listening for a take worth keeping. One captured take read as almost percussive and got repurposed as a drum fill at the loop's end rather than used melodically — reverse + chop the tail to land the transition by ear. → [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]] — loner, [do you need pedals to make industry level melodies!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H-3SmPmGJOg)
