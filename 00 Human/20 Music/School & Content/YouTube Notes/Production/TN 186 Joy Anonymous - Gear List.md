## Synopsis

A Patreon-exclusive gear-list PDF (6 pages) accompanying Tape Notes' Joy Anonymous episode (TN 186) — no video/audio, a clickable "explore the gear" spread with one item per page, each carrying only a one-line vendor-style description, no artist-specific settings or technique (same shape as [[TN 187 Overmono - Gear List]]). Companion piece to [[TN 186 Joy Anonymous - Full Video Episode]] (processed separately). Shorter list than Overmono's — 12 items, split fairly evenly between plugins, hardware synths/samplers, and studio mics.

## What's shown

**Plugins:**
- Excite Audio Bloom Bass — virtual bass instrument line: white keys trigger tempo-matched bass samples/loops/one-shots, black keys modify playback in real time.
- Forever 89 Visco — hybrid sample-modeling drum machine/polysynth; resynthesizes a user-supplied audio sample into a malleable 2D "blob" shape that can be morphed/stretched/sculpted across time and frequency, rather than just played back.
- Safari Pedals (plugin line, animal-themed branding) — saturation/compression/EQ/time-based effects (Gorilla Drive, Time Machine, Flamingo Verb); **Dirty Dog Reverb** specifically called out — dual overdrive stages + flexible gating + a lush-to-lo-fi reverb engine, developed with Grammy-winning engineer Joe Chiccarelli.
- LHI Audio ST4B (William Light) — low-latency multiband transient shaper, up to 45dB of clean boost/cut, built for very fast attacks (electronic drums, foley, single-sample impulses). *(Owned equivalent already logged: [[Multiband Dynamics]]/[[kHs Dynamics]] transient-shaping lane — vet whether this unit's speed/cleanliness claim is worth chasing beyond what's owned.)*
- Soundtoys MicroShift — width/pitch-shift widener. *(Owned: [[MicroShift]].)*
- Soundtoys Decapitator — analog-style saturation/distortion. *(Owned: [[Decapitator]] — see [[TN 188 Tame Impala - Full Video Episode (Part 1)]] for an owned-lane technique already logged from this channel.)*
- Coda Labs Szechuan Saturator — free, input-level-reactive saturation/modulation plugin (McDonald's Szechuan-sauce-themed branding).

**Hardware:**
- BOSS RC-505 Loop Station — 5-track hand-operated stereo looper, independent volume faders, onboard effects.
- Moog Matriarch — 4-note paraphonic semi-modular analog synth, 49-note keybed, 4 analog oscillators, stereo ladder filters, dual envelopes, stereo analog bucket-brigade delay, 90 patch points.
- BOSS SP-505 Groove Sampling Workstation — 2002 hardware sampler/sequencer (between the SP-303 and SP-808): 8-voice CD-quality sampling, 26 DSP effects, 4-track sequencer.
- Roland TR-1000 Rhythm Creator — flagship hybrid drum machine (already documented from [[TN 187 Overmono - Gear List]] — a third confirmed sighting of this unit in the vault, all three from Tape Notes guests).
- Native Instruments Traktor Kontrol S8 — 4-channel all-in-one DJ controller for Traktor Pro (out of scope for studio production — a DJ/performance tool, not logged to the production wishlist).

**Mics:**
- Coles 4038 — passive ribbon mic (BBC design, 1950s, "waffle iron" housing), smooth/uncolored, no harsh high-frequency buildup.
- Neumann U87 (Ai) — large-diaphragm condenser, industry-reference vocal/broadcast/film mic since 1967.
- (Logic Pro is also shown as their DAW — no technique attached, noted for context only; Nate produces in Ableton Live, not directly actionable.)
