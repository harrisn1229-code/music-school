# UAD Brigade Chorus

![[Images/UAD Brigade Chorus.png]]

**Category:** Modulation (Chorus)
**Vendor:** Universal Audio — owned (confirmed 2026-07-17).

## What it is

Emulation of a bucket-brigade (BBD) analog chorus pedal — warm, watery chorus and vibrato with vintage analog movement.

## What it's used for

Width and shimmer on guitars, keys, synths and backing vocals; subtle for thickening, deep for lush 80s modulation.
