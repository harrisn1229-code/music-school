# BAS-C05 — Bass and keys as a conversation

**Q.** State the soul formula for arranging bass against harmony.

> [!question]- Answer
> Keep the harmony dead simple — Lophiile's Leon Thomas "Mutt" example is two chords — and make the **arrangement** carry it: *what story is the bass telling, what story are the keys telling, and do they talk to each other?* Three instruments going, arranged as a call and response rather than as stacked parts. The polyphonic version of the same instinct: a bass patch playing its own upper harmony voice a 3rd or 5th above the root, in **contrary motion** to the root line.

## Why it matters

It's the difference between bass as root support and bass as a voice. Very Beatles — McCartney lines are melodic statements, which is the tradition closest to your own writing.

## Drill

Take a two-chord loop. Write a keys part and a bass part that answer each other rather than moving together.
