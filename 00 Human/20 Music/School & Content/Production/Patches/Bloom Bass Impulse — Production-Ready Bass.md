# Bloom Bass Impulse — Production-Ready Bass

**Instrument:** [[Bloom Bass Impulse]] · **Slot:** [[BASS Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]]

## What it's for
Production-ready bass when speed wins — one of Bloom's 8 sample-and-impulse-driven banks, played and tweaked rather than synthesized from scratch.

## Settings
- Pick a bank matching the target genre/character from the 8 available (each bank is a different bass type) — white keys trigger the bass samples/loops, black keys alter playback speed/pitch
- Edit page: set sample start/end to trim to the transient; Playback Direction = Forwards for a standard bassline (Backwards/Pendulum/Loop are texture/creative options, not the default)
- Playback Mode: Hold for sustained notes, Latch/Relative for rhythmic/played parts
- Macro: Sub-EQ to dial in low-end weight against the kick/808 in the arrangement
- Macro: Width kept low — bass stays close to mono ([[Stereo Field]])
- Macro: Noise and Crush left off by default; use only for an intentionally lo-fi/gritty variant
- Effects modules: Saturation light for warmth, Envelope Filter off unless the part wants audio-reactive movement, Reverb off (space from chain sends, not the patch)

## In context
- The speed option against building a bass from scratch in [[Serum 2 — Reese Growl Bass]] / [[Drift — Analog Synth Bass]] / [[Model 84 — Juno Bass]] — same [[BASS Slot]], produced-and-done instead of designed.
- Followed by EQ and compression in the chain to sit it against the kick, per [[Bloom Bass Impulse]]'s FX chain notes.
