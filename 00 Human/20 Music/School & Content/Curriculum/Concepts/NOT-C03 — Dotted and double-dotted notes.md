# NOT-C03 — Dotted and double-dotted notes

**Q.** In 4/4, how many beats does a dotted half get? A double-dotted half?

> [!question]- Answer
> **3 and 3½.** A dot adds half the note's own value (2 + 1 = 3). **The second dot adds half of what the first dot added** (+ ½), not half the original again.

## Why it matters

The single dot is straightforward; the double dot is where it breaks down. Answering '3 and 4' is the classic error — it assumes the second dot repeats the first.

## Drill

Write beat values for dotted and double-dotted half, quarter and eighth notes.
