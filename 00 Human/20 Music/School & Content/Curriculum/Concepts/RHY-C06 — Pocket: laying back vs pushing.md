# RHY-C06 — Pocket: laying back vs pushing

**Q.** What does it mean to play "in the pocket", and what's the difference between laying back and pushing?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Pocket is where a part sits relative to the grid — consistently, on purpose.** **Laying back** = slightly *late* → relaxed, heavy (hip hop, soul). **Pushing** = slightly *early* → urgent, driving. Roughly **5–30 ms**; past ~40 ms it reads as a mistake.

## Why it matters

Pocket isn't the same as 'tight' — a part can be perfectly consistent and sit 15 ms behind. The move that actually creates pocket is **elements disagreeing**: kick and snare on the grid, hats late. Everything equally late just sounds slow.

## Drill

On a hip hop loop, leave strong beats on the grid and nudge only the weak-beat hits late. Then try pushing the hats early instead and hear the energy change.
