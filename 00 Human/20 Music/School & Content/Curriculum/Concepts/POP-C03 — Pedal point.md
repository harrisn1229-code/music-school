# POP-C03 — Pedal point

**Q.** What is a pedal point, and what is an EDM build doing harmonically?

> [!question]- Answer
> **A sustained bass note held while the chords above it change** — usually the tonic or dominant. An EDM build is a **dominant pedal**: sitting on V and refusing to resolve, so the drop provides the release.

## Why it matters

It had **zero coverage anywhere in the vault** before 2026-08-04, despite being everywhere in the music — the EDM build that sits on V and resolves at the drop is a pedal point. The application usually arrives long before the name does.

## Drill

Build 8 bars over a held tonic pedal, changing chords above it. Then do the same over a dominant pedal and hear the tension build.
