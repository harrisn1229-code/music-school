# kHs Trance Gate

![[Images/kHs Trance Gate.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A rhythmic amplitude-gating snap-in (classic 'trance gate'/sidechain-style chopping effect) with a step sequencer.

## What it's used for

Rhythmic gating/chopping effects on pads and synths, classic trance/EDM sidechain-pump substitute.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain, often the final rhythmic effect before output.
