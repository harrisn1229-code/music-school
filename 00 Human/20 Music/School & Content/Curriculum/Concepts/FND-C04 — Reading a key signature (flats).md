# FND-C04 — Reading a key signature (flats)

**Q.** How do you work out the major key from a key signature with flats? What's the exception?

> [!question]- Answer
> The **second-to-last flat names the key**. B♭ E♭ A♭ D♭ → **A♭ major**. The exception is **one flat = F major**, which you memorize because there's no second-to-last.

## Why it matters

The other half of FND-18, and the rule that would have completed FND-08 — you knew four flats is A♭ major but couldn't produce the flats. With the order memorized (FND-C02) this rule runs in both directions.

## Drill

Name the key: 1 flat · 2 flats · 3 flats · 5 flats. Then: list the flats in E♭ major.
