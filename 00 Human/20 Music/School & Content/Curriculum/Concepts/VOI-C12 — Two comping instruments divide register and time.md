# VOI-C12 — Two comping instruments divide register and time

**Q.** Guitar and piano are both in the band. Name the three things that keep them from colliding.

> [!question]- Answer
> **Assign roles out loud at the top of the tune** — who comps the head, who comps each solo. **Comp in the holes** the other player and the soloist leave, rather than on top of them. **Track register and move opposite** the soloist: they go up, you go lower. And the fourth answer that is always available — **lay out.** Not playing is a legitimate choice, not a cop-out.

## Why it matters

Two harmony instruments playing independently reads as clutter to a listener no matter how good each part is. The unrehearsed default is that **piano is primary and the guitarist behaves like a horn player** until cued. The exception proves the rule: a Freddie Green straight-quarter-note part works under a comping piano precisely because it has stopped being comping and become a second drummer.

## Drill

15 min: play along with a recording that already has a comping instrument. Comp only in its gaps, and lay out entirely for one chorus.
