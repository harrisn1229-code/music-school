# POP-C10 — Dorian IV vs Aeolian iv

**Q.** Compare Dm–G and Dm–Gm. What note differs, what mode does each imply? And what's ♭VI–♭VII–i called?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Dm–G is Dorian** — G major needs a **B natural**, Dorian's raised 6th. **Dm–Gm is natural minor** (B♭). **One note is the entire colour difference** — Dorian lifts, Aeolian stays dark. **♭VI–♭VII–i is the Aeolian cadence** (F–G–Am in A minor), all diatonic to natural minor.

## Why it matters

All three blank. Dorian vamps are everywhere in neo-soul, house and modern R&B precisely because they're minor without being bleak; the Aeolian cadence is the harmonic bed under a large amount of trap.

## Drill

Loop Dm–G for 8 bars, then Dm–Gm. Change nothing else. Name the feeling difference.
