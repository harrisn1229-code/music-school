# RX Mouth De-click

![[Images/RX Mouth De-click.png]]

**Category:** Pitch/Vocal
**Vendor:** iZotope

## What it is

An RX module specifically targeting mouth noise (lip smacks, saliva clicks) in vocal recordings. Owned in both the RX 10 and RX 11 suites.

## What it's used for

Removing distracting mouth noise from vocal takes.

## FX chain notes

Used early in a vocal cleanup pass, alongside De-click and De-plosive.
