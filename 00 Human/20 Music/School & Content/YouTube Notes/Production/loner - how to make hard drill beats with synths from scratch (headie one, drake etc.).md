# how to make hard drill beats with synths from scratch (headie one, drake etc.)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rcJ-j6X9bAU)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rcJ-j6X9bAU)

## Synopsis

loner breaks down a from-scratch dark UK drill beat aimed at artists like Headie One and Central Cee (also name-checking American drill), built almost entirely from two synth/sample layers plus a compact drum kit — a good demonstration of how little material a hard drill beat actually needs when the harmonic color and the drum bounce are both dialed in. The build has three real phases: (1) a dark minor-chord pad from Analog Lab 5's Solina engine (captioned "Selena"/"Celina"), voiced with a deliberately "wrong" chord tone for menace, then pushed through a ShaperBox half-time-style preset, reverb, and EQ; (2) a Splice "middle eastern vocals" sample chosen for a dark/airy character, pitched into key, time-stretched, chopped, and layered with a gating ShaperBox preset, a second half-time pass, EQ, an automated octave-up pitch shift that only engages at certain points, reverb, and panning tremolo; (3) a compact, sound-selection-led drum kit (808 → hi-hat → rim → snare roll/triangle → a granular percussion switch-up → kick last, deliberately spaced out) plus a manually-auditioned 808-slide workflow. The transition into the hook layers a reversed, pitched-down, time-stretched element with automated left-to-right panning; the hook itself reuses the pad's exact chords on a second Solina preset, half-timed and EQ-carved so it doesn't clash with the original pad, while the vocal sample's pitch shifter is automated to kick the vocal up a full octave only during the hook. The outro breakdown automates a half-time effect on the MASTER bus with a deliberate fade-back-out "slope," then drops all drums out for one final big hook moment. ## Techniques

- **Dark chord voicing: replace the minor 3rd with the major 2nd.** *(~0:15)* Starting from a plain C minor triad, drop the 3rd (D♯/E♭) down a half-step to D (the 2nd) instead of leaving it as the "obvious" chord tone — a small, deliberate wrong-note substitution stated directly as the reason the chord reads dark rather than just minor. → [[Rap Subgenres]] (Drill section), [[Melody Writing]]
- **ShaperBox "Time & Pitch" category, 4th preset along, as a built-in half-time effect on the pad** — pitches the signal down and slows it, described as "almost acts like a half time," rather than reaching for a dedicated half-time device. → [[Shaper]], [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **Reverb specifically to "fill the frequencies"** on a thin pad, then an **EQ cutting the lows** to keep the low end clear for the 808/kick. → [[Reverb]], [[EQ]]
- **Splice search term "middle eastern vocals," picked for a dark/airy character** — pitched down to fit the song's key, time-stretched, and chopped down to only the wanted portion rather than the whole phrase. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Vocal-sample processing chain: a gating ShaperBox "basic" preset (pulses the signal in and out, stop-start) → a half-time device's "quarter" preset → EQ cutting both highs and lows → an octave-up pitch shifter automated to only engage at certain points → a big/heavy-mix reverb → a tremolo control panning the signal side to side.** → [[Shaper]], [[Pitch and Formant]], [[Modulation]], [[Reverb]], [[Automation]]
- **"Sound Shifter" pitched up 12 semitones (a full octave), with its on/off state automated** so the shift only lands at chosen points rather than running the whole time. Near-certainly the same captioned "sound shifter" already tracked elsewhere in this vault as Waves SoundShifter (unowned) — owned lane: [[kHs Pitch Shifter]] for the same ±12-semitone move. → [[Pitch and Formant]], [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **Once two melodic elements (pad + vocal sample) existed, deliberately stop writing more melodic material and move to drums** — an explicit "that's enough" restraint call rather than continuing to layer. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Drum build order, hi-hat treated as the single most important sound-selection call in the kit:** 808 first → **hi-hat** ("if you're making a drill bit you're going to want to make sure your hi-hat is super fire," both sample AND pattern, "a very big part of the bounce") with two knob moves (one raising a character/tone control, a second pitching the hat down) and its timing shifted back off-grid by **less than his normal amount**, specifically because a busy/layered hi-hat reads "off" if pushed as far as a sparser part would tolerate → a basic rim shot → a roll built from a "counter snare" plus a triangle hit for texture → a granular percussion layer (a perc sample dragged into a granulizer, settings tweaked, a preset used) added specifically to "switch up and do something a little bit different" → **kick last**, picked for maximum hit ("a super hard hitting one") on a deliberately spaced-out pattern, since the percussion above it is already filling the rhythmic space. Full write-up on [[Genre Drum Patterns]]. → [[Genre Drum Patterns]], [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[Granulator II]]
- **808-slide workflow: stretch the 808 across the whole beat first, THEN audition by ear.** Play the beat back, and wherever a slide feels right, use the chop tool to cut the 808 pattern at that point, then right-click → "Make Unique" to detach that one segment into its own independent instance so only it can be edited/slid without touching the rest of the pattern. Ableton-equivalent mechanics (warp mode + clip transposition envelope) already on [[Bassline Writing]]. → [[Bassline Writing]]
- **Transition-into-hook element: reverse effect, pitched all the way down, time-stretched to fit the BPM, with automated panning that starts hard left and moves to hard right exactly as the beat drops into the hook.** Built by right-clicking the pan knob in the mixer channel and creating an automation clip. → [[Automation]], [[Modulation]]
- **Hook adds a second melodic layer reusing the FIRST pad's exact chords** — a different Solina/Analog Lab preset ("air string"), half-timed, run through ShaperBox's basic pulse preset, then EQ'd with a heavy low-cut specifically so it doesn't clash with the original pad. → [[Masking and Carving]], [[Rap Subgenres]]
- **Outro breakdown: automate a half-time effect on the MASTER bus with a deliberate fade-back-out "slope" (not a hard on/off toggle), then drop ALL drums out entirely for one final, big hook moment.** → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[Automation]]
