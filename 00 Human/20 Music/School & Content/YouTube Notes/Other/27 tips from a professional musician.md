# 27 tips from a professional musician

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mbmh3srDZlo)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mbmh3srDZlo)

## Synopsis

Bennett's practical tips from a decade of full-time gigging (weddings, bars, restaurants, hotels) before YouTube became his main income. Almost entirely working-gig-musician logistics rather than production or theory — most of it assumes a live-performance career (bands, venues, being hired for events), which is a different lane from home/studio production. A handful of tips generalize beyond gigging.

## What's taught

27 rapid-fire tips, mostly gig-logistics specific: network by giving out a phone number (not just email) so you can be called last-minute; learn to drive, since transporting gear can be the deciding factor in getting hired; be a multi-instrumentalist so you're the flexible pick when a show needs coverage on more than one instrument; post high-quality performance videos online — Bennett landed an Abbey Road gig because a hirer found his YouTube footage unprompted; check your spam folder for missed opportunities; keep a shared, synchronized calendar to avoid double-booking; know "Happy Birthday" cold, because you will eventually be asked to play it live with no warning.
