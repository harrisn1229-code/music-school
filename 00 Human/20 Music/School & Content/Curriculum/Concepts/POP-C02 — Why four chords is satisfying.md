# POP-C02 — Why four chords is satisfying

**Q.** Why are so many successful pop songs built on four repeating chords? And if the harmony isn't moving, what creates motion?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Predictability is the feature.** Once the ear stops tracking the harmony it attends to the vocal and the groove instead — the progression becomes a **bed rather than a series of events**. Motion comes from **rhythm, melody/topline, timbre and arrangement**, not harmony.

## Why it matters

POP-26 blank; POP-05 half credit (you said 'drums', which is one of four sources). The corollary is the useful part: **if a loop is boring, the chords usually aren't the problem** — nothing above them is changing.

## Drill

List everything that changes across 8 bars of one of your loops, other than the chords. If it's nothing, that's the diagnosis.
