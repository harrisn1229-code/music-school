# SYN-C07 — Subtractive vs FM

**Q.** What's the fundamental difference between subtractive and FM synthesis?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Subtractive starts with a harmonically rich waveform and filters harmonics away.** **FM uses one oscillator to modulate another's frequency, generating new sidebands** that weren't there. One removes; the other creates.

## Why it matters

The organizing distinction of the whole strand. The consequence: a subtractive filter can only remove what the oscillator produced — start on a sine and nothing can brighten it. That limitation is precisely what FM exists to escape.

## Drill

Explain why you can't make a bright lead starting from a sine wave in a subtractive synth.
