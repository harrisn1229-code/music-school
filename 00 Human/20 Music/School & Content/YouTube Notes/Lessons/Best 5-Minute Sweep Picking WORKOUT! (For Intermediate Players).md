## Synopsis

A 30-day daily sweep-picking challenge built on just two shapes (E minor, E major), alternated, with an escalating speed-burst twist added each repetition.

## Techniques

- **Two-shape alternation + speed-burst structure.** Play the E minor shape, then the E major shape, alternate between them, then raise the tempo. On each repetition, add a fast burst of sweep picking on top — the burst is explicitly meant to unlock higher-tempo technique by pushing outside the comfort zone, not to be played clean on day one.
- Same day-over-day tempo-plateau tracking convention as BERNTH's other workouts (comment/log the tempo you got stuck at, try to beat it next session) — explicitly framed as okay to fail early on.
