# DS FM

![[Images/DS FM.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Ableton (Drum Synths pack, Max for Live)

## What it is

One voice from Ableton's Drum Synths pack — an FM-synthesis-based percussion generator for metallic, bell-like, or aggressive percussive tones.

## What it's used for

Percussive FM tones and hybrid drum layers that don't fit the standard kick/snare/hat mold.

## FX chain notes

Used as a drum-voice instrument in a Drum Rack; typically followed by a short transient-shaping compressor and a drum-bus saturation stage.
