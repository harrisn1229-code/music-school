## What's taught

- **Core road-map concept: build each lick from two adjacent pentatonic box shapes, not one.** Every one of the five licks in the video is deliberately constructed by combining two neighboring positions of the minor pentatonic scale rather than staying inside a single box — the explicit goal is escaping "the typical pentatonic scale box" mentality *(general framing, direct from the transcript)*.
