# Sie-Q

![[Images/Sie-Q.png]]

**Category:** EQ
**Vendor:** Soundtoys

## What it is

Soundtoys' EQ plugin modeled on a classic passive/console-style EQ circuit for musical, characterful tonal shaping.

## What it's used for

Musical, characterful EQ moves as an alternative to a clinical digital EQ.

## FX chain notes

Used as either the corrective or tonal EQ stage in a chain.

## Used in my chains

My **musical broad-strokes EQ** (Siemens-console voiced) — for a dB or two of "seat the section" tone, not surgery (that's [[Pro-Q 4]]):

- **[[3 Guitar Bus|Guitar Bus]]** — *slot 1, musical tone*: a dB of top (Höhen) if the section's dull, a dB off the low-mids if thick.
- **[[3 Inst-Synth Bus|Inst-Synth Bus]]** — *slot 1, musical tilt*: a dB brighter or darker to seat the whole section behind the vocal.
