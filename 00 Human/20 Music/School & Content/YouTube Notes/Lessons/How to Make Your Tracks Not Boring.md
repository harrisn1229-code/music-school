## Synopsis

A short follow-up to an earlier energy-levels video, making a single point directly: subtractive variation on one great loop (muting elements, shifting layers, bolting on an extra arp) has a hard ceiling — no amount of it turns one 4–8 bar loop into a full song. Eventually a genuinely second, different loop is required, and the honest expectation-setting here is that most attempts at that second loop will be mediocre before one actually flows well with the first. Mostly a motivational/conceptual reinforcement of loopitis-cure material already well documented in the vault, rather than a new mechanism.

## Techniques

- **Subtractive variation on one loop has a ceiling — a genuinely second loop is eventually required, and most attempts at it will be discardable** — removing/re-adding elements from a single loop can't sustain a full song by itself; treat writing several mediocre "second loop" attempts as the normal, expected cost of eventually finding one that actually flows into the first, not a sign something's wrong. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
