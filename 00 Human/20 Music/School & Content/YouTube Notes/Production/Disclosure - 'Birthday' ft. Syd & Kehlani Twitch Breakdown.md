# Disclosure - 'Birthday' ft. Syd & Kehlani Twitch Breakdown

Deep 2-hour teardown of "Birthday" (ENERGY), a two-step-garage R&B duet with Syd and Kehlani — Guy's *favorite drums on the album*. Career-best material on: **bass that's felt *and* heard** (four layered basses + a self-made bit-crushed sub sample that moves with the note), **kick-tuning in a very low key** (bend a fifth above the root), **surgical low-mid removal on stacked vocals** (Surfer EQ note-following), the **"glue bus" philosophy**, and a clean articulation of his whole **two-bus routing** (everything → Instrument bus + Vocal bus → master). Plus a great **harmony-writing tutorial** and the "keep it simple, then go jazzy in the middle-eight" (Pharrell/Motown) songwriting move. Captions transcript — high confidence, some chatty stretches.

## Techniques

### Songwriting & harmony
- **Simple everywhere, jazzy in the middle-eight** — verses are restrained (Cm7–Fm7–Ab maj9–G7, a pentatonic "so-me-re" R&B chorus melody), then the middle-eight goes harmonically wild: descending chromatic **major-7ths**, switching to **minor-7ths in a new key** (F♯ minor-major7), a momentary key change. Learned from a Pharrell session (Motown/Stevie "fast-forward to the middle-eight" love). Melodies there are just ad-lib oohs/ahs accentuating the chords. → theory / arrangement.
- **Write chords by cycling synth patches while someone noodles** — the pad sound and the progression arrived together (Howard played while Guy auditioned sounds), *then* melody, *then* bass all grew from it.
- **Harmony-writing method** — study Four Freshmen / Beach Boys / Take 6: follow *one* singer's line (start with the top voice), relearn the song following the next voice down, and you learn the interval spacing they favor — often *wider* than a closed inversion (voice C major as C–G–E–C across octaves). Same skill as brass/horn arranging. → [[Song Practice]] / theory.

### The signature "Disclosure sine" pad
- **Logic Vocoder in synth mode = sine bleeps** — mode 49, top knob at 0 gives pure sines; the reason to use it is the **white↔blue noise blend** for a Prophet-5-ish noise character. The day-one Disclosure lead sound. → [[Sound Selection Guide]].
- Chain: **DBX 160** comp (make the two held notes sit at equal volume + a little saturation/character) → **2nd comp, slow attack** to lengthen the notes (bite at the start, longer tail) → **[[Decimort 2]]** bit-crush for top "sizzle." Delay + reverb automated for rise/fall; sidechained to the kick. → [[Saturation and Distortion]].
- **Layer [[Keyscape]] "toy piano" with the *tone* knob fully down** — leaves only the mechanical *clatter* (the mic-over-the-hands layer) as a clunky texture under the sine. Copied the MIDI to two Keyscapes and used **Logic MIDI Transform → Humanize** (hit *Operate* repeatedly to incrementally mess up timing) so it *flams* around the sine. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]].
- Extra pluck = **[[Omnisphere]] "kalimba plucked with a coin"** patch (+ Roto Cab + delay, bounced to audio). A **Poly6** "garage string" counter-melody you only notice at the very end ("take it out and the mix feels empty").

### Bass — felt *and* heard (four layers)
- **Self-made bit-crushed sub sample that *moves with the note*** — make one nice sub note (with a little noise), bounce to audio, and load it on a sampler so the bit-crusher's character shifts as you pitch the note around (a static bit-crusher on a live synth wouldn't move). Trick from Mount Kimbie / James Blake; great for noise/vinyl/dink tones that need movement. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]].
- **Make a sub *audible*, not just felt, with octave-up + bass-guitar layers** — a low-C sub (very low; normally wouldn't go below E♭, considered a key change) sits *low* in the mix; a Juno at +1 octave, a ~100 Hz "tel-juno" harmonic layer, and a self-made bass-guitar sampler note (all low-level, low-cut) let the ear reconstruct the fundamental. "Howard says: I like the part but can't hear it" → add the octave-up. → [[BASS Slot]] / [[SUB Slot]].
- **Velocity = volume** (not automation) — red notes loud, blue quiet, so the low C doesn't jump out.
- **Glue the four basses** with a Waves Blue-Stripe / CLA-76 (barely compressing — a *perceptual* "make them one"), plus a bit-crusher + Decapitator; chorus + Roto Cab. → [[Compression]].
- **Multiband sidechain the *sub only*** to the kick (let mids/highs through) so kick and sub never clash — reinforces the same move in [Never Enough](https://youtu.be/lGopQwQo9Ag). → [[Sidechaining]], [[Multiband Compression]].

### The "glue bus" philosophy
- **Run parts through one comp/tape so they interact** — like a drum kit into a single bus comp, so the kick squashes the cymbals. Foals "Blue Blood" cited (bus comp pumped so hard you hear the cymbals breathe — "sounds like something you couldn't do in real life"). Glue is perceptual as much as level. → [[Parallel Processing]], [[Compression Staging]].
- **Full routing:** every part → its instrument bus → **Instrument bus (bus 33)**; every vocal → **Vocal bus (bus 34)**; both → master. Mute the vocal bus = instrumental, mute the instrument bus = acapella. Sub-buses nest ("a bus in a bus": clap bus 16 → drum bus 10). **Leave a little processing headroom on the summing buses** — pre-limiting on the instrument bus, and a **final EQ on the vocal bus** so you can fix "too much vocal top-end" in a 5-min project revisit instead of the mastering engineer touching the whole mix. → [[Master Chain]], [[2 Main Vocal Group|Vocals]] chains.

### Drums (two-step garage — his favorite)
- **Automate kick *length*** across the arrangement — long kick in the sub-less intro/verse, short kick in the chorus where the sub enters; an easy anti-clash move. → [[Kick Design]].
- **Kick note in a very low key: bend a fifth above the root** — song in C minor → a C sub-kick is too low (speakers flap, inaudible outside a club), so tune the kick to G (a fifth up) or lower and make it *bend* (boom-boom). They always tune the kick to the key — a kick on the wrong note vs the song "pisses us both off." → [[Kick Design]], [[SUB Slot]].
- **Two-layer clap: one upfront mono sample + a big stack of *recorded* real claps** (finger clicks, mouth clicks, rim, garage snare) bussed together purely for **stereo width + texture** under the main clap. "They don't sound nice alone, but do the job in the mix." → [[2 Drums Group|Drums]], [[Width and Stereo]].
- **Human-touch percussion** — an offbeat triangle, a chopped/reversed hat loop (recorded live at Sleeper Sounds), MIDI hats, white-noise sweeps, and a **reversed Splice breath instead of a crash cymbal** (softens the drums for airy R&B vocals). → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[TEXTURE Slot]].
- **Send tape hiss / vinyl crackle *through the drum bus*, sidechained to the kick** so the noise interacts with (ducks under) the drums — "makes it sound old, like my favorite records." Uses different tape/vinyl each track. → [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]].
- **Vinyl "Burial move"** — a bandpass filter dragged around by mouse and recorded on the vinyl layer (Ghost Hardware / Archangel). → [[Automation]].
- **White-noise build from Logic ESP** — solo the noise oscillator (kill the rest), EQ heavily → a free riser. → [[Depth and Space]].
- **Swing per part, not per project** — set a guide swing then nudge individual hits (especially hats) so it feels played, not robotic. → [[House - EDM]].
- **Drum bus chain:** API 2500 (light comp) → Studer tape (gentle sat — *don't* crush, the vocals are soft/airy) → [[Oxford Inflator]] (a little saturation) → EQ (control kick low-mids) → Ozone (a touch of comp) → EQ (cut lows / kick high-mids / a hair of top) → **[[Oxford Inflator|Oxford]] Limiter that only bites when kick+clap coincide** (stays clean). → [[2 Drums Group|Drums]] chains.

### Vocals (Syd + Kehlani)
- **Record vocals in Pro Tools (engineer drives), then bounce back to Logic** — frees you to focus on guiding the singer. Syd (ex-The Internet, Odd Future) ran her own session. → [[2 Main Vocal Group|Main Vocal Group]].
- **Cut low-mids *hard* on stacked vocals** — one vocal's low-mids are fine, but stack many and they pile into a huge boost that fights the bass/kick/sines. Kendrick (GKMC) & Drake solo-vocal-sound "missing" low-mids on purpose — the air/top carries. → [[Masking and Carving]].
- **Surfer EQ to remove low-mids that *follow the moving vocal*** — a note-tracking −5 dB low-mid cut that stays locked no matter where the melody goes; "magic… better than FabFilter *for this one job*." Bounce it and move on. → [[Plugin Wishlist]] (Surfer EQ), [[EQ]].
- **Stereo de-esser link knob** — link 100% = ducks L+R together (mono-ish); 0% = two independent de-essers (L and R move separately) for a more natural top. De-ess individually *and* lightly on the bus. → [[De-essing]].
- **Little AlterBoy an octave below on a send** — subtle low-octave thickening instead of re-singing the part. → [[Little AlterBoy]], [[Pitch and Formant]].
- **Vocal Rider = the automatic version of hand-drawn volume automation** — on Kehlani (sparingly, 2–4 dB); on Syd he did it manually (boost breaths, ride falling notes to hold level). Both valid. → [[Automation]].
- **Fix an accidentally-printed reverb with iZotope RX De-reverb** — Syd's take was bounced from Pro Tools with a reverb still on; RX pulled it back dry. → [[Referencing and Translation]].
- **Syd lead detail:** recorded Telefunken → Neve 1081 → Tube-Tech; Blue-Stripe comp *hard* (ratio 4, slow attack) to tame tails ("aggressive but punchy — the flavor I like"); light Melodyne (timing/note-ends only); **echo-boy automation to fill the gaps *between* phrases** (or sidechain the delay bus to the vocal); two delays + plate reverb.
- **Kehlani came pre-tuned (stylistic skippy sound) + pre-compressed** — matched the assumed chain (added Tube-Tech emulation + Pro-Q saturation "just in case"), cut rumble + low-mids, tiny Gem Dopamine air, safety de-ess, Inflator; **re-placed her ad-libs with Melodyne** to fit new chords (the Syd↔Kehlani "Brandy & Monica" call-and-response). **No auto-tune ever — only Melodyne, sparingly; prefer more takes.**
- **Vocal panning:** two vocals near-center (one much quieter), one panned nearly-full L and R.

### Master
- SSL G-bus comp → Studer tape → **The Drama** (multiband) → **[[Saturn 2]]** → **[[Oxford Inflator]]** (into the red, tastefully) → ([[Soothe2]] normally) → EQ as a **bypassed guide** (+1.8 dB high-mids, a little top off; he pulled bass but "don't take away bass ever — no point"). → [[Master Chain]].
