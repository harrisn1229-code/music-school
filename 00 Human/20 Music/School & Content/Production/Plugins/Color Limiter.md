# Color Limiter

**Category:** Compressor/Limiter
**Vendor:** Ableton

## What it is

A free brickwall limiter shipped in Ableton's **Creative Extensions** pack (free for Suite owners, installed separately from the base Suite install), distinguished from a plain transparent limiter by a built-in **Color**/saturation stage that adds harmonic density as it limits, rather than just clamping peaks cleanly.

## What it's used for

Squashing a dense, saturated sound (a neurobass, a heavily-processed bus) into a flat, consistent level while adding extra harmonic "glue" in the process — a limiter that's also a light saturator.

## FX chain notes

Used as a final stage after saturation/distortion processing, ahead of freeze/print — its own Color/saturation adds to whatever grit already exists in the chain rather than cleaning it up.

## Techniques

- **Saturation and Color maxed, Release fast, Ceiling pulled down, for a dense "flat sausage" neurobass** — turn **Saturation** all the way up, push **Color** up, pull **Release** down for a fast/responsive limiter, and lower the **Ceiling** to start real gain reduction — the combination flattens a dynamically-uneven, heavily-processed bass into a consistent, dense result while the Color/saturation stage adds extra harmonic thickness rather than just limiting transparently. → [[Bass Design]] — You Suck at Producing, [How to make (and PERFORM) a Neurobass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5RNBLgC455s)
