# RHY-C08 — Quantize, humanize, groove templates

**Q.** When do you quantize, when do you humanize, and what is a groove template actually storing?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Quantize** snaps to the grid — for anything machine-tight (808s, sidechained elements). **Humanize** adds controlled timing and velocity variation — for anything meant to read as played. **Neither at 100%:** partial quantize strength (50–75%) pulls a performance *toward* the grid while keeping its feel. A **groove template stores timing and velocity offsets** extracted from one piece of material and applies them to another.

## Why it matters

Your best two answers in the strand, and both were the ones with an Ableton control surface — you knew *when* and not *what*. The groove-template payoff is extraction: pull the feel off a break you like and put it on your own stiff MIDI.

## Drill

Extract the groove from a break you like and apply it to your own drums. Then re-do it at 50% quantize strength instead and compare.
