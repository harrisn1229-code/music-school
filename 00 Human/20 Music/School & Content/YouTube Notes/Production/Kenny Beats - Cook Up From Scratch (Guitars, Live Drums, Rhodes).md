# Kenny Beats — Cook Up From Scratch (Guitars, Live Drums, Rhodes)

![](https://youtu.be/l7AjRTXex2U)

## Synopsis

The hour-long cook-up where Kenny publicly discovers Soothe2 ("the secret plugin") and then records live drums from scratch with no template — narrating the whole processing chain as he builds it: Pultec first on the snare, gate for the noise between hits, Soothe2 on the drum group, Devil-Loc for crush, warp the timing, pitch the drums down, bus everything out to UAD hardware. Music-heavy stream; the spoken segments carry the chain.

## Techniques

- **The Soothe2 origin story** — "who's ready for the secret plugin… it soothes all the harshest things, all the gnarly things hurting your ears or making your sounds muddy. It's a catch-all for fixing your mixes — one of the biggest cheat codes I've ever seen." Charlie Puth told him about it long ago; Alex Tumay re-raised it (and used it on his 808 — "not what you'd expect"); he demos with a **Josh Goodwin setting** on guitar. "You're going to see me using it a f***-ton" — and cluster-2's mix video confirms it became his first-reach plugin. → [[Soothe2]]
- **Live-drum scratch chain, in order: Pultec EQ → Gate → Soothe2 (on the GROUP) → Devil-Loc** — Pultec first on the snare top ("what I've been doing lately and it's been working"), gate clears the noise between hits, Soothe2 tames the group (not per-mic), [[Devil-Loc Deluxe]] for the crush. Owned one-to-one: [[UAD Pultec EQP-1A]] → [[Gate]] → [[Soothe2]] → [[Devil-Loc Deluxe]]. → [[2 Drums Group|Drums Group]], [[1 Drums Snare-Clap Track|Drums Snare-Clap Track]]
- **Soothe2 goes on the drum GROUP, not each mic** — one resonance pass over the summed kit; same group-has-the-chain logic as vocals. → [[2 Drums Group|Drums Group]]
- **Warp the timing, then PITCH THE DRUMS DOWN** — after comping the best loop, timing fix first, then a pitch-down for weight/vibe (the classic sampled-break move applied to your own live drums). → [[2 Drums Group|Drums Group]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Bus the drums out to hardware** — one drum bus → UAD/hardware sends for the return color (his BG2/Terry EQ lane; ours: [[3 Drums Bus|Drums Bus]] → [[Kramer Tape]]/[[Sony TC-630]]). → [[3 Drums Bus|Drums Bus]]
- **Live-sounding hats win** — chat's verdict on the take; loose played hats over gridded loops (same conclusion as Disclosure's zero-loops rule in [[Disclosure - 'Never Enough' Twitch Breakdown]]).
- **Scrappy monitoring beats no recording** — headphone cable too short → bounced the beat to his *phone*, played drums to the phone, kept rolling. Don't let setup friction kill a take. → session craft
