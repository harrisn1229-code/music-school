# Gefell UMT70S

**Category:** Microphone (large-diaphragm tube condenser)
**Vendor:** Microtech Gefell

## What it is

A large-diaphragm tube condenser from Microtech Gefell — the East German half of the original Neumann/Gefell company that split after WWII, with both resulting companies continuing to build mics on the same M7 capsule lineage. Described as sounding close to a Neumann U87 at roughly a third of the price. Not currently owned — a comparison point to the owned [[Neumann TLM 102]], and a sibling reference to the other unowned M7-lineage mic already tracked here, [[Neumann U47]].

## What it's used for

General-purpose "big, gorgeous, musical" mic sound — first adopted as a vocal mic, since used broadly across sources. In one session, Gefell's UM92 (the UMT70S's close relative in the same product family) was the choice for the closest, most-featured pair on a grand piano, right at the hammers.

## Signal chain notes

🎬 *From video:* Full piano array in one session: two Gefell UM92s near the hammers (the detail/close pair), a pair of Gefell UM300s for room mics, and a Violet Amethyst about nine feet back at the foot of the piano for low end. The hammer pair ran through a Neve 88R console (preamp + a bit of top/bottom EQ) and a Chandler TG-1 compressor in **limit mode** — see [[Piano Recording]] for the full signal-path and mode-choice writeup. — Greg Wells, [[Producing and recording piano with Greg Wells]]
