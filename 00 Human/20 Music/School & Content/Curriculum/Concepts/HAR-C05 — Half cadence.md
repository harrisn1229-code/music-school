# HAR-C05 — Half cadence

**Q.** How is a half cadence different from an authentic cadence, and when would you use one?

> [!question]- Answer
> A half cadence **ends ON V**, unresolved — a question mark. An authentic cadence goes **V→I** and closes. Use the half cadence at the end of an antecedent phrase so the consequent has something to answer.

## Why it matters

The question/answer phrase structure it creates is the backbone of how 8- and 16-bar sections are built — this is a *form* tool as much as a harmony one.

## Drill

Write an 8-bar phrase: 4 bars ending on V, 4 bars ending V–I. Hear the question and the answer.
