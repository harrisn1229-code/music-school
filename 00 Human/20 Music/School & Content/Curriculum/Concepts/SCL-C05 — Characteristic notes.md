# SCL-C05 — Characteristic notes

**Q.** Which mode has a raised 4th? Which interval makes Locrian unusable as a tonic?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Lydian** has the ♯4. **Locrian's ♭5** makes it unstable — the tonic triad is diminished, so there's nothing stable to resolve home to. Characteristics: Dorian ♮6 · Phrygian ♭2 · Lydian ♯4 · Mixolydian ♭7 · Locrian ♭2 and ♭5.

## Why it matters

Both blank. The characteristic note *is* the mode — it's the one note distinguishing it from plain major or minor, and it's what your ear should be listening for.

## Drill

For each of the seven modes, name its characteristic note without looking. Then play each one and hear it.
