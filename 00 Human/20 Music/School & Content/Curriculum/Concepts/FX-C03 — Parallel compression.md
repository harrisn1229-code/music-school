# FX-C03 — Parallel compression

**Q.** What is parallel compression, and why does it work?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Blend a heavily compressed copy underneath the dry signal.** The dry path keeps its transients, so you get density and sustained detail without losing punch — it behaves like **upward** compression, lifting the quiet parts rather than pushing the loud ones down.

## Why it matters

And you don't need a return track: Ableton's stock Compressor has a **Dry/Wet knob** that does it in one device — already noted in [[Compression]].

## Drill

Set it up both ways on drums — a return track, and stock Dry/Wet at ~50%. Confirm they behave the same.
