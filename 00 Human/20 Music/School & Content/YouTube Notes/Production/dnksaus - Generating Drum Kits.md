# dnksaus - Generating Drum Kits

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Beyps5u4DSg)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Beyps5u4DSg)

## Synopsis

A quick 2:37 demo of a Live 12 browser feature — **Similar Samples** — applied kit-wide inside [[Drum Rack]]. With a kit loaded (a demo pattern generated by his own in-progress "dank instant garage MIDI" Patreon tool), pressing a rotate arrow on any pad swaps that pad's sample for one the browser judges sonically similar to the current one — searching the *whole library*, not a pre-loaded selector pool. The same rotate control appears on every pad and works on **any** kit, including a stock kit dragged straight from the Packs tab's drum-clip MIDI files, not just a kit he built himself. Pads can be locked out of the rotation individually, and a bonus tip covers rotating an entire kit's samples in one action. This is a distinct mechanism from the "Sketch Kit" build-your-own-selector-rack approach covered in [[Randomized Rack Design]] and [[Drum Rack]] from an earlier video in this queue — that one macro-maps a pre-loaded pool of your own samples; this one live-searches your whole library for the closest sonic match, one click at a time.

## Techniques

- **Similar Samples button + rotate arrow, per pad** — press it on a Drum Rack pad and that pad's sample rotates through library-wide matches the browser judges similar to the previous one, one press per swap; you can also change a sample by hand instead of rotating. → [[Drum Rack]], [[Sample Browser and Clips Workflow]]
- **Works on any kit, not just self-built ones** — go to the Packs tab, filter for **drum clip**, preview ready-made drum MIDI patterns, and drag one onto a blank track — it brings its own kit along, fully rotatable pad-by-pad the same way as a kit you built yourself. → [[Sample Browser and Clips Workflow]]
- **Lock a pad out of rotation** — while rotating the rest of the kit, lock a specific pad's sample (e.g. a kick you already like) so it's excluded from the swap and only the others cycle. → [[Drum Rack]]
- **Choke groups via the pad's I/O section** — while exploring a rotated kit, open a pad's I/O button and put related key samples (e.g. open/closed hat) into the same choke group so one cuts the other off. → [[Drum Rack]]
- **Bonus: whole-kit similarity rotation** — the same Similar Samples mechanism can be triggered for an entire kit at once rather than pad by pad, for a faster full-kit reroll.
- **"Dank instant garage MIDI" tool (Patreon, early beta)** — his own in-progress tool for generating a drum MIDI pattern on the spot, used here just to get a demo pattern going; explicitly an early concept, not a finished product. → [[Plugin Wishlist]]
