# Dahi — SZA "Used" (ft. Don Toliver) Beat Breakdown

![](https://youtu.be/SPyDimvPNb0)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/SPyDimvPNb0)

## Synopsis

Producer Dahi (Kendrick, SZA) breaks down "Used" from SOS: a 3–4 year journey from a sound-pack loop, through a jam session with John Hill writing new melodies *around* the sample, to cutting the best 90 seconds from a 6-minute jam. Heavy on sound-source recommendations — Mellotron, OB-6/Prophet-6, Moog Grandmother, pedal-processed live guitar, flatwound bass, UVI Falcon's retro drum-machine bank — and the arrangement philosophy that a great producer "cuts the fat and recognizes the moment."

## Techniques

- **Resample the pack loop into your own song** — a Splice-style melody loop was "fire but just a loop"; he and John Hill **wrote new melodies around the sample** until it became a composition. The loop is a seed, not the song. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Song Seeds Index|Song Seeds]]
- **Jam long, cut the fat** — they jammed ~6 minutes, he took the best ~90 seconds and arranged from that; "what makes a great producer is someone who can listen and go: THAT's the moment." Record the whole process, edit later. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Mellotron for "sample-authentic but real" textures** — strings/flutes/choir keys that sound like a sample but play like an instrument; Mellotron V is the plugin version → [[Plugin Wishlist]], [[Vintage Gear Worklist]]
- **Synth sources:** OB-6 (initial pad sound), Prophet-6 (Arturia's emulation exists — the [[Sequential Prophet-5]] lineage), **Moog Grandmother** for lead lines and basslines with real analog drift ("get the right person to play and tweak the sound live"). → [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[Instrument Preset Map]]
- **Live guitar through a pedalboard, printed** — Eventide H9 + Strymon Mobius on the way in; plus a separate **delay track** of the same guitar and an **Output Portal** track for granular reflection textures behind the dry part — one performance, three layers. → [[1 Electric Guitar Amp Track|Electric Guitar Amp Track]], [[TEXTURE Slot]]
- **Flatwound bass, dry** — "flatwound basses just got a tone that sounds old school but got pocket"; played straight in, no processing. (The [[Hofner B-Bass H-Series]] with flats IS this sound.) → [[1 Electric Bass DI Track|Electric Bass DI Track]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **UVI Falcon "Beat Box Anthology" for retro drum-machine sounds** — the budget path to SP-1200/LinnDrum/Mini Pops character without buying hardware ([[Vintage Gear Index]] covers the originals); he programmed his own pattern from its sounds. → [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[2 Drums Group|Drums Group]]
- **Retro keys from a Kontakt library** layered under (name garbled in captions — Keyscape-adjacent). *(low confidence)*
