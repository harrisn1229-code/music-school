# Best 5 MIN Tapping WORKOUT! (For Intermediate Players)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0RPZaRl5e3A)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0RPZaRl5e3A)

## Synopsis

A short daily tapping play-along riff — multi-finger tapping plus tapping slides — starting at 30 BPM and climbing in a progressive tempo ladder, framed as both a tapping-technique drill and (since tapping demands precise fretting-hand control) a fretting-hand technique workout in disguise.

## Techniques

- **The riff:** a single tapping-based riff combining multi-finger tapping (more than one tapping-hand finger involved, not just the index) and tapping slides, played along to a backing track that steps up through tempo blocks from 30 BPM.
- **Framing:** tapping is presented as valuable beyond its own vocabulary — because it demands very precise fretting-hand control (the fretting hand does most of the actual note-triggering work in tapped passages), the workout doubles as general fretting-hand technique training even for players not primarily chasing a "tapping style."
- **Daily-progression tracking mechanic** (same pattern as the alternate-picking shred challenges on this channel): comment the date and the tempo you got stuck at, then return the next day and try to beat it.
- **Positioned as a follow-on** to a longer 30-day tapping fundamentals course (paid/Patreon, not routed here) for players who hit a hard technique ceiling within this workout and need earlier-stage material first.
