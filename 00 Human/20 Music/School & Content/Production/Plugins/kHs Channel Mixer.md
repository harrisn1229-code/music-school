# kHs Channel Mixer

![[Images/kHs Channel Mixer.png]]

**Category:** Utility
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A stereo-channel routing/mixing snap-in for combining or swapping left/right channel content.

## What it's used for

Custom stereo-channel routing tricks within a Snap Heap chain.

## FX chain notes

Used as a routing utility alongside other snap-ins.

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Left Gain | Level of the left input channel | Turn down to rebalance a lopsided stereo source |
| ② | Left Pan | Position of the left channel in the field | Pan both sides center to sum toward mono |
| ③ | Right Pan | Position of the right channel in the field | Cross L and R over each other to swap sides |
| ④ | Right Gain | Level of the right input channel | Match against Left Gain to re-center balance |
