# kHs Formant Filter

![[Images/kHs Formant Filter.png]]

**Category:** Pitch/Vocal
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A vowel-formant filter snap-in that imposes vocal-tract-like resonances on a signal.

## What it's used for

Vowel-sweep, talk-box-style effects on synths and vocals.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain for creative sound design.

## Key Parameters

*Tap a numbered circle for what that control does, the technique, and any artist example.*

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Vowel pad | XY pad — drag the point to morph between vowel formant shapes (i, e, a, o, u…) | Sweep for a talking/vocal-filter effect |
| ② | Q | Sharpness / resonance of the formant peaks | More for a pronounced vowel; less for subtle color |
| ③ | Lows | Toggles the low formant band in/out | Off to thin the vowel toward its upper formants |
| ④ | Highs | Toggles the high formant band in/out | Off for a darker, more closed vowel |
