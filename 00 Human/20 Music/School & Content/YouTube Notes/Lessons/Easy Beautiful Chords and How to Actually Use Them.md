# Easy Beautiful Chords and How to Actually Use Them

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdMdGgEmQCU)

## Synopsis

Six one-note embellishments to common open chords (Em→Em add9, D-shape→Dadd4, C→Csus2, Am→Am add9, F→Fadd9/maj9, G→G6/9), revealed to spell out the "Hallelujah" chord progression, plus a rule for safely placing a maj7♯11 chord using the key's IV chord.

## Techniques

Full extraction routed to [[One-Note Chord Embellishments]]. Highlights:

- Each embellishment changes exactly one fretted note from a familiar open-chord shape.
- The worked progression (Em add9 → Dadd4 → Csus2 → C → G7 → Csus2) turns out to be the "Hallelujah" progression — a demonstration that famous-sounding progressions are often just familiar chords lightly embellished.
- **maj7♯11 placement rule:** safely usable on the key's IV chord (find it by counting a 4th up from the root — trivial on guitar since standard tuning is in 4ths).
