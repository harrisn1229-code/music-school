# 5-Minute Multi-Finger Tapping Workout

A single tapping riff (multi-finger tapping plus tapping slides) played along at a progressive tempo ladder starting at 30 BPM. Doubles as a fretting-hand technique workout, since tapping demands very precise fretting-hand control.

## How to run it

- Play along at each tempo step; log the date and tempo you get stuck at, come back the next day and try to beat it.
- If genuinely stuck (not just "hard," but uncontrolled) well below a reasonable target, that's a signal to work tapping fundamentals in isolation before returning to this workout.

## Why `reinforces: []` is correct here

Pure tapping-hand/fretting-hand coordination on a fixed riff — no scale, mode or chord-tone knowledge involved.
