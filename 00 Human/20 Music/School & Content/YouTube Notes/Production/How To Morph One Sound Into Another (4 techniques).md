# How To Morph One Sound Into Another (4 techniques)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jdXhjfQhk3Y)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jdXhjfQhk3Y)

## Synopsis

A tight, technique-per-minute rundown of four distinct ways to morph one sound into another, each demoed on real material: an Instrument Rack Chain Selector crossfade (manual, then LFO-automated) between two instruments; a Vocoder driven by an External carrier (a bass track feeding a vocal) rather than the default noise carrier; Hybrid Reverb's convolution side used to impose a found object's resonant character (teapot, wooden frog, water drips) onto a bongo loop; and Ableton's Morph device used not just for a simple two-source blend but routed so an entire non-drum submix crossfades against a single external bass loop. All four techniques are genuinely reusable and map cleanly onto existing plugin/technique notes.

## Techniques

- **Chain Selector crossfade between two instruments, then LFO-automate it** — group two instruments into an Instrument Rack (Cmd/Ctrl+G), balance their relative levels first, then in Chain view overlap both chains' zone bars across the full selector range (rather than splitting them into separate zones) so dragging the Chain Selector bar blends between them; map an **LFO** to the Chain Selector (slow Rate, ~2, and try non-triangle/random shapes) for a continuous, automatic morph instead of a manual drag. → [[Randomized Rack Design]], [[Morph]]
- **Vocoder with an External carrier for a vocal/bass morph** — switch Vocoder's Carrier from Noise to **External** and feed it a second track's audio (a bass loop) so the vocal takes on the bass's tonal character; mute the carrier track while dialing it in, trade intelligibility vs. grit with the band count, restore clarity with **Enhance**, and finish with [[OTT]] to glue and reinforce the low end. → [[Vocoder]], [[OTT]]
- **Hybrid Reverb convolution-only mode with found-object impulse responses** — Dry/Wet to 100%, Blend fully to the convolution side (isolating that engine from the algorithmic half), then drag in ANY sample as the impulse response — a teapot, a wooden frog toy, water drips — to impose that object's resonant character onto a source (a bongo loop). Shape the borrowed character with Size (doubles as pitch), Attack, and Decay. → [[Hybrid Reverb]]
- **Route a whole submix into Morph's second input for a group-vs-loop crossfade** — group every non-drum element into one Group track with Morph inserted, then route a separate track's audio output into Morph's Audio 2 input (via that track's Audio To routing) — Morph's crossfader now blends the ENTIRE submix against a single external loop, not just one instrument against another. → [[Morph]]
