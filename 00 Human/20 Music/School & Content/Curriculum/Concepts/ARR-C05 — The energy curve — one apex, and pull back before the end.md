# ARR-C05 — The energy curve — one apex, and pull back before the end

**Q.** Sketch the shape of a whole song's energy, and give the two rules about how energy changes at boundaries.

> [!question]- Answer
> Start deliberately **low** so there's room to build; build to exactly **one** clear apex, not several equal peaks; then **pull the energy back down a notch after the apex** before the ending, because sustaining the peak to the outro reads as exhausting rather than climactic. At each major boundary, add or remove **3–4 elements simultaneously** rather than gradually, so the transition reads as a felt jump — and punctuate it with a crash.

## Why it matters

Both rules cut against instinct: gradual change reads as drift rather than transition, and refusing to come down after the peak flattens the peak retroactively.

## Drill

Draw the energy curve of a reference track you love, boundary by boundary. Then draw yours. Compare where the apex sits.
