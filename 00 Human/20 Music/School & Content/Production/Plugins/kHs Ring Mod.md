# kHs Ring Mod

![[Images/kHs Ring Mod.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A ring-modulation snap-in multiplying the input signal by a carrier oscillator for metallic, robotic tones.

## What it's used for

Robotic, bell-like, and dissonant textures for sound design.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain for creative sound-design effects.
