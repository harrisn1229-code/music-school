# HAR-C02 — Authentic cadence

**Q.** Which two chords make an authentic cadence, and what does it do?

> [!question]- Answer
> **V → I.** Dominant to tonic — a full stop. The strongest closure in tonal harmony.

## Why it matters

This is the default ending of most phrases in most Western music; not having the name means not having a way to think about *why* a phrase feels finished.

## Drill

Find the authentic cadence in three songs you know. Then remove it — end on V instead — and hear what changes.
