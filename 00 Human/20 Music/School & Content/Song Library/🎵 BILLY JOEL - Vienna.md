## Production & Arrangement
Phil Ramone's clean, uncluttered late-'70s production — the arrangement is **built around Billy Joel's solo piano**, which does most of the harmonic and rhythmic work; everything else supports it:

- **Piano front-and-center:** virtuosic, stride/jazz-inflected, wide dynamic range — recorded warm and close, minimal processing (let the performance carry it).
- **Accordion** (Dominic Cortese) is the signature color on the intro/verses — the "old-world Vienna" cue that makes the arrangement instantly identifiable.
- **Restraint:** bass + light drums enter to lift sections, not to drive — a lot of the song is nearly solo piano + voice. Dynamics come from *arrangement* (adding/removing parts), not from a loud mix.
- **Vocal:** intimate, upfront, lightly compressed — a storytelling vocal, not a belted one.
