# Synthet - Crucial Tip for SOME Music Producers

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PTnRnI10vDM)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PTnRnI10vDM)

## Synopsis

The shortest video in this batch: a tape-stop-then-reverse recipe (demoed with DBL's tape-stop plugin) that turns the normal "slowing down and stopping" effect into a "rewinding backward" riser when the slowed section is reversed — usable on instrumental sections and, with a different reversal point, on vocals for a "whoosh in from a tape stop" entrance effect.

## Techniques

- **Reverse a tape-stop for a riser/rewind effect instead of a wind-down** — take a slower-than-normal tape-stop on a section of the track and reverse that clip; the normal winding-down character plays backward into a rising, "rewinding" riser. Amount/character is tunable via how slow the original tape-stop is set. → [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]]
- **On vocals, reverse the tape-stop (not the source) for a "whoosh-in" entrance** — applied to a vocal instead of an instrumental section, this reads as the vocal "whooshing out from a tape stop" into its entrance, rather than a wind-down exit effect.
