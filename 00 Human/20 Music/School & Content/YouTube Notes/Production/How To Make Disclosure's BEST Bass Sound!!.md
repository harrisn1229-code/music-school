# How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)

## Synopsis
A tight, six-minute Sol State breakdown of the full signal chain behind one specific Disclosure bass sound, built around Ample Sound's Ample P Bass. It walks the whole path front to back: picking a modeled P Bass over a synth for a naturally warm/muddy starting tone, driving the instrument's own built-in amp simulator hard while stripping the top end, deliberately voicing the bass "dead" (near-zero upper harmonics) because the rest of the arrangement — kick, sine-wave pads, vocal, sparkles — already owns that space, then finishing with a light compressor, a tiny touch of Decapitator, a bit-crusher, and a clean tape-saturation stage for amp-like fizz and grit. The most reusable idea is the multiband sidechain move: only the bass's own sub band gets crushed hard against the kick (~80–90%) while the rest of the signal barely ducks (~30%), so all the finger noise and movement in a busy bassline stays audible while the sub still clears out of the kick's way. It closes on arrangement/performance choices — a Monarch synth layered on top for compressed fatness, the entire bassline written as one continuously-evolving MIDI region with a different fill nearly every time rather than a looped pattern, heavy swing with deliberately "sloppy and late" timing, and slightly different slide/glide rates between the two bass layers for extra motion.

## Techniques

- **Start from a modeled P Bass, not a synth, for a "warm and pure" foundation.** Ample Sound's Ample P Bass gives a naturally muddy, honest low end before any processing is added — pull plugins away first and lean on what the instrument itself already sounds like, rather than reaching for a synth bass and shaping it into "bass guitar" from scratch. → [[Bass Design]] — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
- **Use the instrument's own built-in amp simulator to pick a character before adding outboard processing.** Ample P Bass includes a selectable amp-sim stage (an Ampeg-style model, a "bass head" option, and others) — choosing an amp model here does real tonal work before any EQ or saturation plugin gets stacked on top of it. — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
- **Drive the amp sim hard, strip all the top end, and watch for peaking.** Push the amp simulator's input aggressively, then cut the high end completely — nothing above the low-mids survives — while checking the meter for a bit too much peak and pulling back slightly rather than letting it clip unchecked. → [[Saturation and Distortion]] — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
- **Boost around 100 Hz for body, but be ready to duck the same range again later.** A deliberate low-mid boost gives the bass its weight up front; the same ~100 Hz zone gets partially ducked a second time later on via the sidechain once it started clashing with the rest of the low end — an EQ boost and a later dynamic duck at the same frequency are two different tools solving two different problems, not a contradiction. → [[EQ]] — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
- **Make the bass "dead" on purpose because the rest of the arrangement already owns the top end.** With kick, bass, sine-wave pads, lead vocal, and sparkles all stacked and busy above it, the bass is deliberately voiced with almost no upper-harmonic content and ducked out of the way instead of competing — think of the arrangement as a vertical chart (kick → bass → sine waves → vocal → sparkles) and decide who owns each zone before mixing. → [[Masking and Carving]] — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
- **Let the instrument's own velocity response do tone-shaping instead of automating a filter.** The bass patch reacts to MIDI velocity: hard-hit (high-velocity) notes buzz and distort more, soft (low-velocity) notes stay clean and "dead" — programming dynamics into the MIDI itself becomes a tone-shaping tool, not just a volume one. → [[Bassline Writing]] — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
- **A tiny amount of Decapitator, not a heavy dose, adds amp-like "fizz."** Rather than driving Decapitator for obvious distortion, a very small amount blended in on top of the amp-sim adds the extra fizz/grit character you'd get from a real bass amp — used as a finishing touch, not a main drive stage. → [[Decapitator]] — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
- **Layer in a bit-crusher for extra buzz alongside the saturation.** A touch of bit-crushing stacks with the Decapitator fizz for more analog-style buzz in the tone. → [[Redux]] — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
- **A clean tape-saturation setting adds harmonics without changing the tone's character.** A subtle tape-style saturation stage — a clean setting, not driven hard — rounds off harmonics and glues the signal together. → [[Saturation and Distortion]] — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
- **Real analog performance is never identical twice — lean into that instead of fighting for perfect consistency.** Even a single real 909 clap sample is slightly different every time it's triggered on real hardware; getting lazy with digital/sampled sounds (the same chord or hi-hat repeating identically) is what makes a track feel digital — introduce deliberate per-hit variation instead. → [[Automation]] — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
- **Multiband-sidechain ONLY the bass's own sub, not the whole signal, so finger noise and detail survive the duck.** Rather than sidechaining the whole bass volume against the kick, a multiband compressor squashes just the sub band hard (roughly 80–90% reduction) while the rest of the signal only ducks lightly (~30%) — on a busy bassline with lots of movement/finger noise, this keeps all that detail audible while still clearing sub-frequency space from the kick. → [[Multiband Compression]], [[Sidechaining]] — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
- **Layer a second synth (Monarch) on top of the bass for extra fatness via heavy compression, not more low end.** Adding Monarch over the P bass and running it through a hard, "badass" compressor setting fattens the composite bass sound — the character reads as "really compressed" rather than as an added sub layer. → [[Compression]] — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
- **Write the entire bassline as one continuous, never-repeating MIDI region instead of looping a pattern.** Guy and Howard went through the whole part "meticulously," writing a genuinely different bass fill almost every time it plays rather than copy-pasting a loop — the goal is a performed, jam-like feel rather than a repeating pattern. → [[Bassline Writing]] — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
- **Push the groove's swing hard and let the bass sit deliberately late/sloppy.** The bassline is tracked with heavy swing and intentionally "sloppy and late" timing rather than quantized-tight — treated as a feel choice, not a mistake to fix. → [[Rhythm and Groove]] — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
- **Automate slightly different slide/glide rates between layered bass parts for extra motion.** The P bass and the Monarch layer on top use slightly different portamento/slide rates from each other, adding a small amount of movement between the two layers rather than having them glide in lockstep. → [[Automation]] — Sol State, [How To Make Disclosure's BEST Bass Sound!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqb2o8fj4-E)
