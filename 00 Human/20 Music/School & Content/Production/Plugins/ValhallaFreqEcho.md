# ValhallaFreqEcho

![[Images/ValhallaFreqEcho.png]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Valhalla DSP

## What it is

Valhalla's free frequency-shifting echo plugin, combining delay with frequency shifting (rather than pitch shifting) for inharmonic, evolving echo textures.

## What it's used for

Shifting, metallic, evolving delay textures distinct from standard tempo-synced repeats.

## FX chain notes

Used as a creative send/insert delay effect for texture design.

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Shift | Shifts the echo's pitch by a fixed Hz (inharmonic) — the big center knob | Small for shimmer; large for clangy Barberpole echoes |
| ② | Delay | Echo time | Short for metallic; long for spacey |
| ③ | Feedback | Repeats/regeneration | High for spiraling frequency-shifted trails |
| ④ | Low Cut / High Cut | Band-limits the echoes | Cut lows/highs so the shifted trails don't clutter the mix |
| ⑤ | Mix | Dry/wet blend | Low insert; high on a send |
| ⑥ | Modes (Delay Sync / Output) | Free vs. tempo-synced delay, and mono/stereo output | Sync for rhythmic shifts; Stereo for a wide barber-pole |
