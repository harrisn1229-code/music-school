# How I programmed the Baby Be Mine MiniMoog Bass on Michael Jackson's Thriller

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LAONVvvixE4)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LAONVvvixE4)

## Synopsis

The bass companion to the "Baby Be Mine" warble-lead breakdown — a two-oscillator Minimoog bass, both oscillators at the SAME octave (not layered an octave apart, unlike most of this channel's other bass recipes), one sawtooth, one square, both cranked.

## Techniques

- **Sawtooth + square in UNISON (same octave, not an octave apart) is a distinct bass-layering option from this channel's more common octave-doubled recipe — mixing waveform character rather than register.** Both oscillators at 32', one sawtooth one square, both full volume; the waveform mix alone (not an octave split) supplies the harmonic complexity — a square-only pairing was demonstrated as noticeably "boomier"/rounder, sawtooth-only as thinner/harsher, and the sawtooth+square combination as the chosen middle ground.
- **A DELIBERATE small amount of natural mistuning between the two oscillators produced audible chorusing, and it's framed as worth keeping rather than "fixing."** General advice given in the same breath: tune oscillators regularly since they drift on their own (especially older/analog instruments) — but a little residual imperfection, here, is the source of the patch's character, not a flaw to eliminate outright.
- **Filter keyboard tracking used here in SMALL amount for bass** — brightens slightly going up the keyboard so the part "talks a little more" in its upper range — a third data point (alongside "off for bass," "half for a mid-register lead," "inverted") on how variable this single setting is depending on the specific patch and part, not a fixed bass-vs-lead rule.
- **Filter sustain level and VCA sustain level do two DIFFERENT jobs during a held note, and both matter**: filter sustain level controls how BRIGHT the note stays while held (prevents the tone going dead/dull on a sustained note); VCA sustain level controls how LOUD it stays. Both were set to keep the note "alive" through its hold, but along independent axes — worth remembering that "sustain" on two different envelopes isn't the same control duplicated, it's two separate things to dial in.
- **"Use these Thriller sounds as an example of proven starting points, then match the settings to your own song's tempo/character"** — stated directly as the intent of the whole breakdown series: not "recreate exactly," but "here's a working reference to adapt."
