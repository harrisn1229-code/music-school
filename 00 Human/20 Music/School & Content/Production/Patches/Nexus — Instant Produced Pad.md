# Nexus — Instant Produced Pad

**Instrument:** [[Nexus]] · **Slot:** [[BED Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Synth Bed Track|Synth Bed Track]]

## What it's for
Instant produced pads for pop/trap when speed matters more than customization — Nexus patches arrive already mixed and effected, so the job is picking well and getting out of the way, not sound design.

## Settings
- Browse the `Pads` category, audition ≤5 patches, commit — Nexus patches are pre-produced, so patch choice IS the sound design here
- The 4 macro knobs (mapped per-patch via the mod matrix) are the only real-time controls — use them for brightness/movement instead of digging into oscillators
- FX tab: trim or disable any internal reverb/delay that duplicates the chain's own sends — Nexus patches often ship "wet"
- Attack/release are baked into the patch; pick a patch whose envelope already matches the Step 1a universal pad rule (slow in, slow out) rather than trying to re-shape it
- No sub octave — most Nexus pads already roll off the low end, confirm with a solo/HPF check before the chain's own HPF

## In context
- Reach for this over [[Omnisphere — Layered Dark+Airy Bed]] / [[Wavetable — Evolving Motion Pad]] / [[Drift — Evolving Motion Pad]] specifically when there's no time budget for layering or filter automation — same [[BED Slot]], produced-and-done instead of built.
