# Do This 5 MIN Routine Every Morning!! (FUN Technique Workout)

## Synopsis

A daily 5-minute morning workout built on a single 4-notes-per-string alternate-picking lick that forces a big fretting-hand stretch — the stretch requirement is deliberate: you physically can't play it with sloppy fretting-hand technique, so the drill self-corrects hand position.

## Techniques

- **4-notes-per-string alternate-picking lick, deliberately wide-stretch.** Forces correct fretting-hand form because incorrect form makes the stretch physically impossible — technique correction built into the drill's geometry rather than taught separately.
- **Thumb placement: support from behind the neck, not draped over the top.** Draping the thumb over the neck makes the required stretch impossible; supporting from behind lets the fingers spread to their maximum reach. Direct, concrete fretting-hand-position instruction.
- **Keep the picking wrist relaxed and moving even at tempos above 200 BPM**, rather than locking into a stiff full-arm motion — same relaxed-wrist-through-speed principle as [[Directional Alternate Picking System]] and BERNTH's other workouts this batch.
- **Diagnostic framing**: getting stuck at a tempo plateau on this drill is treated as a signal of an underlying picking-technique problem, not just "not fast enough yet" — points toward BERNTH's separate Alternate Picking Masterclass for fundamentals rework (not owned/pulled into the vault).
