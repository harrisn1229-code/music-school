## Synopsis

A left-hand technique workout, framed around a diagnostic: wrist pain, fatigue, or a burning sensation while playing is a technique-error signal, not just discomfort to push through.

## Techniques

- **Pain/fatigue/burning as a technique diagnostic, not a toughness test.** BERNTH names an extreme fretting-hand-angle habit (some students stretch/angle so far it becomes physically painful) as the direct cause of wrist pain, fatigue, or burning — treat those sensations as a signal something in the technique is wrong, not something to play through.
- **Flat-finger angle blocks neighboring strings from ringing** — reiterates [[Fretting and Picking Hand Mistakes Checklist]]'s finger-angle point with a concrete failure case: flat fingers physically muting the string needed for the next note in a spider-style exercise, making the exercise impossible to complete cleanly rather than just sounding worse.
- Same "fingers close to fretboard, minimize movement" economy-of-motion principle as the rest of this batch.
