# SYN-C01 — The signal path

**Q.** Describe the signal path of a basic subtractive synth voice, and say where envelopes and LFOs act.

> [!question]- Answer
> **Oscillator → Filter → Amplifier.** Oscillators generate the raw waveform (optionally mixed with a sub and noise); the filter removes harmonics; the amplifier controls level over time. **An envelope drives the amp** — nothing is audible without it. A second envelope usually drives the **filter cutoff**, which is where character comes from. **LFOs modulate whatever you route them to** — pitch for vibrato, cutoff for movement, amp for tremolo.

## Why it matters

The single most load-bearing fact in synthesis. Every subtractive synth you own is these three blocks in this order — learn it once and every synth becomes the same instrument with a different skin. It also explains the three classic problems: no attack (amp envelope), dull no matter what (wrong waveform), doesn't evolve (nothing modulating).
