# 5-Minute Alternate Picking Workouts (BERNTH)

Two paired 5-minute daily play-alongs covering two distinct alternate-picking sub-skills:

- **Hand synchronization** — a scale pattern with open strings woven in, moved to four fretboard positions. The open strings expose sync errors a fretted note would hide.
- **Endurance** — sustaining fast picking over a longer passage rather than a short burst, with a specifically lighter/relaxed attack (a hard "burst" attack doesn't hold up over a sustained passage).

## How to run it

- Run either (or both) daily; log the tempo/point you got stuck at and try to beat it next session.
- For the endurance workout specifically, keep picking attack light — resist the urge to attack harder as fatigue sets in.
