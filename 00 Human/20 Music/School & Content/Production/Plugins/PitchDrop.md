# PitchDrop

![[Images/PitchDrop.png]]

**Category:** Pitch/Vocal
**Vendor:** Max for Live

## What it is

A Max for Live pitch-drop effect that automates a smooth pitch-down sweep on a triggered note/signal.

## What it's used for

Classic pitch-drop transition/riser effects at drops and transitions in electronic production.

## FX chain notes

Runs inline in the signal/MIDI chain at the point it's inserted; combines with whatever device follows.
