# Ian Kirkpatrick — Using AutoTune to Craft Hit Songs

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RbhaAZihry8)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RbhaAZihry8)

## Synopsis

An Antares interview: Ian on why Auto-Tune is core to his workflow (speed and taking pressure off the singer), how he treats it as a creative *instrument*, the "Pretty Please" vocal that never left the tracking session, his approach to designing reverb presets, and his #1 advice for new artists: **give me a reason to care.**

## What's taught

- **Auto-Tune for efficiency, not just pitch** — its real value is speed: get songs written fast, take pressure off the vocalist, and "tune 30–40 layers of background vocals in a jiffy." "If I didn't have Auto-Tune I probably wouldn't have a career — I'm very impatient." → [[Auto-Tune Pro]], [[Dua Lipa Vocal Chain]]
- **Auto-Tune as a deliberate instrument** — tune *harder here, softer there* as a creative choice; even on non-vocals (he's put Auto-Tune on a **guitar**). Sia's "Chandelier" hard-tuned line is the reference. → [[Auto-Tune Pro]], [[Pitch and Formant]], [[Ian Kirkpatrick - The Production Secret Behind Huge Pop Hits]]
- **"Pretty Please" — the tracking take is the record** — recorded the day-of on an **SM7B, real-time Auto-Tune, on the couch**, and it never went past **EFX** — he left it on the track and that was it. Honest delivery > perfect capture. → [[Dua Lipa Vocal Chain]], [[Shure SM57]], [[Ian Kirkpatrick - Pretty Please (24 Big Tips)]]
- **Keep honest takes Auto-Tune makes usable** — a delivery a few cents off but emotionally right shouldn't be wasted; Auto-Tune saves the take without distracting the listener from the message. → [[Auto-Tune Pro]]
- **Reverb-preset design philosophy (Antares Vocal Reverb)** — he built presets for **every situation he ever needed**: big 80s hall, small intimate room, "next-door-neighbor's-music" far sound — so he never has to tweak parameters mid-flow. → [[Reverb]], [[Depth and Space]]
- **Auto-EQ in the reverb replaces post-reverb Soothe** — cranking Vocal Reverb's auto-EQ knob killed resonances so well he "didn't put anything after the reverb," which is unusual for him; gives clarity + space with less de-essing and no post-reverb dynamic EQ. → [[Reverb]], [[De-essing]], [[Soothe2]]
- **Discipline: "am I doing this out of habit, or serving the song?"** — when he has no clear vision he defaults to habit; lately he opens the compressor category and picks a *random* one to break routine. → [[Compression]], [[Ian Kirkpatrick - New Rules (25 Big Tips in 13 Min)|serve the song]]
- **#1 advice — "give me a reason to care."** "You love your music because you made it" — get past that to *is it actually good?* Stacking loops isn't enough; if you're selling to an audience, consider the audience. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[Ian Kirkpatrick - On Loops, Artist Ego and Originality]]
