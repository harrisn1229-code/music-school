# BAS-C07 — Decide who owns the sub — don't EQ both

**Q.** Kick and bass are colliding in the low end. What's the diagnostic and the fix?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Diagnostic:** put an analyser EQ on each and play them together to find where each fundamental actually peaks — a real case had the kick peaking ~55 Hz with a second bump at ~120 and the bass also at ~55, a direct collision. **Fix:** pick **one** to hold the sub (the source with more low-end content, if the kick is otherwise interchangeable) and high-pass the *other* up until the clash stops. If high-passing the kick guts it, swap in a kick with more inherent high-end character first, then cut that one.

## Why it matters

Ownership is a decision, not a compromise. EQing both leaves you with two thin sources and the same collision. Sidechaining and kick-length automation by section are the same decision made in time rather than frequency.

## Drill

Analyse your current kick and bass fundamentals. Write down which one owns the sub. High-pass the other until it stops fighting.
