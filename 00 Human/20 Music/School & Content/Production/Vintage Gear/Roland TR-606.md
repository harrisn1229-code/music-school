# Roland TR-606

## What it is
The Drumatix — a silver pocket-sized analog drum machine sold as the companion to the [[Roland TB-303|TB-303]] Bassline (same case, same sequencer DNA, DIN-sync them together and you had a two-box band). Like everything in that pairing it flopped, then became a cult machine: tinny, hissy, aggressive little analog drums that cut through anything.

## What makes the sound
- **Fully analog voices, miniaturized** — bridged-T kick and snare (thinner and faster than the 808's, almost no sub), and a **detuned square-oscillator stack for hats/cymbal** like a shrunken 808 — the 606's sizzly, metallic hats are widely considered its best feature and sample-pack staple.
- **Tiny envelopes everywhere**: everything is short, clicky, and forward — the 606 doesn't sit *behind* a mix, it perforates it. Great raw material for distortion/processing because there's no low-end mud to fight → [[Saturation and Distortion]].
- **The 303-family step sequencer** with DIN sync — the hardware handshake that made the 303+606 acid combo a self-contained live rig.
- No individual voice tuning (mods exist for exactly this reason) — the kit is the kit.

## Digital versions
- **Owned:** [[Drum Rack]] / [[Impulse]] with 606 samples; [[Microtonic]] to synthesize 606-style thin analog kits from scratch; [[Saturator]]/[[Decapitator]] for the distorted-606 techno move.
- **Paid:** Roland Cloud TR-606 (official), D16 Nithonat (dedicated 606 emulation); Behringer RD-6 in hardware.
- **Free:** 606 sample packs everywhere.

## Used on
- Genre/era: the acid-house rig companion to the 303 (Chicago acid, [[EDM Subgenres]] acid card), 80s electro and synth-punk, and a whole lineage of IDM/lo-fi techno artists who prize its hiss — where specific credits are contested, the association is genre-level by design.
- **Pitched down and blended quietly under the main drum part, for a soft understated low layer rather than a rhythmic lead.** Elsewhere on the same album, layered again with a [[Vermona DRM1]] hit pattern for "heavy but crunchy" character alongside a Gamechanger Audio Plasma Coil-driven guitar sharing the same percussive attack quality. → [[Layering and Stacking]] — Charli XCX / A.G.
