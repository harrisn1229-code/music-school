# Reaktor 6

![[Images/Reaktor 6.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Native Instruments

## What it is

Native Instruments' modular sound-design and synthesis environment, running both NI-built and community-built instruments/effects ('ensembles').

## What it's used for

Deep modular synthesis and running community-built instruments/effects not available anywhere else.

## FX chain notes

Used as the instrument source (or effect, depending on the loaded ensemble).

## Key Parameters

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Ensemble / Instrument | Hosts a Reaktor synth/instrument ensemble | The parameters come from the loaded ensemble |
| ② | Macros | The ensemble's exposed controls | Depends on the instrument loaded |
| ③ | — | Modular instrument host | See the specific ensemble's controls |
