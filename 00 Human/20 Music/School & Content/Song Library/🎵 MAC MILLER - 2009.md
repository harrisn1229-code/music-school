## Production & Arrangement
From *Swimming* (2018) — Mac's late-period pivot to **live instrumentation and jazzy, reflective production**. Opens with a **live string arrangement + piano** before the beat drops; warm, organic, and confessional.

- **Live players over samples:** strings, piano, real bass give it a lush, band-like depth.
- Jazzy [[Extended Chords|extended-chord]] harmony under a conversational flow; the arrangement breathes (intro → beat → strings return).

## Steal this
- **Live-string/piano intro** before the drums for a cinematic, reflective open ([[Keyscape]] + a string patch, played not programmed-feeling).
- **Jazzy extended chords** under hip-hop flow → [[Extended Chords]] · [[Seventh Chords]].
- **Let the arrangement breathe:** intro → beat → return the strings; dynamics by adding/removing, not by loudness.
