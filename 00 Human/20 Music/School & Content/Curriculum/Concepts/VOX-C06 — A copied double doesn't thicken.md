# VOX-C06 — A copied double doesn't thicken

**Q.** What's the difference between a real double and a copied-and-delayed one, and why does it matter?

> [!question]- Answer
> A **real double** is a second performance — timing, pitch and tone differ naturally, and **that variation is the thickening**. A **copy is identical**, so summing it just makes the signal louder; delay or pitch it and you get comb filtering or slapback, not width.

## Why it matters

A copied-and-pasted double is the same performance twice, so it sums like one louder track rather than two voices — the Lennon-era fix for exactly that is **ADT**. Kept as a concept because it's the reason the whole stack section works, and because [[Artificial Double Tracking (ADT)]] is the deliberate exception: a *sound*, not a substitute.

## Drill

Duplicate a vocal and sum it — note it only gets louder. Now delay the copy 20 ms. Then compare both to a genuinely re-sung double.
