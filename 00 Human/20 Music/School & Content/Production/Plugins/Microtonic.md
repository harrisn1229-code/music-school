# Microtonic

![[Images/Microtonic.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Sonic Charge

## What it is

Sonic Charge's classic analog-style drum synthesizer, generating an entire electronic drum kit from synthesis (no samples).

## What it's used for

Fully synthesized electronic drum kits for techno, house, and IDM-style production.

## FX chain notes

Used as the drum instrument source, typically followed by a drum-bus compressor and saturation.
