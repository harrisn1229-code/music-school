# ValhallaSpaceModulator

![[Images/ValhallaSpaceModulator.jpg]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Valhalla DSP

## What it is

Valhalla's modulation effect combining chorus, flanger, and phaser-style modulation with spacious ambience.

## What it's used for

Lush, spacious modulation effects (chorus/flanger/phaser hybrids) for width and movement.

## FX chain notes

Used as an insert or send effect, post-EQ, for modulation-driven width.

## Techniques

- **Chorus BEFORE reverb, purely to thicken a dry lead vocal — a custom preset run as the very first FX-chain decision, ahead of any reverb.** On Sam Smith's lead ("Dancing With A Stranger"), a personal Space Modulator setting ("with a couple of things I've added to it") goes on first, stated goal "making them sound a little thicker" — body-adding, not width/movement, and sequenced before the plate-reverb blend that follows it. → [[Little Plate]], [[Modulation]] — Kevin Davis (Mix with the Masters), [Mixing Vocals from 'Dancing With A Stranger' with Kevin Davis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bQIZONdZvk8)
