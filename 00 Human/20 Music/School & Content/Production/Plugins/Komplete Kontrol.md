# Komplete Kontrol

![[Images/Komplete Kontrol.png]]

**Category:** Utility
**Vendor:** Native Instruments

## What it is

Native Instruments' browser/host plugin for NKS-format instruments and effects, providing unified browsing and hardware-controller integration (for Komplete Kontrol keyboards).

## What it's used for

Browsing and loading Kontakt, Massive, Reaktor, and other NI/NKS instruments with deep hardware controller mapping.

## FX chain notes

Acts as a host wrapper around an instrument, sitting where that instrument would normally go in the chain.

## Key Parameters

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Browser / Preset | Unified browser for NI instruments | Audition + tag-search Komplete library sounds |
| ② | Macros | Mapped one-knob controls per preset | Ride the macros for fast sound tweaks |
| ③ | — | A host/browser for NI instruments | The instrument itself lives in the loaded plugin |
