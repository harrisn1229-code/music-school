# BLU-C02 — Why every chord is a dominant 7th

**Q.** In a blues all three chords are usually dominant 7ths — including the tonic. Why, when the major scale doesn't give you that?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Because blues doesn't follow major-key functional harmony.** The dominant 7th is the **home sound**, not a chord that needs resolving. The ♭7 is colour, not tension.

## Why it matters

This is the single most important idea in the strand. It explains why one scale works over all three chords, why the ♭3 isn't an error, and why the form circles rather than resolving. Without it, every other blues fact looks like a violation of the rules.

## Drill

Play I–IV–V in A as plain triads, then as A7–D7–E7. Name what changed.
