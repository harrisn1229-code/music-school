# DRM-C03 — Tune the kick to the key — and what to do when the key is too low

**Q.** Why tune a kick at all, and what's the move in a very low key?

> [!question]- Answer
> A kick on the wrong note fights the song; tuned to the root it *reinforces* the key (Latch's kick hits F every bar and establishes an F minor the track otherwise avoids). In a very low key the root is unusable — a C sub-kick flaps speakers and vanishes outside a club — so **tune it to the fifth above and let it bend**. Related: a knock kick that lives around 100 Hz with nothing at 50 leaves the low end entirely to the sub or 808.

## Why it matters

This is the decision that connects drum design to harmony, and it's the one most people never make. It also determines whether you *need* a separate sub layer at all.

## Drill

Take a track in C minor. Tune the kick to C, then to G, then A/B on headphones and on a phone speaker. Note which register disappears where.
