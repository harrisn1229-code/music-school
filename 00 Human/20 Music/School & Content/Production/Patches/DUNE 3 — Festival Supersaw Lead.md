# DUNE 3 — Festival Supersaw Lead

**Instrument:** [[DUNE 3]] · **Slot:** [[LEAD Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Synth Lead Track|Synth Lead Track]]

## What it's for
Big festival/EDM leads and supersaws — DUNE 3's unison engine is the whole point here, wide and anthemic instead of tight and modern (that's the [[Serum 2 — Modern Lead]] lane).

## Settings
- Derived from [[DUNE 3#Patch Recipes|DUNE 3 — Lead / pluck]], widened for the festival lane: Osc 1 VA saw or WT with a swept Position, ② SWARM tuning for internal shimmer
- ⑨ Unison: 8–16 voices (wide end of DUNE 3's range — the recipe's default 2–4 is too tight for this use), moderate–wide Detune and Spread
- Filter Env → ⑥ Cutoff, fast decay for pluck shape at the front of each note
- Amp Env: fast attack, medium decay
- Kill content below ~100 Hz at the patch — sub stays with the bass/808
- FX: tempo-synced delay send; chorus off (unison already supplies the width)

## In context
- Wide by design — check [[Stereo Field]] mono-fold before printing; this patch is the one Step 1a exception where max width is correct, not a mistake.
- Vibrato via mod wheel, not baked in, so the part can stay static when it needs to.
