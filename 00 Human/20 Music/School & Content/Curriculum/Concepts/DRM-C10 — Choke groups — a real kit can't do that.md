# DRM-C10 — Choke groups — a real kit can't do that

**Q.** An open hi-hat rings through the following closed-hat hit. Why is that wrong, and what fixes it?

> [!question]- Answer
> A physical kit can't do it — one hand, one hat. Enable **"cut itself"** on the sampler channel or group so a new hit truncates the previous tail; a zero-velocity note at the cutoff point is an alternative or a stack. Watch the same problem in the low end, where overlapping tails are far uglier — bass sample tracks have it too.

## Why it matters

Part of a family of physical-plausibility constraints that make programmed drums read as played — the four-limb ceiling (a real drummer has two hands and two feet) is the same idea applied to pattern writing rather than tails.

## Drill

Find an open-hat into closed-hat transition in an existing beat. Enable choke. A/B it. Then check your 808 track for the same overlap.
