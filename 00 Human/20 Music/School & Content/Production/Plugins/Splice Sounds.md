# Splice Sounds

![[Images/Splice Sounds.jpg]]

**Category:** Utility
**Vendor:** Splice

## What it is

Splice's sample browser/manager plugin for searching and previewing the Splice sound library.

## What it's used for

Browsing and dragging in samples/loops from the Splice catalog during production.

## FX chain notes

A browsing utility, not part of the audio signal chain.

## Techniques

- **A chopped string loop as the fallback when no VST string sound was "sounding really good."** On a 2000s R&B piano sample, after trying (and rejecting) VST string instruments, a Splice string loop was pulled instead, chopped up, and layered directly on top of the piano bed — a reminder that Splice is a legitimate first-class source, not just a backup for one-shots/vocals. → [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[2000s R&B Piano Sample Chain]] — loner, [how to make a 2000's r&b sample & flip it into a beat for tory lanez/bryson tiller](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sPqBmZLaDM4)
- **Pull a melodic vocal one-shot/phrase and reverse it, for a softening contrast layer against harsh synths.** Recreating Future's *Looking Exotic*: found a melodic vocal phrase on Splice, reversed it, and layered the resulting backwards-attack swell in specifically to contrast the sample's harsher synth elements — a found-vocal-texture move, not a lead vocal. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]] — loner, [the secret samplemaker behind Southside's best beats!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WLOHe9CJChY)
- **Source a vocal pack, then stretch the whole take to double speed before finishing with lo-fi treatment** — when a Splice vocal pack's processed take (reverb/delay/EQ) doesn't have the right character, stretching the sample to double its original speed can be what actually unlocks the tone ("sounds so fire"), done BEFORE the final sample-rate-lowering + chopping pass rather than as an afterthought. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]] — loner, [[the secrets behind OZ's 'her loss' production!?]]
- **Auto-transpose a whole batch of loops to the song key** — filter the Splice browser tab for loops in a given mode (e.g. minor only), then click any result's **Key** field and pick the song's key — every loop shown transposes to match on the spot, so a pile of random melodic loops can be dragged in already in-key rather than key-matched one at a time after the fact. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]] — dnksaus, [Auto Transpose Trick - Live 12.3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aUvQ8Bvv3lY)
