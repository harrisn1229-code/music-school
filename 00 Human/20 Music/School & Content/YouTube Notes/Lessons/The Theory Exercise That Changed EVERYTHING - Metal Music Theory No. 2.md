## Synopsis

Second entry in BERNTH's "Metal Music Theory" mini-series. Builds a full chord→arpeggio→7th-arpeggio→scale progression from a single known E minor chord shape, so every layer stays visually anchored to the same starting shape rather than requiring separately memorized patterns.

## What's taught

Full extraction routed directly — see [[Positional CAGED Soloing with Matched Modes and Arpeggios]] for the complete breakdown: starting from a basic E minor voicing (root on the 7th fret A string), the E minor triad arpeggio is built by locating the same three chord tones (root/♭3/5th) in nearby fretboard positions (max 2 notes per string); the minor 7th arpeggio adds exactly one new note (the ♭7) with nothing else changed; a 3-note-per-string E minor scale extends the same visual anchor into shred-oriented material. Demoed in a worked lick moving from the m7 arpeggio into the scale (with a chromatic passing-tone twist) and back to the original chord voicing.
