**Category:** Reverb/Delay

## What it is

Sonic Charge's glitch/bitcrush delay effect inspired by vintage sampler hardware, offering unusual buffer-manipulation effects.

## What it's used for

Glitchy, granular, bitcrushed delay and buffer-manipulation effects for sound design.

## FX chain notes

Used as a creative send/insert effect, often automated for glitch transitions.
