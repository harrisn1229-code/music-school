# Disclosure - 134bpm Halftime Vibe Beat Jam

A from-scratch **beat jam** with Howard ("the other guy") — not a breakdown, so low technical density and heavy on banter (a long Harry-Potter track-naming bit; they named it "Chinny Weasley"). Value is in watching the *workflow* of building a halftime idea live: hats-first, tiny soft kicks, a vocoder-synth chord bed Howard "speaks through," and a lot of "establish the rhythm, then go wrong, then come back." Captions transcript — high confidence, but genuinely thin on new reusable technique; most of it reinforces notes already in the vault.

## Techniques
- **Build hats-first, then tiny soft kicks.** Start with a swung hi-hat groove, then a *short/soft* kick layered as "lots of little ones" (dense but sparse-feeling), left slightly out of time on purpose ("it's good out of time"); pitch the kick once the key is decided. → [[Kick Design]] (reinforces short-and-punchy).
- **Vocoder-synth chord bed the singer "speaks through"** — a Roland vocoder-synth on a **sawtooth LFO going slightly out of tune each pass** (8th/16th), with Howard talking the melody into it (block chords, since a vocoder needs held notes). Bounce an 8th-LFO version *and* a more-extreme 16th-LFO version, then pick/choose which plays when. → [[Modulation]].
- **Sample the chords into a sampler to replay them rhythmically** — bounce-in-place the chord synth → make a sampler instrument → trigger the chords in a tight rhythm (fix any overlapping/ghost notes first so chord *changes* land clean). → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]] (same chord-chop idea as [Blue World](https://youtu.be/lFTEptvdBg8)).
- **"Adding chords changes your entire idea of everything"** — drums first, then chords reframe the whole direction; expect to rethink the beat once harmony lands. → songwriting.
- **Bass:** Repro-1, made attacky; a **triplet/syncopated pattern with gaps** ("boom-bom-bonk … then something more syncopated"), mono/legato on the downbeats (303 as the alt); sidechain. → [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]].
- **Fade an element in when it enters at an awkward time** — a ride that came in "at a weird unpredictable time" was smoothed by fading it in, paired with a shaker fade-in so they arrive together. → [[Automation]].
- **"Establish the rhythm, then go wrong, then come back."** For glitchy/unpredictable stab moments: set the groove, then break it (ghost notes at lower velocity, slightly late; a remix-effects plugin on the pad bus for stutter), then resolve — and do a **ton of takes, commit favorites to audio**. Don't commit to the wonky version early. → [[Automation]].
- **Leave decisions that need fresh ears** — "that's one of those decisions you'd need fresh ears for; I'll do something else and in 10 minutes I'll know." → workflow.
