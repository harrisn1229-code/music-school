# S1 Shuffler

![[Images/S1 Shuffler.png]]

**Category:** Pan/Imaging
**Vendor:** Waves

## What it is

Waves' frequency-dependent stereo shuffler, widening low-mid content differently than highs for more natural-feeling width.

## What it's used for

Natural-feeling stereo widening that avoids over-widening the low end.

## FX chain notes

Used late in a mix or master chain for width shaping.
