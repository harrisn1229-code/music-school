# Reaktor 6 MFX

![[Images/Reaktor 6 MFX.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Native Instruments

## What it is

The Max for Live-hosted version of Reaktor 6, integrating Reaktor ensembles directly into Ableton's M4L framework.

## What it's used for

Same modular synthesis/ensemble-running role as Reaktor 6, integrated via Max for Live.

## FX chain notes

Used as the instrument or effect source within a Live session.
