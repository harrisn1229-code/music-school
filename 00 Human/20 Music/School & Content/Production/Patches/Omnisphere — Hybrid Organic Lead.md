# Omnisphere — Hybrid Organic Lead

**Instrument:** [[Omnisphere]] · **Slot:** [[LEAD Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Synth Lead Track|Synth Lead Track]]

## What it's for
Hybrid/organic lead texture no subtractive patch has — Omnisphere's soundsource library instead of built-from-scratch synthesis. Reach for it when the part needs a real acoustic or found-sound character riding underneath the melody.

## Settings
- Derived from [[Omnisphere#Patch Recipes|Omnisphere — Hip hop lead/bell (Mike Dean lane)]]: ② `Keyboards` or `Bells` category soundsource for the organic core
- ④ Harmonia: +12 voice at ~40%, slight detune, for width without a synthetic unison stack
- ⑥ Mono-ish width, portamento on (Step 1a universal lead rule: glide is expected, vibrato via mod-wheel not baked in)
- Fast attack, short-to-medium release so it stays a lead and not a pad
- Kill content below ~100 Hz at the patch
- ⑩ Saturation applied in Ableton, not in the patch — keep the source clean

## In context
- The go-to when Serum/DUNE/Massive/Drift/Model 84 all sound "too synth" for the part — texture over precision.
- Sits after the chain's saturation stage on [[1 Synth Lead Track|Synth Lead Track]]; the patch itself should stay dry and clean.
