## Synopsis

A comparison-format episode (borrowed format from Paul Davids' guitarist series, done with his blessing and one of his own backing tracks): six different YouTube piano players each improvise a solo over the identical chord progression, and Bennett reacts to how differently each approached it.

## What's taught

Not a structured lesson — a reaction/comparison video. The one generalizable observation worth keeping: Charles Cornell's approach is framed as "a lesson in keeping things simple" — he initially planned elaborate reharmonizations and cool substitutions, but found the backing track didn't actually support that much complexity, so he scaled back to a simpler approach that fit the material better. Worth remembering as a real-time example of a soloist adjusting ambition down to match what a track actually calls for, rather than forcing a pre-planned complex approach onto it.

## Routed to
