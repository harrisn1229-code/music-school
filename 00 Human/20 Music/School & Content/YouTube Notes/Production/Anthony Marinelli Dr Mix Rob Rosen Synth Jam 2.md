## Synopsis

The finale companion to [[Anthony Marinelli Doctor Mix Synth Jam 1]] — same day, same cabin studio at Rosen Sound, a three-way live improvisation. Dr. Mix plays Oberheim OB-X, OB-8, Prophet-5, and Prophet VS; Rob Rosen plays Oberheim Eight Voice and CS-80; Anthony plays CS-80, Oberheim Matrix-12, and two ARP 2600 units. Pure jam, essentially no spoken content beyond intro/outro.

## What to watch for

Same value as Jam 1 — watching three experienced players' hands and patch/performance choices across a genuinely large spread of vintage polysynths (OB-X, OB-8, Prophet-5, Prophet VS, Oberheim Eight Voice, CS-80 ×2, Matrix-12, ARP 2600 ×2) in one improvised ensemble piece, rather than reading an explanation. The companion narrated tour on Doctor Mix's own channel ("Welcome To Synthesizer Nirvana") remains the better source for explained technique from this same collection.
