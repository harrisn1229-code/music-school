## Production & Arrangement
From *Blonde* (2016), produced with **Pharrell Williams**, with **Beyoncé on (buried) backing vocals**. Built on a gorgeous **rising piano figure** and lush, string-like synth pads that swell into a soaring outro — one of the album's few tracks that leans *lush* rather than stark.

- The **ascending piano arpeggio is the hook** — harmony and motion in one gesture.
- Warm, wide, reverb-forward; the star feature (Beyoncé) is mixed *into* the texture, not spotlighted.

## Steal this
- **Arpeggio-as-hook:** write a rising piano/keys figure that carries the harmony and the melody at once ([[Keyscape]] / [[Operator]]).
- **Bury the feature:** mix a guest vocal *into* the pad/stack for depth, not as a spotlight — texture over credit.
