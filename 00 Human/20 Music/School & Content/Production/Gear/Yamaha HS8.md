# Yamaha HS8

**Category:** Studio Monitors (pair, 8" powered nearfield)
**Vendor:** Yamaha
**Ballpark value (used, 2026):** $450–600 (pair)

## What it is

Yamaha's 8" powered nearfield monitors — the white-cone descendants of the NS-10 philosophy: honest, mid-forward, flattering nothing.

## What it's used for

The main mixing/production monitors — if a mix translates on these, it translates; the 8" woofer reaches low enough to judge 808s/[[SUB Slot]] decisions without a subwoofer.

## Signal chain notes

Interface main outs; use the ROOM CONTROL/HIGH TRIM switches to compensate wall proximity. Check low-end decisions against headphones + phone speaker anyway ([[FX Chain System]] translation checks).
