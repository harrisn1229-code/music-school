# BLU-C03 — Quick change, 8-bar and minor blues

**Q.** What's a quick change? How does an 8-bar blues differ? How does a minor blues differ harmonically?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Quick change** — bar 2 moves to the **IV** instead of staying on I. **8-bar blues** is a real, separate form ("Key to the Highway"), not a truncation. **Minor blues** uses **i7 iv7 v7**, often with a ♭VI at bar 9 — but the **V frequently stays dominant**, because you still want the leading tone pulling home.

## Why it matters

Quick change and 8-bar both blank; minor blues got half credit for 'minor 3rd instead of major 3rd', which is the right core idea. The V-stays-dominant detail is the part that's easy to get wrong.

## Drill

Write a quick-change 12-bar in A and a minor blues in Am. Note where the V differs.
