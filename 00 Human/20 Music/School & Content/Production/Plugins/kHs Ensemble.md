# kHs Ensemble

![[Images/kHs Ensemble.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A multi-voice chorus/ensemble snap-in for rich, thick modulation effects.

## What it's used for

Thick, lush widening on pads, synths, and vocals — heavier than a standard chorus.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain, post-EQ.
