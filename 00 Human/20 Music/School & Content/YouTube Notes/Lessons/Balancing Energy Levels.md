## Synopsis

A whole-song energy-curve planning lesson, walked through against a real track: start deliberately low so there's genuine room to build, add or remove several elements simultaneously at each major boundary (rather than gradually) so transitions read as a felt jump, punctuate those moments with a crash, build toward one clear apex rather than several equal peaks, and pull the energy back down a notch after the peak before ending. Most of the underlying mechanics (tiered energy-building, crash+fill transitions, 3-changes-per-section) are already well documented elsewhere in the vault from more detailed videos — this one's distinct contribution is the whole-song-level planning frame: graphing the entire arc before/while arranging, not just building one section-to-section tier at a time.

## Techniques

- **Graph the whole song's energy curve as a planning tool** — start low deliberately (so a later big boost has somewhere to go and doesn't need to be sustained for the whole track), add/remove 3–4 elements simultaneously at each major structural boundary for a felt jump rather than a gradual drift, punctuate those moments with a crash, build toward exactly one clear apex, and deliberately pull back a notch after the peak before the ending rather than sustaining maximum intensity all the way out. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[Rhythm and Groove]]
