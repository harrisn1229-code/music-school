## Synopsis

A 7-scale, easiest-to-hardest tour (chromatic, whole tone, pentatonic, blues, major-scale modes, melodic minor, harmonic minor) plus a "hardest bonus" of playing a full major scale as climbing drop-2 chord voicings. Mostly reconfirms scale/mode theory already documented in depth elsewhere in the vault; two practical application tips were new enough to route.

## Techniques

- **Whole tone scale over a ii-V-I's V chord: start from the chord's 3rd**, not its root — the easiest practical entry point, since the root is already contained in that run.
- **Chromatic scale, practically:** best for tension and as passing tones; specifically effective in the last bar or two of a section, signaling an upcoming change.
