# MIX-C05 — LUFS and streaming normalization

**Q.** What does LUFS measure, and why does it matter for streaming?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Integrated perceived loudness over time** — not peak level. Streaming services **normalize playback** to a target near **−14 LUFS**, so a master pushed to −7 simply gets turned down on playback.

## Why it matters

'Perceived loudness' is where most people stop. The commercial half is the normalization: pushing past the target costs dynamics and buys nothing, because the platform undoes the loudness and keeps the damage.

## Drill

What happens to a −7 LUFS master on Spotify versus a −14 one? Which sounds better, and why?
