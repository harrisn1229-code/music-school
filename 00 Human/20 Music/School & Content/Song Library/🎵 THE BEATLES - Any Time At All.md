# Any Time At All — The Beatles

## Pollack's analysis

Formulaic-looking and not: the form belongs to neither the one- nor the two-bridge model. A frequently recurring refrain dominates folk-ballad style, there are only two verses, the bridge is held back to near the end, and it brings genuinely new material rather than recycling.

**Form.** An 8-bar closed refrain opens the record on a drum thwack landing on beat 2 — you can't place it in the meter until the second refrain. The verse is an unusual **14 bars**, its lopsided 6 + 8 really the same 6-bar phrase twice with the second's first bar elided onto the first's last. The bridge runs 10 bars; the outro is a petit reprise of the refrain's tail.

**Harmony.** A small chord set close to home, carrying two Lennon–McCartney trademarks: the vi → I cadence that opens the refrain, and a nearly fully chromatic walking bass under the verse ([[POP-C04 — Four chords with a moving bass]]). The **minor iv in a major key** isn't a colour chosen for itself — the bass walking B down to B♭ to A is what puts it there ([[HAR-C08 — Borrowed chords and modal interchange]]). The bridge builds tension by approaching V and backing off repeatedly before releasing, and its opening phrase is built from contrary motion in the outer voices, which is why ordinary seventh chords land in unusual inversions.

**Melody.** Arch-shaped, somewhat pentatonic, and stuffed with appoggiaturas ([[HAR-C09 — Non-harmonic tones]]); bluesy F naturals cross-relate with the F♯s underneath and make the leaning tones bite. The tune is short interjections with gaps, and the guitar and bass figures filling those gaps are real motifs — one returns quietly at the end of the verse and comes back transformed at the bridge's climax ([[MEL-C03 — Motifs and how to develop them]]).

**Arrangement.** John's double-tracked lead carries almost everything, except that Paul takes the refrain's second line in hocket — a texture change and a range fix at once. The whole track rides one flavour of syncopation, the harder "4-and" kind where the following downbeat isn't explicitly marked ([[RHY-C02 — Syncopation]]).
