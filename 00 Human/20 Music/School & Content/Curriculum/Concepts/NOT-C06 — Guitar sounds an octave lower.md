# NOT-C06 — Guitar sounds an octave lower

**Q.** Guitar is written in treble clef but doesn't sound at the written pitch. What's the relationship?

> [!question]- Answer
> **It sounds an octave LOWER than written.** Guitar is a transposing instrument — written pitch sits an octave *above* sounding pitch (effectively 8vb).

## Why it matters

Direction matters — 'an octave higher' is the reverse and is wrong. Directly relevant to reading anything written for you, and to writing parts for other instruments.

## Drill

Play written middle C on the guitar. Find the same sounding pitch on a piano. Note the octave difference.
