# Anthony Marinelli Doctor Mix Synth Jam 1 | CS80, 73 Moog Modular, FVS, ARP 2600

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3ez6ChhvMas)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3ez6ChhvMas)

## Synopsis

A pure live-improvisation jam, not a technique video — recorded at "the cabin" studio at Rosen Sound, the first time Anthony introduced Rob Rosen to Doctor Mix. Dr. Mix plays a CS-80 and an Oberheim Eight Voice; Eli Abstein plays LinnDrum and DMX drum machines; Anthony plays a vintage Moog Modular System 55 alongside two vintage ARP 2600 units simultaneously. Essentially no spoken content beyond the intro/outro — captions carry almost nothing usable, and there is no narration to extract technique from during the jam itself.

## What to watch for

Since this is an unnarrated improvisation, the value is in watching the performers' hands and patch choices in real time rather than reading a description of them — worth watching for how a Moog Modular System 55 and multiple ARP 2600 units get played together live (patching/performance choices happening in real time, not verbally explained), and how a CS-80 and Oberheim Eight Voice sit alongside that in a group improvisation. A companion video on Doctor Mix's own channel ("Welcome To Synthesizer Nirvana") gives a full narrated tour of the same Rosen Sound cabin studio and gear collection with Rob Rosen — likely the better source for actual explained technique from this same setup.
