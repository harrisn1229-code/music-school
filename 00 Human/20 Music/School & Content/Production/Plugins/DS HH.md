# DS HH

![[Images/DS HH.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Ableton (Drum Synths pack, Max for Live)

## What it is

One voice from Ableton's Drum Synths pack — a synthesized hi-hat generator (metallic noise through a resonant filter bank) for open/closed hat sounds.

## What it's used for

Fully synthesized hi-hat patterns for house/techno-style beats without needing sampled hats.

## FX chain notes

Used as a drum-voice instrument in a Drum Rack; typically followed by a short transient-shaping compressor and a drum-bus saturation stage.
