# Within You Without You — The Beatles

## Pollack's analysis

**Form:** a sprawling **ABA** conceived in three large blocks — two vocal sections around a central instrumental. Intro (drone fades in, dilruba lays out the main arch riff) · regular Verse (AAB, the parallel phrases six measures each, the third rhythmically free) · extended Verse (identical opening, third phrase stretched so it feeds the refrain — a trick lifted from their ordinary pop songs) · Refrain (freer still; the beat count per measure changes about as often as it would in Stravinsky or Bartók) · Instrumental (two free variations on the extended verse) · Intro · extended Verse · Refrain.

**There is one chord for the entire song.** A tamboura drone holds I throughout ([[POP-C03 — Pedal point]]), so every structural event has to be carried by melody and meter alone — which makes this the cleanest available demonstration of how much a tune can do without harmony.

The scale is essentially Mixolydian, but a raga is not a mode: Western modes map one scale to one name, ragas map one scale to many, distinguished by which degrees carry emphasis and which riffs characterise them ([[SCL-C05 — Characteristic notes]]). Verses keep the melodic floor on 3 and hold the octave back until late; refrains push 2, 4 and 6 and leave the tune hanging on 2 for the dilruba to answer.

The best move is in the extended verse, which **breaks its own scale with a ♭3** by transposing the verse motif's interval pattern onto scale degree 1 ([[MEL-C03 — Motifs and how to develop them]]). And the floating meter is faked — Indian practice fixes a tala; here the meter itself simply changes.
