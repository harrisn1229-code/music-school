# Kenny Beats — How to Make a Guitar Sample

![](https://youtu.be/i86bG2OsOSQ)

## Synopsis

A live tutorial recording DI guitar into Ableton and turning it into a sample: clean signal in, his named "Jai Paul" Guitar Rig + RC-20 preset chain, CLA Guitars as first-aid for thin DI, stack-and-pan doubles, a contrasting layer, then "f*** it up like a sample." Music-heavy stream — transcript covers the spoken segments (which carry all the technique). **Directly relevant to the comp-tool guitar-loop lane and [[1 Electric Guitar DI Track|Electric Guitar DI Track]].**

## Techniques

- **Clean signal in > expensive guitar** — "you could have a really cheap guitar and if the signal is really, really clean, processing it and making it sound great doesn't even matter." Buffer ~128, low sample rate if the machine chokes, tune BEFORE the effects. → [[1 Electric Guitar DI Track|Electric Guitar DI Track]], [[Latency-Free Tracking]]
- **The "J Ruff / J1" (Jai Paul) preset chain** — DI guitar → **[[Guitar Rig 7 FX|Guitar Rig]] funky patch → [[RC-20 Retro Color]] on top**; the J1 variant adds a **very short room reverb**, RC-20 on the **Flux chorus preset with wobble pulled down**, and a **MicroShift-style widener** ("makes the guitars super f***ing wide" — owned lane: [[Doubler4]]/[[Width Send]]). The instant lo-fi-funk guitar-sample tone. → [[1 Electric Guitar DI Track|Electric Guitar DI Track]]
- **CLA Guitars = first-aid for weak/thin DI** — "no one really knows exactly what it does" but it boosts, cleans, adds body instantly; **kill its corny little delays** first. He rates Kemper/CLA fuller-feeling than Guitar Rig's presets. Unowned → [[Plugin Wishlist]]; owned first-aid lane: [[UAD Neve 1073]] drive + [[Saturn 2]]. → [[1 Electric Guitar DI Track|Electric Guitar DI Track]]
- **Real bass needs sub help** — CLA Bass / R-Bass / MaxxBass "give a real bass the kind of sub your 808s have" (second Kenny endorsement of the R-Bass idea — see [[Kenny Beats - Bass Mix and Effects Chain]]). → [[1 Electric Bass DI Track|Electric Bass DI Track]]
- **Cut the ~15k super-high-end when stacking guitars** — DI guitars throw top-octave energy "you're not used to"; stacked, it becomes "this weird white-noise ringing thing." LPF/shelf each layer as you stack. → [[2 Guitar Group|Guitar Group]], [[Masking and Carving]]
- **Stack immediately: same part twice, panned opposite** — record the part, "off top just stack that immediately," second take, pan the pair opposite; then a **contrasting layer** on the other preset for a different color. → [[Width and Stereo]], [[1 Acoustic Guitar Track|Acoustic Guitar Track]] §2 logic on electrics
- **Treat the finished loop like a SAMPLE** — "once we take all this we're gonna f*** it up like a sample… it's got to get a bunch of parts": pitch it, chop it, don't leave it precious. The whole point of recording your own guitar loop is sample-flip freedom without clearance. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Cmd+U quantize works on audio** — quick timing fix on recorded takes before committing.
