# Get Lightning FAST Fingers In 2 WEEKS! (Full Workout)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-qsPg0xCu_4)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-qsPg0xCu_4)

## Synopsis

A 10-minute, six-block comprehensive technique workout meant to be repeated daily for two weeks, touching alternate picking, legato/stretching, sweep/economy picking, odd-meter string transitions, tapping, and hybrid picking in one pass.

## Techniques

1. **Warmup — alternate-picking ascending arpeggios in 5/8, key of Bb major**, tempo increasing with each repetition; strict alternate picking (not economy) specifically to drill string transitions.
2. **Legato/stretching block** — hammer-on/pull-off exercise with the fretting thumb positioned behind the index finger (rather than the middle finger) specifically to support a wider stretch; picking-hand middle finger used for a hybrid-picking note right before the last note of each sequence.
3. **Sweep/economy picking block** — several common arpeggio shapes with a specific, tab-defined picking pattern (not left to interpretation) rather than free-choice fingering.
4. **String-transition block — alternate picking with mixed odd meters, switching between 6/8 and 7/8 measures.** Requires both downstroke-escape and upstroke-escape motions to avoid getting stuck between strings; explicitly calls out staying relaxed through the meter switches, not just the technique itself.
5. **Tapping block** — a practical lick combining multi-finger tapping and tapping slides; pick held between index finger and thumb throughout so the middle and ring fingers are free to tap.
6. **Hybrid-picking block** — pick plus middle and ring fingers plucking the D/G/B strings, aimed specifically at fast string-skipping lines that would be awkward with the pick alone.
