# Model 84 — Juno Bass

**Instrument:** [[Model 84 Polyphonic Synthesizer]] · **Slot:** [[BASS Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]]

## What it's for
Analog-style synth bass for indie/synthwave — the real Juno-circuit alternative to [[Drift — Analog Synth Bass]], with the documented "HPF at 0" low-bump trick for extra body.

## Settings
- From [[Model 84 Polyphonic Synthesizer#Patch Recipes|Model 84 — Juno bass]]: ① square + sub full, saw off (or saw + sub for a rounder tone)
- ③ **HPF position 0** — the low-bump trick, not a typo: position 0 boosts rather than cuts here
- ④ VCF ~40–50%, res low
- ⑤ Env → VCF: medium amount, fast attack, short decay — the pluck
- ⑥ Chorus OFF — bass stays mono ([[Stereo Field]])
- ⑧ Unison for a fat mono stack; sidechain it for a synthwave 16ths pulse if the arrangement calls for it

## In context
- Same BASS-slot lane as [[Drift — Analog Synth Bass]]; pick this one for the real Juno chorus-off circuit character rather than Drift's cleaner modern filter.
- Chorus deliberately off here (unlike [[Model 84 — Juno Lead]]/[[Model 84 — Juno Pad]]) — width on a bass patch causes mono-fold problems.
