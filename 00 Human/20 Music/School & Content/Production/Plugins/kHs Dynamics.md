# kHs Dynamics

![[Images/kHs Dynamics.png]]

**Category:** Compressor/Limiter
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A combined compressor/gate/expander dynamics snap-in.

## What it's used for

Flexible dynamics shaping combining gating and compression in one snap-in.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain, early, before tonal shaping.
