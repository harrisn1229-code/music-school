# 13 Songs that Interpolate other songs

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s6dVJtLFZpA)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s6dVJtLFZpA)

## Synopsis

A dedicated interpolation deep-dive (re-recording an existing melody/composition rather than sampling the original audio) — clean definitional examples plus a real crediting dispute that shows interpolation still requires songwriter credit even without a literal sample.

## Techniques

- **Interpolation definition, reinforced with worked examples**: taking an excerpt of an existing composition and re-performing/re-recording it as part of a new song — not a cover (the surrounding song is new), not a sample (no original audio used). "7 Rings" (Ariana Grande) interpolates the melody of "My Favorite Things" (Sound of Music) with new lyrics/chords. "Play That Song" (Train) interpolates "Heart and Soul" (Hoagy Carmichael, 1938), also re-set with new chords and lyrics.
- **A commonly-misidentified case, useful as a calibration example**: "Gangster's Paradise" (Coolio) is widely assumed to sample Stevie Wonder's "Pastime Paradise," but the instrumentation was entirely re-recorded — it's interpolation, not sampling, despite sounding almost identical.
- **Interpolating the accompaniment, not just the melody**: interpolation isn't limited to a vocal hook — Amy Winehouse's "Tears Dry on Their Own" interpolates the *instrumental* of "Ain't No Mountain High Enough" (Marvin Gaye & Tammi Terrell), and "Rapper's Delight" (Sugar Hill Gang) raps over a re-recording of Chic's "Good Times" instrumental, not the original recording.
- **Crediting isn't automatic just because it's a re-recording — the "Rapper's Delight" cautionary case**: Sugar Hill Gang initially didn't credit Chic's Nile Rodgers and Bernard Edwards at all; they were only added to the songwriting credits after Chic threatened legal action. A concrete reminder that interpolation still uses someone else's composition and still owes a songwriting credit/clearance, even though no master-rights (audio) issue exists.
