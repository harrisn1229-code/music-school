# SCL-C02 — Harmonic and melodic minor

**Q.** What single note changes between natural and harmonic minor? What does melodic minor do ascending?

> [!question]- Answer
> Harmonic minor **raises the 7th**. Melodic minor ascending raises **both the 6th and the 7th** — A melodic minor asc = **A B C D E F♯ G♯**.

## Why it matters

Both blank. Harmonic minor exists for a reason worth knowing: raising the 7th creates a real leading tone, which gives a minor key a functioning **V** chord. Melodic minor raises the 6th too, to smooth out the augmented 2nd that harmonic minor leaves between ♭6 and ♮7. Direct link to [[Course 02 — Chord Function]].

## Drill

Spell A natural, harmonic and melodic minor side by side. Then build the V chord in each and say which works.
