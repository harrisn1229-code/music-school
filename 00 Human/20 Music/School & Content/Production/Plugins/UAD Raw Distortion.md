# UAD Raw Distortion

![[Images/UAD Raw Distortion.png]]

**Category:** Distortion
**Vendor:** Universal Audio — owned (confirmed 2026-07-17).

## What it is

Emulation of the ProCo RAT distortion pedal — gritty, aggressive hard-clipping distortion with a filter tone control.

## What it's used for

Guitar and bass grit, but also great as a color/parallel distortion on drums, synths and vocals for edge.
