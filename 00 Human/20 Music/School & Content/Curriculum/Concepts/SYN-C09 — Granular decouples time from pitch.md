# SYN-C09 — Granular decouples time from pitch

**Q.** What is granular synthesis, and what's its defining property?

> [!question]- Answer
> It **chops audio into very short grains (~1–100 ms) and replays them** with independent control over **position, size, density and pitch**. The defining property: **playback position and pitch are decoupled**, so you can freeze a moment indefinitely or stretch enormously without transposing.

## Why it matters

'Glitchy stuff' is a real use but undersells it badly. The decoupling is what makes everything else possible — texture, pads, extreme stretching, clouds from a half-second source. Its best use is as a **resampling target**: print something, granulate it, print again.

## Drill

Load one second of a bounce into [[Granulator 3]]. Find three different instruments in it at 5 ms, 40 ms and 150 ms grain size.
