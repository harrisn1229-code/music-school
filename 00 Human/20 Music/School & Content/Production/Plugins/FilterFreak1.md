# FilterFreak1

![[Images/FilterFreak1.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Soundtoys

## What it is

Soundtoys' resonant filter effect modeled on classic analog synth/mixer filter circuits, with tempo-synced modulation.

## What it's used for

Creative resonant filter sweeps and rhythmic filter modulation.

## FX chain notes

Used as an insert effect, often automated or synced for filter-sweep transitions.

## Techniques

- **Perform it, don't preset it** — for wavy alternate drum sections he skips the LFO/pattern presets and records himself moving the filter manually ("I like to just do it myself... record what you're moving"), printing the performance — Lophiile, [Maria Isabel Beat Breakdown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RXrF27xqogg)
