# Disclosure - 'Waterfall' Twitch Breakdown

Guy and Howard (with Raye's "Waterfall") break down a UK-garage tune, and it's one of the most tip-dense of the batch. Highlights: the **"fake compression" transient trick** (nudge a hi-hat/second kick ahead of the main kick), a whole philosophy of **drum-bus saturation that reads louder while the meter reads lower**, the **Gem Dopamine air-plus-de-ess** move, **slapback vs reverb** for chorus presence, and a very honest **mix→master handoff** (send at −10 to −6, let the mastering engineer do loudness). High caption confidence.

## Techniques
- **Carve vocal highs so hi-hats don't collide** in a bright genre — the vocal [[AIR Slot]] and the hats fight for the same top end; carve one or make the drums a touch less bright, but never dead drums. Garage lives in the treble. → [[Masking and Carving]], [[EQ]].
- **Lead vocal EQ:** pull ~4 dB of lows (HPF/shelf ~100–200 Hz), use dynamic EQ ([[Pro-Q 4]]) to tame a couple of mid resonances, boost the [[AIR Slot]] highs. → [[LEAD Slot]], [[EQ]], [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]].
- **Gem Dopamine trick:** boosts the highs *while* de-essing → air without harsh sibilance; follow with a subtractive de-esser.
- **De-ess the drum bus** a touch to control harsh hi-hats (de-essers aren't just for vocals).
- **One [[Soothe2]] on the vocal bus** (not per track — CPU-heavy) to tame harshness across everything.
- **Auto-tune as texture** on a super-low setting — the phasey/flangy artifact is character, not correction (more tracks left untuned than tuned).
- **Blue-stripe (1176) compressor for harmonics/distortion** on a bus, not dynamics control; minimal-to-no per-track compression when the recording is good (just EQ + de-esser).
- **"Fake compression" transient trick:** place a hi-hat/second kick a split-second *before* the main kick so a transient precedes the body — mimics how a comp lets a transient through. Don't quantize it (quantized sounds worse).
- **Layer two kicks:** a punchy sub kick ([[SUB Slot]]) + a kick with a hi-hat baked in; nudge the punchy one slightly ahead ([[TRANSIENT Slot]]). Short punchy garage kicks so they don't clash with the [[BASS Slot]]. → [[Kick Design]].
- **Drum-bus saturation philosophy:** tape sat + tube + a third saturator, each adding ~10% harmonics across the *whole* bus — it reads **louder and fuller while the meter reads lower** (6.9 vs 8). Tame the transients that would push into the red when summed; a safety limiter that barely engages. → [[3 Drums Bus|Drums Bus]], [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[Gain Staging]].
- **Overdrive to "pump up" a bass** before EQ; **Logic overdrive + Softube** for bass-amp fizz on top of the [[BASS Slot]] (mimics a real bass amp; you miss it when gone). EQ rolls the super-lows (HPF ~30 Hz), boosts the front-end click (~700 Hz–1.5 kHz so it reads on phones). → [[3 Bass Bus|Bass Bus]], [[EQ]], [[Saturation and Distortion]].
- **Decapitator "Punish" on a rap vocal**, violently driven, for heavy fizz as an effect.
- **Slapback delay instead of reverb** on the chorus bus so it feels "right there," while other sections use long reverbs — keeps the [[LEAD Slot]] present without flooding it. Delay automation in the vocal gaps for space. → [[Delay]], [[Delay Send Slap]], [[Reverb]].
- **Rotary/Leslie (Logic Roto Cab) on vocals** for an underwater, fluttery, less-conventional chorus/flanger. → [[Modulation]].
- **Old-radio string tone:** scoop all low-mids *and* highs to a narrow midrange band ([[MIDS Slot]], roughly a 400 Hz–3 kHz bandpass); distort a little. → [[EQ]].
- **Put a chop of the singer's voice in the drums** for balance and to introduce the vocalist before they enter → [[TEXTURE Slot]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]].
- **Effects build-up then mute** so the drop hits extra hard; let reverb hang over a drop to blend it.
- **Repro-1 (u-he) bass** for its front-end attack (moog VSTs miss it); using resonance as tone means no velocity control, so limit to keep top notes from being as loud as low ones. **Pigments/Omnisphere/DX7/ Poly Six** for pads and bells; **battery/LinnDrum/909/Prince clap/Hand Clap Studio** for drums.
- **Master bus, minimal:** gentle glue at ~60% wet, a touch of low-end control (−0.5 dB), another **[[Oxford Inflator]]**, **[[Saturn 2]]** subtle tube, a linear-phase master EQ (push a bit of mids, −1 to −1.5 dB super-highs) — bounced as a *screenshot guide* for the mastering engineer, limiter off.
- **Handoff loudness:** send at −10 to −6 dBFS, never hotter than −6; a controlled, saturated, quieter mix beats a hot one.
