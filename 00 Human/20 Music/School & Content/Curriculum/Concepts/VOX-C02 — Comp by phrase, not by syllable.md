# VOX-C02 — Comp by phrase, not by syllable

**Q.** How do you comp a vocal — what are you listening for, and where do comps go wrong?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Comp by phrase, not by syllable.** Listen for **delivery first** (which take means it), then pitch, timing, tone. Watch the seams — **cut on a breath**, keep the breaths, crossfade every edit. The failure mode is a **Frankenstein comp**: technically perfect, assembled from the best fragment of every word, and worse than your best single take because the performance stops being continuous.

## Why it matters

1.5/5 — you named pitch, timing and delivery, but ranked timing above delivery and had no method. The check that matters: **a comp should beat your best single take. If it doesn't, use the single take.**

## Drill

Comp one vocal twice — by syllable, then by phrase. A/B both against the best full pass.
