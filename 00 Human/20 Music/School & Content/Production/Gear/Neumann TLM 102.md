# Neumann TLM 102

**Category:** Microphone (large-diaphragm condenser, cardioid)
**Vendor:** Neumann
**Ballpark value (used, 2026):** $450–600

## What it is

Neumann's compact large-diaphragm cardioid condenser — transformerless (TLM) circuit, 144 dB max SPL, and a gentle presence lift above ~6 kHz designed for close vocal work.

## What it's used for

The vocal mic: lead vocals, acoustic guitar, and anything that needs detail and air without harshness.

## Signal chain notes

Into the interface preamp with a pop filter ~4" out; its gain-friendly output and high SPL handling also make it usable on the [[Fender Blues Junior]] cab or percussion.

## Manual

- 📄 [[Neumann TLM 102.pdf|Local PDF]] · [Official product/docs page](https://www.neumann.com/en-en/products/microphones/tlm-102/)

**High-level points:**
- **Fixed cardioid, no pad, no HPF switch** — all shaping happens by placement and in the DAW; the mic itself is deliberately simple.
- **144 dB max SPL** — it will take a guitar amp, drums, or a screamed vocal without a pad.
- The **slight rise above 6 kHz** is the built-in "air" — plan vocal EQ around it (you rarely need to add top).
- **TLM = transformerless**: very low self-noise (11.5 dB-A) and clean low end — quiet sources (fingerpicked FG730S, soft vocals) stay noise-free.
- Very compact body changes placement geometry vs a big-body LDC — the grille sits closer to where you point it than you think.
- Needs 48V phantom; shock mount recommended (small body transmits stand rumble).
