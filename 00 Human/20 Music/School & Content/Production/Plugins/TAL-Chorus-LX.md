# TAL-Chorus-LX

![[Images/TAL-Chorus-LX.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** TAL-Togu Audio Line

## What it is

TAL's chorus plugin modeled on the classic Juno-60-style analog chorus circuit.

## What it's used for

Classic analog-style chorus thickening on synths, guitars, and vocals.

## FX chain notes

Used as an insert effect, post-EQ, for width and movement.

## Techniques

- **A default, habitual reach on the vocal group bus — "which we rinse on," i.e. run near-automatically on almost anything passing through that bus stage.** One stage in a multi-part vocal-bus chain alongside occasional CLA Vocals, a "Helpless" EQ preset, sidechain ducking and a slapback delay. Not a considered per-song choice — closer to a default they lean on constantly. → [[1 Main Vocal Track]], [[Compression Staging]] — Overmono (Tape Notes), [[TN 187 Overmono - Full Video Episode]]
- **First effects move on a Juno-emulation synthwave pad, applied BEFORE the arpeggiator, not after.** Building an 80s synthwave/pop pad (attack/envelope pulled down first for a dry starting tone): chorus goes on the pad, THEN the arpeggiator is applied on top of the chorused sound — order matters here, since arpeggiating a dry patch and then chorusing it would read differently than chorusing the sustained chord first. Cassette/reverb/EQ/delay follow. → [[Cassette]], [[Model 84 Polyphonic Synthesizer]], [[Synthwave]], [[5 Inst & Synth/1 Synth Bed Track|Synth Bed Track]] — loner, [how to make an 80's synthwave/pop beat for the weeknd.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWhh27UFWhY)
- **Second stage of a 6-device "fake sample" Rhodes chain — chorus reached for right after Cassette, ahead of reverb/delay/EQ/a heavy Juno layer.** On a from-scratch Bryson Tiller-style '90s R&B chord bed (a Lounge Lizard Rhodes 4-chord progression, no real sampling), this device runs as "the chorus" stage second in line: [[Cassette]] → **chorus** → heavy reverb → heavy delay → EQ (cut lows + some mids) → a heavy free Juno emulation (owned-lane [[Model 84 Polyphonic Synthesizer]]) — the deliberately heaviest-processed of the build's three chord layers. → [[Cassette]], [[Model 84 Polyphonic Synthesizer]], [[BED Slot]], [[Bryson Tiller Fake-Sample Chord Bed Chain]] — loner, [how to make smooth rnb beats for bryson tiller from scratch (no samples)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZMB_sqF_Y3Q)
- **A SECOND, separate stage in a 9-device guitar chain — stacked after a corrective EQ and a first generic chorus, ahead of compression — called out by name as "the Juno chorus" and a repeat go-to ("I've used this in quite a lot of videos").** On a 6/8 "smooth/sexy R&B" guitar build: amp sim → reverb → chorus #1 (+ EQ boost) → [[EQ Eight]] (cut lows, boost highs, cut mids) → **this device** → [[Compressor]] ("rhythm guitar" preset) → [[Kramer Tape]] ("Reels," hiss up) → [[Kickstart 2]] (pulled back) → reverb (heavy, final). Two independent chorus stages plus a tape pass is what actually builds the width here — this device is the second of the three. Full chain: [[Smooth R&B 6-8 Chain]]. → [[Kontakt 7]], [[Width and Stereo]] — loner, [making a smooth/sexy rnb beat in 6/8 time signature](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mMZaktspUAA)
- **Stacked after a reverb (not before it) on a flanging preset, as the "make it unique" stage of a chorus-modulated-reverb macro chain.** Recreating one KMA Cirrus hardware-pedal feature: reverb (decay/mix macros) → this device on its **flanging preset** → a separate Mix macro on the chorus itself, three knobs total. Called out specifically as an arbitrary second device chosen to make an otherwise-generic reverb tail personal, rather than using the reverb's own built-in modulation. In a second, fully-wet chain from the same video, a chorus (name captioned "Clever" — unconfirmed, *medium confidence*) loaded with a **"vocal chorus" preset** sits between a granular texture stage and a half-time device. → [[ValhallaVintageVerb]], [[EffectRack]], [[Modulation]], [[TEXTURE Slot]] — loner, [this secret plugin will change the way you process your melodies!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xwuodd40bug)
- **Reached for over Ableton's stock Chorus specifically for a more musical width on synths** — called out by name as an upgrade over the built-in device rather than a from-scratch pick. → Sol State, [I found the 15 BEST FREE PLUGINS on the internet (and they are soooo good)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0BTcW2PNrnA)
- **One of his two favorite choruses (both free)** — the Juno-style TAL for keys/synth beds ("sounds awesome on this sound"), Blue Cat Chorus as the alternate flavor for guitars/vocals — Lophiile, [Sound Design With a Musical Mindset](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spPaJzYPgzk)

- **Send a mono vocal to a chorus for a touch of width** — kept subtle so the vocal stays centered and clear — Disclosure, [NO CAP](https://youtu.be/kz5dmb3QdI8)
