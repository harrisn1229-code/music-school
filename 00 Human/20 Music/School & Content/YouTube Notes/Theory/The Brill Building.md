# The Brill Building: where all the greatest hits were written!

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ulh3Izep0SU)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ulh3Izep0SU)

## Synopsis

A history of the Brill Building (1619 Broadway, NYC) and neighboring 1650 Broadway — the concentrated hub of professional songwriting duos who wrote the bulk of the era's rock and roll/pop hits for other performing artists, late 1950s–early 1960s. Primarily historical/attribution content rather than transferable technique, kept as a page-only reference.

## What's taught

- **Jerry Leiber and Mike Stoller** wrote much of Elvis Presley's early catalog ("Hound Dog," "Jailhouse Rock") plus 50s rock and roll standards ("Yakety Yak," "Charlie Brown" for The Coasters; "Love Potion No. 9" for The Clovers; "Kansas City," later covered by the Beatles) and, with the Drifters, "On Broadway" (co-written with Barry Mann and Cynthia Weil).
- **Barry Mann and Cynthia Weil** (a husband-and-wife duo) wrote "You've Lost That Lovin' Feelin'" (The Righteous Brothers), "We Gotta Get Out of This Place" (The Animals), and "Make Your Own Kind of Music" (Mama Cass).
- **Doc Pomus and Mort Shuman** wrote several more Elvis hits ("Surrender," "Viva Las Vegas," "His Latest Flame") plus Drifters classics ("Save the Last Dance for Me," "This Magic Moment," "Sweets for My Sweet") and "Can't Get Used to Losing You" (Andy Williams).
- **Gerry Goffin and Carole King** (also a married duo) broke through with "Will You Still Love Me Tomorrow" (The Shirelles).
