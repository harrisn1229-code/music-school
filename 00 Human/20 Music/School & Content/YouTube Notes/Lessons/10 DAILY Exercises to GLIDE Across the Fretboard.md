# 10 DAILY Exercises to GLIDE Across the Fretboard

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w_o8gonyGKk)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w_o8gonyGKk)

## Synopsis

Ten daily exercises spanning fretting-hand strength/coordination, picking-hand speed, chord-shape stretches, sweep picking, away-from-the-guitar rhythm counting, and a pre-playing warm-up routine. Sourced/credited across several teachers (Joe Satriani, Paul Davids, John Petrucci, Thomas Macroclin).

## Techniques

Full ten-exercise writeup routed to [[Ten Daily Fretboard-Gliding Exercises]]. Highlights:

- Exercises 1–2: classic 1-2-3-4 finger drill and an anchored-finger variant.
- Exercise 3: right-hand-only 16th-note string-crossing speed drill, deliberately starting on an upstroke.
- Exercise 4: the "Macroclin" two-string interleaved-interval pattern.
- Exercises 5–6, 8: Satriani's staircase chord shape, Paul Davids' arpeggiated/fixed-finger version, and a Petrucci-credited sweep-picked version of the same shape.
- Exercise 7: away-from-the-guitar metronome rhythm counting, down to 16th-note subdivisions at very slow tempos.
- Exercise 9: a two-finger micro version of exercise 1, playable as trills.
- Exercise 10: a full pre-playing hand warm-up routine (hot water, massage, stretching, optional callus trimming).
