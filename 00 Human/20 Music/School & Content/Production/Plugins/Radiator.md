# Radiator

![[Images/Radiator.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Soundtoys

## What it is

Soundtoys' saturation plugin modeling vintage tube preamp and power-amp circuits for warm analog coloration.

## What it's used for

Analog-style warmth and harmonic saturation across vocals, drums, and busses.

## FX chain notes

A coloration stage, typically before EQ and compression.

## Techniques

- **A subtle mix-bus warming stage, run "quite a lot" on their buses — not a distortion effect here.** Used right after a deliberately unconventional dry+wet-hard-panned-to-mono synth-doubling trick (see [[Layering and Stacking]]), ahead of a very light preamp-emulation top-end lift and a subtle reverb — its job in this chain is glue/warmth, not character distortion. → [[Layering and Stacking]] — Overmono (Tape Notes), [[TN 187 Overmono - Full Video Episode]]
- **Distortion stage on a woozy guitar-effects chain, paired with iZotope Trash (unowned)** — Mike Dean's *Rodeo* intro-track guitar trick runs a dry electric guitar through auto-tune, tape/cassette, EQ, delay, then this device plus Trash for "more Distortion," finished with half-time automation for the psychedelic texture. → [[Cassette]], [[Auto-Tune Pro]], [[Saturation and Distortion]] — loner, [the secrets behind mike dean's 'rodeo' production!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9FhndW8t74)
- **Drum-group glue saturation, noise OFF** — his go-to on a drum group so kick hits push a little distortion through the hats ("your hi-hat ducks a bit — more life"); first move is switching Radiator's noise off ("I like having control over it — I don't like it coming from my plugin"), then pull input/mix down if it's too much — Lophiile, [Make Your Drums Slap](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v4ZhwlhF5yE)
