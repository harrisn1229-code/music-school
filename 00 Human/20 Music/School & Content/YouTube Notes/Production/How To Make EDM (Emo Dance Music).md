# How To Make EDM (Emo Dance Music)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=skrcNjB4NIc)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=skrcNjB4NIc)

## Synopsis

Underbelly builds a full "emo-EDM" hybrid track end to end: fake-guitar sound design (Real 8 + Eli derp's Fat Rack + a long tour of chorus/reverb/lo-fi plugins), a two-bass stack (a synth bass and a guitar-sourced bass, both run through Redux/Gate for a choppy character), a minimally-processed drum section built on already-processed Splice sounds plus New Fangled Saturate and Duck Buddy, a vocal chain built from pitched "moans" and an out-of-key vocal-chop pack corrected with Zynaptiq Pitch Map, and a genuinely notable mastering philosophy: because every individual track is already heavily saturated/compressed, the master needs almost nothing beyond a single hard clipper — or even just Utility gain pushed until it clips. One of the densest plugin-name-drop videos in the channel's catalog; most mentions are real, usable techniques rather than passing references.

## Techniques

- **Fake-guitar instrument (Real 8) + Fat Rack (Amount & Focus maxed) + Bridge Mute/pinch-harmonics modes, double-tracked for stereo** — build full guitar parts from a virtual-guitar instrument rather than recording; push Eli derp's Fat Rack hard (Amount and Focus both maxed) for the amp/distortion character, switch modes for Bridge Mute or pinch-harmonics variations, and double-track (two separate takes, not a duplicated copy) for real stereo width. → [[Width and Stereo]], [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **Acon Digital Multiply (free chorus) for width/roominess on a plucky part; automate a stock Reverb's Dry/Wet for rhythmic "swells"** — a free chorus for a light width/roominess pass on percussive/plucky guitar content, plus automating the stock Ableton Reverb's Dry/Wet knob directly (not a send fader) so the reverb swells in and out with the groove instead of sitting static. → [[Reverb]], [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **ValhallaShimmer's Bright Hall / Center mode for an ethereal guitar-arp wash** — a specific Shimmer setting (Bright Hall algorithm, Center width) turns a clean arpeggiated guitar into a lush, ethereal texture; layer BLEASS Granulizer's pitched-delay mode on top for even more depth. → [[ValhallaShimmer]]
- **Escalating crunch/distortion options: Pedal's Fuzz mode, Emissary amp sim, and Redux's "bits-to-zero + DC Shift" trick** — several distinct paths to guitar grit: Ableton's stock Pedal on the Fuzz setting with Gain cranked; a dedicated amp-sim plugin (Emissary); and, less obviously, dragging Redux's bit-depth all the way down (which reads as near-total mute) then flipping DC Shift on to reveal a hidden crunchy/gritty character rather than silence. → [[Redux]], [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **"Gabriella" chorus-stack rack (Eli derp) + BLEASS Dragonfly for extreme modulation textures** — a custom rack built from many stacked chorus effects, with Dry/Wet cranked and automated for a metallic feedback texture; separately, BLEASS Dragonfly's Dry/Wet cranked gives a fast, spinning, crunchy/choppy modulation effect on guitars. → [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **Two free lo-fi layers: Color's Age knob, and Sketch Cassette's Worn + Dropouts** — Color (free Max for Live) with Age maxed for an exaggerated degraded/lo-fi character; Sketch Cassette with Worn cranked plus the Dropouts section's Depth control for a randomized auto-panning lo-fi effect on the same guitar layer. → [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **Two-bass emo/EDM stack: a widened/gated synth bass + a guitar-sourced distorted bass, both cleaned with a post-Redux Gate** — bass #1: a synth-bass preset widened (Serum FX), shaped with Multiband (cut lows, compress highs), band-pass EQ, Redux toggled for a deliberate white-noise character, Gate to cut the noisy tail. Bass #2: the same fake-guitar instrument (Real 8) run through Redux + Emissary + Fat Rack for a heavily-compressed, crunchy guitar-as-bass tone, with the same Gate-the-tail move to clean up reverb left by the heavy compression. Group both and stack more processing on the group. → [[Bass Design]], [[Gate]], [[Redux]]
- **A sub layer can just be one oscillator's Sub shape — confirm it's earning its place with an on/off A/B** — rather than a dedicated sub patch, the sub here is simply Serum's built-in Sub oscillator shape on a second oscillator; A/B muted vs. active to confirm it's actually contributing ("a good sub is like a push-up bra — it enhances what's already there"). → [[Bass Design]], [[Serum 2]]
- **Pre-processed Splice drum sounds need almost no further processing — the sound designers already did it** — resist re-processing already-well-designed sample-pack drum hits; the only processing applied here is on the drum GROUP (New Fangled Saturate to shave peaks and even out hit-to-hit volume) rather than per-element chains. Confirms the [[Plugin Wishlist]] entries for New Fangled Saturate and Duck Buddy (group-wide sidechain pump) already documented from an earlier video.
- **Pan vocal "moans" hard L/R, tame with a free auto-tune plugin, then re-distort** — hard-pan pitched vocal ad-libs/moans left and right for width, run them through MAutoPitch (free) with Width increased to tame the raw pitch character, then reintroduce grit deliberately: Fat Rack to squash, Pedal's Fuzz for extra distortion, EQ to remove low end, and a second Fat Rack pass. → [[Pitch and Formant]], [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **Check a vocal-chop pack's own key, then fix the whole pack with Zynaptiq Pitch Map** — a free vocal-chop pack (Voice Box, "velocity chords" instrument) turned out to be in G while the song is in Bb minor; rather than avoiding the pack or manually re-chopping, Pitch Map (Auto-Tune-on-steroids, polyphonic) set to the song's actual key re-pitches the whole pack in one pass. Widen the result afterward with Ozone Imager (free, Stereo Eyes on, Width cranked). → [[Pitch and Formant]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Width and Stereo]]
- **Minimalist mastering: when every track is already saturated, the master needs almost nothing** — the entire mastering chain here is a single hard clipper (New Fangled Saturate); the video argues you could even skip that and just crank a Utility's Gain until it clips, since loudness/character were already built into every individual track during mixing rather than being a job saved for the master. → [[Clipping and Loudness]]
