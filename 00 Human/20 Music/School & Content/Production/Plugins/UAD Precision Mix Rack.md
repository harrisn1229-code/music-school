# UAD Precision Mix Rack

**Category:** Mixing Tools (Collection)
**Vendor:** Universal Audio — owned (confirmed 2026-07-17).

## What it is

UAD Precision Mix Rack Collection — UA's clean, surgical mixing bundle (Precision EQ, buss/multiband compression, de-essing and metering) for transparent mix-stage processing.

## What it's used for

Mix-bus and channel cleanup where you want precision over color — surgical EQ, glue compression and de-essing that stay out of the way.
