# Doubler4

![[Images/Doubler4.png]]

**Category:** Pitch/Vocal
**Vendor:** Waves

## What it is

An expanded version of Doubler2 offering up to 4 additional simulated voices for thicker doubling.

## What it's used for

Thicker artificial doubling/unison effects than Doubler2 provides.

## FX chain notes

Same placement as Doubler2 — late in the chain, blended in parallel.

## Used in my chains

The **4-voice widener** — reached for when you want obvious spread/thickness, not just a subtle double:

- **[[1 Electric Guitar DI Track|Electric Guitar DI Track]]** — *Jai Paul lo-fi-funk chain*: widener after [[Guitar Rig 7 FX|Guitar Rig]] + [[RC-20 Retro Color]] (4 voices hard-panned, Detune ~10 cents, Delay 15–30 ms, Dry/Wet ~30%). Alt to the [[Width Send]].
