# Beware of Darkness — George Harrison

> From All Things Must Pass (1970), produced by Phil Spector. Devotional warning against illusion, maya, and negative thought. Possibly Pete Drake on pedal steel. Slow, hymn-like progression.

## Chords
**Key:** E major (the vault's C♯ minor is its relative minor) · slow, hymn-like

**Verse:** B – B/A – G7 – G♯m – C♯m – E – A
→ **V – V/♭7 – ♭III7 – iii – vi – I – IV** (the **G7** is a chromatic borrowed chord — that haunting, gospel sideways turn)

**Bridge ("Watch out now…"):** E – D – A – F♯m – Bm (passing G7)
→ **I – ♭VII – IV – ii – v**

> Verified via Ultimate Guitar / Hooktheory.


## Production & Arrangement
All Things Must Pass (1970) — **Phil Spector's Wall of Sound**: layered acoustics, slide guitar, lush reverb, and a huge, cavernous production around Harrison's spiritual ballad. Grand and reverby.

## Steal this
- **Wall of Sound:** stack many acoustic/rhythm layers and drench in reverb for a huge, unified bed → [[Reverb Send Long]] · [[Width Send]].
- **Slide guitar** as the singing lead voice (Harrison's signature).
- **Dynamic lift** from intimate verse to grand chorus.
