# Three-Tempo-Zone String-Jumping Ladder

Four progressively harder string-jumping/wrist-independence exercises, built around the idea that picking motion has to adapt across three tempo zones (slow <~90 BPM, mid ~90–160 BPM, fast >~160 BPM) even though grip stays fixed.

## The four exercises

1. Classical-style pattern jumping between non-adjacent strings, always returning to one repeating "dominant string" phrase — wrist-unlocking foundation.
2. One measure of single-string tremolo picking alternated with one measure of a harder 2-string phrase, aiming to make both feel identical in hand motion.
3. Switch strings after almost every single stroke — the hardest exercise of the set; brute force fails, precision and relaxation are required.
4. A fast burst and a mid-tempo phrase chained with no rest between them — requires instant, automatic switching between the two motions.

Pure wrist/string-transition mechanics across tempo zones — no scale, mode or chord-tone knowledge involved, just picking-hand motor control.
