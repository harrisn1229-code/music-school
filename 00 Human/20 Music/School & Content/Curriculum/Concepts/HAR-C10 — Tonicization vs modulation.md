# HAR-C10 — Tonicization vs modulation

**Q.** What's the difference between tonicization and modulation, and what does a pivot chord do?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Tonicization** points at a chord briefly — a secondary dominant leaning on ii, then back. **No new key.** **Modulation** relocates the tonic and **confirms it with a cadence**, so the ear resets. It's a spectrum set by duration and whether you cadence. A **pivot chord is diatonic to both keys** — approach it in the old key, reinterpret it, cadence in the new one. Am is vi in C and ii in G.

## Why it matters

HAR-16 blank; HAR-13 got 'shared chord' and missed the reinterpretation and the confirming cadence — which are the two things that make it a modulation rather than just a chord both keys happen to own.

## Drill

Modulate a loop from C to G using Am as the pivot, confirmed with D7–G. Then do the same move but leave immediately — that's tonicization.
