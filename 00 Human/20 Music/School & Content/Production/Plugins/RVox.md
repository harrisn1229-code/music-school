# RVox

![[Images/RVox.png]]

**Category:** Compressor/Limiter
**Vendor:** Waves

## What it is

Waves' combined vocal rider/compressor/gate plugin designed for fast, simple vocal leveling.

## What it's used for

Quick, automatic vocal leveling and gating in a single plugin.

## FX chain notes

Used early in a vocal chain for overall leveling before tonal EQ and creative processing.

## Techniques

- **Named as the direct comparison point for why a chain runs separate dedicated plugins instead of RVox's all-in-one compressor+gate.** Illangelo (The Weeknd), introducing his own [[Pro-G]] gate stage: "I know a lot of people use RVox from Waves and you're able to quickly throw the compressor down and there's also another feature on RVox there, the Gate... I really love RVox and it's super good but I feel that I'm able to get more out of what I'm looking for by having it separated into all these separate plug-ins. So it's a little bit more work but the quality in the end, I like much more." A useful two-philosophy contrast on the same job: RVox for speed/simplicity (one plugin, compressor+gate together) vs. [[Pro-G]] + a dedicated compressor as separate stages for more control per stage. → [[Pro-G]], [[The Weeknd Vocal Chain]] — Illangelo (Mix with the Masters), [[Producing The Weeknd's vocals with Illangelo]]
- **Placed AFTER EQ and light compression, specifically to push the vocal out in front of the track — not as the first leveling stage.** In a fixed, always-the-same tracking chain, RVox lands third: Renaissance EQ (fixed high-end boost recipe) → Renaissance Compressor (light, peaks only) → **this plugin** — "I'll put the RVox on there to push it out a little bit because I want it to stand out in front of the track." Built live in the artist's headphones while they sing, so this push is shaping the take itself, not a mix-stage move. *(qualitative — no Comp/Gate knob values given)* → [[EQ]] (effect), [[Compression]] (effect), [[Latency-Free Tracking]], [[1 Main Vocal Track]] — Kuk Harrell, [[Radio-ready vocal effects with Kuk Harrell]]
