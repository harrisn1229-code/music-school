# I STARTED A RECORD LABEL!! (Friends & Family)

**Not a production breakdown** — this is Guy's stream announcing his **Friends & Family** label (an outgrowth of his intimate back-to-back club nights, built to put on younger up-and-coming DJs/artists). Most of the hour is label context, the first releases, and playing signed music. The one production-relevant stretch is a **guest artist walking through a track's bass and synths**, which is where the few notes below come from. Whisper transcript (no captions) — high confidence.

## What's in it
- **The label:** Friends & Family — springs from Guy's small-club b2b nights; a home for the younger artists he's been playing with, "like how I was starting out." First release out the next day.
- **Guest track breakdown (another producer's tune):**
  - **Bass layering** — a main [[Monark]] saw bass (a "u-he/Hive-style" patch) + a quiet sub layer you only miss when it's gone + a "far"/crisp top layer for definition. Classic layered-bass thinking → [[BASS Slot]] / [[SUB Slot]], [[3 Bass Bus|Bass Bus]].
  - **Sample-first workflow** — prefers finding a sample and making it unique (chop, bounce to audio) over preset-diving; a more preset-oriented approach on emotional/piano tunes. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]].
  - **Transient Master on drums/bass** — used for shaping attack/sustain; paired with light compression. → [[TRANSIENT Slot]], [[Compression]].
  - **"Endless Smile"-style filter+reverb build plugin** for one-knob tension risers into drops; a Serum instance "with tons of effects" rendered to audio for the main synth. → [[Delay]] / [[Reverb]].
  - DAW note: ended up on **Logic** because the collaborators' computers all had it; personally now leaning **Ableton** more.
