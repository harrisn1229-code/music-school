# UAD Marshall Plexi Classic

![[Images/UAD Marshall Plexi Classic.png]]

**Category:** Guitar Amp
**Vendor:** Universal Audio — owned (confirmed 2026-07-17).

## What it is

Emulation of the Marshall Super Lead 'Plexi' 1959 head — the archetypal British crunch guitar amp.

## What it's used for

Electric guitar crunch and lead tones; drive a clean DI/guitar track for classic-rock grit and sustain.
