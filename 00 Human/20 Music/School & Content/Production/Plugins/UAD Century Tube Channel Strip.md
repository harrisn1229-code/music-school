# UAD Century Tube Channel Strip

![[Images/UAD Century Tube Channel Strip.png]]

**Category:** Channel Strip (Preamp/EQ/Comp)
**Vendor:** Universal Audio — owned (confirmed 2026-07-17).

## What it is

UAD original all-tube channel strip — mic preamp, EQ, compressor and de-esser in one module, with switchable tube drive for vintage coloration on the way in.

## What it's used for

One-stop tracking front-end when you want tube weight, tone-shaping and level control in a single pass — vocals, bass DI, guitars.

## Techniques

- **Best-match owned equivalent for a "tube pre-amp preset" run alone (no other processing) on a Scarbee Rickenbacker bass, deliberately kept clean and simple.** On a from-scratch jazzy R&B sample: the bass is the one element in the whole build given a single-stage chain — just the tube pre-amp, no compressor or EQ — to keep it "super clean and simple" against the more heavily processed piano/guitar/brass around it. → [[Kontakt 7]], [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[Bass Design]] — loner, [how to make moody vintage r&b samples for partynextdoor (sounds like a real jazz sample)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wD4RzEKo7vU)
- **Preamp stage of a 6-step Dez Wright guitar-sample chain, opening the chain ahead of the amp.** Recreating *Numbers* (A Boogie): **preamp** → amp → cassette → flanger → spring reverb → EQ, with a perc loop + texture layered underneath the processed guitar for ambience. Guitar chords for the sample were found via a chord progression clicked in blind on a placeholder sound, then matched to a chord-finder website before recording the real guitar part. *(general starting point — no exact preamp gain given on screen)* → [[Morgan Amps Suite]] (amp), [[Cassette]], [[Phaser-Flanger]], [[Ghz DC19 3]], [[EQ Eight]], [[Dez Wright Guitar Sample Chain]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]] — loner, [dez wright's synth secrets will elevate your melodies!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_8vOz54DTiY)
- **Preamp stage in a bright, high-end contrast-guitar chain (preamp → amp → spring reverb → EQ).** Recreating Jake One's *Percocet and Stripper Joint* (Future) sample: a second, brighter guitar is layered under a dry low-octave guitar accent purely for high-end contrast, run through this fuller chain rather than the amp+EQ-only treatment given to the low guitar. *(general starting point — no exact preamp gain given on screen)* → [[Morgan Amps Suite]] (amp), [[Ghz DC19 3]] (spring reverb), [[EQ Eight]], [[Layering and Stacking]], [[Sound Selection Guide]] — loner, [the secret samplemaker behind Southside's best beats!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WLOHe9CJChY)
