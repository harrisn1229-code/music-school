# MEL-C06 — Building tension across a section

**Q.** Melodically, how do you build tension across a verse so the chorus lands?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Climb** — rise in register, **shorten the note values** so the line gets busier, and **repeat a fragment in ascending sequence** so it gathers. **Withhold the tonic** so nothing resolves until the chorus does it. **The counter-move also works:** strip everything back right before the chorus so it arrives against space.

## Why it matters

The counter-move is the one worth remembering for your genres — in loop-based production the drop *is* the arrival, so subtraction before it often beats building into it.

## Drill

Take a verse. Build one version that climbs into the chorus, one that strips back. Pick the better.
