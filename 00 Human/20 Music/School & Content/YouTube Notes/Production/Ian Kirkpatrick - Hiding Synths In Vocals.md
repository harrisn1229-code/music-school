# Ian Kirkpatrick — Hiding Synths In Vocals

![](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/9Eza4jngazI)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/9Eza4jngazI)

## Synopsis

30-second clip of Ian's current obsession: **hiding synths behind vocals** to make a single word stand out. He wanted one word to pop; his first instinct was a reverb throw, but instead he put a **synth playing the same note behind the word, mixed loud enough to feel but low enough that "you don't even know."** The signature move behind a lot of his pop candy.

## Techniques

- **Synth-behind-the-word instead of a reverb throw** — to make a lyric word stand out, layer a synth on that note underneath it; loud enough to register subconsciously, hidden enough that the listener can't name it. → [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]], [[Ian Kirkpatrick - Hiding Synths In Vocals (pt 2)]]
- **He deliberately rejected the reverb throw** — "that sounded cool, but what if I put a synth behind it" — the hidden synth reads as excitement without the wash of a throw pulling the word back in space. → [[Reverb]], [[Depth and Space]]
