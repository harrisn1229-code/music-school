# Drift — Analog Synth Bass

**Instrument:** [[Drift]] · **Slot:** [[BASS Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]]

## What it's for
Indie/synthwave analog-style synth bass — a Moog-ish saw/square with a filter-envelope pluck, not a sub-focused patch. Reach for it when the part wants a played, articulated bassline rather than a static 808.

## Settings
- Adapted from [[Drift#Patch Recipes|Drift — Bass / sub]]: Voice Mode Mono
- Osc 1: **Saw or Square** (the recipe's default is Sine/Triangle for a clean sub — swap the waveform here for the Moog-ish harmonic content this chain note wants); Osc 2 off or an octave down for weight
- ③ Filter: MS2 24 dB, moderate–high resonance
- Filter envelope: fast attack, short-medium decay, low sustain — the pluck shape
- ⑦ Detune: 0 — stays mono and clean despite the more aggressive waveform choice
- ⑩ Glide legato for slides
- FX: none pre-bus — keep it dry into the mix bus

## In context
- Same BASS-slot indie/synthwave lane as [[Model 84 — Juno Bass]] — pick Drift for a cleaner modern voice, Model 84 for the real Juno chorus/circuit character.
- Filter-envelope pluck is the defining move here — without it this collapses into a plain sub patch.
