## Queue

### Vocal-chain candidates
- **Mk.gee** — the current guitar/vocal zeitgeist; Dijon-adjacent blown-out intimacy, everything band-limited and bent (SOS/interviews exist on his chain)
- **Bon Iver** — the Messina rig (prismizer/harmonizer stacks, 22 A Million era) — buildable from [[Little AlterBoy]] + harmony layers; the vocoder-choir lane
- **Steve Lacy** — the direct-in charm school (iPhone-era to Gemini Rights polish) — natural extension of [[Bedroom Pop - Indie]]
- **Kendrick Lamar** — voice-as-characters: per-song formant/tone shifts, MixedByAli's dry upfront rap chain (pairs with the bass/808 research already in the vault)
- **SZA** — modern R&B stacked-harmony architecture; the melisma-comp workflow
- **Paul McCartney** — deserves his own beyond [[60s - Beatles]]: RAM-era home-recording vocal lanes (your core lane, and you already practice his catalog)
- **Phoebe Bridgers** — whisper-folk double-tracked lead (both-takes-equal, no "lead") + shimmer verb
- **Daft Punk** — vocoder/talkbox chain ([[Bitspeek]] + [[Echobode]] can fake it) — Discovery-era robot-soul

### Playbook candidates
- **Boom Bap / Dilla-deep** (Madlib, Alchemist) — sample-flip chains, SP-1200 crunch ([[Serato Sample]] + [[Decimort 2]] lane)
- **Shoegaze / Dream Pop** (MBV, Slowdive, Cocteau Twins) — the reverse-reverb + wall-of-detune guitar science; pairs with psych-pop
- **Ambient / Eno lane** — generative beds, the [[Granulator II]] + [[ValhallaShimmer]] toolkit
- **Funk / Prince** (1999–Sign o' the Times) — LinnDrum + minimal-band maximal-groove; the one-man-band discipline shared with Parker/McCartney
- **Indie Rock modern** (The Strokes, Arctic Monkeys AM-era) — the AM-radio vocal + room-glue drums

## How to promote one
`/research <artist> production techniques` → write the chain note in the family folder with the standard grammar (Step 1 capture matters most — half of every artist sound is the recording decision) → add reference tracks → **write the `## Arrangement` block** (what plays when, not just what it sounds like — see [[spec-175-arrangement-blocks]] and the [[Genres Index]] comparison table) → link it here and in [[FX Chain System]].
