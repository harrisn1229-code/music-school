# NOT-C04 — Octave signs: 8va and loco

**Q.** What does `8va` above a passage mean, and what cancels it?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Play an octave higher than written.** Cancelled by ***loco*** (or by the end of the dotted bracket). **`8vb`** below the staff means an octave lower.

## Why it matters

These exist to keep ledger lines readable — worth knowing, because ledger lines are commonly blamed for guitar reading being hard when they aren't the reason (see [[NOT-C07 — Why guitar reading is hard: position]]).
