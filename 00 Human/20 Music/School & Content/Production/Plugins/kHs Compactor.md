# kHs Compactor

![[Images/kHs Compactor.png]]

**Category:** Compressor/Limiter
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A fast, simple compressor snap-in from the Kilohearts ecosystem, designed for quick, musical compression.

## What it's used for

Fast compression duty inside a modular Snap Heap chain without loading a full standalone compressor.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain, typically after EQ.
