## What to watch for

- **Listen for the parent-scale trick.** Every mode here is the same seven notes of G major; what changes is which note the harmony makes home. That single idea is the whole of [[Diatonic Modes]].
- **The harmonization under each mode** — that is what the description says he layers in, and it is the part that separates hearing Dorian from merely playing it.
- **Which mode you can pick out blind.** Lydian and Mixolydian have the clearest single-note tells (♯4 and ♭7); Aeolian and Dorian differ by one note and are the genuine ear test. See [[Scale Degree Ear Training]].
