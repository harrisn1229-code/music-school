## Synopsis

Release-day track-by-track Q&A over the whole ENERGY album — anecdote-dense rather than screen-share technical, but several production reveals are keepers: Lavender's lead vocal chain (SM-58 → Distressor → distortion), the harmonica-mic hook vs clean-verse mic contrast, Watch Your Step's one-chord/busy-bass harmony, and the DJ-mentality arrangement rule. Whisper transcript, high confidence.

## Techniques

- **Lavender's lead vocal: SM-58 straight into a Distressor + "a bunch of distortion" — on the released album.** "It sounds banging — it depends how you use it." The $99 dynamic + hard commitment beats a precious chain when the song wants attitude. Owned lane: [[Shure SM57]] → [[UAD UA 1176LN]] slammed → [[Decapitator]]. → [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]] variations, [[Sound Selection Guide]] lo-fi mics
- **Different mics for different sections** — the hook sung into a **harmonica mic** for the crushed old-school lo-fi sound, verses on a clean upfront mic: section contrast built at the SOURCE, not with automation. → [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]], [[Gorillaz Vocal Chain]] lane
- **One chord + a busy bassline = implied harmony** — Watch Your Step "has only one chord — the bassline is so busy it implies the I-IV / I-V movement"; Howard wrote it on a bass guitar while waiting for the session. Third independent confirmation of bass-implies-harmony. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Vocoder + sidechain glue a too-weird part** — a swung section that felt wrong sat perfectly once the vocoder unified the timbre and the sidechain locked the rhythm. → [[Sidechaining]]
- **The DJ-mentality arrangement rule** — always leave a stretch of loopable drums (intro/outro) so a DJ can actually mix the song; "I'd hate to love a song I couldn't play." → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[House - EDM]]
- **Guest-vocal processing recipe (Q&A):** compression + mostly *dynamic* EQ (cut low mids, boost highs) + two delay types (1/4 + 8th triplets) + reverb — the same lean chain across the album's features. → [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]]
- **Fractal interlude = channeling J Dilla** — drum sounds and deliberately-imperfect mix manipulation channeling an era, not ripping a song. → [[Neo-Soul - Lo-fi]]
- **Patterning (iOS circular step sequencer) presets** seeded one album beat — grooves from outside the DAW read differently. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Clearance as quality control** — they refused a Latch sample request because the added notes didn't fit the key; owning your catalog means protecting its harmony too. *(anecdote)*
