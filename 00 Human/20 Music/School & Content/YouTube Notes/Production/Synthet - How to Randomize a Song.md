# Synthet - How to Randomize a Song

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jn-7yqOFQS8)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jn-7yqOFQS8)

## Synopsis

A small workflow story rather than a broad technique: stuck for a bassline idea, Synthet ran FL's randomizer tool (with a scale lock applied) over a sampled/portamento'd bass fragment until something usable turned up, then kept and refined the one usable idea out of an otherwise-unusable batch, layering the result with a plain sine sub-bass. The larger point is treating randomization as a fast way to get *some* idea flowing, not a finished-result generator.

## Techniques

- **Randomize with a scale lock, then cherry-pick the one good result** — running a randomizer plugin/tool over a MIDI part with the scale set to the song's key constrains the output to musically-usable notes; most randomized passes will still be unusable, but scanning for the one workable moment (the video's own result: only the first second of the randomized pass was kept) is fast compared to writing from scratch when stuck. → [[Randomized Rack Design]]
- **A small nudge beats a blank page** — described explicitly as a fast way to get ideas flowing when starting from zero inspiration, not a tool for finishing a part outright.
- **Layer the result with a plain sine sub-bass** — once a usable bass idea is extracted, duplicating it onto a simple sine-wave sub-bass voice underneath is a quick, convenient way to reinforce the low end. → [[Bassline Writing]]
