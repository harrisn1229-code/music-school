# Filter Delay

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Ableton

## What it is

Ableton's stock three-way filtered delay — an independent, separately-timed delay line for the left, right, and center channels, each with its own filter.

## What it's used for

Stereo-imaging-focused delay work where the left/right/center content each need genuinely different delay/filter treatment, rather than one delay time mirrored across the stereo field.

## FX chain notes

Insert on a source that needs asymmetric per-side delay character; automating each side independently (rather than leaving all three static) is where the device earns its keep over a simpler stereo delay.

## Techniques

- **Automate the three sides via a third-party LFO-cluster device instead of hand-mapping one LFO per side** — with independent delay times set for the left, right, and center channels, pair Filter Delay with **LFO Cluster** (a free Max for Live device by Kentaro bundling several independently rate/shape-set LFOs in one device — see [[Plugin Wishlist]]) to automate the per-side delay times all at once, rather than wiring up a separate [[LFO]] device for each side by hand. → [[LFO]], [[Plugin Wishlist]] — dnksaus, [Ableton Delay Sound Design](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DCIEayyv1bk)
