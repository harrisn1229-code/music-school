# BAS-C06 — Syncopate off the kick to make it funky

**Q.** What single move makes a bassline funky, and what's the connective device between phrases?

> [!question]- Answer
> Switch to a **16th-note grid** and deliberately move some bass notes **off** the kick's strong beats (1, 2, 3, 4) onto the weaker in-between spots — named as "the key to making a bassline funky fresh". Layer octave uppers and downers on top for bounce. Between phrases: a **chromatic walk-up or walk-down**, stepping through out-of-scale passing tones toward the next section's starting note — it works independently of whatever scale the line is in.

## Why it matters

Bass has three simultaneous jobs — rhythmic (locking with the kick), harmonic (playing the root) and melodic (carrying hooks and fills). Syncopation is where you deliberately loosen the first one without losing it.

## Drill

Take a line locked to the kick. Move a third of its notes onto 16th-note offbeats. Then connect two phrases with a chromatic walk-up.
