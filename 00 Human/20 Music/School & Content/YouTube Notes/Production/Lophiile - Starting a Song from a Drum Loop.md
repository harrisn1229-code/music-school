# Lophiile — Starting a Song from a Drum Loop

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DBK3n5pX35A)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DBK3n5pX35A)

## Synopsis

Live cookup from a drum loop into a bossa-flavored idea. The keeper technique is his reverse-chords workflow: make played chords feel like a sample (speed up + octave up + freeze + flatten + stretch back), then reverse **each chord separately** — not the whole clip — so chord tones never smear over the wrong chord, yielding lines "I would never play." Also honest live decision-making: auditioning bass sounds (synth → DI bass guitar), filtering the whole mix to think, and the "fix it in the mix" attitude while the idea is hot.

## Techniques

- **Make played chords "samply":** speed up + put up an octave → freeze with all FX on → flatten to audio → stretch back down. (Same family as his flagship resample trick; here judged per-sound — it squared off a guitar patch badly but worked on piano. Always audition.) → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Reverse each chord SEPARATELY.** "You can't just hit reverse on the whole thing — you've got to reverse each chord separately, or you'll have chord tones going over the wrong chord." Chop at the chord boundaries, reverse each slice in place: harmonic motion preserved, texture reversed. Result: "lines I would never play... a million times cooler." → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[TEXTURE Slot]]
- **Dry the source before reversing** — he pulled the reverb off (kept only a short one) before the reverse pass so the tails don't wash the chord changes; reverse-then-verb, not verb-then-reverse. → [[Reverb]], [[Depth and Space]]
- **[[Kickstart 2|Kickstart]] on the reversed bed** to pump it into the groove immediately.
- **Audition bass sounds in tiers:** tried a synth bass ("not sold"), a G-funk-style patch, then "one last thing — let's try it on a bass guitar," DI'd in and put "in the pump" (sidechain). When a part is right but the sound is wrong, walk the sound-source ladder: synth → different synth → real instrument. → [[Bassline Writing]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Filter everything to think** — throws a low-pass over the whole mix "just for a second so I can f*ing think," then builds the next section from that filtered state (which itself becomes a build). → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[Automation]]
- **Build with subtraction moments** — planned an "absolute silence... sun's coming up in the morning" moment as a section; silence as arrangement material. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **The "goat birds" atmosphere sample** — the medicine-bird/loon sample "I've heard in so many records" dropped in for instant nature air. → [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Fix it in the mix (while cooking).** "There's a whole bunch of sh* going on but I don't give a f* — we'll fix it in the mix." Don't stop the idea to clean up; capture takes ("no you didn't get that take" → keep rolling). → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Widener taste check** — stock move didn't do anything on the guitar ("I need a different widener, I don't want chorus though") — width tools are not interchangeable; audition several. → [[Width and Stereo]]
