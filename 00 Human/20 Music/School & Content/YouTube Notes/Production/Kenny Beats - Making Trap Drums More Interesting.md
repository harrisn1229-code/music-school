# Kenny Beats — Making Trap Drums More Interesting

![](https://youtu.be/fo-E8dY81Nw)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/fo-E8dY81Nw)

## Synopsis

Rapid-fire answers on dynamic trap drums: freakier/off-grid patterns, weirder percussion, multiple beat-sections (Sicko Mode), panning the auxiliary percussion, drum-drop arrangement, multiple *bass sections* per song — and the humbling artist-simplicity lesson (Lil Baby reacting to 5–7 sounds).

## Techniques

- **Swing it, break the grid** — freakier patterns, move hits off-grid, weirder perc sounds; "try stuff that's against the rules — sounds you'd assume won't work." → [[Hip Hop - Trap]], [[2 Drums Group|Drums Group]]
- **Multiple sections, not one loop** — Sicko Mode is "three different beats in one of the biggest songs of our generation"; give the drums sections that do different things. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Pan the auxiliary percussion** — keep trap kick/snare/808 center, but extra hi-hat rolls, open hats and percussion belong off-center; "a lot of people don't pan their drums — everything's right in the middle." → [[Width and Stereo]], [[2 Drums Hats Group|Drums Hats Group]]
- **Drum drops as arrangement** — drop the claps for a section, drop kick+808 for a section, drop ALL drums at the end of a bar; programming interest = scheduled absence. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[Automation]]
- **Multiple BASS sections in one song** — a "Mike Dean section" (big synth/Reese/live bass) vs an 808 section, or live bass playing between the 808 hits; the bass doesn't have to be one thing per song. → [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]], [[1 Electric Bass DI Track|Electric Bass DI Track]], [[Hip Hop - Trap]]
- **Artists are the simplicity referee** — Lil Baby reacted to just 5–7 sounds ("Smack the Baby"/"Toes" sessions stopped early); the extra perc layer matters less than you think when someone's waiting to perform. "Too busy is a relative term" — the performer is the best common denominator. → [[Hip Hop - Trap]], session craft
