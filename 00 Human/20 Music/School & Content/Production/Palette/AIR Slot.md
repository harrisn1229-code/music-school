# AIR Slot — 6 kHz+

Sheen, openness, "expensive." The slot listeners don't notice until it's missing.

## Swap list (interchangeable occupants)

- **Hats/shakers** — [[1 Drums Hat Track|Drums Hat Track]] chain; usually the main occupant
- **Shimmer reverb** — [[ValhallaShimmer]] on a send
- **Vocal air/breath** — [[Fresh Air]] or a high shelf on the vocal chain
- **Pad harmonics** — a bright bed's top end reaching up (bed does double duty)
- **Sparkle layers** — bells, tambourine, vinyl-crackle brightness ([[RC-20 Retro Color]])

## Mix rules

- Additive slot: multiple quiet occupants coexist better here than anywhere — but each needs its own rhythm or register.
- Brightness is relative: one bright element makes the mix bright; three make it harsh.
- [[Fresh Air]]/shelf boosts on *one* element per mix; the rest earn air by arrangement.
- Lo-fi genres deliberately **empty this slot** (LP the mix ~10 kHz) — absence of AIR is the aesthetic ([[Bedroom Pop - Indie]]).

## When it's the hook
Rarely alone — but a signature hat pattern or shimmer swell can be the section's identity (trap hat rolls are AIR-slot hooks). Push the pattern, keep everything else's top rolled.

## When it competes
- **vs Vocal sibilance** ([[LEAD Slot]]): de-ess the vocal; don't dull the hats.
- **vs itself** (hats vs shimmer vs sparkle): pick one dominant texture per section; LP the others ~10 kHz.
- Harshness anywhere up here: [[Soothe2]] sides-only on the bus tames wash without dulling the center.

## Drum lanes in this slot (from books)

- **Hat sizzle is a shelf, not a peak, and the "harsh" high cuts up here are mostly headroom hygiene.** Hats: high **shelf 7–10 kHz+** for sizzle. Snare crisp **5–7 kHz** and tom punch **5–10 kHz** reach in from [[LEAD Slot]]. The source's "remove harsh freq" high cuts (snare **15–20 kHz**, hats **18–20 kHz**) are worth treating as headroom moves only — genuine harshness is a 2–5 kHz (snare) / 5–12 kHz (hats) problem, better handled dynamically with [[Soothe2]] as the rules above already say. Vocal air still beats hat sparkle when they collide. → [[EQ]], [[De-essing]], [[1 Drums Hat Track]] — Whipped Cream Sounds, [[Drum Mixing EQ Guide - Enrichment|Drum Mixing EQ Guide]]
