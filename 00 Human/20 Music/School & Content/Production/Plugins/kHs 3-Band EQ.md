# kHs 3-Band EQ

![[Images/kHs 3-Band EQ.png]]

**Category:** EQ
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A simple, fast three-band EQ snap-in from Kilohearts' modular Snap Heap ecosystem.

## What it's used for

Quick, lightweight tonal shaping when a full parametric EQ isn't needed inside a Snap Heap chain.

## FX chain notes

Used as one snap-in among several stacked inside a Snap Heap effect rack.
