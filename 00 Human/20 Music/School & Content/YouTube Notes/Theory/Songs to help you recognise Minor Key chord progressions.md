# Songs to help you recognise Minor Key chord progressions

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XYv0L0AyOhI)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XYv0L0AyOhI)

## Synopsis

The announced minor-key companion to [[Songs to help you recognise chords in a progression]] — the same chord functions sound meaningfully different in a minor key than a major key, so a separate anchor-song set is given, minor-key this time. Also settles a notation convention: functions are named relative to the **major** scale even in a minor key (so the note a minor 3rd above tonic in a minor key is called "♭III," not "III"), specifically because it stays unambiguous when a piece blurs between major and minor.

## Techniques

- **Notation convention stated directly**: Roman numerals here are always relative to the major scale, even in minor keys — a chord the "minor-key-relative" system would just call "VI" is called "♭VI" in this system, because it stays unambiguous without needing to track whether the local key is major or minor.
- **i→V** — "Therefore I Am" (Billie Eilish), oscillates i↔V for its entire duration. "Exit Music (For a Film)" (Radiohead), i→V.
- **i→iv** — "Sing" (Ed Sheeran), i→iv.
- **i→IV (major, borrowed)** — not unusual even though iv (minor) is the diatonic default. "Mad World" — i→IV (major).
- **i→ii° (diminished, the diatonic default)** — "Because" (Beatles), i→ii° — described as having "quite a distinctive sound."
- **i→ii (minor, non-diatonic but common)** — "505" (Arctic Monkeys), oscillates i↔ii (minor, not diminished) for its entire duration.
- **i→♭VI** — "Something in the Way" (Nirvana), oscillates i↔♭VI throughout.
- **i→♭VII** — "The Sound of Silence" (Simon & Garfunkel), i→♭VII.
- **i→♭III** — "House of the Rising Sun," opening two chords.
- **i→♭II ("spooky")** — "It Was a Very Good Year" (Frank Sinatra), i→♭II.
- **Minor tonic → major tonic (the parallel-major pivot, "bridging the gap between the minor key and the major key")**: "Comfortably Numb" (Pink Floyd) — opens on B minor, then a slide-guitar note change turns it into B major (the 3rd raised a half step).
