# Massive — Growl Lead

**Instrument:** [[Massive]] · **Slot:** [[LEAD Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Synth Lead Track|Synth Lead Track]]

## What it's for
The growly/aggressive 2010s-EDM lead character — a "quack"-attack digital lead, thinner and more upfront than a pad, built for a single melodic line that needs bite.

## Settings
- From [[Massive#Patch Recipes|Massive — Lead/pluck]]: ① Osc 1 bright digital-style wavetable
- ③ Modulation Oscillator → Phase (FM) on Osc 1, amount driven by a fast envelope for the "quack" attack transient
- ⑥ Filter: Scream or standard LP for pluck character, ⑧ envelope decay ~150–250 ms, sustain near zero
- ⑨ Play Mode: One Shot — consistent pluck length regardless of how staccato it's played
- Unison/detune minimal — thin and upfront, not a pad
- Mono + legato + glide 40–80 ms for expressive single-line playing (Step 1a universal lead rule); kill content below ~100 Hz

## In context
- Pairs well with the [[Frequency Shifter]] → [[Modulation]] move noted on [[1 Synth Lead Track|Synth Lead Track]] — the growl gives the shifter something with real harmonic content to work.
- Keep release tight so it doesn't smear against the next hit; space comes from the chain's delay/reverb sends, not the patch.
