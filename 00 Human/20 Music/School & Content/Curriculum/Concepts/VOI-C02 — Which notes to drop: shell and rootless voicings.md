# VOI-C02 — Which notes to drop: shell and rootless voicings

**Q.** Six strings, two hands, four notes wanted. What is the priority order for which notes stay, and what are a shell voicing and a rootless voicing?

> [!question]- Answer
> Keep, in this order: **the 3rd and the 7th** (the guide tones — they define quality and function), then **the extension you actually want**, then **the root** (the bass has it), then **the 5th** — which is the first thing to go, unless it is altered. **Shell** = root + 3rd + 7th. **Rootless** = 3rd + 7th + extensions.

## Why it matters

The interesting decision on guitar is not which notes to play but which to leave out, and this order answers it every time without recalculating. It is also why the named families exist: **drop 2** takes a close voicing and moves the 2nd-highest note down an octave, **drop 3** the 3rd-highest — both are ways of making four notes reachable and spaced rather than clustered.

## Drill

Comp a tune you know with shell voicings only, then again with guide tones alone — 3rds and 7ths, no root, no extension. Notice how little you actually needed.
