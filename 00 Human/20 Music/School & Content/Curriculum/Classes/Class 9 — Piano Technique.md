# Class 9 — Piano Technique

Hanon's *The Virtuoso Pianist* in order, one course per stretch of the book and **one lesson per exercise** — 60 in all — building the hand facility to voice chords and play parts. Worked as a daily block: the facility decays if you stop.

## What it assumes

- Nothing. Hanon starts from zero.

## What closes it

- Finishing [[Course 24 — Hanon VI]]. There is no written exam — the check is a performance rep, logged on the course note — but the class does now have a finish line, which it did not when it was standing.
