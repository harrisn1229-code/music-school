# TL EasyComp

![[Images/TL EasyComp.png]]

**Category:** Compressor/Limiter
**Vendor:** Tonelib

## What it is

Tonelib's simplified, easy-to-use compressor plugin aimed at fast, musical compression decisions.

## What it's used for

Quick, straightforward compression on tracks and busses without deep parameter tweaking.

## FX chain notes

A general-purpose compression stage, after EQ, before saturation/limiting.
