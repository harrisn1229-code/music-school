## General Theory
- [The Best Theory Explanation of Tertiary Chords](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JL7lnX3CmDM) — Jared Borowski (Sound Guitar Lessons With Jared); derives *why* each diatonic seventh-chord quality (maj7/dominant7/m7/half-dim) lands where it does by checking which stacked thirds straddle the major scale's half-steps, plus a dominant-7/half-diminished-as-key-fingerprint song-analysis tip — see also [[The Best Theory Explanation of Tertiary Chords]]
- [30+ Chords in C major? 🤔 - Boost your chord progressions and jazz harmony](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sn9ZLRbRlgo) — Jens Larsen; systematic root-by-root survey of all 12 chromatic roots × 5 chord qualities against the key of C major (secondary dominants, borrowed/parallel-minor chords, the ♯4-diminished family, the Neapolitan subdominant, tritone subs), with song citations for nearly every functional chord — see also [[30+ Chords in C major? - Boost your chord progressions and jazz harmony]]
- [How To Practice And Use Music Theory in Jazz](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j37vDF6FHjg) — Jens Larsen; five escalating practice drills (diatonic chords of a scale incl. harmonic minor, reverse chord-to-scale, secondary cadence to every degree, transpose by function not interval, transpose the melody too) plus a worked ♯4-diminished reharmonization of "All the Things You Are" — see also [[How To Practice And Use Music Theory in Jazz]]
- [Secondary Dominants - What You Want To Know](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8dp1upexGCQ) — Jens Larsen; finding secondary dominants in "There'll Never Be Another You," the major/harmonic-minor/Lydian-dominant scale-choice rule, comping/soloing insertion patterns — see also [[Secondary Dominants - What You Want To Know]]
- [Memorizing Chord Progressions and Jazz Standards - Jazz Theory and Analysis put to use!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZJf8f_pZxKg) — Jens Larsen; a chunking-by-form-and-cadence memorization method (the phone-number analogy), fully worked on "There'll Never Be Another You" (ABAC, E♭ major) — see also [[Memorizing Chord Progressions and Jazz Standards - Jazz Theory and Analysis put to use!]]
- [Music Theory Overview](https://youtube.com/watch?v=DvA_wDOVD10&si=vFQzi3BS1lqcmwXF)
