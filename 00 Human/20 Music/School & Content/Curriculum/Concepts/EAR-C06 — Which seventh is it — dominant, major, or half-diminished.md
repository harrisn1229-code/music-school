# EAR-C06 — Which seventh is it — dominant, major, or half-diminished

**Q.** You heard a dominant 7th and answered "major 7", then heard a half-diminished 7th and answered "dominant 7". What is the actual gap, and how do the three qualities differ?

> [!question]- Answer
> The gap is that you're hearing **that there's a seventh** but not which one — the two answers were swapped relative to what played, which is the signature of a size-not-quality read. **Major 7** — the 7 feeling on top: melancholy/bittersweet, sharpened by the half-step rub against the root. **Dominant 7** — ♭7 on top: light, powerful, playful, wants to move. **Half-diminished** — a diminished triad carrying that same ♭7; unstable underneath *and* unresolved on top.

## Why it matters

Every seventh-chord question is one distinction, asked three ways. Train each quality as its own whole flavour rather than assembling it from a triad plus an interval.

## Drill

Play Cmaj7, C7 and Cm7♭5 back to back. Name the feeling of the top note in each. Then shuffle and identify blind, ten reps.
