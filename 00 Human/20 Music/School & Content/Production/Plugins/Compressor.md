# Compressor

![[Images/Compressor.jpg]]

**Category:** Compressor/Limiter
**Vendor:** Ableton

## What it is

Ableton's standard compressor with a full envelope section, sidechain filtering, and adjustable knee — the everyday dynamics tool on Ableton tracks.

## What it's used for

Level control and glue on individual tracks, sidechain ducking (pump effects), and general dynamics tightening across the mix.

## FX chain notes

Typically follows an EQ cut and precedes a limiter or saturation stage; the sidechain input is the standard tool for kick-driven pumping effects.

## Techniques

- **"Rhythm guitar" preset used purely to tighten a part, dialed by ear via the input knob rather than a stated ratio/threshold — "takes some of the peaks out."** Stage 6 of a 9-device 6/8 R&B guitar chain (…→ [[EQ Eight]] → [[TAL-Chorus-LX]] "Juno chorus" → **this device** → [[Kramer Tape]] "Reels" → [[Kickstart 2]] pulled back → reverb, heavy/final) — a pure peak-taming stage, no stated GR. *(general starting point — no exact ratio/threshold given)* Full chain: [[Smooth R&B 6-8 Chain]]. → [[Kontakt 7]], [[Compression Staging]] — loner, [making a smooth/sexy rnb beat in 6/8 time signature](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mMZaktspUAA)
- **First stage on a piano sample, purely to "tighten the sound up and make it super together" before any tonal shaping.** On a 2000s R&B piano bed (Kontakt's "Gentleman" patch, A-minor chords), Compressor is the very first processing move — ahead of a small room plate reverb and a make-dynamic [[Pro-Q 4|Pro-Q 3]] carve — used purely for glue/tightness rather than a stated ratio/attack. *(general starting point — no exact settings given)* → [[Little Plate]], [[Pro-Q 4]], [[Compression]], [[2000s R&B Piano Sample Chain]] — loner, [how to make a 2000's r&b sample & flip it into a beat for tory lanez/bryson tiller](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sPqBmZLaDM4)
- **Group everything except drums, then sidechain that whole group off the kick** — select every non-drum track, Cmd/Ctrl+G to fold them into one Group, add a Compressor directly on the Group track, turn on External sidechain input, and key it from the kick so the *entire* rest of the arrangement (bass, synths, vocal chops, etc.) ducks together on every kick hit instead of sidechaining each element individually. → [[Sidechaining]] — You Suck at Producing, [The Most Slept On Ableton Synth for UK Dubstep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLsEQAxir1o)
