# Kenny Beats — Bass Mix & Effects Chain

![](https://youtu.be/ivvV0iiiCxQ)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/ivvV0iiiCxQ)

## Synopsis

His current live-bass chain, answering "how does your live bass have so much sub": Pro-Q 3 from a dynamic factory preset → Pro-C → **R-Bass adding ~69 Hz sub a 1969 Fender bass can't physically produce** → mid/side EQ with the low end forced dead mono. Four devices, and the philosophy that the EQ matters more than which compressor. Direct blueprint for the [[1 Electric Bass DI Track|Electric Bass DI Track]] vintage lane (the Hofner has the same no-sub problem as his '69 Fender).

## Techniques

- **The chain: EQ → compression → sub addition → mid/side mono** — "the idea is EQ, compression, a little bit of that sub addition, and then some mid-side." Four jobs, in that order. → [[1 Electric Bass DI Track|Electric Bass DI Track]]
- **Start EQ from the DYNAMIC factory presets** — Pro-Q's "Bass Guitar Brighten" preset (then edit: pull highs back down, tame peaks); the dynamic presets "evolve with the sound as it moves." He does the same on drums — there are presets per drum mic (snare top/bottom, overhead, kick, bus). A dynamic starting point beats a static blank slate. → [[Pro-Q 4]]
- **Compressor brand doesn't matter — the EQ does** — his is [[Pro-C 2]] (the older FF Pro-C in the video) ("Attack Enhancer DI" lineage preset), but "you just need some kind of compression; the EQ part is probably even more important." Don't buy a plugin to copy a chain. → [[Compression Staging]]
- **R-Bass ~69 Hz, low amount ("Live Bass Love" preset, edited)** — generates the sub fundamental a vintage passive bass can't ring off; keeps the woody finger-noise character while the bottom punches. Owned lane: [[Saturn 2]] low-band harmonics or [[Bloom Bass Impulse]]; R-Bass itself → [[Plugin Wishlist]] (also endorsed in [[Disclosure - SSL SIX Mixing Al Green Stems]]). → [[1 Electric Bass DI Track|Electric Bass DI Track]], [[SUB Slot]]
- **Mid/side: stereo width to ZERO on the bass** — dead-mono bass for anything 60s/70s-feeling ("real old and kind of muddy" on purpose); makes panning + low-end decisions trivial. Stylistic, but his default for vintage lanes. Owned: [[Utility]] width 0% / Pro-Q 4 low band to mid-only. → [[Width and Stereo]], [[1 Electric Bass DI Track|Electric Bass DI Track]]
