# FabFilter Micro

![[Images/FabFilter Micro.png]]

**Category:** EQ
**Vendor:** FabFilter

## What it is

A free single-band parametric EQ/filter from FabFilter, offered as a lightweight promotional plugin with a simplified interface.

## What it's used for

Quick single-node tonal correction when a full multi-band EQ (Pro-Q) is overkill.

## FX chain notes

A simple corrective EQ stage early in a channel's chain.
