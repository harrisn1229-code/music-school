## What to watch for

- **Which shape he picks for each degree** — the diatonic set of G is fixed (G, Am, Bm, C, D, Em, F♯dim), but the voicing chosen for each is a judgement call. Compare against [[Chord Voicings]].
- **The seventh-chord pass.** Gmaj7, Am7, Bm7, Cmaj7, D7, Em7, F♯m7♭5 — watch where the shape barely moves from the triad and where it changes hand position entirely. See [[Seventh Chords]].
- **The half-diminished on vii°.** F♯m7♭5 is the one degree beginners skip; note whether he plays it as a full shape or a rootless grip.
