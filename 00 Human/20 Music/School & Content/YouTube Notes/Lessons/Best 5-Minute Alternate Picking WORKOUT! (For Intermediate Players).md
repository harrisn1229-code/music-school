## Synopsis

A 5-minute daily workout aimed specifically at picking **endurance** — the follow-on skill after short-burst sync training, needed for sustaining fast alternate picking over a whole lick, riff, or song rather than a few notes.

## Techniques

- **Endurance is a distinct training goal from initial hand-sync bursts.** Short, fast picking bursts (BERNTH's usual entry point for beginners) build hand synchronization but don't by themselves build the stamina to sustain fast picking over longer musical passages — a separate daily workout is needed once sync is solid.
- **Warning against excess picking attack for endurance work.** Aggressive, hard picking attack learned during short-burst training doesn't hold up over a sustained passage — the hand/wrist/arm tire and the motion becomes unsustainable at gig or recording length. Endurance work specifically should use a lighter, more relaxed attack than burst work.
