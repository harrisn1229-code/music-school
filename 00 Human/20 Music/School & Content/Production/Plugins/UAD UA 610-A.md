# UAD UA 610-A

![[Images/UAD UA 610-A.png]]

**Category:** Preamp & EQ (Unison)
**Vendor:** Universal Audio — owned (confirmed 2026-07-17).

## What it is

Unison emulation of the UA 610 tube console preamp/EQ (A-series voicing) — rounder, tubey iron; sibling of the stock-bundle [[UAD UA 610-B]] and companion to [[UAD Neve 1073]].

## What it's used for

Warm tube weight on vocals, guitars and bass DI; drive the input for thick harmonic push, keep low for clean tube body.
