### Delta — the only two things new in this video

- **"Ain't No Sunshine" named as the concrete song example of the common wrong turnaround.** Seriff states guitarists often assume the minor-blues turnaround is 5-4-1 (Em7-Dm7-Am7) — "you hear that in Ain't No Sunshine" — but it's rare in a classic blues form; the actual standard is ♭6-5-1. The sibling video and [[L-Shape Root Map for Blues Chords]] already document the 5-4-1 misconception itself, just without a named song example. *(~9:20–9:40)*
- **"James Brown chord" given as a nickname for the F9 turnaround voicing**, alongside the already-documented "Hendrix chord" nickname for E7♯9: "many times people will play that James Brown chord, which is the F9, to an E7... the Hendrix chord." *(~8:40–9:00)*

- **No plugin, effect, mixing, or production content** — a pure guitar instrument/theory lesson; Steps 4/6/7 of the enrichment workflow have nothing to do here.
