# Little MicroShift

![[Images/Little MicroShift.png]]

**Category:** Pan/Imaging
**Vendor:** Soundtoys

## What it is

A simplified version of MicroShift focused on quick stereo-widening pitch-shift doubling.

## What it's used for

Fast, simple stereo widening via subtle pitch-shifted doubling.

## FX chain notes

Used late in a chain for stereo width, after EQ/compression.

## Used in my chains

The **one-knob widener** — the simplified [[MicroShift]] snap for quick micro-pitch width:

- **[[1 Electric Piano Track|Electric Piano Track]]** — part of Pensado's "silky bed": micro pitch-shift + [[Saturn 2]] multiband saturation + Dimension-D chorus; judge by *feel*, not solo sound.
- **[[2 Ensemble Stack Group|Ensemble Stack Group]]** — kept **off/low** for the "Thick & Centered" (Bon Iver) stack where the weight comes from the octave-down anchor, not width.
