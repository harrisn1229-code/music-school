## Production & Arrangement
Erik Satie's proto-ambient piano — **sparse, modal, and harmonically unresolved**. Satie stripped Romantic drama away: slow tempo, lots of space, gentle dissonances that hang without resolving (he pioneered the idea of music as atmosphere — 'furniture music,' a century before ambient). The **Gnossiennes** famously have **no bar lines or time signatures** and quirky written directions; the **Gymnopédies** are gentle 3/4 melancholies over a rocking left-hand ostinato.

- **No. 3** is the most subdued — darker, resigned; the same slow 3/4 melancholy.

## Steal this
- **Space as an instrument:** slow the tempo, leave silence, let single notes ring — atmosphere over motion (the [[🎵 FRANK OCEAN - Ivy|drumless]] idea at its origin).
- **Unresolved/suspended harmony:** hang on [[Suspended Chords|sus]] / [[Seventh Chords|maj7]] / modal color that never fully cadences → [[Scale Modes Reference]].
- **Left-hand ostinato + floating melody:** a simple rocking accompaniment under a sparse tune ([[Keyscape]] felt piano + long [[Reverb Send Long]]).
- **Interpolate/sample it:** these are public-domain moods — loop a phrase as a lo-fi/hip-hop bed.
