# MIDS Slot (COMP) — 800 Hz–2.5 kHz

Rhythmic harmony, comping, riffs — the mix's density and drive. Articulation lives here.

## Swap list (interchangeable occupants)

- **Comping keys** — [[Keyscape]] Wurli 200A pop bark, tight Rhodes comps
- **Clavinet** — [[Keyscape]] Clavinet C + auto-wah ([[Envelope Follower]]) for funk
- **Muted/rhythmic guitar riffs** — the funk/indie engine
- **Plucks** — [[Serum 2#Patch Recipes|Serum 2 pluck recipe]]
- **Stabs** — [[Omnisphere#Patch Recipes|Omnisphere poly stab recipe]] · [[Model 84 Polyphonic Synthesizer]] brass stabs
- **Vocal chops** — chopped hooks functioning as an instrument

## Mix rules

- Pan off-center — this slot loves 30–60% L/R; the center belongs to vocal + bass + kick.
- Short > long: this slot is rhythmic; long tails push it into BED territory.
- Saturation reads as energy here more than anywhere; light drive > level.
- Standard chains: [[FX Chain System]] keys/guitar chains.

## When it's the hook
Riff-driven songs (funk clav line, the indie guitar riff): push it toward center, +presence 2–4 kHz, and dip the BED's top so the riff articulates. Vocal-less sections hand this slot the wheel by default.

## When it competes
- **vs Vocal presence** ([[LEAD Slot]] 1–2.5 kHz overlap): the vocal wins — dynamic dip keyed by vocal, or arrange the comp *between* vocal phrases (the best fix is a writing fix).
- **vs BED** ([[BED Slot]]): LP the bed ~2 kHz, keep this slot HPF'd ~300 Hz so they interlock instead of overlap.
- **vs Snare** ([[TRANSIENT Slot]]): transient collision — nudge the comp's timing or shorten its attack hits.

## Drum lanes in this slot (from books)

- **Kick snap begins here.** Kick boost **2–4 kHz** for beater snap runs from the top of this slot into [[LEAD Slot]] — worth knowing because it collides directly with comping keys/guitar riffs living in this slot; if the kick's snap and the comp fight, move the comp rather than the kick (the kick's snap is what makes it read on small speakers). Hats also get a **2–4 kHz cut if clanky** ([[1 Drums Hat Track]]) — the same band, opposite direction. → [[EQ]], [[Kick Design]], [[Masking and Carving]] — Whipped Cream Sounds, [[Drum Mixing EQ Guide - Enrichment|Drum Mixing EQ Guide]]

## Bass tone lanes in this slot (from videos)

- **Plucking-hand position as a zero-gear bass/mids control — the bridge end of the spread.** Moving the plucking hand toward the bridge bumps this slot up and pulls [[BASS Slot]] down (visible directly on a spectrum analyzer); bridge position specifically reads clear/defined/punchy/bright (also thin/quacky) — good for a part that needs to stand out, e.g. a disco bass brief ("bass poppin', sexy octaves"). No EQ, no gear — full three-position framework: [[Plucking Position — Fingerboard, Neck, and Bridge Tone Shaping]]. → BassBuzz, [[Better Bass Tone with “The Producer Switch” (costs $0)]]
- **"Bitey the dwarf" — ~1.5 kHz harsh edge.** Cut it (a high-mids knob near 1.2–1.5 kHz, or the passive tone knob rolled ~20% as a fallback) when an overly aggressive bass tone bites through a mellow song — but judge in context, not soloed, since bite that reads bad alone can sit fine in the full mix. Boost it (plus fresh strings and clacky plucking technique — EQ alone isn't enough) to chase Geddy Lee's cutting bite on Rush's "Closer to the Heart." Full breakdown: [[Bass EQ Frequency Zones — The Five Dwarves]]. → [[EQ]], [[BED Slot]] — BassBuzz, [[How to Get the PERFECT Bass Tone]]
