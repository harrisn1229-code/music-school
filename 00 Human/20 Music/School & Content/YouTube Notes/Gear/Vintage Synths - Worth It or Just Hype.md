## What's taught

- **A real technical claim worth remembering in any "does the original unit have magic the clone lacks" debate**: per Rob Rosen, an exact circuit clone of a vintage module — same component types, same values, same tolerances (e.g. a faithfully copied Moog module) — would sound "effectively the same" as the original. The vintage-unit mystique isn't about an unreproducible circuit; it's about correct components and build quality, which a careful clone/reissue can match.
- **Repairability differs by era, independent of sound**: older gear uses large, hand-solderable through-hole components that are straightforward to service; modern surface-mount gear packs tiny components that are much harder to repair by hand, compounded by RoHS-mandated lead-free solder (required to keep lead out of landfills) which behaves differently from traditional leaded solder in repair work.
