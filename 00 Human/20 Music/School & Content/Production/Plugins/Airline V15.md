# Airline V15

![[Images/Airline V15.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Kazrog

## What it is

Kazrog's guitar amp simulator modeling a Vox AC15-style combo amplifier and cabinet.

## What it's used for

Classic British chime-style guitar amp tone for clean and mildly overdriven guitar parts.

## FX chain notes

Used as the amp-sim stage on a DI guitar track, typically before cab-specific EQ and compression.
