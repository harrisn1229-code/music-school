## Synopsis

A timed, one-take constraint exercise: build a full beat (kick, snare, hats, bass) out of a single source sound, using only pitch-shifting, length changes, and Drum Rack pad duplication — no additional samples. Closes with a quick Beat Repeat glitch pass and a Re-Pitch-mode Simple Delay for texture. Loose and improvised, but the core constraint (one source, many roles, via pitch/length alone) is a genuinely useful practice exercise for getting more mileage out of a single well-chosen sound.

## Techniques

- **Build a whole kit — kick, snare, hats, bass — from ONE source sound via pitch and length alone** — duplicate a single sound across several Drum Rack pads and differentiate each purely through pitch-shift and length: pitched way down and shortened for a kick, just the tail-end slice for a snare, pitched way up (with rolls) for hi-hats, and pitched/loop-shortened into a playable register for a bass part. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Kick Design]], [[Snare Design]], [[Bass Design]]
