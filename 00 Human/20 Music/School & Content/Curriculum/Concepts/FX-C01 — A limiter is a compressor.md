# FX-C01 — A limiter is a compressor

**Q.** What's the practical difference between a limiter and a compressor?

> [!question]- Answer
> **A limiter is a compressor with a very high ratio, acting as a ceiling.** Same device, extreme setting — not a different category of tool.

## Why it matters

Reframing it this way makes the master-chain conversation obvious, and it connects directly to [[MIX-C01 — No limiter on the 2-bus]] — a limiter isn't a special mastering object, it's a compressor squashing so hard there's nothing left for the engineer.

## Drill

Take a compressor, set the ratio to 20:1 with a fast attack, and compare it to a limiter on the same source.
