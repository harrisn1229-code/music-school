# ARR-C09 — Strip the layers right before the payoff

**Q.** You've layered elements in across a build. What happens immediately before the drop?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Cut all of them** — so only the most essential element carries through into the structural boundary. The sudden reduction reads as dramatic tension release rather than a mistake, and it reinforces whatever hits at the payoff. Related devices on the same principle: a big reverb wash sells an emptied section as intentional, and scheduled silence is legitimate material.

## Why it matters

It's the counterintuitive half of building — the last gesture before maximum is minimum. Subtraction reads as suspense because a listener can't track many simultaneous parts, so less makes what remains read as more.

## Drill

On a build you've written, mute everything but one element for the last two bars before the drop. Compare against the stacked version.
