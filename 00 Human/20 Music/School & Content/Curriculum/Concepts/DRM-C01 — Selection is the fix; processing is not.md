# DRM-C01 — Selection is the fix; processing is not

**Q.** A kick doesn't hit right. You've added a compressor and two EQs and it's still wrong. What's the actual move?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Swap the sample.** Pull it out, audition a different one, and drop the processing back to near-nothing. A chain fixes a close-but-not-quite sample; it does not rescue a wrong one. Same logic downstream: if high-passing a kick to resolve a bass clash guts its punch, don't keep EQing it thinner — find a kick with more inherent high-end character and cut *that* one instead.

## Why it matters

It's the single most expensive habit in drum production — chasing upstream problems with downstream tools. Selection is the recording step for programmed drums.

## Drill

Take a beat where the kick feels weak. Bypass every device on it and audition six replacements before touching a single parameter.
