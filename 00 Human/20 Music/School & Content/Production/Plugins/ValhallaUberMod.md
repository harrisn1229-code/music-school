# ValhallaUberMod

![[Images/ValhallaUberMod.jpg]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Valhalla DSP

## What it is

Valhalla's all-in-one modulation plugin covering chorus, flanger, phaser, vibrato, and rotary-speaker-style effects.

## What it's used for

Broad modulation-effect duty across chorus, flange, phase, and rotary-speaker character in one plugin.

## FX chain notes

Used as an insert or send effect, post-EQ, for modulation and width.
