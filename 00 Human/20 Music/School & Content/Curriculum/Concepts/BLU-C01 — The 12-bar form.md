# BLU-C01 — The 12-bar form

**Q.** Write out a standard 12-bar blues in A. What are the three chords?

> [!question]- Answer
> **A7, D7, E7** — I7, IV7, V7. `A A A A / D D A A / E D A E` — three four-bar phrases, with bar 12 as the turnaround back to the top.

## Why it matters

This is the foundation the whole strand sits on, and it's transposable — the same shape in any key, which is why blues is the standard first improvising form.

## Drill

Write it out in A, E and G from memory. Then play each.
