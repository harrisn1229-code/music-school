## Techniques — the big lessons

- **Additive EQ instead of subtractive (the anti-mud revelation)** — surgical cuts leave "all the mush around" the frequency you wanted; instead **turn the whole track down and boost only the prominent frequencies you actually need** (e.g. −8 dB on the whole track, +17 dB on the dominant band). "EQ *is* a volume knob" — a huge fix for muddiness. → [[EQ]], [[Masking and Carving]], [[Pro-Q 4]]
- **Peak-reduction automation as dynamic control** — automate the **compressor's peak reduction** (not just fader volume): when the reduction backs off, the vocal is compressed *less*, so a "just you and her, naked" moment pops out of the lush treatment. Combine with lowering the group volume; also **lower the volume of loud words going into the comp** so they don't over-trigger it. → [[Compression]], [[Automation]], [[Depth and Space]]
- **Post-de-ess air boost = "sibilance back without the sibilance"** — de-ess heavily, then **boost the highs back** (gem Dopamine air / a 16k Pultec lift) so the vocal is loud, bright, and not harsh. Placement matters — after the de-esser. → [[De-essing]], [[EQ]], [[AIR Slot]]

## Techniques — the full lead vocal chain (Charli "Move Me")

Tracked in-session on the SM7. Order: **Squasher** ("grown man's OTT" — barely doing anything) → **mostly-dry SSL bus compressor** (dry/wet parallel to avoid phase issues) → **Soothe 2** (taming ~1k–8k "thrash"; he's learning **soft-algo-cranked vs hard-algo-subtle** react differently) → **FabFilter Pro-C2** (the reduction-automation comp) → **gem Dopamine** (air back post-de-ess) → **Pultec Pro** (cut ~30 Hz, boost **16k**). → [[Charli XCX Vocal Chain]], [[Soothe2]], [[UAD Pultec EQP-1A]], [[De-essing]], [[Dua Lipa Vocal Chain]]

## Techniques — production & sound design

- **Foundation = a DecapBeats Splice loop; cut every hat from it** — he loved DecapBeats' hat placement, so he chopped just the hats out and kept them over new drums/kick. Write to a loop, then re-record/replace the sounds. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[1 Drums Hat Track]]
- **Reaktor "The Mouth" (effect mode) for vocal-chop textures** — route MIDI to The Mouth on an audio track; each key triggers different loop types/high-passes; **record the output of random key-mashing**, then chop little loops out of it. → [[Modulation]], [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Crystallizer on drums for room; mid-side EQ for stereo control** — Crystallizer adds a short delay/room; a mid-side EQ turns down the sides and can **mono everything below a frequency** (great for bassy synths). → [[Crystallizer]], [[EQ]], [[Width and Stereo]], [[Stereo Field]]
- **Reversed snare-echo into the next part** — echo a snare reversed to inject rhythm into an open space. → [[Delay]], [[Reverb]], [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]]
- **Identity synths:** verse arpeggio = **Razor** (NI); the verse's defining synth = **Concerto** ("Sector 7" patch, via Rolling Cloud — "Tame Impala uses that sound"); **Nexus + OTT-on-max** for low-volume atmospherics; pre-chorus strings via **Pitch Map**. → [[Nexus]], [[OTT]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **A "happy accident" ambient blip that mirrors the lyric** — Jason Evigan's guitar-pedal drone runs the whole song; little blips "explode" right on a line's meaning — kept because it fit. → [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]], [[Ian Kirkpatrick - The Production Secret Behind Huge Pop Hits|commit to accidents]]
- **Bookending theme** — a first-verse sound is withheld, then returns at the very end "like a theme." → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Charli is "a real-life compressor"** — she backs off the mic when she gets loud, naturally, so the level stays even at the source. → [[Charli XCX Vocal Chain]]
