# DS Clang

![[Images/DS Clang.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Ableton (Drum Synths pack, Max for Live)

## What it is

One voice from Ableton's Drum Synths pack — a metallic clang/noise-based percussion generator for industrial or textured percussive hits.

## What it's used for

Industrial-style metallic percussion accents and texture hits in electronic beats.

## FX chain notes

Used as a drum-voice instrument in a Drum Rack; typically followed by a short transient-shaping compressor and a drum-bus saturation stage.
