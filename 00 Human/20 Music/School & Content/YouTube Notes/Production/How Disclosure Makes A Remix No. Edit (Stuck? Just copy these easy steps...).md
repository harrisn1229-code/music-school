# How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit (Stuck? Just copy these easy steps...)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)

## Synopsis

A Sol State breakdown video condensing Disclosure's edit-building process for a YouTube-ripped, unreleased 1987 Prince tune ("The Line") into a tight 17-minute walkthrough — dragging a low-quality rip into Ableton, healing it with EQ and de-essing, layering saturation for shine, building a sidechained drum foundation, and stacking small, playful ear-candy moves (panned percussion, filter sweeps, pitchless toms) until the flip feels like a finished club edit. **This covers the same underlying session as the existing marathon-stream note** [[Disclosure - Listening Sesh + Prince Edit + Talk VIP]] (Disclosure's own 3:46:45 Twitch VOD, "MAKING PRINCE EDIT!" segment) — most of the core moves (rip-rescue EQ/Soothe chain, Gem Dopamine, warp-in-Ableton, overdriven shakers, Haas-panned cabasa, "key of Prince") are already routed there. This note only adds the handful of concrete steps that stream note doesn't capture: the FabFilter Saturn clean-tube pass, Big Kick as the sidechain-source kick synth, the 909-hat volume-envelope trick, and the 707 tom as a pitchless bass-adjacent layer.

## Techniques

- **Drag the rip straight into Ableton and rough-mix first** — get an honest read on how bad it sounds before deciding what needs fixing, rather than pre-judging it. — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **Warp low-quality rips in Ableton even when producing elsewhere** — "I like to warp it in Ableton, I just think the algorithms are really good" — pull in only the usable chunks ("gems"), not the whole file. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]] — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **EQ out digital hiss first, then de-ess harsh vocals** — a YouTube rip has "not a lot going on up here, just digital hiss"; once that's gone the vocals cut through too harshly and need de-essing before anything else. → [[EQ]], [[De-essing]] — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **Don't chase missing top end on the sample — replace it with hats and shakers instead** — accept the source is dark and rebuild the top end in the mix rather than trying to EQ highs back into audio that never had them. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]] — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **Gem Dopamine to lift highs after cleanup** — "see if it brings out any highs... yeah, that's better." → [[Gem Dopamine]] — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **Chorus + Roto (rotary cab) for width and lift on the rescued sample** — sends a touch of chorus and rotary-cabinet character to widen and lift a thin source after the EQ/de-ess pass. → [[Width and Stereo]] — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **FabFilter Saturn's clean tube setting as a shine-adder without EQ** — "a good place if you want to add some shine without using EQ... really like the clean tube setting, it boosts a lot of highs" — saturation as an alternative top-end tool, not just distortion. → [[Saturn 2]], [[Saturation and Distortion]] — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **Big Kick as the sidechain-source kick synth** — writes the drums to sidechain off a Big Kick ("kind of a kick synthesizer") kick drum once the sample is in a good enough place, rather than sidechaining off the sample itself. — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **808 jam-and-feel session to find if an edit is possible at all** — jams freely on the 808 without recording, purely to feel out whether the sample has enough structure to support a full edit before committing to build one. — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **Haas-delay pan a percussion element hard left/right for width** — pans a shaker/cabasa element super left and right, delaying one speaker relative to the other, for an exaggerated stereo trick. → [[Width and Stereo]] — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **909 hat volume-envelope hold trick** — pull the volume envelope's hold stage all the way up on a 909 hat: hold the key down and the hat sustains for as long as you hold it; release early and it stays short — turns a single hat sample into both long and short hats from one key. — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **Always overdrive shakers, deliberately and heavily** — "always overdrive your shakers, always, big time — that distortion reminds me of how a shaker sounds on a 7-inch record, it just breaks up"; a permanent rule, not a per-track judgment call. → [[Saturation and Distortion]] — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **Layer in garage snares for a UK garage-flavored section** — swaps in dedicated garage snare samples to pivot part of the edit toward a UK garage feel. — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **Filter-sweep the loop to fight sample fatigue instead of adding new material** — "getting a bit bored of the sample looping round and round" — reaches for a filter chase/sweep on the existing loop rather than immediately writing something new. → [[Auto Filter]] — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **"It's in the key of Prince" — stop hunting for a key on a chord-dense rip** — the sample "has literally... there's no point" trying to pin an exact key; find the pocket by ear and move on. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]] — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **707 tom as a pitchless low element instead of a bassline** — when a proper bass note doesn't fit the ambiguous key, drops in a 707 tom hit as a percussive low-end element that doesn't need to be "in key" at all — "we don't need notes, screw notes." — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **Stack bass elements freely, then rebalance in the mix rather than pre-limiting how much bass goes in** — treats "too much bass" as a mix-stage problem to fix later, not a reason to hold back while writing. — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
- **Rough-mix "mastering" pass to close out the edit** — finishes with a light mastering-style pass on the rough mix rather than a full mastering chain, since the edit isn't a final release. → [[Master Chain]] — Disclosure (Sol State), [How Disclosure Makes A Remix/Edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jS9rs9y3kFs)
