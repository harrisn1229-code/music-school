# ARR-C03 — Two harmonic routes into a B section

**Q.** Name two ways to pick B-section chords that guarantee contrast without breaking the song.

> [!question]- Answer
> **The relative key** — find the relative major/minor (up three semitones from a minor root) and centre the B progression there. Identical scale tones, different centre, so contrast is guaranteed and the return resolves cleanly. **The withheld chord** — audit which diatonic chords the verse and chorus never used (say both used C, D, E, G, A but never F) and *open* the bridge on exactly that one; the ear has never heard it in this song, so its arrival alone signals somewhere new.

## Why it matters

Both solve the same problem — new but not random — from opposite ends. The withheld-chord move costs nothing and works before any instrumentation change lands.

## Drill

Take a finished song of yours. List every chord used in verse and chorus. Name the diatonic chords you never used. Start a bridge on one.
