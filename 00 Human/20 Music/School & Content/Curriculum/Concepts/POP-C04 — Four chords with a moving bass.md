# POP-C04 — Four chords with a moving bass

**Q.** What does "four chords with a moving bass" mean, and why does it feel like more movement than it is?

> [!question]- Answer
> **The chords stay the same; the bass walks through inversions underneath them.** `C – G/B – Am – F` gives a descending bass line over plain I–V–vi–IV. It reads as more harmonic movement because **the ear takes the lowest note as the root**, so changing the bass changes the perceived chord.

## Why it matters

It's the cheapest way to make four chords feel like eight, and it pairs with the inverse move — hold the bass, move the chords ([[POP-C03 — Pedal point]]) — and with [[Chromatic Bass Lines]], which the vault already covered.

## Drill

Take a 4-chord loop and write a descending bass line under it using inversions only. Change no chords.
