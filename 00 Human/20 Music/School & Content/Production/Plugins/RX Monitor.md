# RX Monitor

![[Images/RX Monitor.png]]

**Category:** Utility
**Vendor:** iZotope

## What it is

An RX module/plugin that runs real-time issue detection (clipping, clicks, hum) across a session for monitoring during tracking or mixing. Owned in both the RX 10 and RX 11 suites.

## What it's used for

Catching audio problems in real time while recording or mixing, rather than after the fact.

## FX chain notes

Left running on the master or input bus purely for detection, not processing.
