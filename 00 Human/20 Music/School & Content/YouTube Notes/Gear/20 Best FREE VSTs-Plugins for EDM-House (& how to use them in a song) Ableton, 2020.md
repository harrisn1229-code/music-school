# 20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House (& how to use them in a song) Ableton, 2020

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)

## Synopsis
A curated tour of 20+ completely free VSTs/plugins for EDM and house production, built around one running Ableton project so every plugin is heard both isolated and in context — the same track is played "before" (stock/dry) and "after" (fully processed) at the top of the video to sell the payoff.  The list runs in rough signal-chain order: one analog-style synth used for the entire demo track, movement/character effects (filters, chorus, vinyl, widener, panner, reverb/delay, saturation), then a run of mixing and mastering utilities (compressors, gain-matching EQ, a mastering EQ, analyzers, an oscilloscope, and finally two "free limiters" used as an all-in-one mastering chain).  Several of the free tools are explicitly framed as filling gaps that Ableton's stock devices don't — most notably a drawable-modulation panner pitched as "much more useful for complex panning than Ableton stock Auto Pan," and a spectrum analyzer that (unlike Ableton's own EQ Eight analyzer) can stay open across every track for translation checks.  A recurring theme: several tools here are close functional cousins of plugins already in the collection (a chorus, a widener, a reverb/delay, a multiband upward compressor, a tape-stop effect), so those are called out below as already covered rather than re-added.

## Techniques
- **u-he Tyrell N6 (free analog-modeled synth)** — the only synth used in the entire video; picked specifically because low-octave sub-bass content on cheaper synth emulations (including paid ones) sounds "fake plastic," while Tyrell N6 holds up. NEW find, not yet on the wishlist. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **TAL-Filter-2 (Togu Audio Line, heard in captions as "Tao filter")** — a filter plugin whose standout feature is a hand-drawable, tempo-synced modulation shape (not just a fixed LFO), with toggleable depth, resonance, and filter mode; used to add movement to synths "so the listener doesn't get bored." NEW find, not yet on the wishlist. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **TAL-Chorus-LX** — ALREADY OWNED (emulates the Juno-106's famous chorus). Demoed on two detuned oscillator layers alongside TAL-Filter-2 modulation to fatten a synth pair; skip re-adding. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **iZotope Vinyl (free)** — simulates worn-record vinyl character via Warp Depth and Era controls; demoed for adding "old-school character" to vocals or synths. NEW find, not yet on the wishlist (the owned collection has Ableton's own stock [[Vinyl Distortion]], so this is worth vetting against that first rather than treating as a pure gap). — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **Polyverse Wider** — ALREADY OWNED. Video's own honest caveat: not perfectly mono-compatible depending on source material, but a quicker/gentler alternative to a destructive Haas-effect widening trick; skip re-adding. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **Cableguys PanCake 2 (free)** — a drawable-curve auto-panner explicitly pitched as "much more useful for complex panning than Ableton stock Auto Pan"; demoed making a hi-hat "suck into the sides" in a nonlinear pattern and giving a drone layer a randomized, atmosphere-building pan drift. NEW find, not yet on the wishlist — a genuine capability gap vs. the owned stock Auto Pan. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **Valhalla Supermassive** — ALREADY OWNED. Reverb/delay pitched as adding "absolutely massive spaces"; tip is to dig into its modulation section for the best results. Skip re-adding. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **Xfer OTT** — ALREADY OWNED. Standard "love it or hate it" upward/downward multiband compressor call-out; try it on synth sounds. Skip re-adding. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **Camel Crusher (Camel Audio, free — company discontinued by Apple but the plugin is still findable free)** — a distortion + compressor + filter + dry/wet-knob all-in-one "add character to literally any sound" box; demoed on a bass. NEW find, not yet on the wishlist, though the collection already owns many character/saturation tools (Decapitator, Erosion, FETish, Devil-Loc, etc.) so this is closer to "another saturator" than a true gap. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **Klanghelm MJUC Jr (free tube compressor)** — described as natural/smooth/musical, best suited to bass and vocals; in the demo it's the gentler of a two-compressor pairing, "just kind of catches those final peaks" without over-squashing. NEW find, not yet on the wishlist. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **AdHd Audio Tools Leveling Tool (free tube leveling compressor, heard in captions simply as "the ADHD leveling tool")** — the more aggressive half of the same compressor pairing, with more explicit control (notably attack and release) than MJUC Jr; "it really levels it, it does what it says." NEW find, not yet on the wishlist. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **TDR VOS SlickEQ (Tokyo Dawn Records, free)** — an EQ whose standout feature is automatic gain matching, so tonal adjustments can be judged without being fooled by the "louder = better" bias; demoed pulling more mids out of a bass and confirming the gain-matched version genuinely sounded better, not just louder. NEW find, not yet on the wishlist — a real, non-redundant capability (none of the owned EQs auto-gain-match on every move). — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **Acustica Audio EQ, heard in captions as "Black CQ" (name low-confidence — captions are garbled here; likely one of Acustica's impulse-response-based Pultec-style/mastering EQ freebies, e.g. Coffee "The PUn" or a Black-series unit)** — a mastering EQ modeled on hardware praised for "magical top-end sparkle," built from impulse responses (great sound, heavy CPU load); demoed with roughly a 1dB low shelf cut under 300Hz plus a ~5dB boost around 2.5kHz and above, described as sounding musical rather than "digital." NEW find, not yet on the wishlist — flagged low-confidence, worth re-identifying from the video itself before buying/installing anything. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **Voxengo SPAN (free spectrum analyzer)** — its differentiator over Ableton's own EQ Eight analyzer: it can stay open continuously across the whole project, even while switching tracks, which is called out as the actual point — "use SPAN for mix translation" since your speakers/room/headphones can fool you but the analyzer won't (e.g. catching too much 30Hz in a clap you can't hear on small speakers). A hover feature reads the exact frequency and dB at any point on the curve. NEW find, not yet on the wishlist — vet against owned [[MMultiAnalyzer]] first since that may already cover the same job. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **Wavesfactory Cassette** — ALREADY OWNED. A tape-stop effect; the tip is that stop speed can be tempo-synced to the song or set freely rather than only using a fixed preset speed. Skip re-adding. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **Streaky "Smiley" (free one-knob mastering EQ)** — adds the classic mastering "smiley curve" (boosted highs+lows for more hi-fi) or, turned the opposite way, a "frown" curve that cuts highs and lows instead — used here backwards, cutting rather than boosting, specifically to bring the mids of a bass forward rather than making the mix more hi-fi. NEW find, not yet on the wishlist. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **MeldaProduction MNotepad (free, "a notepad for your DAW")** — a simple text-note utility that lives inside the plugin chain. Functionally already covered: the collection owns Ableton's own stock/Max for Live [[Notepad]] device, which does the identical job inside Live; skip adding this specific (different-vendor) version. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **MeldaProduction MLoudnessAnalyzer (free)** — an EBU R128/loudness meter showing momentary LUFS, integrated LUFS, PLR, and loudness range over time; recommended specifically "if you're mastering your own music." NEW find, not yet on the wishlist — but functionally overlaps the wishlist's existing High-impact #3 recommendation, **Youlean Loudness Meter 2 (FREE)**, for the exact same LUFS-metering job; not proposed as a separate routing edit since that gap is already flagged. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **MeldaProduction MStereoScope (free)** — a stereo-field/phase visualizer for checking stereo balance (e.g. too many sounds panned to one side, or a sound with extreme phase issues); demoed catching vocal parts that were out of phase. NEW find, not yet on the wishlist, though the collection already owns several stereo-imaging/metering tools (S1 Imager, Ghz Midside, kHs Stereo) that likely cover the same ground. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **Smart Electronix s(M)exoscope (free oscilloscope, heard in captions as "XO scope")** — a raw waveform oscilloscope, useful because it doesn't require bouncing the song to a file to see the actual audio shape; the specific technique demoed is checking whether a mix's waveform looks like a flat, over-limited "sausage" versus a dynamic waveform with real kick/snare transients — a visual gut-check on over-compression before finalizing a master. NEW find, not yet on the wishlist — a genuinely distinct capability (nothing owned does this raw-waveform-shape check). — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **D16 Group Frontier (free adaptive limiter)** — marketed as a limiter but used here more as a character/tone tool: adds a little harmonic color and weight rather than being pushed as a pure loudness maximizer; demoed on drums, where it gently muffles the kick transient in a way described as "kind of cool," and used elsewhere to add weight and note-holding character. NEW find, not yet on the wishlist. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
- **TDR Limiter6 (Tokyo Dawn Records, free tier — GE is the paid Gentleman's Edition, exact edition used here unconfirmed from captions)** — an all-in-one mastering toolkit (compressor + limiter + clipper in one plugin) for squeezing extra perceived loudness out of a mix; explicit caution given on camera that "mastering is a skill, not a plugin," and the demo deliberately over-drives it to show what happens when it's pushed too far. NEW find, not yet on the wishlist. — Sol State, [20 Best FREE VSTs/Plugins for EDM/House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7oHh7dyC28)
