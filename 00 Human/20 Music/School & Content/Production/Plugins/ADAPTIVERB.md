# ADAPTIVERB

![[Images/ADAPTIVERB.jpg]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Zynaptiq

## What it is

Zynaptiq's adaptive reverb plugin that analyzes the harmonic content of the input signal and tunes the reverb's resonances to match it in real time.

## What it's used for

Musically 'in-tune' reverb tails that follow a track's harmonic content rather than a fixed algorithmic space.

## FX chain notes

Used on a send/return, often for pads, vocals, or ambient textures where harmonic-matched reverb adds cohesion.

## Key Parameters

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Reflection / Reverb | Blend of sparse reflections vs. harmonic reverb tail | Harmonic tail resynthesizes a smooth, musical space |
| ② | Harmonic Tracking | Tunes the reverb to the input's harmonics | Removes dissonant reverb buildup — clean, pitched tails |
| ③ | Decay / Size | Tail length + space size | Long, evolving, non-metallic ambiences |
| ④ | Freeze / Mix | Infinite-hold + blend | Freeze for pads; blend on a send |
