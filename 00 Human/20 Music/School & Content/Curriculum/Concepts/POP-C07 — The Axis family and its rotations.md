# POP-C07 — The Axis family and its rotations

**Q.** Name the Axis progression and a song that uses it. I–V–vi–IV and vi–IV–I–V contain the same four chords — what actually changes?

> [!question]- Answer
> **I–V–vi–IV** (or any rotation) — "Let It Be", "Don't Stop Believin'", "With or Without You". Between rotations **the chords are identical; what changes is which one the ear hears as home.** I–V–vi–IV feels bright and lifting; vi–IV–I–V reads as darker and more yearning.

## Why it matters

POP-02 blank *immediately after* you spelled the progression correctly at POP-01 — you could build it and couldn't name it. POP-13 blank too. Whichever chord the loop starts and returns to becomes the tonic, which means you can flip a section's mood without changing a single chord.

## Drill

Write one 4-chord loop and play all four rotations. Pick one for a verse and one for a chorus.
