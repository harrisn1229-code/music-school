# Gated Delay

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Ableton (Creative Extensions pack, w/ Amazing Noises)

## What it is

A free Max for Live device from Ableton's Creative Extensions pack (bundled with Live Suite) — a delay with a step sequencer gating the incoming signal into the delay line on a defined rhythm, with a reverse option per step and a one-click randomize ("dice") control.

## What it's used for

Rhythmic, sequenced reverse/gate patterning distinct from a plain echo or from [[Beat Repeat]]'s repeat-based glitching — a step-programmed, half-random alternative for chopped delay patterns.

## FX chain notes

Insert on a texture or transition layer; the dice-randomize button makes it fast to audition new patterns live rather than hand-programming the step sequence every time.

## Techniques

- **Step sequence with linked/reversed and gated steps, plus a randomize dice** — same family as [[Pitch Hack]] but with no pitch component: a step sequence where certain ("blue") steps are linked and get reversed, and another ("yellow") step is where the gate is actually applied. A built-in dice button randomizes the whole pattern in one click — a fast, [[Beat Repeat]]-adjacent way to get new rhythmic delay patterns without manually reprogramming steps. → [[Delay]], [[Beat Repeat]] — dnksaus, [Ableton Delay Sound Design](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DCIEayyv1bk)
