# SCL-C08 — Matching a scale to a chord

**Q.** Which mode fits a Cmaj7♯11, and why doesn't plain C major work? Why does minor pentatonic work over a dominant?

> [!question]- Answer
> **C Lydian** — its ♯4 *is* the ♯11 the chord asks for. Ionian's natural F sits a half step above the E, the avoid note over a major chord. Minor pentatonic works over dominants because its **♭3 grinding against the chord's major 3rd is the blues sound**, and because three of its five notes are the chord's own tones (root, 5th and ♭7). It does *not* sidestep the natural 4th — the 4th is in the scale (A minor pentatonic: A C **D** E G) — blues passes through it rather than resting on it.

## Why it matters

Both blank. This is where the strand stops being trivia — the chord tells you which scale, and the characteristic note is the reason.

## Drill

For Cmaj7♯11, C7, Cm7 and Cm7♭5, name a fitting scale and the specific note that makes it fit.
