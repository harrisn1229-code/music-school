## What's taught

Full extraction routed directly — see [[Harmonic Ear Training]] for the complete breakdown: prioritize ear training by riff frequency rather than drilling every interval equally — **perfect 5ths/power chords first** (the most common riff-building interval), then **octaves** (used to make a lead line stand out by doubling it up an octave rather than as harmony), then **major/minor 3rds** (used instead of full triads under heavy distortion, since a 3rd alone carries the major/minor identity without turning to mud with gain), then **perfect 4ths**. Stated payoff: a trained ear on these intervals lets you transcribe riffs/songs by ear without tabs — directly useful for session work learning many songs fast.
