# NOT-C05 — Navigation: D.S. al Coda

**Q.** You reach "D.S. al Coda" in a chart. Walk through exactly what you do.

> [!question]- Answer
> **Three moves.** 1) Go back to **the sign (𝄋 / segno)**. 2) Play from there until the **To Coda** mark. 3) **Jump to the Coda** section.

## Why it matters

'Go to the coda and play it' is the usual half-answer — it skips the first two steps. The jump is precisely where people get lost, and it's the reason the 30-second scan checks the roadmap before anything else.

## Drill

Find a chart with a D.S. and trace the whole route with your finger before playing a note.
