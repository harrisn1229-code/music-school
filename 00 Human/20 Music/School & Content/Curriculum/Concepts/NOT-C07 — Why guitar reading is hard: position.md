# NOT-C07 — Why guitar reading is hard: position

**Q.** Sight reading is harder on guitar than piano. What's the actual mechanism, and what's the standard solution?

> [!question]- Answer
> **The same written pitch exists in five or six different places on the fretboard.** Notation says *what*, not *where* — on piano the mapping is one-to-one, so the decision doesn't exist. The fix is the **position system**: a fixed four-fret window, one finger per fret, making the mapping unambiguous.

## Why it matters

There's a confident *wrong* model in wide circulation here — that it's ledger lines. It isn't, and a wrong belief is more useful than a blank because it can be corrected directly. It's also why Leavitt keeps Volume 1 in first position for 24 pages.

## Drill

Find one written phrase in three different fretboard positions. Then commit to first position and read it again.
