# Mark Studio 2

![[Images/Mark Studio 2.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Overloud

## What it is

Overloud's guitar and bass amp/cabinet simulation suite, offering a range of modeled amps and mic'd cabinets.

## What it's used for

Full guitar/bass amp and cabinet tone shaping for DI-recorded parts.

## FX chain notes

Used as the primary amp-sim stage on a DI guitar or bass track.
