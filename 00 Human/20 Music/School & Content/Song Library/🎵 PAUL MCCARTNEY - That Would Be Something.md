# That Would Be Something — Paul Mccartney

> Loose, groove-driven rocker from McCartney (1970) — same album as Junk and Every Night.


## Production & Arrangement
From **McCartney (1970)** — his first solo album, recorded mostly **at home on a Studer 4-track** (plus some Abbey Road time) with **McCartney playing every instrument himself**. A deliberate reaction to Beatles polish and the breakup: loose, intimate, lo-fi, unfinished-on-purpose.

- A loose, bluesy one-man jam — mostly a rhythm groove, percussion, and a repeated vocal hook; raw and unpolished by design.
- Almost an exercise in how little you need: groove + a phrase.

## Steal this
- **Groove-and-a-phrase minimalism:** a repeated vocal hook over a simple groove can be a whole song.
- **Raw one-take energy:** capture a loose jam rather than composing every part.
- **Percussion-forward** homemade rhythm bed.
