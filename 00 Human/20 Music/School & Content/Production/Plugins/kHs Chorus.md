# kHs Chorus

![[Images/kHs Chorus.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A classic modulated-delay chorus snap-in for thickening and widening a signal.

## What it's used for

Widening synths, guitars, and vocals with classic chorus movement.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain, typically post-EQ.

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Delay | Base delay time of the chorus voices | Short for tight shimmer; longer for a doubling/slap feel |
| ② | Depth | Modulation depth | Low for subtle width; high for obvious chorus |
| ③ | Rate | Speed of the modulation LFO | Slow for lush drift; fast for warble |
| ④ | Taps | Number of chorus voices (2 / 3) | More taps for a thicker, denser ensemble |
| ⑤ | Spread | Stereo spread of the voices | Widen for a big stereo image; narrow to keep it centered |
| ⑥ | Mix | Dry/wet blend | ~30–50% for width without smear |
