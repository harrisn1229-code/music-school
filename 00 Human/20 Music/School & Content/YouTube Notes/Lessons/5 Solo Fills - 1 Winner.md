## Synopsis

Five short solo fills played back to back, presented as a pick-your-favourite. The description gives no analysis at all — it asks viewers to vote in the comments — so the five fills exist only in the playing. A minute long, five discrete phrases: the ideal shape for learning one by ear.

## What to watch for

- **Take one, not five.** Five fills at a minute total means roughly ten seconds each — enough to loop and steal. Pick the one that sounds most unlike what you already play.
- **Where each fill sits rhythmically** — a fill that lands on the and-of-four reads completely differently from the same notes starting on the downbeat. [[Using Space and Pauses in Phrasing]].
- **Whether the phrase resolves or hangs.** A fill that ends on a chord tone closes; one that ends on a tension pulls into the next bar. [[Whole-Note Chord-Tone Targeting with Scalar Resolve]].
- **Bends, slides and the touch.** In a one-minute demo the articulation is most of the identity — see [[Blues Lead Phrasing and Touch]] and [[String Bending Technique]].
