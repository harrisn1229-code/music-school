# Replika

![[Images/Replika.png]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Native Instruments

## What it is

Native Instruments' delay plugin offering multiple delay modes (Vintage, Modern, Tape) with a straightforward, musical interface.

## What it's used for

General-purpose rhythmic delay effects across vocals, synths, and guitars.

## FX chain notes

Used on a send/return or as an insert delay effect.

| ⑥ | Mix / Wide | Dry/wet blend + stereo width | Low Mix as an insert; Wide for a big stereo delay |
