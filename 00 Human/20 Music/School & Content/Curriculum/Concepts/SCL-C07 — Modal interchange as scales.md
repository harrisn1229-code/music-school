# SCL-C07 — Modal interchange as scales

**Q.** What does borrowing from the parallel minor actually mean, in terms of scales?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Keep the tonic, swap the collection.** In C major you pull from C minor — Fm (iv), A♭ (♭VI), B♭ (♭VII), E♭ (♭III). The key centre never moves, so it reads as colour rather than modulation.

## Why it matters

This is the *scale* view of what [[HAR-C08 — Borrowed chords and modal interchange]] covers as chords — those four chords are just the triads you get building on C natural minor. Same idea, two lenses.

## Drill

Build every triad on C natural minor. Compare to C major's. The differences are your borrowed chords.
