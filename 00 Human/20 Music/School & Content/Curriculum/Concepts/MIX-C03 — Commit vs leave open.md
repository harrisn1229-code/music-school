# MIX-C03 — Commit vs leave open

**Q.** Before handing off, what should you commit and what should you leave open?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Commit what defines what a part *is*** — character distortion, synthesis, creative effects, sample choice, tuning and timing edits. **Leave open everything relational** — track-to-track balance, corrective EQ, bus compression, reverb amounts, and the 2-bus entirely.

## Why it matters

The governing rule of the whole handoff. You couldn't answer this at MIX-07, then answered it backwards at MIX-11 by putting track-by-track EQ and compression on the commit side — those are relational, so they're the engineer's. Over-commit and they work around you; under-commit and they mix somebody else's record.

## Drill

Take ten elements in a current session. Call each commit or leave-open and say why. The test: would removing it change what the part *is*, or only how it sits?
