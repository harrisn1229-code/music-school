# Kenny Beats — Beat Arrangement & Song Form

![](https://youtu.be/7ocje4uT9fI)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/7ocje4uT9fI)

## Synopsis

The cure for loop-itis — "does anybody make a loop you really like, and an hour later it feels boring and you delete it?" His answer: the idea was never bad, it was missing a **B section**. How to write one (same drums, new bass/keys), why the right B section makes the A section feel like coming home, "Let the Beat Build" as the sequencing masterclass, A-B-C chord-section form, and the filtered-intro trick. The single most Nate-relevant mindset video of the batch — this is the bridge from song seeds/loops to finished arrangements.

## Techniques

- **Loop-itis diagnosis: the idea isn't bad, it's alone** — when a loop you loved starts feeling worthless after 30 minutes, "you might need another section," not a new idea. Most abandoned projects are good A sections missing a B. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **How to write the B section:** drag the same drums over, strip the bass/keys/melody, and write a complementary part — different chords, different density (simple vs busy), different mood. His Leon Michaels story: a sparse, sad, loose B section made his full/happy/soulful A chords "feel so good" on the return. **The right B section makes your A section better.** → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Coming-home effect** — playing OUT of the B back into the main idea makes the main idea feel exciting again ("oh my god, we're home"); once you trust that, you stop deleting loops. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **"Let the Beat Build" = the sequencing masterclass** — hi-hat → clap → clap+snares → kick → bass, and by the time everything's in, Wayne's at the hook. One idea stays interesting for minutes purely through when things enter and leave. Take the kick out for a stretch, the 808, ALL drums, the melody — arrangement is subtraction scheduling. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[Automation]]
- **A-B-C chord-section form** — verse chords → different pre-hook chords → different hook chords; extendable (A-B-C-D…). You don't need Sicko Mode's three-beats-in-one every time — just more than one section. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[Song Seeds Index|Song Seeds]] workflow
- **The filtered-intro trick** — take the main loop and run it filtered / different octave / reversed / darker as the intro; when the real thing drops, "that feels big" — because the ear was primed by a similar-but-lesser version. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
