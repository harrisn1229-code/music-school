# 5 PAINFUL Exercises That Actually Work!

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X91MHwRNJDc)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X91MHwRNJDc)

## Synopsis

Five dexterity/technique drills, ranked worst-to-best by how much they actually transfer to musical playing: a one-finger-per-string fan exercise (Pumping Nylon-derived), a ring-finger anchor drill, a 3-minute continuous chromatic picking exercise (with a generalizable "3-minute rule"), an odd/even finger-pairing pattern, and three-notes-per-string modal shapes (ranked best — the only one judged genuinely musical).

## Techniques

Full drill writeups routed to a dedicated drill note — see [[Five Painful Finger and Picking Exercises]]. Highlights:

- **The 3-minute rule generalizes to any exercise** — play anything continuously for 3 minutes, track pick slips as a data signal (>4 slips = tempo too fast, dial back), repeat up to 3 sets/day with rest between sets.
- **Ranking logic:** exercises are judged not just on raw dexterity gain but on musicality and transfer to real playing — the fan exercise (highest raw finger-independence demand) still ranks last because it's the least musical, while the three-notes-per-string modal exercise ranks first for combining technique, fretboard/key knowledge, and actual musicality.
