# Audio-Technica ATH-M50x

**Category:** Headphones (closed-back monitoring)
**Vendor:** Audio-Technica
**Ballpark value (used, 2026):** $70–100

> [!note] Assumed M50x (the standard ~$150 AT monitoring can) — confirm the model number on the inner headband if it's actually the M40x/M30x.

## What it is

The default closed-back studio headphone of the last decade — punchy, slightly hyped lows and highs, excellent isolation.

## What it's used for

Tracking (closed-back = no bleed into the [[Neumann TLM 102]]), late-night production, and the second opinion against the [[Yamaha HS8]]s.

## Signal chain notes

Headphone out on the [[Universal Audio Apollo Twin MkII]]. Known bias: the low end reads ~2 dB hyped vs the HS8s — bass decisions that sound "right" here often need a nudge up in the mix ([[SUB Slot]]/[[BASS Slot]] checks happen on the monitors).
