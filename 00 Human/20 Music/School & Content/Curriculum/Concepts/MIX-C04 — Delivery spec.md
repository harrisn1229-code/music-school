# MIX-C04 — Delivery spec

**Q.** What format, sample rate, bit depth and headroom do you deliver?

> [!question]- Answer
> **WAV or AIFF** (never MP3), at the session's **native sample rate** with no conversion, **24-bit** minimum, **no dither**, peaks **−6 to −3 dBFS**, every file consolidated from the same timeline zero, every track descriptively named.

## Why it matters

Format, bit depth and headroom are the easy three. The two that get missed are *native* rate (don't convert — conversion artifacts are permanent) and *no dither* (dither is the last step of the last process, and that isn't you).

## Drill

Recite the spec without looking. Then check your last export against it.
