## Techniques

- **VCA envelope attack set slightly FASTER than the filter envelope's attack/contour time**, rather than identical — described as "matching, but a little quicker" — for tighter note articulation where the amplitude arrives just ahead of the tonal brightness change, instead of both ramping in lockstep.
- Reconfirms established patterns from prior breakdowns: square-bottom/sawtooth-top octave stack, filter fully closed with contour+decay doing all the shaping, keyboard tracking off for consistent tone across register (toggleable live for a brighter alt-voice), decay time tuned to the track's feel, and a small nonzero sustain so held notes don't go choppy.
