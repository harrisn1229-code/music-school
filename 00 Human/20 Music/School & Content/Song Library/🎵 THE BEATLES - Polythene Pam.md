## Pollack's analysis

*Extracted from [[AWP — Polythene Pam]] — Alan W. Pollack, "Notes On…" #187.*

**Form.** Sibling track to "Mean Mr Mustard" — same key, nearly the same chord set, opposite harmonic behaviour. Mustard starts on I and opens out to V; Pam **never starts on I and converges on it every time**. Intro is a 2-bar instrumental tile stated twice, recurring essentially unchanged three times and also supplying the outro material. The verse is an odd **10 bars** — a quatrain AABA′ plus a spacer phrase — and the three groupings deliberately fall out of register with each other: the phrase tiling runs AABCC, while the harmonic shape reads AABA′A′. The outro is 22 bars, roughly 40% of the track: eight passes of the double plagal phrase, this time with each chord's own root in the bass, then six bars building on I, the last four carrying a descending bass scale that re-hears the E chord as V of A and delivers "... Bathroom Window."

**Harmony.** `Intro: ♭VII – IV – I` over an **E pedal for the entire phrase** ([[POP-C03 — Pedal point]]), so the double plagal sits on top of the tonic instead of travelling to it. `Verse: ♭VII – IV – I (×2) | V-of-♭VI – V | ♭VI – ♭VII – I (×2)`. The one chord Mustard does not have is G — on paper ♭III, in the ear a delayed dominant into the C major a bar later. And since V always goes to ♭VI, **every real arrival on I comes from ♭VII**: V is demoted to a deceptive resolution ([[HAR-C04 — Deceptive cadence]]) and cadence duty is handed to the double plagal ([[POP-C09 — Mixolydian ♭VII]], [[HAR-C03 — Plagal cadence]]).

**Notable.** The tune is pattering, even more than in Mustard, its contour coming almost entirely from transposing simple descending motifs up and down the scale. The backbeat contrast with Mustard is a syncopation choice — Mustard marches and drops hard syncopations on the eighth before the downbeat; Pam swings on less syncopation, a faster tempo, and a motif accenting the last three eighths in the first half of the bar.

**Against this page.** Pollack reads it in **E major** — which matches this page's Chords section but not the `key:` field, which says A major. Nothing above has been changed.
