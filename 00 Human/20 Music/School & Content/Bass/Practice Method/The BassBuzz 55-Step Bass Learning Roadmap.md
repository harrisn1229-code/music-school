# The BassBuzz 55-Step Bass Learning Roadmap

The master "what to learn next" reference for self-taught bass, distilled from BassBuzz's curriculum-overview video [[What to Learn on Bass (in Order)]]. Six levels (0 through 5), 55 numbered steps, each linked to the vault's existing dedicated technique/theory note where one already exists — this note is the connective index between the roadmap's sequencing and the vault's much deeper individual notes, not a re-teaching of any of them. Use it as a checklist: find your current level, confirm the steps below it are solid, and use the linked notes to actually go learn the next step rather than treating this page itself as the lesson.

**Companion framework, not a duplicate of it:** [[The Five-Tier Bass Theory Curriculum]] also ladders material into five levels, but by a different axis — that note sorts *theory topics* by which player *goal* needs them (Bedroom Player → Extreme Jazz-Fusion Shredder); this note sorts the *entire skill curriculum* (technique, rhythm, theory, and style all mixed together) by the *order BassBuzz recommends learning it in*. They overlap in content (both cover scales, keys, chord progressions, modes, etc.) but are genuinely different cuts through the same material — cross-reference both when planning what's next, don't treat one as a superset of the other.

## The load-bearing move: tier-recognition, not step-completion

Same closing point [[The Five-Tier Bass Theory Curriculum]] makes about theory specifically, applied here to the whole curriculum: the video deliberately gives Level 5's steps 42–55 "rapid-fire" and largely undetailed, framing the full list not as homework but as a **recognition tool** — so a self-taught player can spot when a teacher, course, or algorithm is pushing Level 4/5 material at a Level 1/2 goal. Read this table against wherever you actually are, not top to bottom.
