# Pitch Loop 89

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Ableton

## What it is

A stock Max for Live device bundled with Live Suite — a stereo multi-effect combining a non-tempo-synced delay, a pitch section, and low-pass/high-pass filtering, with independent left/right channel parameters.

## What it's used for

Pitched, filtered repetition loops with genuinely different left-vs-right character — a stereo-specific pitch/delay tool distinct from a plain mirrored stereo delay.

## FX chain notes

Insert on a texture or transition layer specifically for the per-ear pitch-delay difference; reach for this over [[Echo]]/[[Shifter]] when the goal is an asymmetric stereo pitch-delay effect rather than a centered one.

## Techniques

- **Independent per-ear pitch-delay with vibrato-style modulation for a genuinely stereo effect** — sets a different pitched-delay result on the left vs. right ear, with modulation (vibrato-type movement) layered on top of the pitch/delay — called out specifically as "really cool for stereo-effect kind of stuff," distinct from a centered mono delay panned wide. → [[Delay]], [[Shifter]] — dnksaus, [Ableton Delay Sound Design](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DCIEayyv1bk)
