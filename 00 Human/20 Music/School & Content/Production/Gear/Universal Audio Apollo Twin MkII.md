# Universal Audio Apollo Twin MkII

**Category:** Audio Interface (2-in/6-out, Thunderbolt, UAD-2 **DUO** DSP)
**Vendor:** Universal Audio
**Ballpark value (used, 2026):** $500–650 (DUO)

## What it is

UA's desktop Thunderbolt interface with Unison mic preamps (impedance-modeling preamp emulations) and onboard UAD-2 DSP for near-zero-latency tracking through UAD plugins.

## What it's used for

The studio hub: [[Neumann TLM 102]]/[[Shure SM57]] go in through Unison preamps, monitors out to the [[Yamaha HS8]] pair; Console app handles low-latency tracking with effects printed or monitored.

- **A small Apollo is the anchor of a deliberately bare-bones mobile writing rig** — Myles Smith / Peter Fenn: desk + a small Apollo + a vocal mic + guitars + a MIDI controller + speakers, chosen specifically to feel like a living room rather than a studio (low-pressure creative environment); paired with [[UAD Spark]] (unowned, needs only an internet connection, no Apollo DSP) so the same plugin chains travel from the anchor studio to any Airbnb/hotel room. → [[Creative Process and Idea Capture]] — Myles Smith / Peter Fenn (Tape Notes), [[TN 184 Myles Smith & Peter Fenn - Full Video Episode]]

## Signal chain notes

Unison preamp emulations (610, Neve-style, API-style) change the *input* character — pick per source before touching plugins. Line outs can also feed the [[Sony TC-630]] for the tape-print loop.
