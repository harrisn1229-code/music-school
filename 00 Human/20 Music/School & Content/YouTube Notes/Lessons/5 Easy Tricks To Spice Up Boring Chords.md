# 5 Easy Tricks To Spice Up Boring Chords (Guitar Lesson)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XmVlFVzlBE4)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XmVlFVzlBE4)

## Synopsis

Five extended-chord variations built from one static E minor shape (root stays on the 7th fret of the A string throughout), each tied explicitly to the scale it implies — a direct, worked example of how a single extension note commits a chord to a specific parent scale/mode.

## What's taught

- **Em9 vs. Emadd9 — naming rule stated directly.** "9" in a chord symbol implies the 7th is also present (E-G-D-F♯); "add9" means the 9th only, no 7th (E-G-F♯). Both fit plainly inside E natural minor (F♯ = the 9th/major 2nd), no scale conflict.
- **Em11 — voiced low in the chord, reads close to a sus4 sound but keeps the minor 3rd** (distinguishing it from a true sus4, which would replace the 3rd). The 11th = the perfect 4th one octave up; stays inside E natural minor, no clash.
- **Em13 (and its lower-voiced Em6 relative) commit the chord to Dorian, not natural minor — concrete clash warning given.** The 13th (C♯) is a **major** 6th; E natural minor's own 6th is C natural. C♯ only exists in **E Dorian** (natural minor with a raised 6th, same notes as [[Dorian from the Minor Pentatonic Box]]'s core technique). Playing C natural from habit (defaulting to natural minor) against a C♯ voiced in the chord creates an audible clash — a concrete, chord-tone-driven instance of "know which mode a voicing commits you to."
- **Em(maj7) commits the chord to harmonic minor, with the same clash logic.** The major 7th (D♯) only exists in **E harmonic minor** (natural minor with a raised 7th) — playing the natural minor 7th (D) against the voiced D♯ clashes the same way the C/C♯ pair does above.
- **General framing (stated as the video's real lesson):** the 9th = major 2nd, 11th = perfect 4th, 13th = major 6th one octave up — knowing these equivalences lets you read any "boring" triad and reason directly about which scale degree to add, and which parent scale/mode that addition locks you into for soloing over it.
