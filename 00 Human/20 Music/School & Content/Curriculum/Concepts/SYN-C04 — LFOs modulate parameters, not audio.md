# SYN-C04 — LFOs modulate parameters, not audio

**Q.** What is an LFO, and how does it differ from an audio-rate oscillator?

> [!question]- Answer
> A **Low Frequency Oscillator** runs below the hearing range (~under 20 Hz) and is wired to **modulate a parameter** rather than be heard as a tone. Same circuit as an audio oscillator — just slow, and routed to a knob instead of the output. **You never hear the LFO; you hear what it moves.**

## Why it matters

The name is common knowledge; the distinction isn't. The payoff: push an LFO past ~20 Hz and it stops sounding like movement and starts sounding like tone — at which point you've built FM. The line between modulation and audio is only speed, which is why [[FM Synthesis]] and this are the same idea.
