# EAR-C03 — Major 3rd vs minor 3rd — the first drill

**Q.** Describe the **3** and the **♭3** as feeling states, and say why this specific pair is your named starting line.

> [!question]- Answer
> **3** — sweetness, warmth, glow, vitality; a whole extra dimension that 5 doesn't have. **♭3** — the thing that *defines* minorness: dark, serious, gloomy, a bit of sorrow. Number tells you how far; quality tells you which colour. Hearing "a 3rd" without hearing *which* 3rd is the standard gap at this stage, and quality is the half that does the emotional work.

## Why it matters

The results note names this pair as literally the first drill: you're hearing *that there's a third* and not which one. Every chord-quality question downstream is the same failure at a larger scale.

## Drill

Against a C drone, alternate E and E♭. Say the feeling word out loud before you name the degree. Twenty reps, then switch the drone to A.
