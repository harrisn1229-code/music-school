# Serum 2 — Reese Growl Bass

**Instrument:** [[Serum 2]] · **Slot:** [[BASS Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]]

## What it's for
EDM-lane reese/growl bass — a clean sub layer under a moving, detuned growl layer. Different technique from [[Serum 2#Patch Recipes|Serum 2's Dubstep/trap growl recipe]] (which uses FM "talking" movement on a single osc pair): this is the classic two-layer reese build the chain note calls for.

## Settings
- Osc A: sine, unprocessed — the SUB-slot foundation. Route it to its own output/chain and keep it clean of any distortion or filter movement downstream
- Osc B (+ C if needed): saw or wavetable, detuned against itself for the reese beating/growl character — this layer carries the BASS-slot harmonics
- ⑥ Filter on Osc B/C only: LP or notch, with an LFO or envelope sweeping cutoff for movement — Osc A stays unfiltered
- FX: distortion → [[OTT]]-style multiband on the Osc B/C bus only, never on the sub layer
- ⑩ Mono, legato, glide for slides

## In context
- Osc A (sub) must stay untouched by the chain's distortion/saturation — that's the one interaction that matters; everything aggressive happens on the growl layer only.
- Swap in [[Serum 2#Patch Recipes|Dubstep/trap growl]]'s single-osc FM approach instead when the part wants "talking" movement rather than a beating reese texture — same slot, different technique.
