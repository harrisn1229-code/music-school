# HAR-C07 — Secondary dominants

**Q.** What is a secondary dominant? Spell V/V in C major, and say what A7 is doing in `| Cmaj7 | A7 | Dm7 | G7 |`.

> [!question]- Answer
> A dominant chord built on the **V of a chord other than the tonic**, temporarily treating that chord as a tonic. **V/V in C = D7** (V of G). In that progression **A7 is V/ii** — diatonically the chord on A is Am; raising the 3rd to **C♯** makes it a dominant pointing at Dm, tonicizing it before the fall through G7 to C.

## Why it matters

Both questions blank. This is the single most common way popular music leaves the key for a moment, and the tell is always a **chromatic leading tone** — the C♯ that isn't in C major.

## Drill

In a C major loop, put E7 before Am. Then D7 before G. Name the borrowed leading tone in each.
