## What's taught

- **All 7 modes taught as one-note alterations from Aeolian (minor modes) and Ionian (major modes), not as independent shapes:**
  - Minor side, from Aeolian: **Dorian** = raise the 6th to major; **Phrygian** = lower the 2nd to minor; **Locrian** = lower both the 2nd and the 5th (its tonic triad is diminished, giving Locrian its dissonant, rarely-used character).
  - Major side, from Ionian: **Lydian** = raise the 4th (BERNTH's own favorite mode, for the "satisfying dissonance" against Ionian's plainer sound); **Mixolydian** = lower the 7th (most associated with blues/jazz and dominant-7 contexts).
- **Play the whole sequence with strict alternate picking** (down-up-down-up throughout) to combine technique conditioning with active mode-derivation thinking — explicitly warns against memorizing finger positions per mode without tracking which note is actually being altered.
- **Practice order:** minor modes first (more immediately useful for rock/metal), then major modes — with an explicit note not to skip Lydian just because it's less common.
