# MIX-C02 — Multitracks vs stems

**Q.** What's the difference between multitracks and stems, and which does a mixing engineer want?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Multitracks** are every individual track, unbundled — each mic, each synth, each vocal layer. **Stems** are grouped submixes (drums, bass, music, vocals) bounced to a handful of files. **Send multitracks** unless they've specifically asked otherwise.

## Why it matters

Stems are for remixers, live playback and sync licensing. An engineer handed stems can't touch the snare without also touching the kick.

## Drill

For a current session: how many multitracks would you deliver, and what would the four stems be?
