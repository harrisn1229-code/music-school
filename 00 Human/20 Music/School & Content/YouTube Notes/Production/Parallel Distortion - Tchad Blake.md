# Parallel Distortion - Tchad Blake

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yi3Cm2DtMmg)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yi3Cm2DtMmg)

## Synopsis

Tchad Blake tells the origin story of his go-to distortion tool — a Tech 21 SansAmp Classic pedal he bought new in the late '80s/early '90s and has used ever since on nearly every instrument, most of all kick drum — and then demonstrates a live, exploratory approach to dialing in parallel distortion on a kick: a high-pass filter, the SansAmp itself, and an expander, all stacked and tweaked by ear with no fixed recipe. His stated method for the whole mix, not just this element, is trial-and-error: try something, listen, keep it or undo it, and don't be precious about backtracking.

## Techniques

- **SansAmp Classic (Tech 21) used as a general-purpose distortion/character tool across nearly every instrument, most consistently on kick drum, and also used as a pseudo-EQ rather than only as distortion.** "It became a bass sound for everything, I didn't use a bass amp after that, literally never since... on drums, especially kick drum, it's on everything that I do, and I use them like an EQ as well — if I just want to change an acoustic guitar sound a little bit, not too much, I'll use it as an EQ, sometimes in parallel, sometimes not." Already tracked in [[Plugin Wishlist]] (Nick Launay's kick-parallel SansAmp entry) — this video adds a second, independent artist endorsement plus the additional "use it as an EQ" application. Owned-lane stand-ins: [[Decapitator]] or [[Devil-Loc Deluxe]]. → [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[Decapitator]], [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **High-pass filter (33 Hz, 18 dB/octave) placed ahead of the distortion stage on a kick, to control what gets driven into the saturation rather than distorting the full low end.** A steep, narrow-band high-pass specifically below the kick's fundamental. → [[Kick Design]], [[EQ]]
- **Distortion audibly interacts with phase on a kick — described directly: "it's definitely messing with my phase."** Worth checking phase/polarity after inserting a hard distortion stage on a low-frequency source, not just tone. → [[Phase Alignment]]
- **An expander used after/alongside the distortion stage to punch the kick back out — deliberately tightening a transient that the saturation stage had softened or smeared.** Exploratory, not a fixed setting — "I'm gonna see if I can punch that out a little bit with an expander, I might not like it later." → [[Kick Design]], [[Compression]]
- **Explicit trial-and-error mixing philosophy applied across an entire session, not just to this kick: try a chain, listen, keep or discard, retrace steps freely.** "I'm just going back and forth between instruments and elements, trying stuff, taking it off... most of the time it works... sometimes it doesn't work so well and I go back, retrace my steps, get rid of a lot of stuff that I've done. Anything goes." → [[Session Templates]]
- **Sound-selection preference stated directly: avoids "ticky" (clicky, thin-attack) kick drums as a rule, favoring some low-mid growl/brown character instead.** → [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[Kick Design]]
