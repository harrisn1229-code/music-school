# How Deep Is Your Love — Bee Gees

> Written by Barry, Robin and Maurice Gibb; from the *Saturday Night Fever* soundtrack (1977).

## Chords
Key: E♭ major · ~104 BPM.

**Verse:** E♭ – Gm7 – Fm7 – C7 – Fm7 – G7 – A♭9/B♭, then repeats with a substitution: E♭ – Gm7 – Cm7 – Fm7 – A♭9/B♭
→ **I – iii7 – ii7 – V7/ii – ii7 – V7/vi – IVadd9/V** — the C7 and G7 are both **secondary dominants** slipping in and out around the diatonic ii7 (Fm7), pulling the ear forward without ever fully tonicizing anywhere else → [[Secondary Dominants]]

**Pre-chorus:** A♭maj7 – Gm7 – Fm7 – D♭7(9) – Gm7 – A♭9/B♭
→ **IVmaj7 – iii7 – ii7 – ♭VII7(9) – iii7 – IVadd9/V** — the D♭7 is a **borrowed ♭VII7**, a soulful ballad color chord lifted from E♭ Mixolydian rather than the parent major scale → [[Borrowed Chords]]

**Chorus:** E♭ – E♭maj7 – A♭maj7 – A♭m6 – E♭maj7 – D♭6(9) – C7 – Fm7 – A♭m6
→ **I – Imaj7 – IVmaj7 – iv(m6) – Imaj7 – ♭VII6(9) – V7/ii – ii7 – iv(m6)** — built on the classic "major-IV-to-minor-iv" cliché plus a second borrowed ♭VI/♭VII color chord.

Worth checking directly against the reputation this kind of soft-rock ballad has: this is **not** the plain I–vi–IV–V doo-wop loop or the axis progression — the actual changes are denser, leaning on secondary dominants (C7, G7) and borrowed minor/♭VII chords throughout, and never settle into that 4-chord shape.
