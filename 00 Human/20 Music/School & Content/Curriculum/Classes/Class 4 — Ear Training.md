# Class 4 — Ear Training

A ~10-minute daily routine, not a reading block. Runs from day one and never stops.

> [!info] Standing class — `load: routine`
> Not a scheduled block. It doesn't consume a track slot, it doesn't gate anything, and **it keeps running after its last lesson completes** — the skill it builds decays if you stop. Its material also appears as the listening section inside both theory midterms and the theory final.

## What it assumes

- Nothing. Runs from day one alongside everything else.

## What closes it

- Nothing. It's standing — there's no exam and no finish line. Its material is examined inside the theory midterms and final instead.
