# ValhallaPlate

![[Images/ValhallaPlate.jpg]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Valhalla DSP

## What it is

Valhalla's plate reverb emulation, modeling the dense, bright character of classic studio plate reverb units.

## What it's used for

Classic plate reverb character on vocals, drums, and instruments.

## FX chain notes

Used on a send/return for a vintage-style reverb tail.

## Used in my chains

My **plate** reverb — dense, bright, no early-reflection clutter; the classic vocal-tail plate:

- **[[Reverb Send Long]]** — *slot 1, the tail*: Mix 100%, Decay 1.8–3.0 s, Predelay 30–40 ms (longer than the room — keeps the lyric intelligible under a long tail). Plate for vocals; swap [[ValhallaVintageVerb]] hall for orchestral/ballad size.
