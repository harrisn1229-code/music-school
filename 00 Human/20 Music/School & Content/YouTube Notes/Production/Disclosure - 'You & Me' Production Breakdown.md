# Disclosure - 'You & Me' Production Breakdown

Guy reopens the 2013 "You & Me" (feat. Eliza Doolittle) Logic session and it's a "less is more" master class: **stock Logic plugins for everything except a custom layered bass**. Most valuable: the Ultrabeat synth-kick, the three-layer sub with a phone-audibility octave, the almost-nothing Eliza vocal chain, and the honest "I double-limited and didn't know what I was doing" hindsight on his old gain staging. Whisper transcript (no captions) — high confidence.

## Techniques
- **Stock Logic plugins for the whole song** (except the bass) — Ultrabeat drums, ESP pad (Juno copy), Logic EQ/comp/reverb; the only third-party is one FabFilter and a transient shaper added later. "You don't need fancy plugins." → mixing philosophy.
- **Ultrabeat synth-kick, envelope-shaped** — the kick is synthesized and shaped (the skill that led to Big Kick / Kick 2). → [[Kick Design]]; body in [[SUB Slot]], click in [[TRANSIENT Slot]].
- **Three-layer sub** → [[SUB Slot]] + [[BASS Slot]]: (1) a sine main body, (2) an **octave-up sine so the bass reads on phones/laptops** (small-speaker rule — 100–200 Hz warmth; see [[Referencing and Translation]]), (3) a pitched **bongo-low** for character, all key-matched. → [[3 Bass Bus|Bass Bus]].
- **Custom bass from layered samples** run through **Logic Overdrive** ("does most of the work") → channel EQ → light comp → transient shaper (1 % attack — negligible) → chorus (careful, "gets too wide"). → [[BASS Slot]] + [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]] + [[Saturation and Distortion]].
- **Drum bus, early-Disclosure:** EQ doing nothing, a **reverb on the drum bus** to give the kick space (feels dead without it, "almost adds distortion"), Logic limiter ~1–2 dB — *plus a second pointless limiter* ("double limiting, didn't know what I was doing"). Bass bus similarly over-limited (~7 dB) — "I'd use a clipper or just turn it down now." → [[3 Drums Bus|Drums Bus]], [[3 Bass Bus|Bass Bus]], [[Gain Staging]], [[Limiting]].
- **Logic strings for a high violin note** (factory setting) to float over the chords → [[AIR Slot]] / [[BED Slot]]; "Ableton doesn't have good strings." **Logic FM synth** for the beeps → [[MIDS Slot]].
- **Ring Shifter on the ESP pad chords** (frequency-mod, wah-like motion) — a Logic sound he re-imports into Ableton for because he misses it. → [[BED Slot]] + [[Modulation]].
- **Eliza vocal, almost untouched** → [[LEAD Slot]] + [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]]: just a compressor + basic EQ (**low-cut + a big high boost**) + quarter-note delay + the **Gymnasium reverb** (the Settle bus verb, ~2 s) + a bit of chorus + a tiny de-ess on the chorus. No tuning ("she never needs it"). **4 chorus takes back-to-back, tiny syllable edits.** → [[De-essing]], [[Reverb Send Long]].
- **Traditional arrangement, no middle-eight** — intro/verse/pre/chorus ×2 + outro; "didn't overthink it." The famous remixes (Flume, Rivo) chopped the **post-chorus B-section**, not the main hook.
- **Philosophy:** Logic vs Ableton is a non-issue — "they're all as good as you are; the tool doesn't write anything." Use whatever gets results.
