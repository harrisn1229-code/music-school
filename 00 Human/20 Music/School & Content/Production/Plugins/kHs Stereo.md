# kHs Stereo

![[Images/kHs Stereo.png]]

**Category:** Pan/Imaging
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A stereo-width and utility snap-in for adjusting the width/mono content of a signal.

## What it's used for

Stereo width adjustments within a modular effects chain.

## FX chain notes

Stacked late in a Snap Heap chain, for final width correction.
