## Synopsis

A short, focused BERNTH lesson: one lick-multiplication trick (start the same phrase from different beat positions) demoed with a legato/hybrid-picking lick, plus a technique note on hybrid picking's role in evening out legato dynamics — credited to Tom Quayle.

## What's taught

- **Rhythmic-displacement lick multiplication:** replay one already-learned lick starting from a different beat/off-beat position each time, shifting every internal accent onto syncopated positions instead of the habitual downbeat-anchored placement most players default to. A generalizable practice system applicable to any existing lick, not tied to this specific one.
- **Hybrid picking as a fix for legato's per-string accent problem (Tom Quayle-credited):** pure legato tends to accent only the first (picked) note of each new string, with the rest hammered/pulled quietly by comparison. Combining pick and fingerstyle plucking smooths the string-to-string attack, putting focus on individual-note timing and dynamics rather than one loud hit per string. Works with straight alternate picking too if hybrid picking isn't the focus.
