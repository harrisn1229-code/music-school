# FND-C08 — Compound intervals

**Q.** What simple interval is a major 9th equivalent to? What's the general rule?

> [!question]- Answer
> A **major 2nd**. **Subtract 7** to reduce any compound interval: 9→2, 11→4, 13→6.

## Why it matters

You half-had this — "2nd or 9th" were both true and the question wanted the simple one. It's the bridge into extended chords: a B♭13 stops being confusing once 13 reads as "the 6th, an octave up."

## Drill

Reduce: major 9th · perfect 11th · major 13th · minor 9th · ♯11. Then spell each above C.
