# MEL-C03 — Motifs and how to develop them

**Q.** What is a motif, and name at least two ways to develop one.

> [!question]- Answer
> **A short musical fragment used as raw material to generate much more music.** Nine named techniques: **repetition · sequence · inversion · interval contraction · interval expansion · diminution · augmentation · rhythmic metamorphosis · imitation.**

## Why it matters

[[Motivic Development]] has had a table of all nine in the vault the whole time. The point that generalizes: **most strong melodies are one short idea developed, not a long line invented.**

## Drill

Write four bars. Generate sixteen more using only techniques from the table. Name each one you used.
