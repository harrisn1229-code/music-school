# kHs Phaser

![[Images/kHs Phaser.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A classic phaser modulation snap-in using cascaded all-pass filters.

## What it's used for

Sweeping phaser effects on guitars, keys, and synths.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain, post-EQ.

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Cutoff | Center frequency of the phaser notches | Set where the sweep sits in the spectrum |
| ② | Rate | Speed of the sweep LFO | Slow for a wide phase sweep |
| ③ | Depth | Range of the sweep | More for obvious movement |
| ④ | Order | Number of allpass stages (notch count) | More stages = richer, deeper phasing |
| ⑤ | Spread | Stereo spread of the sweep | Widen for a big stereo phase |
| ⑥ | Mix | Dry/wet blend | ~50% for the classic phaser sound |
