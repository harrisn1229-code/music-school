# ARR-C04 — Arrangement interest is scheduled absence

**Q.** What does "Let the Beat Build" demonstrate, and what's the general principle?

> [!question]- Answer
> Hi-hat → clap → clap+snares → kick → bass → everything in at the hook. **One idea, minutes of interest, purely from when things enter.** The general principle: arrangement interest is **scheduled absence**, not added layers. Schedule the absences deliberately — pull the kick for a section, the 808, all the drums, the melody. A middle ground between gone and full: temporarily attenuate an element's top end rather than muting it, then restore it later.

## Why it matters

It inverts the instinct. When a section feels flat the reflex is to add, and the move is almost always to decide what leaves and when it comes back.

## Drill

Take a full 8-bar loop. Stagger every element's first entrance across 32 bars without writing a single new note.
