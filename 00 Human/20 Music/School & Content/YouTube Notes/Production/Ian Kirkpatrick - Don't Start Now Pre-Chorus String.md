## Synopsis

The pre-chorus "string" in Don't Start Now isn't a VST instrument or a patch at all — it's **built from bits of two old orchestral sample CDs** ("VLNS US," a sustained one and a "run" one), chopped and manipulated into the part. A window into Ian's decade-old personal sample library and how he composes with raw one-shots rather than playable instruments.

## Techniques

- **The string is chopped sample fragments, not an instrument** — a sustained violin sample + a "run" sample from an old library ("VLNS US"), spliced and manipulated into the melodic figure. No VST, no patch. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[BED Slot]]
- **Compose from one-shots/phrases, not just playable patches** — pull the exact articulation you want out of raw samples and arrange them; more character than a stock string patch. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]]
- **His pre-Splice library: ~300 GB accumulated over a decade** — sample CDs he got in trade (he built websites for broke bands who worked at Guitar Center → paid him in sample CDs). The lesson: build a *personal* sound library that no one else has. → [[Sound Selection Guide]]
