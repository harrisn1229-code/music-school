# VOI-C11 — A fill waits for the rest, and it goes somewhere

**Q.** When does a fill belong in a comping part, and what is the first design question to ask about one?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Only when the soloist or singer has actually stopped.** Check whether anyone else is playing before reaching for one — a fill layered under an active melody is not a fill, it is clutter. And the first question is **where does this lead**: a fill's job is to connect forward to the next bar or the next section, not to decorate the chord it starts on.

## Why it matters

It converts a fill from a melodic flourish into a structural device, which is what makes it worth practising. Length follows from the same rule — read how much space the form actually gives you and take a few notes or a whole bar accordingly. Build one bottom-up: a plain diatonic or blues line first, colour added as a second pass, not composed dense from the first note.

## Drill

15 min: comp a blues and play a fill only in the bars where the melody rests. Every fill has to land on a chord tone of the *next* chord.
