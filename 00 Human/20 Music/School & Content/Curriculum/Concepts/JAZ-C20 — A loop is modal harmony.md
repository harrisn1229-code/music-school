# JAZ-C20 — A loop is modal harmony

**Q.** What does modal jazz put in place of functional harmony, and which similarly-named thing is it not?

> [!question]- Answer
> Four things carry the music once the chords stop moving: **the mode's own character** as the melodic material, **quartal voicings**, **pedal points and drones**, and **rhythm, dynamics and texture** marking the form.
>
> It is **not modal interchange.** Modal jazz holds one mode for many bars — a static approach to form. Modal interchange borrows a chord from a parallel mode inside functional harmony and returns — a chromatic spice. They share a word and almost nothing else.

## Why it matters

This is the jazz idiom closest to how modern loop-based music actually works. A four-bar loop on Fmaj7, or a two-chord vamp, is exactly the situation *So What* is in: the harmony is not going anywhere and the interest has to come from somewhere else. Modal jazz is sixty years of worked solutions to that problem.

The practical consequence for arrangement: with no chord changes to signal a section, **one new element — a texture, a countermelody, a register shift — reads as a whole new section.**

## Drill

Take a two-chord vamp of yours and write four sections over it that differ only in texture and density, with the harmony untouched. Check whether the sections still read as sections.
