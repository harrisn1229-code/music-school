# DS Tom

![[Images/DS Tom.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Ableton (Drum Synths pack, Max for Live)

## What it is

One voice from Ableton's Drum Synths pack — a synthesized tom generator with pitch envelope and decay shaping similar to DS Kick but tuned for toms.

## What it's used for

Synthesized toms for fills and percussive melodic runs in electronic productions.

## FX chain notes

Used as a drum-voice instrument in a Drum Rack; typically followed by a short transient-shaping compressor and a drum-bus saturation stage.
