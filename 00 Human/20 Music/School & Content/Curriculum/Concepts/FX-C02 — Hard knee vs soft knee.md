# FX-C02 — Hard knee vs soft knee

**Q.** What's the difference between a hard knee and a soft knee, and when is each appropriate?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Hard knee** applies the full ratio the instant the signal crosses threshold. **Soft knee** eases the ratio in over a window either side, so compression starts gently *before* threshold. **Soft** for transparent levelling (vocals, mix bus); **hard** for catching peaks or when you want it audible.

## Why it matters

Knee is routinely confused with ratio — "how extreme the compression is" describes ratio, not knee. Knee is about how *abruptly* the ratio engages, and it's most of why two compressors at identical settings sound different.

## Drill

A/B hard vs soft knee on a vocal at the same ratio and threshold. Then on a drum bus.
