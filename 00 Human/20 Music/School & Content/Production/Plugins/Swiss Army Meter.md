# Swiss Army Meter

![[Images/Swiss Army Meter.jpg]]

**Category:** Utility
**Vendor:** Max for Live

## What it is

A Max for Live multi-purpose metering device combining several metering modes (peak, RMS, LUFS, etc.) in one utility.

## What it's used for

General-purpose level/loudness metering across mixing and mastering stages.

## FX chain notes

Runs inline in the signal/MIDI chain at the point it's inserted; combines with whatever device follows.
