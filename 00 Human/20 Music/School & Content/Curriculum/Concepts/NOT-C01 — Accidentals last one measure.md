# NOT-C01 — Accidentals last one measure

**Q.** An accidental not in the key signature applies for how long, and to which notes?

> [!question]- Answer
> **For the rest of that measure only, and only on that exact line or space** (that octave). The bar line cancels it. It does **not** apply to the same letter in other octaves.

## Why it matters

Both halves missed. The octave restriction is the part that catches people — a ♯ on the third-space C doesn't sharpen the C an octave up.

## Drill

Find a bar with an accidental in any chart. Say which later notes it does and doesn't affect.
