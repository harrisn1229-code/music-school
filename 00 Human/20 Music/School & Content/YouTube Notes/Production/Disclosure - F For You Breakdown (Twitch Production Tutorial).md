## What it covers (see the canonical page for detail)
- A **MacBook-mic demo vocal (sung with a cold) became the record** — Howard's first vocal, unplanned.
- **All stock-Logic** production; group vocals stack low-mids fast (cut ~70 dB of lows); ⚠️ **comp attack on 0 = distortion** (self-flagged).
- **Sidechain the chords off the vocal**; air + de-ess beats over-compression for presence.
- **Juno square-wave "beeps"** (velocity-controlled) — the signature's origin; **Sylenth1 square-wave bass through a phaser**, bounced to audio.
- **Bass delay only on the top end**; **rhythmic vinyl crackle per clap** (the Burial move); Ultrabeat kit, two chords (G♯m7).
- Bounced every bass hit to a **Roland SPD** for live; **Mary J. Blige** heard it on MTV and turned it into a duet.
