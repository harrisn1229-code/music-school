# POP-C11 — Chromatic mediants and pop modulation

**Q.** What is a chromatic mediant? What is a truck driver's modulation, why is it a cliché, and what's subtler?

> [!question]- Answer
> A **chromatic mediant** is a chord a **3rd** from the tonic with a quality the key doesn't provide — from C: **E, A, E♭, A♭ major**. Shares a common tone, has **no dominant pull**, so it reads as a lift or scene change. A **truck driver's modulation** shoves everything up a step for the final chorus, unprepared — a cliché because it announces itself. Subtler: **shift mode instead of key**, **approach the new tonic through its own V**, or **pivot on a shared chord**.

## Why it matters

Both blank, and both had **zero vault coverage** before 2026-08-04. Note ♭VI and ♭III are simultaneously borrowed chords and chromatic mediants — same chord, two framings.

## Drill

Drop an E major into a C major loop and let it sit. Then try A♭. Name what each does to the section.
