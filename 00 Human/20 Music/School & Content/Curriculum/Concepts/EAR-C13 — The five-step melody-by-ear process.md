# EAR-C13 — The five-step melody-by-ear process

**Q.** List the five steps for playing a melody back by ear.

> [!question]- Answer
> **1. Memorise** — sing or hum it back until it's internalised. **2. Map the contour** — up/down and roughly how far (step, leap, big leap); no degree numbers yet, just the shape. **3. Sing it slowly** and pencil in whichever degrees you can genuinely feel. **4. Deduce the rest** from the contour plus theory, then sing it back and check the felt states actually match. **5. Verify on the instrument** — and when you're wrong, work out *which* degree got confused for which. That last part is where the learning is.

## Why it matters

Contour before degrees is the step people skip. Getting the shape down first means the degrees you can't hear are constrained by the ones you can, instead of being twelve-way guesses.

## Drill

Take a nursery rhyme. Run all five steps without touching the instrument until step 5. Log which degree you got wrong.
