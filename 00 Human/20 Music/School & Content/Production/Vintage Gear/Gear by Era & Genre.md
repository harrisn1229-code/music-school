# Gear by Era & Genre

Reverse index into the [[Vintage Gear Index|Vintage Gear]] library — find units by decade, genre, or artist/landmark record. **Verifiable credits only**; where a unit is associated with a sound but specific records aren't documented, it's listed at genre level. Grows every batch.

## By decade
- **1960s–70s** — [[Maestro Rhythm King]] (Sly's Funk Box), [[Korg Mini Pops]] (*Oxygène*), [[Minimoog Model D]] (funk/prog/fusion bass+lead), [[Sequential Prophet-5]] (from '78, everything), [[Roland Jupiter-4]] (first Roland poly), [[Roland CR-78]] (art pop, new wave atmospheres)
- **1980s** — [[Linn LM-1]] · [[LinnDrum]] · [[Oberheim DMX]] · [[Oberheim DX]] · [[E-mu Drumulator]] · [[Sequential Circuits DrumTraks]] (the sampled-drum-machine pop/hip hop era), [[Simmons SDS-V]] (the electronic tom), [[Roland TR-808]] (electro, early hip hop), [[Roland TR-909]] · [[Roland TR-707]] · [[Roland TR-606]] · [[Roland TB-303]] (house/acid's birth), [[Roland TR-505]] · [[Roland TR-626]] · [[Alesis HR-16]] · [[Casio RZ-1]] (the budget boxes), [[Roland Juno-106]] · [[Roland Juno-6 - Juno-60]] · [[Roland Jupiter-8]] · [[Roland JX-3P - JX-8P]] · [[Sequential Prophet-600]] (the poly wave), [[Roland SH-101]] (mono bass), [[Yamaha RX5]] (deep-edit PCM), [[Roland D-50]] · [[Sequential Prophet VS]] (the digital turn, '86–87), [[E-mu SP-12]] · [[E-mu SP-1200]] · [[Akai MPC60]] (hip hop sampling from '85 on)
- **1990s** — [[Akai MPC60]] · [[Akai MPC3000]] (boom bap's spine), [[Akai MPC2000]] (the bedroom MPC), [[E-mu SP-1200]] (into French house — "Da Funk"), [[Roland TR-909]] · [[Roland TB-303]] · [[Roland SH-101]] (rave/techno/acid), [[Roland JD-800]] (rave piano, trance leads), [[Roland R-8]] · [[Yamaha RY30]] · [[Alesis SR-16]] (the workhorses), [[Roland Juno-106]] (house staple)
- **2000s→now** — [[Roland TR-808]] (trap's bass instrument, *808s & Heartbreak*), [[Akai MPC2000]] (2000s beat culture), [[LinnDrum]] · [[Simmons SDS-V]] (synthwave revival kits), [[Roland Juno-106]] · [[Roland Juno-6 - Juno-60]] (Tame Impala, bedroom pop, modern house), [[Sequential Prophet-5]] (Radiohead's "Everything in Its Right Place" and the modern-studio staple lane), [[Roland TR-505]] (lo-fi indie cult box)

## By genre
- **Hip hop / trap** ([[Hip Hop - Trap]], [[Rap Subgenres]]) — [[Roland TR-808]] (the low end), [[E-mu SP-1200]] · [[E-mu SP-12]] (boom-bap chops), [[Akai MPC60]] · [[Akai MPC3000]] · [[Akai MPC2000]] (swing + pads across three generations), [[Oberheim DMX]] · [[Oberheim DX]] (old school), [[Minimoog Model D]] (G-funk lead vocabulary)
- **House / techno / EDM** ([[House - EDM]], [[EDM Subgenres]]) — [[Roland TR-909]] (kick/hats/clap), [[Roland TB-303]] (acid itself) with [[Roland TR-707]] · [[Roland TR-606]] (the acid rig), [[Roland SH-101]] (the 90s bassline), [[Roland JD-800]] (rave piano/trance leads), [[Roland Juno-106]] (chord stabs), [[Roland TR-808]] (electro lineage), [[E-mu SP-1200]] (filtered French house)
- **Synthwave / 80s pop** ([[Synthwave]], [[Dance Pop (Dua Lipa, Charli XCX)]]) — [[LinnDrum]] (the snare), [[Linn LM-1]], [[Simmons SDS-V]] (the tom fill), [[Roland Juno-106]] · [[Roland Juno-6 - Juno-60]] (chorus beds), [[Roland Jupiter-8]] (the luxury arp), [[Roland SH-101]] (16th-note bass engine), [[Roland JX-3P - JX-8P]] (VHS pads), [[Oberheim DMX]], [[E-mu Drumulator]]
- **Psych/bedroom/indie** ([[Psych-Pop (Tame Impala)]], [[Bedroom Pop - Indie]]) — [[Roland Juno-106]] (Parker pads/bass), [[Roland CR-78]] (quiet-drama rhythm), [[Roland TR-505]] · [[Casio RZ-1]] (cheap-and-charming kit/crunch)
- **Neo-soul / lo-fi** ([[Neo-Soul - Lo-fi]]) — [[Akai MPC3000]] (Dilla time itself), [[Akai MPC60]] (the swing lineage), [[E-mu SP-1200]] (dust), [[Maestro Rhythm King]] (the 1971 blueprint)
- **Dancehall / digital reggae** — [[Roland TR-626]] (tuned-tom riddims)
- **Film score / ambient** — [[Sequential Prophet-5]] (Carpenter's house sound), [[Sequential Prophet VS]] (morphing texture beds), [[Roland D-50]] (late-80s underscore gloss)
- **Funk / prog / fusion** — [[Minimoog Model D]] (Worrell, Kraftwerk, Floyd)
