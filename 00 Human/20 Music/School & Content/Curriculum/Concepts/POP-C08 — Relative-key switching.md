# POP-C08 — Relative-key switching

**Q.** A chorus switches from C major to A minor. What's the relationship, and what does the switch do?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Relative major and minor — they share a key signature.** Same seven notes, different tonal centre. So the section darkens **without changing the available notes** and without any modulation machinery.

## Why it matters

'Relative minor' and 'happy to sad' are the easy half; the mechanism is the one that makes it usable. The same-notes point is what makes it a free move: no pivot chord, no new material, just a different resolution target.

## Drill

Take a loop in C. Restart it on Am and end it there. Nothing else changes — name what happened.
