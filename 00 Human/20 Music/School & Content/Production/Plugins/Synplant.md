# Synplant

![[Images/Synplant.png]]
*Vendor's current UI (Synplant 2 shown — the vendor's product page for the original Synplant has been fully replaced by Synplant 2).*

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Sonic Charge

## What it is

Sonic Charge's synth that uses a genetic-algorithm 'seed' system to grow new patches from an analyzed source sound, alongside standard subtractive synthesis.

## What it's used for

Quickly generating new synth patches by 'planting' the character of a reference sound, or standard subtractive synth duty.

## FX chain notes

Used as the instrument source at the top of an instrument chain.
