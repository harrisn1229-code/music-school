# BLU-C06 — Blue notes and the blues scale

**Q.** What are the blue notes? Name the notes of the A blues scale.

> [!question]- Answer
> **Blue notes are the ♭3, ♭5 and ♭7**, played against a major or dominant background. **A blues scale: A C D E♭ E G** — minor pentatonic plus the ♭5, where the **♭5 is a passing tone, never a resting note**.

## Why it matters

A common confusion: the ♭5 forms a tritone with the root, but blue notes are the three flattened degrees, not an interval. Sitting on the ♭5 sounds wrong; passing through it between the 4th and 5th is the most characteristic sound in the vocabulary.

## Drill

Play the A blues scale ascending and descending. Then stop on the E♭ — hear why it's a passing note.
