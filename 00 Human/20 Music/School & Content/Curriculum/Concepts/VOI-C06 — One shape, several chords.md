# VOI-C06 — One shape, several chords

**Q.** What is a multifunctional voicing, and give the repeatable rule that turns a min7♭5 or half-diminished grip into an altered dominant?

> [!question]- Answer
> One memorised shape played off **a different chord tone of the target chord** each time — the fingering never changes, only which note you treat as the root. The rule: **play a min7♭5 or half-diminished shape off the ♭7 of a dominant and it reads as that dominant, altered.** Play the same family off the **3rd** of a dominant instead and it reads as a dominant 9.

## Why it matters

It is the efficiency argument for the whole strand: fewer shapes memorised, more chords covered. It is also what makes new voicings affordable mid-tune — you are not finding a grip for G7alt, you are re-pointing a grip you already own. The tritone-substitution version is the same move with the new root chosen a tritone away.

## Drill

15 min: take one min7♭5 shape. Play it as itself, then off the ♭7 of a dominant a minor 7th below (Dm7♭5 read as E7alt, say), then resolve to the I. Say out loud which chord it is each time.
