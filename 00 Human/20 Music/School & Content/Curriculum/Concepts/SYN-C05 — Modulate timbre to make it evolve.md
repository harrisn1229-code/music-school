# SYN-C05 — Modulate timbre to make it evolve

**Q.** You want a pad that evolves over eight bars. What do you modulate, and with what?

> [!question]- Answer
> A **slow LFO or a long envelope** on **filter cutoff or wavetable position** — the *timbre*, not the level. Layer modulators at different rates (an 8-bar LFO on cutoff, a 1–2 bar one on position) so nothing repeats obviously.

## Why it matters

'Use an LFO' with no target is the incomplete version. The rule: **modulate timbre, not level.** Level changes read as volume automation; timbre changes read as the sound being alive. Mixing envelopes with LFOs matters too — an LFO is periodic and eventually reveals its loop; an envelope fires once per note.

## Drill

On [[Wavetable]], hold one chord for 16 bars with two LFOs at different rates. Then remove the position LFO and hear how much deader it gets.
