# Spectral Blur

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Ableton

## What it is

A spectral smearing/freeze effect that ships free in Ableton's **Creative Extensions** pack (installed separately from the base Suite install, but free for Suite owners) — described by dnksaus as "half stock" for that reason. Functions like [[Spectral Time]]'s Freeze section, but reads clearer/less smeared.

## What it's used for

Turning a vocal or any sustained source into an instant ambient texture layer — freeze at any point and it keeps sustaining as its own atmospheric bed, distinct from [[Spectral Time]]'s freeze in being noticeably cleaner/more defined.

## FX chain notes

Used as an insert on a vocal or lead layer specifically to generate a frozen atmosphere bed; the frozen result can sit under the dry source as its own texture layer rather than replacing it.

## Techniques

- **Freeze a vocal anywhere for an instant, clear atmosphere layer** — similar in principle to [[Spectral Time]]'s Freeze, but reads noticeably clearer; freeze at any point in a vocal take and it becomes a standalone atmospheric bed under the mix, still sustaining after the freeze is triggered. → [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]] — dnksaus, [The Underrated Spectral Stock Plugins in Ableton](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoYHYsrnjYo)
