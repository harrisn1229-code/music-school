# RHY-C05 — Half-time

**Q.** What is a half-time feel, and what happens to the backbeat?

> [!question]- Answer
> **The backbeat moves to beat 3** instead of 2 and 4, so the groove **feels half as fast at the same tempo**. A track written at 140 feels like ~70.

## Why it matters

It's the defining rhythmic fact of your primary genre. The ear judges groove speed almost entirely from snare placement, which is why this works. It's the trick that lets trap be fast enough for busy hi-hats and slow enough to rap over, simultaneously.

## Drill

Take any 140 BPM beat with a snare on 2 and 4. Move the snare to beat 3 only. Nothing else changes — listen to the tempo appear to halve.
