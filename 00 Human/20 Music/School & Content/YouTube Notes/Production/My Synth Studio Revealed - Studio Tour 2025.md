# My Synth Studio Revealed | Studio Tour 2025

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dsgxq8oPDxw)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dsgxq8oPDxw)

## Synopsis

A long, casual studio tour with Anthony's father — mostly gear-showcase rather than settings-level breakdown, but real workflow philosophy comes through: keyboards kept low/accessible (not stacked in tiers) so panels stay visible and reachable, every synth wired through its own pedal chain treated as an extension of the instrument, and a fully hardware, largely MIDI-less performance-and-print workflow.

## Techniques

- **Give different voices in a multi-voice poly synth genuinely different filter TYPES, not just different tunings, for real stereo dimension** — an Oberheim Four-Voice worked example: one voice hard-panned right through a highpass filter, another hard-panned left through a lowpass filter (plus additional voices at other octaves), A/B'd to show the highpass voice specifically is what adds "dimension" beyond a normal single-filter-type patch.
- **Print a part BOTH wet (full pedal/effects chain) and dry simultaneously**, specifically to preserve the option to change the effects treatment later — the closest a fully hardware, mostly-MIDI-less performance-and-print workflow gets to a DAW's non-destructive flexibility, since a live hardware take otherwise can't be undone or reprocessed.
- **A modified clavinet with an added Rhodes-tine-style mode, blendable with the stock clavinet pickup** — a hybrid instrument mod giving access to a unique tone neither source alone produces, demonstrated blending the two pickups to taste.
- **Every synth station wired through its own dedicated pedal chain** (filter, phaser, delay, reverb, bit-crush, distortion) treated as an inherent part of that instrument's voice, not an optional mix-stage add-on — described as inspiring the actual playing/writing in the moment (changing delay/reverb character changes what gets played next), matching the philosophy of performer-synthesists like Vangelis who built material around real-time expressive/effects interaction rather than programming first and processing after.
- **Keyboards deliberately kept low and unstacked** so the front panel stays visible and reachable for hands-on editing — a workflow/ergonomics choice, not an aesthetic one.
