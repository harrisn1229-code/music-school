# Ozone 9 Vintage EQ

![[Images/Ozone 9 Vintage EQ.png]]

**Category:** EQ
**Vendor:** iZotope

## What it is

Ozone 9's vintage-modeled EQ, emulating the character of classic analog EQ hardware (broader, musical curves) rather than clinical digital EQ.

## What it's used for

Musical, characterful tonal shaping on a master when a clean digital EQ feels too surgical.

## FX chain notes

Used as an alternative or complement to the standard Ozone Equalizer module.

## Techniques

- **This is the owned stand-in for the boutique analog-emulation curve behavior a video demos on the (unowned) Maag EQ4 — reach for it when a broad, tonal move needs to sound analog-modeled rather than digital-clean.** The lesson: broadband/tonal changes call for an analog-emulation EQ over a digital one, because the character comes from how closely the curve is modeled off real hardware, not from a display hiding what's changing (a frequency/curve analyzer on the Maag EQ4 showed its curves are mostly reproducible on a typical digital EQ — the one genuine exception being its reach into **supersonic frequencies** most digital EQs don't model). This module's own low-shelf/mid/high-shelf-with-Q layout (①–③) is the same broad-curve, musical-shaping approach — use it for overall tone, not a specific narrow fix (see [[Ozone 9 Equalizer]] for that half of the decision). Most analog-emulation EQs, this one included, default to natural phase. → [[EQ]], [[Master Chain]], [[Plugin Wishlist]] — Sage Audio, [How to EQ Your Master](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GF-XMVa3lW8)
