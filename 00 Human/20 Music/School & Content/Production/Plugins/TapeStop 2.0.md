# TapeStop 2.0

![[Images/TapeStop 2.0.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Max for Live

## What it is

A Max for Live effect that simulates the sound of a tape machine slowing to a stop (classic 'tape stop' transition effect).

## What it's used for

Tape-stop transition effects at drops, breakdowns, and outros.

## FX chain notes

Runs inline in the signal/MIDI chain at the point it's inserted; combines with whatever device follows.
