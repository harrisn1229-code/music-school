## Synopsis

A one-minute micro-lesson on the Hirajoshi scale, framed as a "Japanese variation" of the minor pentatonic that swaps two intervals for a more exotic color while staying playable in the same 2-note-per-string approach guitarists already use for minor pentatonic.

## What's taught

- **Formula: A minor triad (root, ♭3, 5th) + major 2nd + minor 6th** — i.e. take minor pentatonic (1 ♭3 4 5 ♭7) and replace the perfect 4th and minor 7th with a major 2nd and minor 6th (1 2 ♭3 5 ♭6). Matches the vault's existing Guitar Grimoire-sourced formula for Hirojoshi exactly (see [[Scale Modes Reference]]'s World & Exotic Scales table).
- **Framed as a drop-in upgrade for players who like minor pentatonic's 2-note-per-string fingering feel but want a more exotic sound** — not a from-scratch new system to learn, just a different 5-note formula played the same way.
