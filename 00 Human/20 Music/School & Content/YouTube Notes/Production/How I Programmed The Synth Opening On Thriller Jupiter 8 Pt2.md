# How I Programmed The Synth Opening On Michael Jackson's Thriller: Jupiter 8 Pt.2

## Synopsis

Part 2 of the Thriller opening breakdown — the Jupiter-8's job in the 4-layer stack (see companion Prophet-5 Pt.1 and Synclavier Pt.3) is specifically to supply sweeping RESONANCE, a distinct function from the Prophet's body/texture role. The clearest content here is a plain explanation of the resonance-vs-fundamental tradeoff on an analog filter.

## Techniques

- **High resonance trades away low-frequency fundamental for emphasis at the (sweeping) cutoff frequency — a direct circuit-design tradeoff, not a side effect to fight.** With resonance up, the filter emphasizes whatever frequency the cutoff currently sits at; as cutoff sweeps downward via envelope, the ear tracks that moving resonant peak — but the more resonance added, the thinner/less fundamental the tone gets underneath it. Explained plainly: "in order to bring out that tone... you would lose some of the low frequency" — worth knowing when a resonant sweep patch feels thin, since more resonance is directly the cause, not an unrelated problem.
- **A downward-sweeping filter (initial cutoff frequency set HIGH/open, then the envelope closes it down over the decay stage) as a distinct sweep direction from the more common "closed → opens on attack" pattern seen elsewhere on this channel** — used here specifically because a downward sweep is what gives this patch its "swirling"/resonant identity; decay time set to match the track's tempo (same discipline as this channel's other bass/lead patches), with sustain voltage kept nonzero so the tail doesn't disappear too fast.
- **Two oscillators in UNISON (same octave, not layered apart) both pulse-wave with matched sine-LFO PWM, purely for extra phasing/chorusing depth from doubling rather than from register spread** — a reminder that unison-doubling and octave-layering are two different tools for thickness, chosen based on what character (phasing vs. register spread) the patch actually needs.
- **Restates the 4-component composite-orchestration finding from Pt.1**: each layer (Prophet-5 = body/texture, Jupiter-8 = resonance, Synclavier = low-end support, real brass = punch/personality) does a job none of the others do — "these sounds sometimes all by themselves don't sound like what you think you're hearing... it's the sum of all the parts."
