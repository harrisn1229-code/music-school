# Keyscape — Clavinet C

**Instrument:** [[Keyscape]] · **Slot:** [[MIDS Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Electric Piano Track|Electric Piano Track]]

## What it's for
Funk comping — the Clavinet's percussive, filtered-pluck character. Unlike the Rhodes/Wurli entries here, this one lives in the [[MIDS Slot]] (rhythmic comping, not sustained harmony) per [[Sound Palette]].

## Settings
- ⑥ Patch: Clavinet C
- ② Custom controls page: the pickup-selector-style tone control is the character knob — brighter pickup positions cut through a busy arrangement, darker positions sit further back
- ⑤ Drive/EQ knob: a touch of modeled drive for extra funk bite, before any Ableton saturation downstream
- ④ Mechanical noise: leave at default — the key-click is part of the Clavinet's rhythmic identity, don't gate it out
- ⑧ Internal FX: reverb OFF always

## In context
- Rhythmic harmony/density role, same slot as comping keys, muted guitar riffs, and vocal chops ([[Sound Palette]]) — watch for masking against those when they're in the same section.
- Short, percussive envelope by nature — no attack/release shaping needed at the patch level.
