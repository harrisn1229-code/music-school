> [!danger]- 🔒 Answer key — do not open until you have answered every question
> **Q1.** **Stack thirds from the root — skip a scale degree, take the next one, repeat**
> One operation, applied as many times as you want notes.
>
> **Q2.** **Two thirds a triad · three a seventh chord · six a 13th**
> Each extra third adds one note, so a 13th chord is seven notes deep.
>
> **Q3.** **Tertian harmony**
> *Also accepted:* tertial harmony · tertiary harmony
> The common-practice default, not a law of nature — which is the point of question 9.
>
> **Q4.** **Major M3+m3 (4+3) · minor m3+M3 (3+4) · diminished m3+m3 (3+3) · augmented M3+M3 (4+4)**
> Quality is an order of two intervals, not a fact to memorise per chord.
>
> **Q5.** **C E G · C E♭ G · C E♭ G♭ · C E G♯**
> Major, minor, diminished, augmented, in that order.
>
> **Q6.** **The 3rd decides major against minor; the 5th decides diminished against augmented**
> Only the middle note moves between major and minor, which is why one half step flips the chord.
>
> **Q7.** **Secundal stacks 2nds · quartal stacks 4ths · quintal stacks 5ths**
> Secundal stacks read as tone clusters — timbral rather than harmonic.
>
> **Q8.** **It respells as the same notes as quartal harmony**
> Stacked 5ths are stacked 4ths re-voiced, so nothing new comes out of naming it separately.
>
> **Q9.** **The system has no honest name for it — quartal harmony does not fit the tertian convention**
> *Rubric (5):* says quartal harmony falls outside the tertian naming system (2) · notes that standard symbols tend to mislabel it, usually as sus4 (1) · concludes that chord names describe harmony built in thirds specifically, so a name is a construct rather than a natural category (2)
> Every symbol in the system encodes a distance from a stack of thirds — that is what "sus", "add", "♭5" and "13" all measure against. A stack of 4ths has no such reference point, so the nearest available symbol is a lie of convenience: sus4 says "a third was replaced", and nothing was. The useful conclusion is not that the system is broken but that it has a domain. Harmony exists whether or not a stack has an agreed label, and knowing where the labelling stops is what stops you hunting for a symbol that was never going to exist.
