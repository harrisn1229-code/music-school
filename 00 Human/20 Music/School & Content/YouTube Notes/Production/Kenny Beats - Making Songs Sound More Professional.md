# Kenny Beats — Making Songs Sound More Professional

![](https://youtu.be/EvQSoNnm0Us)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/EvQSoNnm0Us)

## Synopsis

Two minutes, one answer to "my songs sound empty/simple": A/B your track against professional records of the same genre **on your own speakers**, and ask specific gap questions instead of vague ones. The single habit that moved his music forward in real percentage jumps.

## Techniques

- **A/B against pro records on YOUR speakers, same genre/instrumentation** — live-band track vs your live-band track, dance vs dance (his Skrillex story: "why is it 20 times louder and 20 times clearer?"). Knowing your speakers on *other people's* music is what makes them trustworthy on yours. → [[Referencing and Translation]]
- **Ask specific gap questions, not "is mine as good"** — does my bass have enough sub/body? does the guitar feel thin — wrong recording, wrong guitar, wrong patch? is it a level thing, a sound-choice thing, or an arrangement thing? do I have too many things in the beat? Each answer is an actionable fix. → [[Referencing and Translation]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **The gap closes slowly, if you pay attention** — 5–10–15% jumps per record, from the comparison habit — not from any plugin. → mindset
