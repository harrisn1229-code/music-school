# Ian Kirkpatrick — Don't Start Now Chorus Lead Vocal

## Synopsis

The stream-source version of the chopped-lead-vocal walkthrough (same session as the [[Ian Kirkpatrick - HIT Vocal Chain|HIT Vocal Chain]] re-cut). The vocal ("don't come out…") gets its personality from **aggressive chopping** and getting **"smashed to bits" by the compressor**. Ian details the chop craft (no fade but cut at a zero-crossing so it won't click; ends before the snare), the automated LFO Tool duck at the drop, and the full insert chain. This page captures the extra detail the short re-cut trims.

## Techniques

- **Chop with no fade, but cut at a zero-crossing** — he doesn't fade the chops (auto-fades are on as a safety), but cuts land on zero-crossings so they don't click. The chop **ends before the snare** so you hear it stop. → [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]], [[1 Main Vocal Track]]
- **LFO Tool duck automated ON at the drop** — off in the build, switches on exactly when the chorus hits, "ducking the duck out of that vocal." → [[LFO Tool]], [[Sidechaining]], [[Automation]]
- **Insert chain (pre-fader):** slight **high boost** (intuition) → **Clean Sweep** EQ (removes 22–25 Hz sub junk so the compressor acts on audible signal, not "invisible bass") → **UAD 1176 "smash" (hold shift / all-buttons)** so it distorts a little as it maxes reduction → **responsible OTT** (light multiband up/down) → **Pultec**: boost highs **at 10k into the de-esser**, then de-ess. → [[EQ]], [[UAD UA 1176LN]], [[OTT]], [[UAD Pultec EQP-1A]], [[De-essing]]
- **Don't let the compressor pull up the breaths** — the chop happens *before* the compressor so it isn't dragging in every breath; personality = chop + smash, controlled. → [[Compression]], [[De-essing]]
- **Printed doubles (MicroShift), very low, from the demo** — kept the relaxed demo doubles low in the background. → [[Little MicroShift]], [[2 Backup Vocals Group]]
