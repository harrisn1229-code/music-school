# Ian Kirkpatrick — Make Your Kick Stand Out

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GqBnRpNiHrI)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GqBnRpNiHrI)

## Synopsis

Short stream clip on why he **splits the kick into separate layers** (body vs transient): late in a dense song, when the transient stops poking through, you can **raise just the transient** to keep the kick present — instead of turning the whole kick up and unbalancing the low end.

## Techniques

- **Split the kick into body + transient layers** — with the transient on its own track, you can push its level independently. → [[Layering and Stacking]], [[Kick Design]], [[TRANSIENT Slot]]
- **Late-song move: raise the transient, not the kick** — in a busy outro the transient gets buried; nudging just the transient layer up restores the kick's presence without adding low-end energy that would swamp the mix. → [[TRANSIENT Slot]], [[Masking and Carving]]
- **Why it works** — the ear locates the kick by its attack; preserving the transient keeps it "there" even when the sub is masked. → [[TRANSIENT Slot]], [[SUB Slot]]
