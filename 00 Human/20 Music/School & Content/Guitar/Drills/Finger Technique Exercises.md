# Finger Technique Exercises

Four exercises for finger independence. The instruction printed on page 1 is the whole design:

> **Only use your fretting hand for all exercises.**

No picking at all — every note is hammered on or pulled off. That isolates the left hand completely, so weakness shows up immediately instead of being masked by the pick.

- 11 pages, standard tuning, written at ♩=120 but start well under that
- Four numbered exercises, each drilling a different finger pairing
- Written for distorted guitar (`dist. guit.`) — the compression makes uneven notes audible, which is the point. Playing it clean hides exactly the flaws it's trying to surface.
- Play through [[Finger Technique Exercises.pdf]]

## How to run it

- This is the **warm-up** of the guitar set, not the workout. Six minutes before anything else.
- One exercise per session is fine. Rotating through all four across a week beats grinding all four daily.
- Because there's no picking, it can be done quietly on an unplugged electric — useful when a full practice session isn't possible.
