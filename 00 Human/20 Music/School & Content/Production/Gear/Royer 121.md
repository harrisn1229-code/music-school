# Royer 121

**Category:** Microphone (ribbon, figure-8/cardioid)
**Vendor:** Royer Labs

## What it is

The R-121, Royer's flagship modern ribbon mic — warm, full-bodied low end with a smooth, non-harsh top end; the go-to ribbon for guitar cabs and horns. Not currently owned — referenced as a comparison point to the owned [[Neumann TLM 102]] (both fill the "air/warmth" half of a two-mic amp blend, condenser vs. ribbon).

## What it's used for

Blended with a close dynamic mic on a guitar cab for a warmer, fuller companion voice to the dynamic's mid-rangey cut.

## Signal chain notes

🎬 *From video:* paired with [[Shure SM57]] on a mic'd amp — "the warmer, fuller sound of the Royer ribbon mic combined with the more focused mid-rangey harsher sound of the 57, which really cuts through the mix." Positioned by ear, not formula: a little off-center of the speaker cone, back an inch or two off the grille cloth — move it until it sounds good rather than starting from a fixed angle/distance rule. Fed into a pair of API mic preamps set as hot as possible without clipping. — Jack Ruch, [My Home Recording Setup for Guitar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KYTGFneEJ2A)
