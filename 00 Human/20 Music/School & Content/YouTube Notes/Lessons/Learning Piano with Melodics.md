## Synopsis

A straightforward product walkthrough of Melodics (a gamified keys/pads/drums practice app), following on from an Ionian-scale theory lesson the previous week — the point being that intellectual theory understanding needs to become finger-level intuition through repetition. Light on production technique but genuinely useful as a practice-methodology reference: Melodics' course/lesson structure (isolated technical drill → applying the same idea in a musical context), its rhythm-game-style performance scoring, and two practice-mode features worth knowing about for learning a hard passage — slowing the tempo down, and a "Wait Mode" that pauses progression until the correct note is actually played, rather than plowing ahead at a fixed tempo regardless of accuracy. Chords are labeled by name/inversion during lessons, reinforcing theory recognition alongside the physical playing.

## Techniques

- **Drill-then-apply lesson structure: isolated technical reps first, then the same concept inside an actual musical passage** — Melodics' Courses break a concept (e.g. a scale) into a short repetitions-based drill before immediately applying it inside a real chord progression/song excerpt, rather than teaching theory in the abstract or jumping straight to full performance.
- **Slow the tempo down, then gate progression on correctness (Wait Mode), for a genuinely hard passage** — Practice Mode lowers the BPM for an easier first pass; **Wait Mode** goes further and pauses the lesson entirely until the correct note is played, rather than continuing on a fixed clock regardless of accuracy — useful for actually learning a tricky chord change instead of just running it repeatedly at a speed that produces wrong notes.
- **Chord names/inversions labeled live during a lesson** (e.g. "C major 7, second inversion") — reinforces theory recognition alongside the physical fingering, rather than treating playing and theory as separate skills.
