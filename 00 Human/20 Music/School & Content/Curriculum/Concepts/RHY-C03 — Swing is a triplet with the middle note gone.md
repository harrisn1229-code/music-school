# RHY-C03 — Swing is a triplet with the middle note gone

**Q.** What does swing do to a pair of eighth notes, and how is a shuffle different from a triplet?

> [!question]- Answer
> Swing **lengthens the first eighth of each pair and shortens the second**, toward a triplet feel. A **triplet** divides the beat into three equal parts; a **shuffle** is that triplet with the **middle note removed** (long–short). Full triplet swing is exactly **2:1** — the long note takes two of the beat's three triplet parts and the short note takes one; swing sits anywhere between straight (1:1) and that.

## Why it matters

'Makes them late on the grid' describes the audible result of the second note only. The mechanism is the uneven long-short division — and the triplet relationship is what makes swing amount a continuous dial rather than an on/off switch.

## Drill

Set a swing preset to 0%, 50%, then maximum on a hat pattern. Then program an actual triplet and delete the middle note of each.
