# POP-C01 — Harmonic rhythm

**Q.** What is harmonic rhythm, and why does slow harmonic rhythm suit loop-based production?

> [!question]- Answer
> **How fast the chords change, independent of tempo.** Loop-based production wants it **slow** — one or two chords over several bars — because the loop repeats dozens of times and the interest comes from drums, sound design and vocals. Fast harmonic rhythm pulls attention to the changes and makes a 2-bar loop exhausting.

## Why it matters

It's the most important harmonic parameter in the music you make and the classical library doesn't cover it — harmonic rhythm appears in the vault only in [[Part-Writing]] and [[20th-Century Harmony]].

## Drill

Take a loop. Double its harmonic rhythm, then halve it. Which version survives 16 bars?
