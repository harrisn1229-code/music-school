# Semi-Chromatic No-Lift Finger-Angle Drill

A basic and an advanced fretting-angle exercise.

## Basic

Semi-chromatic pattern, frets 5-8 on every string, alternate picked, 4 notes per string. Don't lift a finger until it's needed again on the next string — this forces a proper perpendicular fretting angle, since a bad angle blocks/mutes the next string's note. Listen for unwanted note-overlap dissonance as the mistake signal, not just watching the hand. 5-15 minutes daily.

## Advanced

Two repeating one-note-per-string shapes, moved across neck positions, sweep-picked (some pull-offs). Not meant to sound musical — pure finger/angle refinement at speed. Nearly impossible to execute with a flying pinky or bad angle, so it self-corrects both.

Pure fretting-hand angle and finger-independence mechanics — no scale, mode or chord-tone knowledge involved.
