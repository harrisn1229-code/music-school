# Ozone 9 Vintage Tape

![[Images/Ozone 9 Vintage Tape.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** iZotope

## What it is

Ozone 9's vintage tape emulation module, modeling analog tape saturation, compression, and frequency response.

## What it's used for

Adding tape warmth and glue to a master for an analog-flavored final sound.

## FX chain notes

Used early-to-mid in the Ozone mastering chain, before EQ/dynamics finalize the tone.
