# Slap and Thump Guitar Technique

No prior home in the vault — created 2026-08-23 from a BERNTH lesson. Distinct from bass slap technique (no vault note yet for that either); registered via cross-link from [[Syncopation and Metric Displacement]] for the odd-meter angle.

## What it is

Bringing bass-style **slap and thump** percussive technique onto guitar for a rhythmic, percussive riff element rather than a purely melodic one. The uploader frames this as new/experimental territory for them personally — recommends **Tosin Abasi**'s material as the reference point for players who want to go deeper on guitar thumping specifically. Demoed on a 7-string in A standard, but stated to translate to a 6-string as well.

## Composing the riff: odd-meter groupings

The example riff is built in **5/8 and 7/8** measures — deliberately "one eighth note short" of the familiar 5/4 or 7/4 feel, which is what gives it its uneven, progressive character. Compositional method demoed step by step:
1. Start with a plain looping riff idea in a normal 4/4 feel.
2. Reduce it in size to fit a **group of 5** — immediately reads as more progressive/unusual.
3. Add a variation using a **group of 7**.
4. Experimenting with groupings and legato notes this way turns one starting idea into several usable riff variations.

Same odd-grouping-for-composition logic as [[Syncopation and Metric Displacement]]'s other odd-meter material — this is a percussive-technique-specific worked example of it.
