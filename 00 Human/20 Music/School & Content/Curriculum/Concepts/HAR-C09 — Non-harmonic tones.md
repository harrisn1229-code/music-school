# HAR-C09 — Non-harmonic tones

**Q.** What is a non-harmonic tone? Name the one approached by step and left by step in the same direction.

> [!question]- Answer
> A melody note **not in the chord underneath it**. The one approached and left by step in the same direction is a **passing tone**.

## Why it matters

Non-harmonic tones are most of what makes a melody sound like a melody rather than an arpeggio — the notes *between* the chord tones are where the line lives. Directly relevant to the MEL strand (20%).

## Drill

Take a melody you've written. Label every note chord-tone or non-harmonic. Count the ratio.
