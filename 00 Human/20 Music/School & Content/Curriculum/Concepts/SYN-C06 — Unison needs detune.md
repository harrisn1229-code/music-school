# SYN-C06 — Unison needs detune

**Q.** What do unison and detune do to a sound — and what happens with unison but no detune?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Unison** stacks multiple copies of the same oscillator; **detune** spreads them slightly apart in pitch. **Unison without detune does almost nothing** — identical copies just sum louder. The thickening comes entirely from the copies beating against each other.

## Why it matters

Detune gets the credit; unison is the one doing the work. Two costs worth remembering: wide detuned voices can partially cancel in **mono**, and detune below ~150 Hz makes low end wobbly — **keep subs and 808s mono with no detune**, and layer if you want width.

## Drill

On [[Serum 2]] or [[DUNE 3]]: unison 1 → 7 with detune at zero (nothing), then bring detune up. Then check it in mono.
