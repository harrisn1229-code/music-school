# 4-Minute Alternate Picking Shred Challenge (200 BPM Ladder)

A short daily play-along: an ascending 3-note pattern (×4) then a descending 3-note pattern (×4), then alternating between the two, run through tempo blocks climbing toward 200 BPM in straight 16th notes.

## How to run it

- Strict alternate picking throughout (no economy shortcuts) — down/up/down/up regardless of string change.
- Stay relaxed; a fully locked/tensed wrist is both a speed ceiling and an injury risk at this tempo.
- Log the tempo where you get stuck plus the date, come back the next day, and try to push past it — day-over-day overload, not a one-off run.
- Good finisher once basic wrist-unlocking work (see [[Directional Alternate Picking System]]) is already in place — this drill is pure speed ladder, no technique-building content of its own.

## Why `reinforces: []` is correct here

Pure alternate-picking speed on a fixed pattern — no scale, mode, or chord-tone knowledge involved. Per the Drill Index's drill-vs-routine test, this is a drill you can run on autopilot once the pattern is memorized.
