# Turnado

![[Images/Turnado.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Sugar Bytes

## What it is

Sugar Bytes' multi-effect performance plugin combining filters, distortion, delay, and glitch effects with an XY-pad-style live-performance interface.

## What it's used for

Live-performance-style glitch/mangling effects and creative transition/build-up sound design.

## FX chain notes

Used as a creative insert effect, often automated or performed live for drops and transitions.
