# The Music Theory Iceberg Explained

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmeogiNhdHI)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmeogiNhdHI)

## Synopsis

A 43-minute "iceberg meme" survey running through roughly 60 music theory concepts, ordered from universally-known (concert pitch, common time signatures) down to genuinely obscure (negative harmony, xenharmonic music, the pitch-equals-rhythm equivalence). A companion piece to Bennett's own tier-list video (also enriched from this batch, [[Music Theory Concepts Tier List]]) — same idea of a study-sequencing roadmap, but organized by obscurity/depth rather than by importance. This is the master record for the survey; the full per-concept breakdown lives on the new reference note it seeded, [[Music Theory Iceberg]], since a 60-concept flat list belongs on a dedicated page rather than duplicated here.

## Techniques

The complete level-by-level breakdown (surface → tip → under the surface → sinking deeper → daylight doesn't reach → ocean floor) is on [[Music Theory Iceberg]]. Highlights that had no existing home anywhere in the vault and got a first real writeup there: **negative harmony** (every chord has a "negative" mirror with identical tension/resolution weight, reached from the opposite voice-leading direction), **pitch = rhythm** (a pitch is a rhythm too fast to perceive as a pulse — demonstrated by slowing a note down into an audible beat, and speeding a 3-against-2 polyrhythm up into a justly-tuned perfect 5th), the **Shepard tone** and **Deutsch's scale illusion** (two genuine psychoacoustic illusions), **xenharmonic music** vs. ordinary microtonality (dividing the octave into something other than 12 entirely, vs. adding extra notes between a 12-tone base), and **irrational time signatures** like 7/12. Concepts that already had vault homes (Neapolitan Sixth, Augmented Sixth Chords, Altered Chords, Bebop Vocabulary, Modal Interchange, Cadences) are linked rather than re-explained.
