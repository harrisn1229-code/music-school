**Category:** Drive/Color (tape / lo-fi)
**Vendor:** SoundGhost

## What it is

SoundGhost's **tape / lo-fi character effect** — a tape emulation with saturation, wow & flutter, dropouts, hiss, and width, distinct from Ableton's stock 'Drift' synth.

## What it's used for

Adding tape warmth, age, and pitch-wobble character to any source — drums, keys, vocals, or a full bus — for a lo-fi, organic feel.

## FX chain notes

Used as a character/color effect, often late in a chain or on a bus for glue and vibe.
