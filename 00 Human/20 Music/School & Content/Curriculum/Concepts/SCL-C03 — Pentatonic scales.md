# SCL-C03 — Pentatonic scales

**Q.** Name the notes of C major pentatonic. Which degrees were removed, and why those two?

> [!question]- Answer
> **C D E G A.** The **4th and 7th** are removed. Those are exactly the notes that create half-step tension against the major triad — take them out and the scale is very hard to play wrong.

## Why it matters

You included an F. This matters more than one question: the major pentatonic is the most-played scale on guitar, and getting it wrong means it's been learned as a fretboard shape rather than as a set of notes.

## Drill

Spell major pentatonic from C, G and A. Then minor pentatonic from A and E. Name what was removed each time.
