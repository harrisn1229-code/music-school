# CHD-C01 — Chords are stacked thirds

**Q.** What single rule builds every chord in the standard system, and what do you get after two, three and four applications of it?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Stack thirds from the root** — skip a scale degree, take the next one, repeat. Two thirds gives a **triad** (1-3-5), three gives a **seventh chord** (1-3-5-7), and continuing gives the **9th, 11th and 13th**. The system is called **tertian** harmony.

## Why it matters

The whole chord vocabulary is one rule applied repeatedly, which is the difference between deriving a chord and memorising a table. Every chart in [[Chords]] is the same skip-a-note operation started on a different quality. It also sets the ceiling: six applications of the rule exhaust the diatonic notes, which is why 13 is the largest ordinary extension.

## Drill

From C, stack thirds all the way to a 13th and name the chord at each stage. Then start on A with a minor 3rd on the bottom and do it again.
