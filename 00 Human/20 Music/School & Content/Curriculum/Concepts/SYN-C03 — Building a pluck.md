# SYN-C03 — Building a pluck

**Q.** What ADSR settings make a plucky bass, and why does that produce a "pluck"?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Attack near zero, short decay, sustain at or near ZERO, short release.** All the energy is front-loaded and then gone — that's what 'pluck' means. Put the same envelope on the **filter** with a healthy envelope amount for the bright-then-dark snap that sells it.

## Why it matters

Attack and decay are the intuitive two; the other two matter more. **Sustain 0 is the pluck** — with sustain up, the note holds and it's a bass, not a pluck. And the filter-envelope half is what separates a synth patch from a volume shape.

## Drill

Build it on [[Analog]] from an initialized patch. Then raise sustain and hear it stop being a pluck.
