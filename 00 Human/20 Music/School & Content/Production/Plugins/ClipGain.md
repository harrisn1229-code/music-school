# ClipGain

**Category:** Utility
**Vendor:** Max for Live

## What it is

A Max for Live device for adjusting clip-level gain automation directly from the device rather than the clip envelope.

## What it's used for

Quick per-clip gain riding without opening the clip envelope view.

## FX chain notes

Runs inline in the signal/MIDI chain at the point it's inserted; combines with whatever device follows.
