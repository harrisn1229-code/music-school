# SCL-C04 — Parallel vs derivative mode thinking

**Q.** Two ways to think about D Dorian: "white notes from D" or "D minor with a raised 6th." What's the difference, and which is more useful when playing?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Derivative** = the mode as a rotation of a parent scale — fast, but keeps your ear anchored to the parent's tonic. **Parallel** = the mode as an alteration of major/minor **from the same root** — puts the characteristic note in front of you and keeps D as home. **Parallel is more useful for playing.**

## Why it matters

You described both views accurately without the vocabulary and couldn't say which to prefer. Parallel is also the view that makes spelling possible from any root, which is what the rest of this strand failed on.

## Drill

Describe G Mixolydian both ways. Then E Phrygian. Say which one you'd actually use mid-solo.
