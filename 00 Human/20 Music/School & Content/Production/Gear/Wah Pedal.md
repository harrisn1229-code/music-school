## What it is

Foot-rocker bandpass filter sweep — the vowel "wah" effect.

## What it's used for

Funk rhythm chops ([[MIDS Slot]]), expressive lead voicing, half-cocked "fixed wah" as a tone filter.

## Signal chain notes

First in the pedal chain, before distortion ([[Big Muff Pi]] / [[Boss DS-2]]) — wah into dirt is the classic order.
