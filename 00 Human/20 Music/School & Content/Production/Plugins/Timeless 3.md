# Timeless 3

![[Images/Timeless 3.png]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** FabFilter

## What it is

FabFilter's current creative delay plugin, adding new filter types, a redesigned modulation system, and expanded routing over Timeless 2.

## What it's used for

Advanced tempo-synced, filtered, and modulated delay effects — from subtle slap-back to complex evolving delay textures.

## FX chain notes

Used as a send-based delay effect, often paired with reverb sends for a full space design.
