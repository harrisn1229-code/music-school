# FabFilter One

![[Images/FabFilter One.png]]

**Category:** EQ
**Vendor:** FabFilter

## What it is

FabFilter's free single-knob low-pass filter plugin, designed as a minimal, CPU-light filtering tool.

## What it's used for

Fast low-pass filtering for simple tone-shaping or DJ-style filter sweeps.

## FX chain notes

Used as a simple filtering effect, often automated for build/breakdown sweeps.
