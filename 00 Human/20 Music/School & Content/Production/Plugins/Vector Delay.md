# Vector Delay

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Ableton (Inspired by Nature pack)

## What it is

A free Max for Live device from Ableton's "Inspired by Nature" pack (bundled with Live Suite) — a delay whose modulation is driven by a physics-style simulation (described as magnet/string-like behavior) on a 2D XY map, rather than a standard LFO, with multiple voices and its own recycle/feedback path.

## What it's used for

Delay modulation that behaves like a physical system rather than a repeating waveform — an organic, non-cyclical alternative to LFO-driven delay movement for sound design and texture layers.

## FX chain notes

Insert on a texture layer where LFO-driven delay modulation feels too mechanical/periodic; the physics-style behavior (bounce vs. wrap at the map's edges) gives a different modulation character each pass depending on where the simulated point is when it's triggered.

## Techniques

- **XY-map physics modulation with selectable edge behavior (bounce vs. wrap)** — add voices and route the output back into the delay's own **Recycle**/feedback path; the simulated point's position on the map (e.g. its Y-axis) can be mapped to pan, velocity, or gain, so movement across the map directly modulates those parameters rather than a fixed-rate LFO doing it. The map's edges can be set to **bounce** the point back or **wrap around** to the opposite side, which meaningfully changes the resulting modulation pattern — described as having "a lot of different possibilities" to explore. → [[Delay]] — dnksaus, [Ableton Delay Sound Design](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DCIEayyv1bk)
