## Synopsis

A one-trick short on FL Studio's humanize function: real drummers/performers are never perfectly on-grid, so nudging MIDI notes slightly off-grid (timing) and varying their velocity is what makes a programmed part read as human rather than "robot timing." Framed as safe to try on anything, drums included, with a quick note on the keyboard shortcut for repeating the last-used command.

## Techniques

- **A perfectly quantized part reads as robotic — deliberately de-quantize it.** FL Studio's humanize function slightly offsets note timing and (optionally) velocity automatically, simulating the natural imperfection of a real performance; demoed A/B as "robot timing" vs. humanized. → [[Rhythm and Groove]]
- **Manual alternative: nudge with Shift+Scroll** — for hand control instead of an automatic humanize pass, Shift+Scroll on selected notes in the playlist offsets them slightly off-center.
- **Don't be afraid to humanize drums, not just melodic parts** — imperfection in timing is "something we generally like," even on the rhythm section.
- **Repeat-last-command shortcut** — no dedicated key command exists for humanize specifically, but once applied once, Ctrl+L repeats the last-used command (fast way to reapply to the next selection).
