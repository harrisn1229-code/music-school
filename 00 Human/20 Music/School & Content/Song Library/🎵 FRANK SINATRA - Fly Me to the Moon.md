# Fly Me to the Moon — Frank Sinatra

> Jazz standard written by Bart Howard (1954); Sinatra's 1964 recording with the Count Basie Orchestra (arr. Quincy Jones) is the definitive version.


## Chords
**Key:** C major. The A section is a real book-standard **descending circle-of-fifths chain**: **Am7 (vi) → Dm7 (ii) → G7 (V) → Cmaj7 (I) → Fmaj7 (IV) → Bm7♭5 (iiø) → E7 (V7) → Am7 (vi)** — the first four chords are a plain **vi-ii-V-I**, and the tune keeps the same descending-by-fifths root motion straight through the turnaround by reinterpreting the IV chord's arrival as the launchpad for a **minor ii-V-i landing back on vi** (Bm7♭5-E7-Am7, treating Am momentarily as a local tonic) rather than resolving home to Cmaj7. Each root drops a fifth in turn except the Fmaj7→Bm7♭5 step, which is a tritone jump rather than a true fifth — the one deliberate "break" in an otherwise unbroken circle-of-fifths run.

The B section continues the same **ii-V-I** engine (per the tune's well-documented chart), consistent with this page's own "the whole tune walks around the Circle of Fifths" framing below. Only the A-section changes above are verified against a specific published chart here; the full AABA lead sheet isn't reproduced bar-for-bar.
