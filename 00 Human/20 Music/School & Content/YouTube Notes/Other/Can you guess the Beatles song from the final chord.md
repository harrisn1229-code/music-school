# Can you guess the Beatles song from the final chord?

## Synopsis

An entertainment-format quiz episode — Bennett plays just the isolated final chord of 15 Beatles songs (twice each) and guesses which song it's from, live-reacting to how much or little the isolated chord gives away. There is no theory instruction here: no chord names, no analysis, just song identification.

## What's taught

Nothing genuinely instructional — this is quiz/trivia content, not a lesson. Songs featured (for reference only): "In My Life," "Penny Lane," "Nowhere Man," "The Long and Winding Road," "Hello, Goodbye," "For No One," "I've Got a Feeling," "Good Night," "Rocky Raccoon," "Can't Buy Me Love," "Blue Jay Way," "Ob-La-Di, Ob-La-Da," "Here, There and Everywhere," plus two others. One mildly interesting observation Bennett makes in passing: "Here, There and Everywhere" begins and ends on the same chord with the same vocal harmony, which is why he recognized it instantly despite it being the last clip.
