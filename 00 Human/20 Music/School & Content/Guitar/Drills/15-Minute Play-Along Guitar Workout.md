# 15-Minute Play-Along Guitar Workout

A six-block daily play-along session (scrolling on-screen tabs, no fixed BPM ladder): Warm-up → Left-Hand Technique → Stretching → 3-Notes-Per-String Patterns → Hand Synchronization 1 → Hand Synchronization 2 → Speed Exercise.

## How to run it

- Play all six blocks back to back in one 15-minute sitting — the shape is the point, not any single block in isolation.
- Left-hand fingers stay close to the fretboard throughout; picking motion stays relaxed and economical, not full-arm.
- Log the tempo/point you got stuck at and try to beat it next session.
