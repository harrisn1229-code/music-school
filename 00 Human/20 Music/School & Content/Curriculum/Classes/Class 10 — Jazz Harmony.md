# Class 10 — Jazz Harmony

The idiom, taught on top of the grammar. Jazz gets its own class because it is **not fundamental** —
it is a shared vocabulary built over the harmony Classes 1–3 install, and it earns a block rather
than a corner of somebody else's.

## Why this class exists

[[School Config]] records that **JAZ was expanded from "dip a toe" to comprehensive on 2026-08-09**,
Nate's own call, and seventeen notes were written to back it — [[The ii-V-I]], [[Guide Tones]],
[[Rhythm Changes]], [[Tritone Substitution]], [[Bebop Vocabulary]], [[Modal Jazz]] and the rest.
**The notes landed and the syllabus never followed.** JAZ sat as a named strand with a full library,
no course and no concepts, until 2026-09-01. This class is that decision finally carried out.

**It came in at 7 lessons and 26 concepts** — nearly triple the original 4/10 estimate, because the sizing rule lets the material decide and `Theory/Jazz/` carries seventeen notes. That is the measure of how far the strand had outrun its syllabus.

It is deliberately **not** in [[Class 1 — Music Fundamentals]] — Nate's rule is that fundamentals go
in Class 1, and jazz is an idiom rather than a fundamental. It is also not folded into
[[Class 3 — Songwriting: Harmony & Melody]], which would have widened [[Theory Final Exam]] to cover
a subject that class was never about.

## What it assumes

- [[Class 1 — Music Fundamentals]] — specifically [[Course 02 — Chord Function]] for function, and
  **[[Course 29 — Chord Vocabulary]]** and **[[Course 30 — Voicings & Comping]]**, which is why
  Course 31 carries `depends_on: ["29", "30"]`. Jazz harmony is chord vocabulary moving fast; you
  cannot substitute a chord you cannot spell or voice.

## What closes it

- **[[Jazz Exam]]** — sat after [[Course 31 — Jazz Harmony]] completes, at `exam_pass: 80`.

## Where it pays off outside jazz

Nate produces hip hop, trap, EDM and pop while studying a jazz-first library. The transfer is real
and specific: **tritone substitution** and **reharmonisation** are how a four-chord loop stops
sounding like four chords, **guide tones** are the minimum voicing that still states a change, and
**modal jazz** is the theory behind a vamp that refuses to resolve. Where a device transfers, the
course says so.
