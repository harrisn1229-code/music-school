# Ian Kirkpatrick — SLDMML pt 1 (Tuning & Timing a Vocal)

## Synopsis

The most useful *vocal-editing* stream in the batch: Ian tunes and times a one-take Claudia Valentina demo (song "makes me laugh," co-written with Simon Wilcox) entirely in **Melodyne**, narrating his real technique — tune only what needs it, chop notes first, tune some notes deliberately hard, match doubles visually, and where to leave things alone. Then a tour of his drum clipping (StandardClip abuse), an air-back-after-de-ess trick, breath handling, and his **"set the scene / responsible panning"** dynamics philosophy. Part 1 of 2.

## Techniques — vocal tuning & timing (Melodyne)

- **Only tune the notes that need it** — blanket-tuning everything creates artifacts; **chop the vocal into note-pieces first**, snap to scale as a starter, then hand-correct. → [[Melodyne]], [[Pitch and Formant]], [[Dua Lipa Vocal Chain]]
- **Melodyne targets the *average* of the pitch line** — a "perfectly in tune" note sits in the middle of the wobble; that's why it sounds natural. → [[Melodyne]]
- **Tune some notes *super hard* on purpose** — slam a note to dead-center when you need to force it across or "throw people off," especially on fast vowels where you can't hear the artifact. → [[Melodyne]], [[Pitch and Formant]]
- **For artifact-sensitive material (vocal over guitar):** chop out *all* the little dips/scoops, then **tune only the prominent obvious notes** and leave the in-between glides untouched — minimizes audible artifacts. → [[Melodyne]]
- **Filter the track (Q3) while tuning** so you only hear the vocal (do it to the drums too). → [[Pro-Q 4]], [[Melodyne]]
- **Bind Melodyne keys; bind "to original"** to keep intentional sweeps/scoops natural while tuning around them. → [[Melodyne]]
- **Timing is trial-and-error and often the *previous* word's fault** — a word that sounds late is frequently the word *before* it being rushed; fix the neighbor. → [[Melodyne]], [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Melodyne "attack" tool** — shortens a note's onset (tightens the transient); experimental, use sparingly. → [[Melodyne]], [[TRANSIENT Slot]]
- **Melodyne time-handle to nudge/stretch** — select notes and move/stretch timing like a stretchy region. → [[Melodyne]]
- **Match doubles to the lead *visually*** — let doubles run through Auto-Tune (don't Melodyne them), then drag their timing to line up with the lead by eye. → [[Auto-Tune Pro]], [[Layering and Stacking]], [[2 Backup Vocals Group]]
- **"Selectively ignore things"** — don't obsess over every syllable; fix what distracts, leave the rest. Demos get "enough that nothing distracts from the song," full time+tune only "if there's paperwork." → [[Referencing and Translation]], [[Dua Lipa Vocal Chain]]
- **ARA hosting note** — Melodyne via ARA (Cubase/Logic/Pro Tools) is convenient but runs slower than standalone; work in **small sections** so a "select-all + tune" can't wreck earlier edits. → [[Melodyne]]

## Techniques — vocal FX & processing

- **Billie-Eilish pre-chorus doubles with Little AlterBoy** — copy the vocal twice, put **Little AlterBoy in Robot mode** on each, and set each instance to a **different formant** (not a different note) so they differ slightly; also pitched **+3 semitones** (a slightly "Arabian" color matching the Cashmere-style sample). Leave them not-100%-wet so a little dry bleeds. → [[Little AlterBoy]], [[Pitch and Formant]], [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]]
- **Add "air" back *after* de-essing** — post-de-esser, use an air/high-end exciter (name garbled — "Dopamine"?) built on the idea that transferring records to tape lost highs, so you'd boost highs into the tape machine; the **wet knob fades the brightened version back in** for a smoother top than the raw de-essed vocal. → [[De-essing]], [[EQ]], [[AIR Slot]]
- **Clean-sweep / Brainworx EQ post-compressor to cut sub-rumble** — take out the super-low end *after* the compressor with a very clean EQ, but **keep the body and the important consonants** (don't gut the "f"). → [[EQ]], [[Pro-Q 4]], [[Dua Lipa Vocal Chain]]
- **Breath handling: duplicate the vocal, keep only the breaths, high-pass hard** — put breaths on their own (or a breath track) with more low end removed, so heavy vocal processing doesn't inflate them; "two breaths like that will sell more records." → [[De-essing]], [[1 Main Vocal Track]]
- **Light Auto-Tune while working** — CPU-cheap and keeps things sounding good during the edit; the real tuning is Melodyne. → [[Auto-Tune Pro]]

## Techniques — instrument/drum processing

- **StandardClip abuse to control peaks** — boost a drum bus **+28 dB into StandardClip with the ceiling on** so it distorts hard but peaks at −3; "the GOAT of controlling your peaks." → [[Clipping and Loudness]], [[Limiting]], [[3 Drums Bus]]
- **Saturn in Destroy (bit-crush) mode + pitch-down** for a lo-fi "bit feel" on drums. → [[Saturn 2]], [[Saturation and Distortion]]
- **Pultec (UAD) to tame drum highs the analog way** — softens harsh top without a surgical cut. → [[UAD Pultec EQP-1A]], [[EQ]]
- **Handsome Audio Zulu (passive tape emulator)** — outboard tape softening on the mix bus for evaluation demos; if he wants to keep it, applies it to the individual stem (since he doesn't mix his own records). → [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[Referencing and Translation]]
- **Subdued sample as a "canvas" for the vocal** — the Cashmere hook sample gets its volume dropped and Valhalla Supermassive added so it becomes atmosphere the vocal sits on, not a competing part. → [[Reverb]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Masking and Carving]]

## Philosophy — "set the scene / responsible panning"

- **Define the frequency + stereo scene, then blow it open** — keep the high end attenuated and everything **panned ~50%** through the verse; the listener's ear sets its "this is how wide/bright this song is" baseline. In the hook, **introduce higher frequencies and pan to 100%** and it feels huge *because you waited*. → [[Width and Stereo]], [[Depth and Space]], [[Stereo Field]]
- **Don't "blow your load" up front** — demos with vocals panned 100% L/R from bar one have already spent an important dynamic tool. "#responsible panning." → [[Width and Stereo]]
- **One take, written weird** — Claudia read the written lines, then freestyled melodies over many takes, spliced into a "Frankenstein" comp. The song was written for fun in ~20 minutes. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
