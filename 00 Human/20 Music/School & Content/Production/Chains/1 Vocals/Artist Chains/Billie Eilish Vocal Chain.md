# Billie Eilish Vocal Chain

**Reference/vibe:** ASMR-close whisper singing over enormous negative space — the quietest vocal presented the loudest. The technique is 80% **capture + editing**: sing genuinely soft inches from the mic, comp obsessively, crossfade between every word to kill the noise floor, then stack quiet harmonies until the whisper becomes a choir.
**Signal source:** [[Neumann TLM 102]] (her mic is the TLM 103 — same family), often **handheld**, 1–3" away. Bedroom, not booth.

## Step 1 — Record (UAD Console)
1. **UAD Neve 1073 (Unison)** — clean, generous gain (whispers need it; watch the noise floor)
2. **UAD Teletronix LA-2A** — 1–2 dB
3. **UAD Pultec EQP-1A** — bypass
- **The real step 1 is takes + comping:** many full takes, something usable in each; Finneas: *"crossfade into silence between every word she sings"* — the between-word editing IS the noise-floor control. ([[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]] Step 1.5 discipline, doubled.)

## Chain overview
Comp/edit → de-pop the proximity → gentle level → de-ess soft → intimate space. Every stage tiny — the whisper must never sound *processed*, only impossibly close.

## Step 2 — Process (Ableton)

### 1. [[Pro-Q 4]] — Proximity manage
- HPF 60–80 · **tame the proximity boom 150–250 Hz** dynamically (handheld distance varies) · zero air boost — her top end is breath already.

### 2. [[Pro-C 2]] — Even the whisper
- Vocal style, 3–4 dB, slow-ish release — whispers have tiny dynamics; this is about consistency, not control.

### 3. [[Pro-DS]] — Soft-S
- Whisper sibilance is huge relative to tone — range 6–8 dB but *listen*: S's are part of the ASMR aesthetic; tame, don't remove.

### 4. [[Saturn 2]] — Warmth
- Warm tape, mix 10–20% — body for a voice with almost no chest.

## Step 3 — Effects (sends)
- **Space as an instrument, per song:** dead dry ("bad guy") vs cathedral ("when the party's over" — [[Reverb Send Long]] hot, ducked). Pick one identity per song, don't blend.
- **Harmony stacks are the sound:** quiet 3–5 part harmonies ([[1 Harmony Vocal Track|Harmony Vocal Track]] rules but *softer than the lead*), sometimes pitched layers ([[Little AlterBoy]] octave-down whisper tucked).
- [[Delay Send Slap]] rarely; [[Width Send]] on stack blooms.

## Vocal arrangement

| Section | Lead | Behind it |
|---|---|---|
| Verse | one whisper, dead center, **no double** | nothing — the negative space is the arrangement |
| Pre / lift | same whisper | a single quiet harmony a third up |
| Hook | whisper, unchanged | 6–10 whispered harmony layers, all low |
| Ad-libs | breaths, consonants, mouth noise, panned wide |

**The move — the choir is built from whispers, never from volume.** Every layer is tracked at the same soft dynamic inches off the mic; the hook gets bigger by *count*, not by anyone singing harder. The lead never changes across the whole song.

**Stack count: 1 → 10, all quiet.** The dynamic contrast is entirely arrangement, which is why the capture-and-edit discipline above matters so much — ten whispers only stack cleanly if each one's noise floor was crossfaded out.

## Notes / variations
- **Handheld capture confirmed directly by Finneas, from an early writing-session verse take:** Billie tracked a verse vocal on a TLM 103 while physically holding it in her hand, at a house Finneas lived in in Highland Park — not a booth, not on a stand. Corroborates this chain's "handheld, 1–3" away, bedroom not booth" signal-source note above from the writer/producer's own account. → [[Neumann TLM 102]] (owned equivalent), [[Vocal Mic Technique]] — Mix with the Masters (Finneas), [[Vocal production for Billie Eilish by Finneas]]
- **Gain-automate a naturally quiet ad-lib by a large, specific amount to make it pop, rather than re-recording it louder.** A laugh Billie did on that same verse take was a genuinely quiet, natural laugh they liked — instead of asking for a louder take, they rode the gain up **+27 dB** on that single moment "just so that it would pop out in the mix." A concrete, quantified example of the ad-lib treatment hierarchy: automation rescuing a good quiet performance instead of discarding it. → [[Automation]], [[Vocal Stacks and Ad-libs]], [[Gain Staging]] — Mix with the Masters (Finneas), [[Vocal production for Billie Eilish by Finneas]]
- **The loudness trick:** the vocal sits far above the sparse track — intimacy = vocal loud + arrangement quiet, never vocal effects.
- **Distorted lane ("you should see me in a crown"):** parallel [[Saturation Send]] under the whisper — clean center, dirty edges.
- Requires a *quiet room* more than any other chain here — the [[Shure SM57]] lane works when the TLM hears too much house.
