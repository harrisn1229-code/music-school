# kHs Transient Shaper

![[Images/kHs Transient Shaper.png]]

**Category:** Compressor/Limiter
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A transient-shaping snap-in with independent attack/sustain controls, similar in goal to FLUX BitterSweet.

## What it's used for

Adding or reducing punch and sustain on drums and percussive sources.

## FX chain notes

Stacked early in a Snap Heap chain, before compression.

## Key Parameters


*Tap a numbered circle for what that control does, the technique, and any artist example.*

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Attack | Boosts/softens the transient (attack) portion | + for punch on drums; − to tame spikes |
| ② | Pump | Adds a pumping/ducking character between transients | Add for an aggressive, breathing groove |
| ③ | Sustain | Lengthens/shortens the body/tail | − for tight; + for roomy |
| ④ | Speed | Detector / envelope reaction speed | Faster to catch sharp transients; slower for smoother shaping |
| ⑤ | Clip | Output clip stage (toggle) — catches the shaped peaks | On to keep boosted transients from overshooting |
| ⑥ | Sidechain | External sidechain source selector | Key the shaping off another track |
