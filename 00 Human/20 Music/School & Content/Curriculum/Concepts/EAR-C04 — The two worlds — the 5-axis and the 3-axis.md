# EAR-C04 — The two worlds — the 5-axis and the 3-axis

**Q.** Which scale degrees group into the "5 world" and which into the "3 world", and what does the split predict?

> [!question]- Answer
> **5 world: 1, 4, 5, 2** — clean, neutral, pure. **3 world: 3, 6, 7** — warm, glowing, sweet. The split predicts *where your mistakes will be*: most confusions happen **within** one family, not across it. Mishearing 3 for 6 is normal; mishearing 3 for 4 is not.

## Why it matters

It turns the 12 feeling states from a flat list into two shortlists. When you can't name a degree, you can usually still name its world — which halves the guess before you start deducing.

## Drill

Against a drone, play a random diatonic degree. Before naming it, say only "5 world" or "3 world". Check. Ten reps — score the world call, not the degree.
