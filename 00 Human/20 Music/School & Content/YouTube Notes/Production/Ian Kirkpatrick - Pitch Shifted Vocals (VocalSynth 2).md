# Ian Kirkpatrick — Pitch Shifted Vocals (VocalSynth 2)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v84pkXjv5To)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v84pkXjv5To)

## Synopsis

Splice clip: using **VocalSynth 2 to add a lower octave to a vocal**. Ian drops VocalSynth on a vocal, sets it to **auto mode**, and turns on **Compuvox** to generate an octave-down layer — a fast way to fatten a vocal or reach notes the singer can't hit. He also names **Polyvox / Biovox** for the low or high octave.

## Techniques

- **Add a lower octave with Compuvox (auto mode)** — instant octave-down doubling to thicken a thin vocal, or to imply a low note the vocalist didn't sing. → [[Pitch and Formant]], [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]]
- **Polyvox / Biovox for the octave layers** — reach for these VocalSynth modules when you want a low octave for weight or a high octave for shimmer. → [[Pitch and Formant]]
- **Use it as a "twist," not the whole vocal** — a supporting octave to add interest on a section, blended under the real take. → [[Layering and Stacking]]
- **Ableton equivalent** — an octave-down double can be done with a Pitch/Shifter rack or **Little AlterBoy** (formant + pitch) since VocalSynth isn't owned. → [[Little AlterBoy]], [[Plugin Wishlist]]
