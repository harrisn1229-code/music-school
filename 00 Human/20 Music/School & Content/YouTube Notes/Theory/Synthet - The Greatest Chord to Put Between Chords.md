# Synthet - The Greatest Chord to Put Between Chords

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z7yLSapKKg4)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z7yLSapKKg4)

## Synopsis

A one-idea short from Synthet's chord-trick series: instead of jumping straight from one diatonic chord to another (D to A), hold the first chord's shape but raise the bass note two scale steps toward the target — the resulting slash chord (D/E) has a "yearning" pull because its bass note isn't a tone of the chord above it. Synthet frames it as a cheap, ear-first trick (you don't need to know it analyzes as a rootless E11) and generalizes it to any "step down a fourth" move, e.g. F → C via F/G.

## What's taught

- **Bridge two chords with a non-chord bass tone** — hold the *first* chord's voicing, raise only the bass note up two scale steps toward the target chord's root, land on the target a beat later (D → [D/E] → A). The pull comes from the bass note not belonging to the chord sitting above it — a "yearning," unresolved feel. → [[Chord Inversions]], [[Chord Progressions]]
- **It analyzes as a rootless dominant, but you don't need the theory to use it** — D/E is functionally an E11 with omitted notes (E is A's 5th, so this is a rootless V-chord push into A) — Synthet's own framing is simpler: "same chord, just move the bass note." → [[Seventh Chords]], [[Extended Chords]]
- **Generalizes to any step-down-a-fourth resolution** — same device moving F → C: raise F's bass tone two steps for an F/G transition chord first. Works in any key. → [[Circle of Fifths]]
- **Works into minor targets too**, not just major-to-major moves — briefly demonstrated, no fixed formula given beyond "raise the bass, aim at the next root."
