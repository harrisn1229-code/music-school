# Funky Monks — Red Hot Chili Peppers

> Funk-rock track from Blood Sugar Sex Magik (1991). Targeted for funk/blues feel and hammer-on/pull-off skill building.

## Chords
**Key:** E · 92 BPM

The verse/intro is genuinely a one-chord funk vamp, not a progression — Frusciante's signature lick is a hammer-on/pull-off riff (A♯ and D hammered together on the G and B strings) worked around a single center, colored with the E blues scale and E minor pentatonic's ♭3 (the riff sits melodically on G, the ♭3 of E). No chord changes happen under it — it's rhythm and scale-tone bending doing all the work, the classic funk move of never resolving because there's nothing to resolve *from*.

The hook/chorus is where actual chord motion shows up: A5 – C5 – D♯5 – D5 – F7 – E → **IV – ♭VI – vii – ♭VII – ♭II7 – I**, a chromatic, non-diatonic power-chord slide (not a functional cadence — ♭II7 sliding into I is a tritone-flavored rock move, not a real Neapolitan resolution) that finally lands the riff back on the tonic E before the vamp resumes.

## Production & Arrangement
From *Blood Sugar Sex Magik* (1991) — Rick Rubin moved the band into a reputedly-haunted **mansion** and recorded them **live as a band in the room** for raw, spontaneous energy (the antithesis of grid-perfect production).

- **Flea's slap/funk bass** and **Frusciante's guitar** interlock live — the groove is a *band performance*, not a stacked arrangement.
- Roomy, live drum sound; minimal overdubs; the vibe of four people playing at once.

## Steal this
- **Track the rhythm section live** in one room for feel/interplay you can't program — commit to a band take.
- **Slap-bass as the lead groove** — let bass + drums be the hook, guitar answers.
- **Roomy, minimally-processed drums** ([[UAD Pure Plate Reverb]]/room verb, light comp) for live energy.
- **Vibe/environment matters:** an inspiring room can be the whole production.
