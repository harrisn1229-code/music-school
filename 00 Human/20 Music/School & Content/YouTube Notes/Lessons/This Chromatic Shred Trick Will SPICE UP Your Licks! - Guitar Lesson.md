## Synopsis

BERNTH teaches a loose, gap-filling chromatic-ornamentation device — dropping a chromatic passing note into select gaps of an otherwise-diatonic scale or arpeggio line — as a blues/jazz/fusion-borrowed alternative to either pure diatonic runs or pure chromatic-scale licks (the latter flagged directly as "not very musical").

## What's taught

Full extraction routed directly — see [[Enclosures Through the Scale]] for the complete breakdown and its comparison against that page's stricter enclosure algorithm: "fill in the holes" of a scalar or arpeggiated line with chromatic passing notes wherever adjacent scale tones sit more than a half step apart — applied selectively (one or two notes), not at every gap, and direction-agnostic (demoed ascending). The underlying mode/scale stays audible because most of the line remains diatonic. Worked on a D Dorian scale run (with a quick Dorian-vs-Aeolian comparison — the major 6th is Dorian's characteristic note) and on an A minor arpeggio with one chromatic passing note filling one gap.
