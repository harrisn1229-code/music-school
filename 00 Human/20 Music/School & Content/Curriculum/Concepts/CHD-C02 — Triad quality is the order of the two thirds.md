# CHD-C02 — Triad quality is the order of the two thirds

**Q.** Give the four triad qualities as an ordered pair of thirds, with half-step counts and a C example each.

> [!question]- Answer
> **Major** — M3 then m3 (4+3) — C E G. **Minor** — m3 then M3 (3+4) — C E♭ G. **Diminished** — m3 then m3 (3+3) — C E♭ G♭. **Augmented** — M3 then M3 (4+4) — C E G♯.

## Why it matters

Quality is not a fact to memorise chord by chord; it is two intervals in an order. Only the **middle** note separates major from minor, so raising it a half step flips the chord — the fastest way to change a chord's mood without rebuilding it. The 3rd decides major versus minor, the 5th decides diminished versus augmented, and the two symmetrical orders (3+3 and 4+4) are the two chords that will cause trouble later.

## Drill

Play a minor triad, raise only the middle note, hear it turn major. Then hold the outer notes and move the 5th instead to reach diminished and augmented from the same root.
