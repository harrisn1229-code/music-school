# Ian Kirkpatrick — "Don't Start Now" Master Chain

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nk1ECJzCiVA)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nk1ECJzCiVA)

## Synopsis

The actual "pre-master" bus chain Ian bounced "Don't Start Now" through before sending to Dua and the label — a light, restrained master. Notable that a chart pop record's producer bounce leans on **very gentle OTT + a touch of limiting**, keeping dynamics rather than crushing.

## Techniques

- **Image of the bass to mono** — narrow the low end to mono on the master for translation/power. → [[Width and Stereo]], [[Stereo Field]], [[SUB Slot]]
- **A *tiny* bit of OTT — ~22% depth, "50" (dry/wet), and NO upward** — "very little… not over-the-top at all"; multiband upward-downward glue used sparingly, upward disabled so it doesn't lift the noise floor. → [[OTT]], [[Multiband Compression]], [[Master Chain|8 Master]]
- **A little LT (limiter) — "because I like dynamic, call me old-fashioned"** — gentle limiting, deliberately leaving dynamics on the bounce. → [[Limiting]], [[Clipping and Loudness]]
- **This is a producer bounce, not the final master** — "not even what I sent to the label" was fully mastered; the point is a clean, dynamic pre-master with just mono-bass + light OTT + light limiter. → [[Referencing and Translation]]
