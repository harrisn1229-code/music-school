# 4-Notes-Per-String Stretch Workout

A daily 5-minute alternate-picking lick built on a deliberately wide 4-notes-per-string fretting-hand stretch — the stretch is the point: you can't fake correct fretting-hand form and still reach the notes.

## How to run it

- Support the thumb from **behind** the neck, never draped over the top — that's what makes the stretch reachable.
- Keep the picking wrist relaxed and moving even above 200 BPM; avoid locking into a stiff full-arm motion.
- Log the tempo you got stuck at and try to beat it next session (daily morning routine, per the source).
- Getting stuck at a plateau here is a signal to check underlying picking technique, not just to push harder.
