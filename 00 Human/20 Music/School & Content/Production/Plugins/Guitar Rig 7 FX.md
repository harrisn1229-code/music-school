# Guitar Rig 7 FX

![[Images/Guitar Rig 7 FX.jpg]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Native Instruments

## What it is

The effects-rack mode of Native Instruments' Guitar Rig 7, offering amp, cabinet, and stompbox-style effect components in a modular rack.

## What it's used for

Building custom guitar/bass tone chains from amps, cabs, and pedal-style effects.

## FX chain notes

Used as the amp/effects stage on a DI guitar or bass track.

## Techniques

- **The crunch/color stage on a Kontakt Scarbee Rickenbacker bass, run specifically "to make it really crunchy and stand out a bit more in the mix."** In a Rhodes-led soulful-sample build, this runs right after the bass instrument and ahead of a very small, all-mono spring reverb and a ~50 Hz low cut — crunch first, then a small amount of space, then clean up the low end. → [[Kontakt 7]], [[Bass Design]], [[BASS Slot]], [[Soulful Sample Stacking Chain]], [[Sound Selection Guide]] — loner, [use this easy technique to take your samples to the next level!? (making a soulful sample)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vN0fgGDxp6E)
- **Closest owned-lane stand-in for an AmpliTube "washed out" amp-sim pass on a Kontakt guitar-hit sample (both Guitar Rig 7 FX and AmpliTube are unowned here — GR7 runs in demo).** The source chain runs a Kontakt "Evolution Strawberry" guitar bank hit through AmpliTube specifically for a washed-out, degraded texture on a key sample-flip element (Webster Lewis "Open Up Your Eyes," under SZA's *SOS*) — Guitar Rig 7 FX is the same-role amp-sim tool already tracked in this collection; dial in a driven amp model + a duller/lo-fi cab or mic component rather than a clean tone. *(general starting point — no specific AmpliTube model named to match)* → [[Kontakt 7]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Plugin Wishlist]] — loner, [the secrets behind sza's 'sos' production!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qbkFwpExtVQ)
- **A wah preset applied to a synth part, not a guitar — a processing-type reused off its original instrument.** Jake One's *A Man* (Travis Scott)-style Oberheim SEM-flavored synth chords run through a Guitar Rig wah preset, borrowed directly from a different Jake One production (*Out of Time*, Future) where the same wah processing sits on guitar chords — the lesson stated on camera: there's no reason a processing move has to stay on the instrument it was invented for. → [[Oberheim OB-Xa]], [[Modulation]] — loner, [the secret samplemaker behind Southside's best beats!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WLOHe9CJChY)
- **Foundation of the "Jai Paul" guitar-sample chain** — funky patch + [[RC-20 Retro Color]] on top; Kenny's caveat: Guitar Rig presets feel thinner than Kemper/CLA — treat the patch as a starting point and add body after — Kenny Beats, [How to Make a Guitar Sample](https://youtu.be/i86bG2OsOSQ)

## Used in my chains

A **modular FX/amp rack** (build a signal path from components) — reached for whole-tone recall via presets rather than a single fixed slot:

- **[[1 Electric Guitar DI Track|Electric Guitar DI Track]]** — *Jai Paul lo-fi-funk chain*: a funky Guitar Rig patch → [[RC-20 Retro Color]] → widener ([[Doubler4]]/[[Width Send]]) for the instant lo-fi-funk loop tone (Kenny's guitar-sample move).

## Key Parameters

Modular — you assemble the rack, so the "parameters" are the components you add and the preset that recalls them:

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique | Artist / record example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Preset / Rack | The saved chain of components (whole tone recalled at once) | Start from a preset near the target, then tweak — faster than building from empty | Jai Paul funky patch |
| ② | Amp + Cabinet | Modeled amp head + speaker cab / mic component | Swap cab/mic for most of the tone change; the amp sets gain character | — |
| ③ | Stompbox components | Drive, fuzz, wah, comp, mod pedals dropped into the chain | Order matters — drive before mod before delay, like a real pedalboard | — |
| ④ | Modulation / Delay / Reverb | Time/mod effect components | A Flux-chorus (wobble down) + short room is the lo-fi-funk glue here | — |
| ⑤ | Modular / Control components | LFOs, envelopes, split/merge routing | Automate a component parameter for movement without printing it | — |
| ⑥ | Output / Master | Final level of the rack | Level-match to the DI so the A/B is tone, not loudness | — |
