# Ghz Lossy 3

![[Images/Ghz Lossy 3.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Goodhertz

## What it is

Goodhertz's Lossy plugin, simulating the sonic artifacts of lossy audio codecs (MP3, etc.) for creative lo-fi effect.

## What it's used for

Deliberately degrading audio to sound like a compressed/streamed lossy file, for lo-fi aesthetic or reference-checking how a mix survives lossy encoding.

## FX chain notes

Used as a creative effect insert, or temporarily on the master bus to preview lossy-codec translation.

## Techniques

- **"Packet Loss" preset run on BOTH a nylon-guitar chord bed and its doubling piano layer (identical processing on both) for a matched, degraded-codec vintage finish, plus a second pass driving the tremolo control on a subtle percussion layer.** Applied late in each melodic chain, after reverb/tape-color/compression/EQ — "gives it a little bit more of a vintage feel, takes away the quality of it." Separately, Lossy's tremolo control (not the main degradation section) was used purely for movement on two hi-hats + an open hat, alongside a band-pass EQ, kept deliberately subtle. This sample was later flipped into a platinum-certified song. → [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[Modulation]], [[Prof Jam Trouble Now Sample Chain]] — loner, [how i made the sample that got me a platinum song!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6aHjhniTOnk)
- **Run a vocal chop through Lossy for a "glitched-out" background texture, then layer a pitched-up loop on top.** Record a vocal chop with a melody you like → basic processing (pitch/reverb tool + compression) → Lossy (presets auditioned until one gave the right "old MP3/nostalgic phone" character) → layer a short segment of a separate texture loop pitched up two full octaves over the top. Inspired by the background vocal chops on Mk.gee's "How Many Miles." → [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]], [[Layering and Stacking]] — loner, [the secrets behind Mk.gee's HYPNOTIC production!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=969p5Q4JSZU)
