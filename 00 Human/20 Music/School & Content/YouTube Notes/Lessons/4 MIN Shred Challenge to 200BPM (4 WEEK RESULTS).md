# 4 MIN Shred Challenge to 200BPM (4 WEEK RESULTS)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=emTIuK7rscU)

## Synopsis

A short, structured alternate-picking speed challenge meant to be repeated daily over weeks: two three-note patterns (ascending, descending), alternated, played along at progressively faster tempo blocks up to 200 BPM 16th notes. The video is the drill itself — a play-along track with built-in tempo progression — plus a few form cues to keep it safe to push at speed.

## Techniques

- **The pattern:** an ascending 3-note pattern played 4 times, then a descending 3-note pattern played 4 times, then alternating between the two patterns for the remainder of each tempo block.
- **Progression:** the play-along track steps up through tempo blocks toward 200 BPM in straight 16th notes (or faster, at the player's own risk/ambition).
- **Form cues while pushing tempo:** stay relaxed, strictly alternate downstrokes/upstrokes (no picking-direction economy shortcuts here), and specifically avoid locking the wrist completely tense — described as both a technique-limiter and an injury risk at speed.
- **The practice-tracking mechanic:** log the tempo where you get stuck plus the date, come back the next day, and try to push past that same boundary — a simple daily overload/progression log rather than a one-off run-through.
- **Positioned as a follow-on** to a longer beginner-to-advanced 30-day alternate-picking progression (paid/Patreon content, not something to route here) — this 4-minute piece is the "finisher" once wrist-unlocking basics are in place.
