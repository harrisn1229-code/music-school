# DRM-C04 — Why one-click humanize fails on drums

**Q.** What does an algorithmic humanize actually do, and why does it read as worse than a quantized part?

> [!question]- Answer
> It scatters timing **randomly** within a threshold. A few hits land well by luck, but the whole reads as *badly played*, because a real drummer's deviations are **informed** — by feel, by groove, by experience — not statistically random. Work instead by playing the part by hand and adjusting after, or quantizing first and nudging manually.

## Why it matters

Random ≠ human is the core insight of the whole realism topic. It also explains why velocity randomisation *within a narrow band* works while timing randomisation doesn't: velocity variance really is close to random, timing variance is structural.

## Drill

Take a quantized 2-bar beat. Make a copy, run auto-humanize on it, hand-nudge the other. A/B blind and describe the difference in one sentence.
