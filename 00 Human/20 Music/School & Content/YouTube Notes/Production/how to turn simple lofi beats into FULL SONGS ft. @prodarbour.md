## Synopsis

The densest and most complete video in this batch — a full lo-fi song build ("Cloud Watching," with Arbor) covering instrument-vs-sample workflow, three free/cheap lo-fi coloring options, a real drum-variation toolkit (duplicate-and-reverb snares, randomized/late hi-hats, layered acoustic drums glued with modulation + OTT, a carefully-tuned Glue Compressor drum-bus pass), a genuinely valuable music-theory workflow for writing a B section (using Ableton's Scale device to reverse-engineer an idea's key, then building the diatonic triad ladder for new chords), and a guest segment from guitarist Arbor walking through his actual guitar signal chains — double-tracked arps, a reverb-vs-dry call-and-response layer, and a deliberately-tinny pitched-up melody via Complex Pro + Formant. Nearly every segment yields a concrete, reusable technique.

## Techniques

- **Write bass/keys as an instrument, not a looped sample, to escape the 4-bar loop trap** — MIDI parts on a real instrument can be extended and rewritten for a B section later; a sampled loop locks you to whatever bars were captured. On Ableton's own Bass pack, very-low-velocity notes trigger a distinct plucky/natural-harmonic articulation layer worth reactivating and boosting. → [[Bassline Writing]]
- **Three lo-fi coloring options, cheapest to richest**: a free Max for Live device (Color) for crackle/wobble/EQ shaping; Spitfire LABS' free Tape Orchestra pack for samples with lo-fi character already baked in; and Multiband + OTT stacked for extra "dusty" character on top of either. → [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **Duplicate a snare into a reverb-drenched twin, then alternate dry/wet hits for texture** — copy a snare to a new Drum Rack pad, drown it 100% wet in Hybrid Reverb's convolution side (Blend fully left) using an evocative built-in IR ("Springs"), then swap only some hits (e.g. every other one) to the wet pad instead of blanket-processing the whole pattern. → [[Snare Design]], [[Hybrid Reverb]]
- **Randomize hi-hat velocity + nudge the whole pattern late, for a sleepy lo-fi pocket** — lowered/randomized velocity plus a deliberate late timing offset on hi-hats specifically (not the whole kit) reads as a laid-back, half-asleep lo-fi groove. → [[Rhythm and Groove]]
- **Layer a real acoustic kit under the electronic drums, glued with Auto Pan's Tremolo preset + OTT** — an acoustic multi-pad kit stacked on the electronic beat, run through Auto Pan (Tremolo preset, faster Rate, chopped Shape) and OTT (~50% Amount, low end reduced) so the two kits meld into one texture instead of sitting apart. → [[Layering and Stacking]]
- **Drum-bus Glue Compressor tuned to preserve transients: Attack maxed, Release minimized, sidechain EQ to ignore the low end** — Attack all the way up lets kick/snare transients through before the compressor clamps; Release all the way down lets it fully recover before the next hit; if the kick still reads over-squashed, engage the sidechain EQ/high-pass so low end doesn't dominate the detector. Finish with Makeup gain and Soft Clip for extra glue character. → [[Glue Compressor]]
- **Mute/unmute pre-built drum layers per section for arrangement energy** — once several drum layers exist (electronic kit, reverb-alternated variant, layered acoustic kit), simply toggling which layers play per section creates distinct energy changes without new parts. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Find an unknown key with the Scale device (Fold view, trial-fit roots), then build the diatonic triad ladder for a B section** — open the idea in Fold view, try candidate roots in Scale until every note lights up in-scale, then triad every scale degree to see the full available chord palette — pick a chord type the A section didn't use for contrast, resolve back into a chord the A section shares for continuity. → [[Scale]], [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Guitar chain (Arbor): double-tracked arps/chords through Stark → Hybrid Reverb → Vibrato → Sketch Cassette → EQ** — the same signal chain run twice (left/right pan) for real stereo width, rather than one guitar duplicated and detuned. → [[Plugin Wishlist]], [[Bass Design]]
- **A reverb-only signal hard-panned against a dry DI, for a guitar "call and response" layer** — one channel carries a fully-wet reverb version of a guitar take, the other the plain dry DI, both panned hard opposite — the dry/wet contrast reads as two guitars trading phrases from one performance. → [[Depth and Space]]
- **Complex Pro warp + Formant pulled to zero, for a deliberately thin/tinny pitched-up guitar melody** — pitching a guitar up via Complex Pro and killing the Formant envelope isn't corrected for — it's kept as the desired character, distinctly "artificial"-sounding on purpose. → [[Pitch and Formant]]
- **A simpler amp-sim chain for a final "high guitar" harmony layer** — Amp + Cabinet on default settings into a heavy distortion stage (100% wet, high Drive) for a late-song string-like harmonizing element, contrasting with the earlier guitars' more elaborate chains.
