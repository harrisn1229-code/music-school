# kHs Resonator

![[Images/kHs Resonator.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A resonant filter-bank snap-in that imposes tuned, ringing resonances on a signal (physical-modeling-adjacent).

## What it's used for

Adding metallic, bell-like, or tuned resonant character to percussive or textural sounds.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain for creative sound design.
