# The Beatles just released their final song

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IE33NPyzGY4)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IE33NPyzGY4)

## Synopsis

A same-day news/history explainer on "Now and Then" (2023) — mostly narrative (why it took 53 years, the 1995 Anthology attempt that got shelved, George Harrison's shelved guitar work resurrected decades later) but with one genuinely notable production technique: the AI voice-isolation method used to make the noisy 1970s John Lennon demo usable.

## Techniques

- **AI stem/voice isolation to rescue an unusable noisy demo**: John's original piano-and-vocal home demo of "Now and Then" had the vocal buried under piano and a constant electrical hum — unusable by 1995 standards, unlike the "Real Love" and "Free as a Bird" demos which were workable. Peter Jackson's team, while restoring footage for the *Get Back* documentary, had already trained an AI model to recognize and isolate each Beatle's individual voice from old recordings (originally built for audio-cleanup on unused session footage). That same trained model was applied to the noisy "Now and Then" demo to **extract an isolated, clean John Lennon vocal track** — explicitly *not* AI-generated or AI-recreated vocals, but real vocal isolation/separation from an existing noisy recording, the same category of technique as modern stem-separation tools, just purpose-trained on a specific voice rather than a general model.
- **Archival-material reuse**: George Harrison's 1995 guitar takes for the song (recorded during the shelved first attempt, before the song was abandoned) were preserved and used directly in the final 2023 version — makes the track a genuine four-Beatles recording despite a nearly 30-year gap between Harrison's part and the rest.
