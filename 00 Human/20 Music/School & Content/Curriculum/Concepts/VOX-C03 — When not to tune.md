# VOX-C03 — When not to tune

**Q.** When should you *not* tune a vocal?

> [!question]- Answer
> **When the deviation is the expression** — blues and soul phrasing lives between the notes; bends, scoops and a note leaned into slightly flat are doing the emotional work. Also when the genre's identity is a human voice, when the singer is strong (fix the two wrong notes, not the take), and when it's already meant to be an effect. **Correct genuine mistakes, leave the rest.**

## Why it matters

2.5/5 — genre dependence was right; the core reason wasn't. Over-tuning is far more common and more damaging than under-tuning, because it's invisible while you're doing it and obvious a week later. Fast retune also flattens vibrato, which is often why the take was good.
