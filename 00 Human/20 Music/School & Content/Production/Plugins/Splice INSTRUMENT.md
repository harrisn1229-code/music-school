# Splice INSTRUMENT

![[Images/Splice INSTRUMENT.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Splice

## What it is

Splice's sample-playback instrument plugin for loading and playing Splice sounds/one-shots as a playable instrument.

## What it's used for

Turning Splice-sourced samples into playable instruments directly inside a track.

## FX chain notes

Used as the instrument source in a track's chain.

## Key Parameters

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Sample Playback | Loads + plays Splice samples as an instrument | Quick sample-to-instrument playback |
