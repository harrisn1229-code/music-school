# DS Snare

![[Images/DS Snare.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Ableton (Drum Synths pack, Max for Live)

## What it is

One voice from Ableton's Drum Synths pack — a synthesized snare generator combining tone and noise components with independent envelopes.

## What it's used for

Synthesized snare/clap-adjacent sounds for electronic beats, or as a layer under a sampled snare for extra snap.

## FX chain notes

Used as a drum-voice instrument in a Drum Rack; typically followed by a short transient-shaping compressor and a drum-bus saturation stage.
