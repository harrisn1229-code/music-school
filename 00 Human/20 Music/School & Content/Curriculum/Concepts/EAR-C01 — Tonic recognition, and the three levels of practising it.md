# EAR-C01 — Tonic recognition, and the three levels of practising it

**Q.** What is the tonic *as a feeling*, and what are the three escalating ways to practise finding it?

> [!question]- Answer
> Home — rest, completion, stability, **independent of the absolute pitch**. The same physical C feels nothing like itself as tonic of C major vs as scale degree 5 of F major. Practise it: **(1) scale-based** — play a scale and stop one note short, then *sing* the resolution before playing it. **(2) progression-based** — play a progression that stays in one key, stop on a non-tonic chord, sing the resolution. **(3) real-music-based** — flag the tonic chord every time it recurs in a song you know, then stop an *unheard* song at a random moment and sing the resolution before checking.

## Why it matters

Every one of your six progression questions came back blank, and all six of them start here. The ear already infers a tonic automatically — the training is becoming *consciously aware* of what it already does. Singing the resolution trains it harder than only listening for it, because producing the feeling locks it in.

## Drill

Play a C major scale and stop on B. Sing the resolution before you play it. Repeat in A minor, then in a key you don't normally play. Then do it once with a song you've never heard.
