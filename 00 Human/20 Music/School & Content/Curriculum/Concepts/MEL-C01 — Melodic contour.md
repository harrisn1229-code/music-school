# MEL-C01 — Melodic contour

**Q.** What is melodic contour, and what makes it strong?

> [!question]- Answer
> **The melody's overall shape over time** — how it rises and falls. A strong contour is **balanced**: what goes up comes back down, producing an arc with **one clear peak** rather than a flat or wandering line. Balance the *motion* too — after stepwise movement, take a leap.

## Why it matters

[[Melody Writing]] already defined it and covered balancing it. This was a retention gap, not a teaching gap. Constraining the range with an explicit top and bottom note before writing does most of the work of producing a shape at all.

## Drill

Draw the contour of three melodies you like as a single line. Find the peak in each.
