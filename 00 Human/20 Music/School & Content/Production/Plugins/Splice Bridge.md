# Splice Bridge

![[Images/Splice Bridge.png]]

**Category:** Utility
**Vendor:** Splice

## What it is

Splice's plugin-side connector that links the Splice desktop app's sample library directly into a DAW session.

## What it's used for

Drag-and-drop sample browsing/auditioning from the Splice library without leaving the DAW.

## FX chain notes

A browsing utility, not an audio-processing device in the signal chain.

## Key Parameters

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | App Connector | Links the Splice desktop app to the plugin | Support/connector utility |
