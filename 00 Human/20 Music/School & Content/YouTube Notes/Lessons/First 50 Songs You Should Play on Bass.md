## Synopsis

A repertoire-selection video, not a technique lesson: BassBuzz plays a rapid-fire montage of ~50 iconic bass riffs (roughly 14 seconds each, back-to-back, almost no spoken narration) as a curated "songs to build your skills on" list for beginner-to-intermediate bassists. The description frames the throughline explicitly — these 50 riffs, collectively, will teach syncopation, plucking speed and endurance, different time signatures, major/minor/blues scales, shifting, hammer-ons and pull-offs, bass slides, locking in with the kick drum, and "extreme bass face mastery" — but the video itself doesn't narrate *which* riff teaches *which* skill; that mapping isn't stated anywhere in the source. Because it's a montage with almost no host speech, the captions mostly transcribe the **sung lyrics of each song clip** rather than instruction, and they're heavily garbled the way music-heavy auto-captioning always is (see the transcript note below) — a handful of phrases are clean enough to identify the song playing at that moment, but most aren't.
