# Softly As in a Morning Sunrise — Sigmund Romberg

> A minor-key jazz standard that's almost a **minor 2-5-1 study** — the A sections are essentially nothing but `Cm(i) – Dm7♭5(ii°) – G7♭9(V7)` chained over and over, with a bridge that escapes to the relative major (E♭) before a final 2-5 walks back home. Learning it well transfers directly to any tune built from the same shape.

## Chords
- **A section:** `i – ii° – V7` repeated (`Cm – Dm7♭5 – G7♭9`), connected by a **passing diminished chord** a half step above the ii and a half step below the V (step-wise tension into G7). Second ending resolves via a 2-5 into the bridge.
- **Bridge:** modulates to **E♭ major**, the relative major of C minor (same key signature, three flats — E♭ sits a minor 3rd / three frets above the C-minor tonic). Runs `iv(Fm) – V7(B♭7) – I(E♭maj)` (a 2-5-1 into the relative major), then `VI7(C7) – iv° – V7(G7)` walking back to the top of the form. C7 is reached "off the major 6" of E♭ (a dominant 7th built on E♭'s 6th degree).
- **G7♭9** carries an implied **♭13** as well — a ♭9 dominant borrowed from harmonic minor conventionally carries the ♭13 too, even when the lead sheet only shows "♭9." → [[The ii-V-I]] (minor version) · [[Extended Chords]]

## Chord-scale map (as taught for guitar improvisation)
Two levels, presented as equally valid (not beginner vs. advanced):

**Complete** — a scale per harmonic zone:

| Zone | Scale |
|---|---|
| i (Cm7) | C natural minor |
| minor 2-5-1's dominant-tension zone | C harmonic minor |
| Bridge ii-V (E♭ major zone) | E♭ major |
| Passing F measure | F Dorian (same notes as E♭ major/C natural minor, re-rooted) |
| Passing diminished chord | F♯ diminished |
| V7 (C7♭9/♭13, the minor-2-5-1's dominant) | **F harmonic minor, rooted C-to-C = C Phrygian dominant** (harmonic minor's 5th mode) |

**Simplified** — three big regions: "basically C natural minor" for most of the A section (the ear adds the chromatic major-7 tone from harmonic minor by feel, not by mechanically switching scales), a real **F harmonic minor** region for the 2-bar zone that can't be faked as anything else, and **E♭ major** for the bridge.

The melody itself never plays the ♭7 (no B♭ anywhere) — since the only difference between C harmonic minor and C natural minor is B♭ vs. B♮, avoiding B♭ keeps the melody safely in C natural minor throughout. → [[Scale Modes Reference]] · [[Diatonic Modes]]

## Steal this
- **Prolongation across a whole tune, not just a turnaround.** The entire A section can be heard as one prolonged C-minor sonority — the ii-V motion is just "tension and back," not a real key change.
