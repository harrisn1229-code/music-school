# kHs Limiter

![[Images/kHs Limiter.png]]

**Category:** Compressor/Limiter
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A brickwall limiter snap-in for peak control within a modular chain.

## What it's used for

Final peak/loudness control within a Snap Heap chain.

## FX chain notes

Placed as the last snap-in in a chain.

## Techniques

- **Free stand-in for the tape→limiter "FL bump" master chain** — suggested (in Lophiile's chat) as the free alternative to Pro-L in his UAD-Ampex-into-limiter trick; pair with [[Kramer Tape]] or [[Cassette]] in front — Lophiile, [All About Basslines stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tnruvmw_OWc)
