# Yamaha FG730S

**Category:** Acoustic Guitar
**Vendor:** Yamaha
**Ballpark value (used, 2026):** $175–275

## What it is

Yamaha's solid-sitka-top FG-series dreadnought with rosewood back/sides — the workhorse tier of the best-selling acoustic guitar family ever made.

## What it's used for

Strummed beds, fingerpicked verses, songwriting; the acoustic layer in the [[Bedroom Pop - Indie]] lane.

## Signal chain notes

No pickup — records via the [[Neumann TLM 102]] (12th-fret/body blend position) or [[Shure SM57]] for a tighter, midrangey strum layer.
