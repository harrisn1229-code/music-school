# Echobode

![[Images/Echobode.png]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Sonic Charge

## What it is

Sonic Charge's combined delay and frequency-shifter effect, creating shifting, inharmonic echo textures.

## What it's used for

Metallic, evolving delay/frequency-shift textures for sound design.

## FX chain notes

Used as a send/insert effect for creative texture design.

## Used in my chains

My **frequency-shifter delay** — a Bode-style frequency shifter feeding a delay for metallic, clangy, inharmonic textures (not pitch-shift; it shifts every frequency by a fixed Hz, breaking harmonic relationships):

- **[[1 Drums FX Track|Drums FX Track]]** — *granular/inharmonic smears* (alongside [[Crystallizer]]): shift a drum hit into metallic clang, delay it into a texture. This track is the FX lane — go wild, then gain-stage back.
