# DS Cymbal

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Ableton (Drum Synths pack, Max for Live)

## What it is

One voice from Ableton's Drum Synths pack — a synthesized cymbal/crash generator using layered metallic noise with a long decay.

## What it's used for

Synthesized crash/cymbal accents for electronic drum programming.

## FX chain notes

Used as a drum-voice instrument in a Drum Rack; typically followed by a short transient-shaping compressor and a drum-bus saturation stage.
