# Max Instrument

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Ableton

## What it is

The blank Max for Live instrument device shell — a host for building or running custom-coded (Max/MSP) instrument devices inside Live.

## What it's used for

Running community or custom-built Max for Live instrument patches that don't ship as their own named device.

## FX chain notes

Functions as the instrument source in a chain; whatever is patched inside determines what follows it.
