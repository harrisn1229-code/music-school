# Corpus

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Ableton

## What it is

Ableton's stock physical-modeling resonator (Suite) — excites an incoming signal against a modeled resonant body (beam, marimba, membrane, plate, pipe, string, or tube) to impose tuned, ringing resonant character, distinct from a filter or EQ.

## What it's used for

Adding a physically-modeled resonant texture layer to drums, percussion, and sound-design elements — a stock alternative in the same territory as [[kHs Resonator]].

## FX chain notes

Inserted on a texture/layering track, usually macro-mapped as a rack so its resonant character can be dialed in fast; often layered under an existing drum hit rather than run as the whole sound.

## Techniques

- **Tune the resonator to the played note for sub-bass reinforcement, not texture** — a distinct role from the drum-texture use below: on a bass patch (Operator/Serum/Wavetable), tune Corpus's resonance to the *same fundamental frequency the bass note is playing* (e.g. ~156 Hz for D♯) so its physically-modeled resonance reinforces the sub rather than adding an unrelated ringing pitch — paired with two or three [[Saturator]] instances each tuned to the note (fundamental, then an octave up) for a "thick bass" starting point. A free, pre-tuned rack version of this exact recipe (every note already tuned) is available as a free download from the source video. → [[Bass Design]], [[Saturator]], [[SUB Slot]], [[BASS Slot]] — dnksaus, [Tighten Your Sub Bass with CORPUS (Not Clickbait) \[I Swear\]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=260MHJtu0pc)
- **Macro-map most controls except Width/L-R/Spread; keep Dry/Wet mapped** — for UK-style drum-pattern sound design, map nearly every relevant Corpus parameter to rack macros, skip the stereo-positioning controls (Width, left/right position, Spread), but do map Dry/Wet so the resonance can be blended in. An LFO on a mapped parameter adds a "splinky" modulated character; cap the Decay so a layered-in resonance doesn't ring out long enough to become annoying under the main hit. → [[Snare Design]] — dnksaus, [craft UNIQUE snares](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WeHN9GPokMA)
