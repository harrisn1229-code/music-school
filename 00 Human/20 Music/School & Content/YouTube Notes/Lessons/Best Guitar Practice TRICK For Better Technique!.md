## Techniques

- **The core trick:** record your own practice/playing footage, then review it afterward with a focus on **both hands** — while actively playing, attention is naturally consumed by execution, so mistakes in hand mechanics are easy to miss in the moment and much easier to spot on a recorded playback.
- **Framed as a viewpoint shift**, not a new drill: the same practice material looks different once you can watch your own technique from the outside rather than only feeling it from the inside.
- **Also mentioned as an existing coaching workflow**: students already send footage for outside feedback and analysis — this trick is the self-directed version of that same review process.
