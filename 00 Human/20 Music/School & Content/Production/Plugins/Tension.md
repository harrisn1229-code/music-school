# Tension

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Ableton

## What it is

Ableton's physical-modeling string synth (Live Suite) — simulates a vibrating string (material, stiffness, damping) excited by a pick/strike/bow model, rather than using recorded samples or subtractive/wavetable oscillators.

## What it's used for

Faking plucked or struck string-like instruments (guitar, harp, mallet-adjacent tones) from a modeled source, useful as a from-scratch alternative to sampled guitar libraries when a track needs a "real-ish" pluck without recording one.

## FX chain notes

Used as the instrument source, typically followed by an amp-sim/cabinet preset (to push a synthesized pluck toward reading as an actual guitar) plus modulation, reverb, and light distortion/lo-fi coloring to sell the illusion further.

## Techniques

- **A believable "fake acoustic/electric guitar" pluck: Tension → amp-sim preset → modulation → reverb → distortion, doubled and diverged L/R** — run Tension's plucked-string output through a guitar amp-sim preset (a clean rhythm/funk-style setting) for amp/cabinet color, add a phasing modulation stage and light reverb, then a saturation/distortion stage on top for character. Duplicate the finished chain onto two tracks, pan them slightly left and right, and — the distinguishing move — let the two copies diverge from each other partway through the arrangement (different automation, different processing) rather than staying identical duplicates, so the sound reads as two related-but-independent parts rather than one mono source split in two. → [[Width and Stereo]] — You Suck at Producing (Underbelly ft. Tyler Aaron), [Making Indie Rock in Ableton Live](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=prIrIHWWo70)
