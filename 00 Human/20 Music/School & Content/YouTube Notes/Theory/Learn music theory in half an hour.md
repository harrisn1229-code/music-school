# Learn music theory in half an hour.

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rgaTLrZGlk0)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rgaTLrZGlk0)

> [!info] Watch-only page — the whole syllabus in 31 minutes, from a producer
> Andrew Huang compresses notes, chords, keys, the number system, inversions, melody and rhythm into
> one sitting. It is not a substitute for Class 1 — it is the **map** you watch before Class 1, so
> the courses arrive as detail on a shape you have already seen rather than as a list of new things.

**He gets one term wrong on purpose-ish, and says so.** From his own description:

> It will forever haunt me that in this video I misremembered one bit of terminology — the chord
> voicings begin with **"root position"** before moving onto "first inversion" and so on.

Worth knowing before the inversions chapter, so the error does not get learned. But the sentence he
draws out of it is the reason this video is on the shelf at all:

> …understanding music — how it works and how you can use these tools to express yourself — is a
> separate and more important thing than knowing all the technical terms.

That is the correct framing for the whole school. The concept cards test terminology because
terminology is *testable*, not because it is the point.

**The number system is the half-hour's real payload.** Everything else here is covered better and
slower elsewhere on the shelf; thinking in numbers rather than letters is the shift that makes
[[Basics of Classical Harmony & Counterpoint]], the POP concepts and Course 09's scale-degree ear
work all turn out to be the same idea.
