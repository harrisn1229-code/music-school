# NOT-C08 — The 30-second chart scan

**Q.** You get 30 seconds with an unseen chart before you play it. What do you look at, and why?

> [!question]- Answer
> 1) **Key and time signature.** 2) **The roadmap** — repeats, endings, D.S., Coda. 3) **Range** — highest and lowest note, so you can pick a position containing the whole phrase. 4) **Rhythm hotspots** — tuplets, syncopation, dotted figures, counted silently. 5) **A tempo you can hold through the HARDEST bar.**

## Why it matters

1/5 — key signature counts, BPM isn't the time signature. Naming only key and time is the half everyone does and the half that doesn't prevent the derailment. The roadmap and the hardest-bar tempo are what actually save the read.

## Drill

Take an unseen line. Run the five-step scan out loud in 30 seconds, then play it once.
