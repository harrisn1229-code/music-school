# Frank Ocean Vocal Chain

**Reference/vibe:** bone-dry, dead-center, *breathing* — the anti-Travis. Dynamics kept (whispers stay whispers, belts stay belts), pitch edited by hand not snapped, and the production interest comes from **many differently-treated voice layers placed in 3D** rather than effects on one voice. Garage-recorded intimacy with the confidence to be nearly naked.
**Signal source:** [[Neumann TLM 102]] close (3–5"), a real room but not a dead booth. Performance-first: comp for *emotion*, not accuracy — the best crack wins.

## Step 1 — Record (UAD Console)
1. **UAD Neve 1073 (Unison)** — clean, low gain
2. **UAD Teletronix LA-2A** — 1–2 dB only (the dynamics ARE the record)
3. **UAD Pultec EQP-1A** — bypass or hair of 12 kHz

## Chain overview
Hand-tune → light shape → gentle 2:1 → placement layers. Less chain than any other style note — restraint is the technique.

## Step 2 — Process (Ableton)

### 1. Manual pitch (Melodyne-style — his documented tool)
- Hand-correct only the notes that *distract*; leave drift that carries feeling. No [[Auto-Tune Pro]] on the natural lane. (For the "Nikes" pitched voice: [[Little AlterBoy]] pitch +3–5 or formant-shifted, printed as its own track — a *character*, not a correction.)

### 2. [[Pro-Q 4]] — Light shape
- HPF 70–90 · small mud cut ~300 · presence untouched (closeness comes from the mic distance, not 4 kHz).

### 3. [[Pro-C 2]] — Preserve
- **2:1, slow attack, 1–3 dB** on peaks only — the documented "let it breathe" setting. Automate clip-gain per phrase instead of compressing deeper.

### 4. [[Saturn 2]] — Barely
- Tape, mix ~15% — analog rounding, nothing audible.

## Step 3 — Effects (placement, not wash)
- **Dry lead, dead center** — often literally no reverb send. Space comes from *other layers*:
  - Harmony layers panned wide with **different treatments each** (one dark + roomy, one pitched-up + dry, one whispered + close) — the Blonde signature 3D stack
  - A distant double: same line re-sung soft, 2–3 ft off mic, tucked −12 dB (real depth beats reverb)
- [[Delay Send Slap]] mono, low, for select phrases · [[Reverb Send Long]] only as an *event* on a single line, automated in and out.

## Vocal arrangement

| Section | Lead | Behind it |
|---|---|---|
| Verse | one voice, dry, dead center, dynamics intact | often literally nothing |
| Lift | same take | a pitched-down "alter ego" layer, or a telephone-EQ'd one |
| Hook | the fullest 3D placement — 3–5 layers | each layer treated *differently* and placed at a different depth |
| Outro | strips back to one, or to none | — |

**The move — layers differ by treatment, not by note.** This is the whole method and it's the opposite of a pop stack. Where Dua stacks four identical takes for width, Frank stacks four *differently-processed* voices — one clean, one pitched, one filtered, one drenched — and places them at different depths. The interest is spatial, not harmonic.

**Dynamics are kept.** Whispers stay whispers and belts stay belts, which means the arrangement can be sparse and still move. Don't compress the sections into one another.

**Stack count: 1 → 5, every one a different chain.**

## Notes / variations
- **The arrangement move:** strip everything under the best vocal moment (Self Control's ending) — the layers enter one at a time, each with its own space.
- **channel ORANGE lane:** more polish — normal pop chain ([[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]]) but keep the 2:1 dynamics philosophy.
- **Pitched-character lane ("Nikes"):** AlterBoy shifted voice AS the lead for a verse — commit, don't blend.
