# Nexus

![[Images/Nexus.png]]
*reFX's current Nexus UI (Nexus 5 shown).*

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** reFX

## What it is

reFX's rompler-style workstation synth built around a massive library of preset-based sounds spanning EDM, pop, and hip hop genres.

## What it's used for

Fast access to production-ready synth, pad, and lead sounds across mainstream electronic and pop genres.

## FX chain notes

Used as the instrument source, typically followed by EQ and light compression to fit presets into a mix.

## Techniques

- **"Tippy top" sparkle layer** — a bright Nexus patch as the last thin layer on a chorus stack; lesson learned when his Nexus install broke: print/bounce irreplaceable plugin layers to audio while they still load — Lophiile, [Paradise breakdown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L968H6x2sBY)

## Used in my chains

A **preset ROMpler** (instrument source) — already-produced sounds, so it usually **bypasses most of the track chain** below it:

- **[[1 Acoustic Piano Track|Acoustic Piano Track]]** — instant pop "stacked piano" (produced/layered already) → [[Nexus — Stacked Pop Piano]].
- **[[1 Synth Bed Track|Synth Bed Track]]** — instant produced pads (vs. [[Omnisphere]]/[[Wavetable]] where you build the motion yourself).

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique | Artist / record example |

| ⑤ | Arpeggiator | Built-in arp/sequencer | For instant rhythmic pluck/pad patterns synced to tempo | — |
| ⑥ | Reverb / Delay | Built-in space | Usually keep, since it's part of the finished preset (bypass the chain instead) | — |
