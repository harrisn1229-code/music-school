# DRM-C02 — Anatomy of a synth kick

**Q.** Name the four parts of a synthesised kick and a starting point for each.

> [!question]- Answer
> **Body/tone** — the pitched sine or triangle fundamental, tuned to the song root, amp decay ~200–450 ms. **Pitch envelope** — the downward "pew" that reads as punch, +12 to +36 semitones falling to zero over 15–40 ms. **Click/beater** — the attack transient, a short noise or HF layer under 10 ms. **Saturation** — light, so it reads on small speakers. Add a **Hold stage (~30–50 ms)** before Decay so the decay curve only ever shapes the tail and never eats the body.

## Why it matters

Every one of the four is a separate decision you lose access to with a fixed sample. Knowing which part is failing is what turns "this kick is weak" into a specific fix.

## Drill

Build a kick from a sine in Operator using only those four stages. Then A/B it against a sample you like and name which of the four differs.
