# CHD-C03 — Stack something other than thirds and the naming system stops

**Q.** Name the three non-tertian stacking families, and say what happens to chord names when you leave thirds behind.

> [!question]- Answer
> **Secundal** — stacked 2nds, i.e. tone clusters, timbral rather than harmonic. **Quartal** — stacked 4ths. **Quintal** — stacked 5ths, which respell as the same notes as quartal chords, so it is not a separate system. **Quartal harmony does not fit the tertian naming convention at all** — the standard symbols frequently mislabel it as sus4.

## Why it matters

The naming system is not a description of harmony, it is a description of harmony *built in thirds*. Once you know where the assumption sits you can tell a chord that has an awkward name from a chord that has no honest name at all — and stop hunting for a symbol that was never going to exist. Chord names are a human construct; harmony happens whether or not a stack has an agreed label.

## Drill

Play a quartal voicing and try to name it with a standard symbol. Notice which reading the symbol forces on you, and what it hides.
