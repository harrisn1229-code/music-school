# Model 84 — Juno Lead

**Instrument:** [[Model 84 Polyphonic Synthesizer]] · **Slot:** [[LEAD Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Synth Lead Track|Synth Lead Track]]

## What it's for
The Juno lane for warm analog mono leads — adds real BBD chorus for instant vintage width, the fastest route to an 80s/synthwave lead tone. Model 84's other recipes cover its BASS and BED roles ([[Model 84 — Juno Bass]], [[Model 84 — Juno Pad]]); this is the LEAD-slot patch, built from the same Key Parameters, not a published recipe on the instrument note.

## Settings
- ① DCO: saw + sub moderate, pulse off (saw carries the lead's harmonic edge)
- ③ HPF position 1 — trims mud, keeps the lead's body
- ④ VCF ~55–65% open, res ~25–30% — bright enough to cut through, not harsh
- ⑤ Env → VCF: light amount, fast attack — the note speaks immediately
- ⑥ **Chorus I or II ON** — this is the "instant vintage width" the chain note calls for
- ⑦ LFO delay 300–600 ms → pitch for vibrato that fades in on held notes (Step 1a universal lead rule: vibrato via mod, not baked in)
- ⑧ Unison mode off / mono — single-line lead, not a fat bass stack
- Kill content below ~100 Hz

## In context
- Chorus is doing double duty here as both tone and width — check the mono-fold ([[Stereo Field]]) since (unlike the bass patch) this one leans into it on purpose.
- Sits in the same synthwave/indie pocket as [[Drift — Warm Analog Mono Lead]]; pick by whether you want the real Juno chorus circuit (this) or Drift's cleaner modern voice.
