# EAR-C05 — Close in pitch is not close in feeling

**Q.** Arrange the degrees by feeling rather than by pitch. What does adjacency on that circle tell you, and what's the counter-intuitive case?

> [!question]- Answer
> Degrees **next to each other on the feeling-circle are the ones you'll mistake for one another** — 3/6, 3/7, ♭3/♭7. The counter-intuitive case: degrees a **half-step apart melodically** are usually *maximally far apart* by feeling. 5 (pure, stable, expansive) and ♯4 (bright, strange, alien, spiky) are one fret apart and about as opposite as the system gets.

## Why it matters

It's why interval-distance training doesn't transfer. The ear isn't measuring distance, it's recognising a role — so a training system organised by semitone count is organised on the wrong axis.

## Drill

Against a C drone, alternate G and F♯ and say what makes them opposite. Then alternate E and A and say what makes them easy to confuse.
