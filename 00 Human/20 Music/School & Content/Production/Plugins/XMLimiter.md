# XMLimiter

![[Images/XMLimiter.png]]

**Category:** Compressor/Limiter
**Vendor:** Xilentch

## What it is

Xilentch's brickwall limiter plugin for final peak control and loudness maximization.

## What it's used for

Final loudness maximization and peak safety on tracks, busses, or masters.

## FX chain notes

The last device in the chain, after all EQ, compression, and saturation.
