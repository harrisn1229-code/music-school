# Ultimate Major Scale Masterclass For Guitarists

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ydJR6cbif2Y)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ydJR6cbif2Y)

## Synopsis

A 7-level major-scale/diatonic-modes practice masterclass — the same format as [[Ultimate Harmonic Minor Scale Masterclass For Guitarists]] from earlier in this batch, applied to the major scale. The mode-construction theory (levels 1–2) duplicates the vault's existing, deeper [[Diatonic Modes]] reference; the genuinely new material is the leveled practice-drill progression, extracted into a standalone practice note.

## Techniques

- **Level 1 — the major scale, one shape.** Duplicate of existing coverage.
- **Level 2 — the seven modes, drilled per-mode across all 12 keys.** Same random-key-generator + stopwatch + metronome method as Routine 1 in [[The Five-Category Practice Method]] from earlier in this batch: generate a random key, play the one mode currently being drilled in that key, repeat across all 12 keys. Framed explicitly as training three things at once — the mode's own sound/shape, interval knowledge (relating the mode's notes back to the key), and fretboard knowledge (finding those notes cold).
- **Level 3 — all seven modes of a given key, played end to end as one continuous octave-spanning pass.** Random key generated, then play through Ionian→Dorian→Phrygian→Lydian→Mixolydian→Aeolian→Locrian (or wherever the mode sequence starts) back to the starting mode an octave up, starting as low on the neck as possible without open strings, timed with a stopwatch.
- **Level 4 — musical application: improvise over backing tracks using everything drilled so far.** The explicit checkpoint that isolated scale-running isn't the goal — application over real harmonic context is.
- **Level 5 — fix the fretboard position, cycle the key (the inverse of the position-per-key methods elsewhere in this batch).** Pick one fixed fretboard position (e.g. the 7th position) and, across all 12 randomly-generated keys, find and play whichever mode's shape happens to sit in that position for each key — the position stays constant, the key/mode identity changes each rep. **Contrast with [[Positional CAGED Soloing with Matched Modes and Arpeggios]] and [[Harmonic Minor Scale Practice Ladder]]'s position-mapping drills, which fix the key and walk through multiple positions** — this drill is the inverse: fix the position, walk through all 12 keys.
- **Level 6 — three-notes-per-string, all 12 keys, ~130 BPM.** Same three-notes-per-string mechanic as the harmonic minor ladder's Level 5/6, applied to the major scale's modes.
- **Level 7 — the chain exercise (same capstone drill as the harmonic minor ladder).** Pick a random key, improvise continuously across the whole fretboard without stopping, in a subdivision matched to skill level (16ths as the demo, 8ths/triplets/quarters all valid). Rated the single hardest and most effective scale-practice method — a real test of key knowledge rather than a fun improvising exercise (that's Level 4's role instead).
