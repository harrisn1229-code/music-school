# Impulse

![[Images/Impulse.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Ableton

## What it is

Ableton's simple one-shot sample playback drum instrument — eight slots ideal for quick drum sample loading with independent transpose, filter, and envelope per slot.

## What it's used for

Fast drum sampling and beat programming when Drum Rack's full complexity isn't needed; a lightweight legacy alternative to Simpler-in-a-Drum-Rack workflows.

## FX chain notes

Feeds directly into a drum bus; pairs well with Ableton's own Saturator and Glue Compressor for a quick punchy drum sound.
