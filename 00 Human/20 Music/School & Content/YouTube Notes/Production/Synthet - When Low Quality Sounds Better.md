# Synthet - When Low Quality Sounds Better

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K21NdON28jU)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K21NdON28jU)

## Synopsis

A bitcrusher/downsampling pitch covering three uses: recreating a specific degraded-playback character (referenced against a phone/app startup sound at different sample rates), adding character/"flavor" to an otherwise-clean sound, and — the most practically useful application — masking a genuinely bad recording (a guitar tracked too far from the mic) by making the degradation read as an intentional lo-fi choice rather than a mistake.

## Techniques

- **Bitcrush/downsample to recreate a specific degraded-playback reference** — reducing sample rate (and/or bit depth) as if the source were played through a worse playback device; demoed across several rate settings against an app-startup-sound reference to show the range from subtle to obviously "worse remote-speaker" character. → [[Saturation and Distortion]]
- **Bitcrushing as a flavor/spice tool on a clean sound** — even when not simulating a specific degraded source, light bitcrushing adds character to a plain pad or similar element.
- **Bitcrush to mask a bad recording as an intentional choice** — a guitar recorded too far from the mic (thin, distant-sounding) can be pushed through a bitcrusher so the degraded character reads as deliberate lo-fi styling rather than a recording mistake — a practical rescue move for otherwise-unusable takes. → [[Referencing and Translation]]
- **Automate the crush amount rather than leaving it static** — via Tools → Last Tweak → Create Automation Clip (same automation-clip workflow used elsewhere in this batch), so the degradation can ramp in/out rather than applying uniformly across a whole part.
- **Owned tool note:** the plugin demoed (Kilohearts' free bitcrusher) isn't in the collection, but the exact role is covered — [[Redux]] (bit-depth/sample-rate reduction with jitter) is the closest match; [[kHs Bitcrush]] and [[Decimort 2]] are the other owned bitcrush-family options.
