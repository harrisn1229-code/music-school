This is a direct **counter-example to hammer-on/pull-off technique**: the general rule (hammer-ons save picking-hand work and add tonal variety, per [[Hammer-Ons and Pull-Offs (Bass)]]'s punk-sourced content) doesn't apply here — sometimes full, deliberate plucking *is* the technique, specifically because of how much presence it holds in a busy mix. → [[Hammer-Ons and Pull-Offs (Bass)]] (added as a "when not to" counter-example alongside that note's existing "when to" content).
