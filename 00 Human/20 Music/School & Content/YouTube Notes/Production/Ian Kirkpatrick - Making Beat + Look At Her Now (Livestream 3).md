# Ian Kirkpatrick — Making Beat + Look At Her Now (Livestream 3)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DQ-ZeEyLKXY)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DQ-ZeEyLKXY)

## Synopsis

Half beat-making, half Look At Her Now teardown. The first half is drum sound-design (Addictive Drums, Battery from Capsun, Torque, a sidechain demo); the second returns to Look At Her Now's split kick, layered bridge, and the **iZotope Stutter Edit** behind the vocal chops. Confirms Reason runs as a VST/rack in his DAW.

## Techniques

- **Addictive Drums preset "Wool" (Fairfax bundle)** as a drum starting point, then customized. → [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[1 Drums Kick Track]]
- **Battery kit from Capsun "LoFi Love Songs"** — a favorite lo-fi drum source; then "fuck with the engine / envelopes" to push the samples further. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Torque for kick thump/harmonics** — Torque tracks a frequency and adds a weird harmonic while keeping the original, "adds a lot of thump" to a kick. → [[Kick Design]], [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[TRANSIENT Slot]]
- **Drum FX chain: Torque → Saturn → Ozone (kill highs) → compressor → OTT** — his default drum-channel stack. → [[Saturn 2]], [[OTT]], [[3 Drums Bus]]
- **StandardClip + Saturn on drums** — clip control + saturation (see [[Ian Kirkpatrick - SLDMML pt 1 (Tuning and Timing a Vocal)|the StandardClip abuse]]). → [[Clipping and Loudness]]
- **Two-layer sidechain demo: everything-but-drums ducked by the drums (FabFilter Pro-C2) *plus* LFO Tool ducking every half-bar** — a Pro-C2 keyed off the kick for the transient duck, and LFO Tool for a steady half-bar rhythmic gate on top. → [[Sidechaining]], [[LFO Tool]], [[Kick Design]]
- **Split kick: transient layer + sub layer = one kick** — "so I have control over how punchy the kick is vs how much sub"; you *can* do it with an envelope, but he layers. → [[Kick Design]], [[Layering and Stacking]], [[TRANSIENT Slot]], [[SUB Slot]]
- **Look At Her Now vocal chops = iZotope Stutter Edit (BT-designed)** — the glitchy vocal-chop stutters come from Stutter Edit. → [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]], [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **Reason as a VST instrument / rack effect inside the DAW** — "it makes amazing sounds, why not use it in your DAW"; a Reason sample of "Candle in the Wind" + image-damage + OTT as a texture. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Retrospective Record (Cubase) as the real capture method** — "I don't even record anymore; I fuck around and use retrospective record to grab everything I just played." → [[Automation]]
- **Bounce 7-layer parts down to 2 to commit** — the recurring commitment device. → [[Layering and Stacking]]
