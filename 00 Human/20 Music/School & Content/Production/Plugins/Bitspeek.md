# Bitspeek

![[Images/Bitspeek.png]]

**Category:** Pitch/Vocal
**Vendor:** Sonic Charge

## What it is

Sonic Charge's vocoder-esque speech-synthesis effect that imposes robotic, low-bitrate speech-codec character on an input signal.

## What it's used for

Robotic vocal effects and retro speech-synthesizer-style vocal processing.

## FX chain notes

Used as a creative vocal effect insert, often blended in parallel with the dry vocal.

## Techniques

- **A second, more-processed layer stacked behind an AlterBoy robot-mode static backing vocal, for an "even weirder" texture.** On nonsense-syllable backing vocals already run through [[Little AlterBoy]]'s robot mode (static, one-note, "not really harmonies"), a second layer runs through Bitspeek instead, pushing the texture further into robotic/vocoder territory as a contrast layer. → [[Little AlterBoy]], [[Pitch and Formant]] — Charli XCX / A.G. Cook / Finn Keane (Tape Notes), [[TN 185 Charli XCX, A. G. Cook, Finn Keane - Full Video Episode]]
