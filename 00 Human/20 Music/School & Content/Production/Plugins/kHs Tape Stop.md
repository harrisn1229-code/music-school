# kHs Tape Stop

![[Images/kHs Tape Stop.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A tape-stop simulation snap-in slowing playback to a halt for transition effects.

## What it's used for

Tape-stop transition effects at drops and section changes.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain, triggered/automated for transitions.
