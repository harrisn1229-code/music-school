# YouTube Notes Index

Every video note in `20 Music/YouTube Notes/` — **667 notes across 48 channels**, grouped by channel and by series. Transcripts are siblings named `… (transcript)` and are deliberately not listed here.

These are the vault's **undistilled source layer.** The lessons in them get pulled into the tool-agnostic pages by `/music-enrich` — [[Effects Index]] for processing, [[Synthesis Index]] for sound generation, [[Techniques Index]] for everything else. A note here that hasn't been enriched is knowledge the rest of the vault can't see.
