# 6 BAD Habits That KILL Your Guitar Progress!

## Synopsis

Six self-taught-player progress killers drawn from BERNTH's Patreon student community, spanning string-noise fixes, timing discipline, and a suggested music-theory learning order.

## Techniques

- **Reduce string noise: don't over-rely on palm muting alone.** Palm muting isn't the right choice for every song/riff/lick; add dedicated hammer-on/pull-off cleanliness workouts to the practice routine instead — improves both technique and string noise as a side effect, rather than depending on one muting method to cover every context.
- **Reduce fretting-hand pressure/tension specifically to cut string noise.** Named as a bigger-than-expected fix, especially for rock/metal players who cramp up as material gets fast — deliberately backing off grip pressure produces an immediately more fluid, less noisy sound; paired with minimizing unnecessary finger/wrist movement.
- **Record yourself practicing/playing to a click and analyze the takes for specific timing habits** — not just "use a metronome," but actually listening back to identify concrete personal patterns (rushing certain phrase types, inaccurate triplets, dragging on faster material). Framed as a daily habit from BERNTH's own studio/session work, not a one-off diagnostic.
- **Suggested theory-learning roadmap for a player starting from nothing**: fretboard note names first → interval names/sounds → chord and scale construction/how they connect → which chords belong in a key. Framed as the order BERNTH would take if starting over, not a rule that every step must be mastered before moving on.
