# FND-C01 — Enharmonic equivalents

**Q.** What does *enharmonic* mean? Give the enharmonic equivalent of C♯, F♭ and E♯.

> [!question]- Answer
> Two different names for the same pitch — same key, same fret, different spelling. **C♯ = D♭. F♭ = E. E♯ = F.**

## Why it matters

Which spelling is correct depends on the key and on which letter name the music needs next. F♭ and E♯ look wrong because there is no black key between E–F or B–C — those two pairs are the only white-key half steps, which is the same fact behind FND-17.

## Drill

Name the enharmonic of: C♯ · F♭ · E♯ · B♯ · G♭ · A♯. Which of those are white keys?
D♭, E, F, C, F♯, B♭.
white: E, F, C
