# BitterSweet

![[Images/BitterSweet.png]]

**Category:** Compressor/Limiter
**Vendor:** FLUX SE

## What it is

FLUX's transient shaper/designer plugin, offering independent control over a signal's attack and sustain/release phases.

## What it's used for

Adding or removing punch and sustain on drums, bass, and percussive elements independent of compression.

## FX chain notes

Typically used before or in parallel with compression, since it reshapes transients rather than controlling overall gain.
