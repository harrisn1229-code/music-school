# kHs Nonlinear Filter

![[Images/kHs Nonlinear Filter.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A filter snap-in incorporating nonlinear (saturating) behavior into the filter response itself.

## What it's used for

Filter tones with built-in harmonic drive/character rather than a clean filter.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain for combined filtering and coloration.
