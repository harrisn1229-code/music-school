## Synopsis

The Look At Her Now teardown (an early IG-live cut at the 1-hour limit): the song is "ear candy city," built on **intensely chopped vocals**, the **PadShop granular crash**, a Max-Martin-style **mono-low-end master**, and his standard vocal chain (Q3 → de-ess → Soothe → air-back). Whisper transcript, so some plugin names are approximate.

## Techniques

- **The "mm-mm" vocal chops** — the hook's chopped vocals are cut to bits with **envelope shaping** (he reached for a transient designer he didn't actually have, and did it by envelope instead). → [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]], [[Layering and Stacking]]
- **Vocal chop from a Splice loop in Ableton's Sampler, ping-ponging two tracks** — chopped a Splice loop with the Sampler; it alternates between two tracks with **different FX (MicroShift on one, off the other)** so the texture shifts back and forth. → [[Little MicroShift]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **The PadShop granular crash** — an acapella dropped into **PadShop** with randomized grains, turned into a crash/impact sound (his recurring favorite). → [[PadShop]], [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]]
- **Automate a whole track to mono at a moment** — a section snaps to mono for contrast. → [[Width and Stereo]], [[Automation]]
- **Master bus: OTT (zero upward, ~43% of 28%) → C4 → Ozone Imager → L2** — restrained multiband, then multiband comp, then imaging, then limiter. → [[OTT]], [[Multiband Compression]], [[Master Chain|8 Master]], [[Limiting]]
- **Max-Martin trick: make the low end more mono on the master** — "something I saw a lot of Max Martin's people doing and copied — it does cool things." → [[Width and Stereo]], [[Stereo Field]], [[SUB Slot]]
- **Vocal chain: Q3 (cut lows so the comp isn't fooled) → FabFilter Pro-DS → Soothe (taming low-end build-up) → a UAD EQ adding air ~12k after the de-ess** — de-ess first, then add the highs back for a smooth bright top. → [[Pro-Q 4]], [[De-essing]], [[Soothe2]], [[UAD Pultec EQP-1A]], [[AIR Slot]], [[Dua Lipa Vocal Chain]]
- **Oxide (UAD tape) on the master for saturation** — tape distortion for glue/character. → [[Saturation and Distortion]]
- **UAD low-latency toggle** — the little mic icon on UAD plugins switches to low-latency mode (more CPU) — useful while tracking. → [[Latency-Free Tracking]]
