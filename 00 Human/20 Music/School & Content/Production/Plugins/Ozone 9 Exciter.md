# Ozone 9 Exciter

![[Images/Ozone 9 Exciter.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** iZotope

## What it is

Ozone 9's harmonic exciter module, adding tube, tape, retro, and tape-style harmonic saturation with band-specific control.

## What it's used for

Adding brightness, warmth, or harmonic 'sparkle' to a master that feels dull or lifeless.

## FX chain notes

Used on the master bus after EQ, before final limiting.
