# Minimoog Model D

## What it is
The first compact, performance-ready synthesizer — Moog's modular system hard-wired into a portable instrument with a keyboard. The Minimoog defined what "a synth bass" and "a synth lead" mean; fifty years later, "make it more Moog" is still a complete production instruction.

## What makes the sound
- **Three VCOs into one mixer** — detune them slightly and the free-running oscillators drift and beat against each other; that chorus-like thickness *is* the Mini sound. (They're always running — early pitch instability became part of the charm.)
- **The 24 dB/oct transistor ladder filter** — Moog's patented lowpass is the most imitated circuit in synthesis: musical resonance that thins the lows as it rises, and it **self-oscillates** into a playable sine. Smooth, creamy, vocal.
- **Overdriving the mixer into the filter** adds harmonic saturation — the "fat" everyone chases is largely this gain-stage trick (feedback-loop mod on reissues formalizes it).
- **Simple, fast envelopes** (no release by default!) and monophony with glide → punchy basses and screaming portamento leads; limitations force strong, committed sounds.
- Warm VCA/discrete signal path throughout — even bright patches stay rounded.

## Digital versions
- **Owned:** [[Analog]] (Ableton's modeled analog — closest stock ladder-style architecture), [[DUNE 3]]/[[Serum 2]] can approximate (use a 24 dB ladder filter model + 3 detuned saws + drive), [[kHs Ladder Filter]] to Moog-ify any source.
- **Paid:** Moog Minimoog Model D (official plugin), Arturia Mini V, Softube Model 72, u-he Diva (Mini-style oscillator/filter modules); Behringer Model D in hardware.
- **Free:** Surge XT's ladder filter + 3-osc patching gets surprisingly close for zero dollars.

## Used on
- Parliament — "Flash Light" (1977): Bernie Worrell's multi-Minimoog bassline, the blueprint for synth bass replacing bass guitar.
- Kraftwerk — *Autobahn* (1974): Minimoog as the melodic voice of electronic pop's founding document.
- Pink Floyd — "Shine On You Crazy Diamond" (1975): the singing Minimoog lead.
- Genre/era: 70s funk/prog/fusion, then the G-funk whistle-lead lane (portamento saw leads on Dre-era records are Minimoog vocabulary — [[Rap Subgenres]] G-funk card); modern trap/pop still reaches for Model D bass patches → [[BASS Slot]], [[LEAD Slot]].
- **Paired with a [[Korg MS-20]] for a closing clipped white-noise-style texture timed to cymbal hits.** One synth carries a low layer, the other the noise character; the combined element only fills in when the cymbals come in rather than running continuously. *(general starting point — no specific patch settings given)* → [[Layering and Stacking]] — Charli XCX / A.G.
