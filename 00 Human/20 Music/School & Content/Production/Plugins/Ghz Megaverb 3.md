# Ghz Megaverb 3

![[Images/Ghz Megaverb 3.png]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Goodhertz

## What it is

Goodhertz's Megaverb, a lush algorithmic reverb plugin known for smooth, musical tails and simple controls.

## What it's used for

Adding smooth, easy-to-dial-in reverb space to vocals and instruments.

## FX chain notes

Used on a send/return track for shared reverb space across the mix.
