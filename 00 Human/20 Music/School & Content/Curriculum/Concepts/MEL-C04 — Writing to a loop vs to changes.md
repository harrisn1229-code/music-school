# MEL-C04 — Writing to a loop vs to changes

**Q.** How does writing a melody over a static one-chord loop differ from writing over moving changes?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Over changes the harmony supplies the motion** — hold one note and the chords recontextualize it (5th, then 3rd, then 9th), so it moves without moving. **Over a static loop nothing does that**, so the melody must generate its own movement through **rhythm, contour and phrasing**. Static loops also **tolerate more repetition**, since the listener is already in a looped frame.

## Why it matters

It's the distinction that matters most in loop-based production. It's why rhythm-first writing suits your workflow: over a static loop, rhythm is the only source of motion you fully control.

## Drill

Write one melody over a moving progression, then play it over a one-chord loop. Note what stops working.
