# Ultimate Alternate Picking Exercises - Kickstart Your Speed in 2020!

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wIEnH2sVoi8)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wIEnH2sVoi8)

## Synopsis

Two alternate-picking exercises built around switching cleanly between different rhythmic note-groupings against a click, rather than pure straight-16th speed drilling — the core skill trained is feeling the *next* note value coming before you're already in it.

## Techniques

- **The exercise shape:** a repeating phrase that switches between different rhythmic groupings (e.g. straight 16ths vs. 16th-note triplets) within the same pass, played along to backing tracks at different tempos.
- **What's actually being trained:** not raw picking speed, but locking onto the *next* rhythmic group's feel before you're already inside it — described as one of the hardest things to internalize, since sloppy note spacing right at the switch point is the most common failure (rushing/spending too long finding the new subdivision).
- **Practice method:** lock in with the bass drum of a backing track that explicitly accents the grouping changes — a drum track built to reinforce exactly where the switches happen, rather than a plain click.
- **The right-hand motion actually changes** between tempo/grouping blocks — not one fixed picking motion reused everywhere; slower and faster picking need visibly different right-hand mechanics, which the exercise makes audible/visible by cycling through several.
- **Reported result:** two days of dedicated work on just these two exercises (across different tempos) was enough to meaningfully rebuild alternate-picking tightness after time away from the instrument.
