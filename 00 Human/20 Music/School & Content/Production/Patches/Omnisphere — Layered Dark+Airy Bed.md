# Omnisphere — Layered Dark+Airy Bed

**Instrument:** [[Omnisphere]] · **Slot:** [[BED Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Synth Bed Track|Synth Bed Track]]

## What it's for
THE pad instrument — Omnisphere's atmospheres/soundscapes library is the deepest bed source owned. Layer two patches (one dark, one airy) and balance instead of hunting one perfect single patch.

## Settings
- From [[Omnisphere#Patch Recipes|Omnisphere — Synth bed]]: ① Layer A — dark/wide pad, ② `Pads + Strings` category, search "analog" or "juno"
- ① Layer B — airy/thin texture, ② search "glass" or "tape", level 20–30% under Layer A
- ⑦ HP both layers ~120 Hz — the chain HPFs it anyway, but starting clean avoids relying on that
- ⑤ Record one slow Orb pass across the two layers for movement
- Attack 0.5–2 s, release 1–3 s (Step 1a universal pad rule — a pad enters and leaves unnoticed)
- Velocity → nothing; beds stay even
- No sub octave on either layer

## In context
- Swappable with a Keyscape Rhodes or [[Model 84 — Juno Pad]] — same slot, different color.
- Unison detune wide, LPF with slow LFO/envelope for motion, per the chain's universal pad rules.
