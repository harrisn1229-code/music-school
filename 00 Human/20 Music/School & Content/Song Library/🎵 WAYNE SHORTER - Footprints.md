# Footprints — Wayne Shorter

> No prior entry existed for this tune. Captured from a real 1-on-1 comping lesson (Sound Guitar Lessons With Jared, filmed with a returning student, Alex) focused entirely on comping vocabulary and voicing variety over the tune's vamp and turnaround — not a soloing breakdown. → [[Private Jazz Guitar Comping Lesson Over Footprints by Wayne Shorter]]

## Chords (as taught in this lesson)

**Tonic vamp:** written on the chart as **Cm11**, played by the student as **Cm7 → Dm7 (passing) → back to Cm7**, occasionally substituting **Cm6**. Jared's reframing: all of this is just **C Dorian** — Dm7 is the iii of the same B♭-major parent scale a whole step up from C, and Cm6 uses the same Dorian natural-6 — so none of it needs separate justification, it's one mode throughout. Because the vamp is genuinely modal rather than a fixed tertian chord, **any diatonic chord from B♭ major works as a substitute voicing over the same vamp** (named but not fully worked in the lesson: B♭maj7, E♭maj7 and their inversions). → [[Diatonic Modes]], [[Jazz Comping]]

**Turnaround** (as played by the student, roughly the last stretch of the form): **F half-diminished (F♯ø7 as voiced) → F7♯9 → E7♯9 → A7♯5**. Practiced as a single memorized "string" of four linked voicings rather than reharmonized chord-by-chord. → [[Turnarounds]]

**Fm chord** (elsewhere in the form): same quartal comping shape used on the Cm vamp, relocated wholesale up to F — the shape tracks the tune's own root motion rather than being rebuilt from scratch. → [[Quartal Chord Voicings]]

## Comping concepts (Jared's breakdown)

- **Voicing-multiplication by top note** — 3–4 inversions of the same Cm7/Cm11, differing mainly in which chord tone (root/5th/3rd/♭7) sits on top, "doubles" then "triples" the perceived variety of a static vamp with minimal new theory.
- **A quartal "hub" shape** (stacked 4ths, strings 4–2, off the 3rd fret) grooved as a repeating whole-step/half-step figure over the Cm vamp, relocated to F for the Fm chord.
- **Turnaround vocabulary chunking** — memorize the whole 4-chord turnaround as one linked unit; build 2–4 total alternate "strings" rather than freely reharmonizing live.
- **Half-step-away approach chords** as a function-agnostic passing color anywhere in the form.
