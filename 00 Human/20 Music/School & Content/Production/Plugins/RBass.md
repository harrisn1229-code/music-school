# RBass

![[Images/RBass.jpg]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Waves

## What it is

Waves' bass-enhancement plugin using harmonic generation to add perceived low-end weight, especially useful on small speakers.

## What it's used for

Adding low-end weight and harmonic reinforcement to bass and kick tracks, especially for translation on small speakers.

## FX chain notes

Placed after EQ, adding harmonic content the EQ alone can't create.

## Techniques

- **Moog + RBass as one leg of a three-part deranged/beautiful sub-bass combo — "my one tool to really lift things is my sub, which is a Moog with an RBass on it."** Jack Antonoff's 'Modern Girl' (Bleachers) sub design: a Moog run through RBass supplies the "perfectly beautiful sub" anchor, layered with a separately-distorted, "totally f***ed up" bass part and a "completely deranged" arp on top — "put the three together and you get a really interesting quality." RBass's job here is specifically to be the controlled, clean element the two wilder companion layers play against, not to add grit itself. *(no specific Frequency/Intensity/Out settings given — qualitative only)* → [[Bass Design]], [[Layering and Stacking]], [[Sound Selection Guide]] — Mix with the Masters (Jack Antonoff), [Behind the Track｜'Modern Girl' by Bleachers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Y1PQt5imXo)
