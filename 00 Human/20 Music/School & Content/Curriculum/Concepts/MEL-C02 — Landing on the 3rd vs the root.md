# MEL-C02 — Landing on the 3rd vs the root

**Q.** A melody lands on the 3rd of the chord instead of the root. What changes?

> [!question]- Answer
> The **root** sounds settled and final — where a phrase goes to end. The **3rd** is more melodic and **colours the chord** (brighter or sadder) **without changing the harmony**, and it's less conclusive. Other tones: **7th** leans and wants to move; **9th** is open and floating, colour without changing function.

## Why it matters

And the consequence is the practical part: **landing on the root constantly is the single most common reason a melody sounds boring** — it agrees with the harmony on every downbeat, so nothing is ever in tension. Target 3rds and 7ths on strong beats.

## Drill

Take a melody. Move every phrase-ending from the root to the 3rd, then the 7th. Name what each does.
