# Disclosure x Zedd - You've Got To Let Go If You Want To Be Free (Twitch Breakdown)

Guy breaks down the Disclosure × Zedd collab, made entirely in his template on his laptop. The most useful parts are the **enormous group-vocal chain** (Oxford Inflator for up-front richness, tune the lead but not the harmonies, Pro-DS in stereo for a group, Gem Dopamine air + a second de-esser), the **Decapitator-Punish-at-9** synth-riding trick, and a **reverse-reverb "dream keys"** sound-design move. Also good detail on suspended-harmony writing that resolves on the hook. High caption confidence.

## Techniques
- **Keyscape chords, never bounced** (Vintage Vibe EP, tremolo off, raw) filling the [[BED Slot]] with a stack of movement FX: EQ filtering up/down, **[[EchoBoy]]** delay, **[[ValhallaVintageVerb]]** reverb — constant motion in the keys. → [[1 Synth Bed Track|Synth Bed Track]] / [[1 Electric Piano Track|Electric Piano Track]].
- **Reverse-reverb "dream keys":** turn reverb + delays full blast, reverse the MIDI so chords play backwards, print through the verb, then reverse the audio region → swelling reverse-reverb texture.
- **Blue-stripe (1176) for front-end punch** on the keys → overdrive → the main tone-shaping EQ → **Decapitator** on top (to sound like it's through an amp) → lush reverb (level automated) → sidechain compression → Logic chorus for width.
- **Decapitator "Punish" at 9** feeding a synth for overdrive craziness — "sounds like I'm riding it but I'm not." H-Delay for ms/feedback movement.
- **Bass:** overdrive to pump it up, then **[[Pro-Q 4]]** boosting the [[SUB Slot]] and cutting low-mids ~150 Hz (boxy); light compression to catch the crazy peaks. *(HPF ~30 Hz, dip ~150 Hz, keep it in the [[BASS Slot]] under the busy keys.)* → [[EQ]], [[3 Bass Bus|Bass Bus]].
- **Group vocal (3: center + ±30, "Logic numbers"):** U87 into SSL; SSL Fusion for vintage drive; comp on the way in; **tune the lead only** and leave harmonies/adlibs untuned for humanity → [[LEAD Slot]], [[Stereo Field]], [[Pitch and Formant]]. Per-vocal chain: comp → cut low-mids → pull highs, notch a resonance → de-ess → **[[Auto-Tune Pro]]** → **[[Oxford Inflator]]** (subtle, adds richness/gain — balance it; "go time" up-front feel; also on the master bus). → [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]].
- **Vocal bus:** glue comp for 3 vocals → Neve 1073 (flat, purely for character/"not done unless it's been through a Neve") → **[[Gem Dopamine]]** (air + de-ess, [[AIR Slot]]) → a second de-esser (**[[Pro-DS]]** in stereo, **link at 0** so it de-esses each side of the group independently) → EQ (cut lows, boost high-mids) → **[[Soothe2]]** + **[[Saturn 2]]** saturation → Pultec (adds vibe/character even at 0; used to pull lows) → H-Delay → reverb → delay sends. → [[3 Vocal Bus|Vocal Bus]], [[De-essing]].
- **Suspended-harmony writing:** harmonies sit on 2nds/4ths/6ths (unresolved tension) until the "let go" hook resolves home — the tension mirrors the lyric.
- **Key-change lift:** the song's in A minor but goes to major for four bars near the end for a triumphant hook (Zedd's idea) — Disclosure "don't usually do key changes."
- **Flex-time abuse:** quantize then stretch MIDI-free audio so extreme that Flex artifacts become a feature.
- **Long kick on purpose** because the bassline is busy/high with no sub-weight — the kick owns the [[SUB Slot]] boom (division of low-end labor between kick and [[BASS Slot]]). → [[Kick Design]].
- **Hidden low vocoder note:** the lowest "be free" note is a vocoder with the highs rolled off and bass boosted — a buried [[SUB Slot]] octave under the [[LEAD Slot]] stack. → [[Pitch and Formant]].
- **Monotribe / Roland System-8** for one-shots and buildups; bit-crushed swung hats for swing.
