# Inst-Synth Bus

**Role:** Finish coat on the harmonic section. Lightest bus in the template — keys/synths arrive already polished (VSTs are pre-produced sounds).

## Chain

### 1. [[Sie-Q]] — Musical tilt
- A dB brighter or darker to seat the whole section behind the vocal.

### 2. [[Kramer Tape]] — Analog unify
- **15 ips** (④) · **Flux low** (①) · **Record low** (②) · **Playback to unity** (③)
- **Why:** running every digital instrument through the same tape stage gives sample-based and synthesized parts one shared "recorded" texture — the cheapest cohesion available.

### 3. Bus glue for presence, not squash — a UA 33609 alternative
- 🎬 *From video:* Marcella Araica adds a **UA 33609** here (an "All Music" bus carrying samples, transitional risers, AND guitars together) deliberately light: "I don't want it compressing too much, I want it to breathe. It's not about really over-compressing it, just allowing it to give it more presence without it feeling like it's being [squashed]." Rode the gain by ear against the mix and pulled the guitars down slightly in the same pass — a light-glue-for-presence move, not a leveling/control move. Unowned — see [[Plugin Wishlist]]; owned stand-in for the same light-touch role is this chain's own [[Sie-Q]]/[[Kramer Tape]] pairing, or [[Solid Bus Comp]] barely engaged. Contrast with the SAME plugin dialed much harder on the drum bus in the same session — see [[3 Drums Bus|Drums Bus]] and [[Compression Staging]]. → [[Committing and Printing]], [[Compression Staging]], [[3 Drums Bus|Drums Bus]], [[3 Guitar Bus|Guitar Bus]] — Mix with the Masters (Marcella Araica), [[Printing stems through analog equipment with Marcella Araica]]

### 4. Duck the section off the vocal (add when there's a lead)
- 🎬 *From video:* Disclosure make room for a soft lead by **ducking the whole synth section off the vocal** — a compressor keyed off the vocal (via a muted empty bus), or **[[Soothe2]] / [[Pro-MB]] in dynamic/sidechain mode** so the synths give up *exactly* the frequencies the vocal occupies (sharper than a wideband ducker like Trackspacer). **Slow release** so it flows. → [[Sidechaining]], [[LEAD Slot]] — Disclosure, [Looking For Love](https://youtu.be/NlJt0pCptGk) / [Higher Than Ever Before](https://youtu.be/9XkHSronZck) / [Help Me Lose My Mind](https://youtu.be/FPgLRkgKoMU).

## Variations
- **If this bus feels redundant:** it often is for sparse arrangements — bypass and let the group feed the master. Buses earn their place in *dense* mixes.
