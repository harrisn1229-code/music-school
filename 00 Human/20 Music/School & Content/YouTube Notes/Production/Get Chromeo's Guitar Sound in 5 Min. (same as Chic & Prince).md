# Get Chromeo's Guitar Sound in 5 Min. (same as Chic & Prince)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bj2dWuFYzMQ)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bj2dWuFYzMQ)

## Synopsis

Chromeo (Dave 1 and P-Thugg) walk through the DI-only funk guitar technique they discovered as teenagers moving from rock into funk, and trace it back to Prince, Nile Rodgers, and specifically Chic engineer Bob Clearmountain, who would secretly print guitars direct-in rather than through an amp and keep that direct sound in the mix. The demo is genuinely a five-minute one: guitar straight into a preamp with zero amp, zero EQ, zero compression on the way in, using the "quack" pickup position, and the punchy percussive tone is already there before any processing happens. The rest is a light compressor pass to exaggerate the percussiveness, a preference for dry (reverb bypassed) over wet, and the reminder that most of the funk vocabulary on top of that tone is just a root/flat-seven pentatonic chug — learn one song like this and "you'll have all of funk figured out."

## Techniques

- **DI, no amp, is the actual secret — not a mic or pedal trick.** Traced to Prince and Nile Rodgers/Chic, who "never used an amp or very seldom used an amplifier" and plugged in direct; Chic engineer Bob Clearmountain would double-track the guitar direct without telling the artist, then keep and work with that direct sound. The single biggest lever on this tone is skipping the amp entirely. → [[1 Electric Guitar DI Track|Electric Guitar DI Track]] — Sol State, [Get Chromeo's Guitar Sound in 5 Min.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bj2dWuFYzMQ)
- **Two-guitar rule for funk vs. thick tones:** Gibson Les Paul when they want a thick sound, a solo, or "anything thicker and with more body" (their reference point for chunky-but-not-funk is the band Phoenix's guitar tone); Fender Strat is the default "for that kind of funk thing." Guitar quality matters more than the chain — "you have to have something that sounds nice." → [[Fender Stratocaster]] — Sol State, [Get Chromeo's Guitar Sound in 5 Min.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bj2dWuFYzMQ)
- **Signal path is just guitar → preamp, nothing else.** No amp, no cab, no sim. Historically they used "the actual just the generic sound cards" (a basic audio interface) as the preamp; now a nicer external preamp, but the principle is unchanged — the preamp itself is the only coloration stage before the DAW. → [[1 Electric Guitar DI Track|Electric Guitar DI Track]] — Sol State, [Get Chromeo's Guitar Sound in 5 Min.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bj2dWuFYzMQ)
- **Start completely flat: no EQ, no compression, on the raw DI.** Demoed live — "let's put nothing on the guitar at all... flat eq no compression nothing just through the preamp" — and it "already sounds kind of punchy," arguably punchier than the same part through an amp. Confirms the raw DI itself (not later processing) is where the percussive character actually lives. → [[1 Electric Guitar DI Track|Electric Guitar DI Track]] — Sol State, [Get Chromeo's Guitar Sound in 5 Min.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bj2dWuFYzMQ)
- **Pickup position 2 (bridge+middle "quack") is the funk position.** Explicitly switches to "the second pickup here, or the second position" before demoing the tone — the classic Strat quack position is the default for this rhythm sound, not neck or bridge alone. → [[Fender Stratocaster]] — Sol State, [Get Chromeo's Guitar Sound in 5 Min.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bj2dWuFYzMQ)
- **Compress hard AFTER the flat DI to exaggerate the percussive attack** — "jack the compression right so you can really hear the percussive aspect of it," using a basic/generic compressor plugin (they call out the stock Waves one specifically — "everybody has that"). The compressor isn't shaping tone here, it's turning up the transient/percussive quality that's already present in the dry DI. → [[Compressor]] — Sol State, [Get Chromeo's Guitar Sound in 5 Min.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bj2dWuFYzMQ)
- **Clean direct (no compression at all) is a legitimate alternative, not just a "before" step** — "sometimes I go to clean direct also, direct sounds good" — the flat/dry DI on its own is a usable end tone, not only a starting point for the compressed version. → [[1 Electric Guitar DI Track|Electric Guitar DI Track]] — Sol State, [Get Chromeo's Guitar Sound in 5 Min.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bj2dWuFYzMQ)
- **Reverb present in the chain but deliberately bypassed** — a Valhalla reverb sits on the channel "but actually it's disabled... it's better when it's dry, I think it's cooler when it's dry." Dry-by-default is a stylistic choice for this percussive rhythm tone, not an oversight — reverb stays available but off unless a part specifically needs it. → [[Reverb]] — Sol State, [Get Chromeo's Guitar Sound in 5 Min.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bj2dWuFYzMQ)
- **The funk vocabulary itself is one simple pentatonic trope: root–♭7–root–♭7.** "Learn the song 'Money' and you'll have all of funk figured out... it's always these very pentatonic movements — flat seven root flat seven root flat seven." That single interval move, muted and chugged, is described as most of what's actually being played live — "half the show I'm just doing those little chugs." → [[Rhythm and Groove]] — Sol State, [Get Chromeo's Guitar Sound in 5 Min.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bj2dWuFYzMQ)
