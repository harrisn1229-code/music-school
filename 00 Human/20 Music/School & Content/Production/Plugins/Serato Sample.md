# Serato Sample

![[Images/Serato Sample.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Serato

## What it is

Serato's sample-chopping and playback instrument plugin, offering quick beat-slicing, time-stretching, and pitch controls for turning a loop into a playable instrument.

## What it's used for

Fast sample chopping and flipping — classic hip hop/sample-based production workflow inside the DAW.

## FX chain notes

Used as the instrument source when building a beat from a chopped sample.

## Used in my chains

A **sample chopper/pitch-'n-time sampler** (instrument source) — fast chops with key/BPM sync, the Serato-DJ pedigree for flipping loops:

- **[[2 Drums Group|Drums Group]]** — chop loops/one-shots (alongside [[XO]] browsing and [[Drum Rack]]/[[Simpler]] playback). Its key/BPM-shift keeps chops in tune and in tempo.
