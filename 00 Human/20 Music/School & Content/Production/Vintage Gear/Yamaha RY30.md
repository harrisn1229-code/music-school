# Yamaha RY30

## What it is
Yamaha's early-90s prosumer drum machine — AWM2 (16-bit sample-plus-synthesis) voices with filters and layering, a data wheel, and velocity pads. A quiet workhorse: rarely famous, constantly *present* in early-90s demos, dance records, and bedroom studios.

## What makes the sound
- **AWM2 = samples through a real synthesis layer**: two-element voices with resonant filter, pitch/amp envelopes, and velocity routing — closer to a sample-based synth than a preset box; the filtered, layered kits sound "produced" out of the gate.
- Clean 16-bit converters with Yamaha's slightly glassy top end — hi-fi early-90s digital, the opposite pole from the [[E-mu SP-1200]]'s dirt.
- Waveform cards expanded the palette (dance/house sets were popular); pitch/filter automation per step gave it proto-parameter-lock tricks.
- Sequencer with groove templates — Yamaha's answer to Linn/MPC swing.

## Digital versions
- **Owned:** [[Drum Rack]] with layered [[Simpler]] instances + [[kHs Filter]] recreates the AWM2 voice architecture directly; RY30 sample sets exist.
- **Paid:** UVI Beat Box Anthology 2; vintage archives.
- **Free:** RY30 kits circulate in sample archives.

## Used on
- Genre/era: early-90s dance/house demos, Eurodance-adjacent productions, and countless songwriter demos — a ubiquity box with no marquee documented credits; genre-level per the rule.
