# Mac DeMarco Vocal Chain

**Reference/vibe:** the slacker croon — lazy, warm, slightly seasick. One voice, barely tuned, wrapped in **tape slap and pitch wobble** instead of reverb. The whole aesthetic is "expensive tape machine in a cheap bedroom": his records are tracked to actual tape with the vari-speed drifting.
**Signal source:** [[Shure SM57]] — a cheap dynamic suits the croon (his classic era was cheap dynamics anyway — an SM57 is *more* period-correct than an SM7B). Relaxed, low-effort delivery *is* the instrument.

## Step 1 — Record (UAD Console)
1. **UAD UA 610-B (Unison)** — tube, pushed a little (soft round drive)
2. **UAD Teletronix LA-2A** — 2–3 dB lazy
3. **UAD Pultec EQP-1A** — bypass

## Chain overview
Level → warm → wobble → slap. Four moves; stopping early is the style.

## Step 2 — Process (Ableton)

### 1. [[LALA]] — Croon leveler
- 3–4 dB, constant gentle squeeze — flatters the lazy delivery into evenness.

### 2. [[Pro-Q 4]] — Round it
- HPF 80 · LPF or high-shelf down above ~10 kHz (no modern air — this voice is AM-radio warm) · small 3 kHz lift if the words blur.

### 3. [[Kramer Tape]] — The machine
- 7.5 ips (darker) · flux medium · **wow/flutter ON and audible** (10–20%) · noise a touch
- **Why:** this stage is the genre — the pitch instability reads as nostalgia/unease. His actual method is vari-speed tape; Kramer's wow/flutter is the plugin path. ([[RC-20 Retro Color]] wobble is the alternative flavor.)

### 4. [[Chorus-Ensemble]] — Seasick (optional, for the synthy cuts)
- Slow vibrato-leaning setting, shallow, ~20% — the Chamber of Reflection queasiness (on that song the *whole track* wobbles, not just the vocal).

## Step 3 — Effects (sends)
- **[[Delay Send Slap]] is the reverb:** 100–140 ms, wobbly EchoBoy tape mode, feedback ~10%, send generous — the doo-wop slapback croon. Real reverb barely used ([[Reverb Send Room]] whisper at most).
- Doubles: loose unison on hooks only, drift kept.

## Vocal arrangement

| Section | Lead | Behind it |
|---|---|---|
| Verse | one voice, barely tuned, tape slap | nothing |
| Chorus | same voice, slap send pushed | maybe one loose harmony, drifting |
| Outro | talking, laughing, or the tape running on | — |

**The move — the wobble is the width.** No stacks, no widening. Vari-speed drift and tape slap do the job doubles would do elsewhere, which is why one voice never sounds thin here.

**Don't tighten the double if you add one.** Drift is the point — the alignment rules that apply to a pop stack are exactly backwards for this lane.

**Stack count: 1, occasionally 2.**

## Notes / variations
- **Full-track move:** print gentle wow/flutter on the *mix bus* for a section (or whole song) — vocal wobble alone reads as an effect; everything wobbling reads as a world.
- **Another One / synth-ballad lane:** more chorus, softer slap, LALA deeper.
- **Rock lane (Freaking Out the Neighborhood):** drop the chorus, slap shorter (80 ms), let the 610-B drive harder.
- Pairs with [[Bedroom Pop - Indie]] and the [[Neo-Soul - Lo-fi]] texture rules.
