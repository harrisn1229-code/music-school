# Ian Kirkpatrick — Produce a Track (Part 2)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AjcENoa_TBk)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AjcENoa_TBk)

## Synopsis

A loose, self-described "extremely stoned" beat-from-scratch stream — more vibe than lecture, but with a few genuinely useful sound-design gems: turning percussion into a playable **PadShop** instrument, **round-robin in Kontakt** for same-note drum variation, and his glitch/transient toolkit (Sausage Fattener, Spiff, Torque, Little AlterBoy). Also the reminder that he "came from IDM" — the pop guy makes weird noise for fun.

## Techniques

- **PadShop as a playable instrument from percussion** — load percussive/drum samples into **PadShop**, and because it randomizes grain + spread, every key gives a different interesting sound, with a slight glide between notes. "Anything percussive can become an instrument." → [[PadShop]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]]
- **PadShop is available for Ableton (Steinberg VST)** — he explicitly confirms it runs as a VST instrument outside Cubase — so the "hide-a-synth-from-a-vocal" PadShop trick is **directly usable in Ableton**. → [[PadShop]]
- **Round-robin same-note drums in Kontakt** — take several kicks that are all the same note into **Kontakt** and set up round-robin/randomize so each hit plays a different sample — instant humanized variation from a static drum. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Kick Design]], [[TRANSIENT Slot]]
- **Glitch/mangle toolkit** — **Sausage Fattener** (fatten/loudness), **Spiff** (transient/spectral shaper), **Torque** (drum-pitch shifter), **Little AlterBoy** (formant/pitch) stacked to turn a boring sample into a "rude," aggressive hit. → [[Sausage Fattener]], [[Little AlterBoy]], [[Saturation and Distortion]]
- **Mono + glide on a granular sound** — collapsing a wobbly granular texture to mono and adding glide made it usable ("if I put it to mono and then the glide — oh my lord"). → [[Stereo Field]], [[Width and Stereo]]
- **Grab the individual hits out of a noise/glitch** — chop the best transients out of a chaotic noise texture to reuse as one-shots. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Crowdsourced songwriting** — leaves the beat unfinished and invites viewers to send top-lines/sounds to a shared email — collaborative, non-precious. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]

## Philosophy

- **"I came from IDM — I'll make the weirdest shit and have the #1 song."** The pop craft sits on top of an experimental sound-design foundation; keep stretching. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Record with mastering OFF on the master** — catches himself printing a sound with the master-bus processing still on; bounce sources clean. → [[Gain Staging]], [[Master Chain|8 Master]]
