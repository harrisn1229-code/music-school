# Six-Step Legato Fretting-Hand Workout

Six short fretting-hand exercises, each targeting one legato mistake, ending in applying the fixed mechanics to a real lick/backing track.

## The six steps

1. Finger-independence pattern with a twist: lift each finger the instant it isn't hammering/pulling — don't leave unused fingers pressed down.
2. Same pattern moved to a "sandwiched" middle string (e.g. G) to force silence on both neighboring strings, not just the outer strings.
3. A two-shape connecting exercise — practice the transition between two legato shapes until the seam disappears.
4. A B/E-string exercise specifically training fretting near the fingernail rather than the fleshy fingertip pad.
5. A full six-string legato scale run, focused entirely on holding one constant wrist angle rather than rotating it per string.
6. Apply the fixed technique to a real lick or improv over a backing track — not another isolated drill rep.

## Why `reinforces: []` is correct here

Pure fretting-hand mechanics (finger independence, muting, contact point, wrist angle) — no scale/mode/chord-tone knowledge being applied. Per the Drill Index's drill-vs-routine test, this is motor skill, not concept application.
