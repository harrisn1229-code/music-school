# SMP-C02 — Clear before release

**Q.** When do you clear a sample, and what happens if you don't?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Before release. Always.** Clearing afterwards means negotiating with no leverage against someone who now knows the song is working. Uncleared can mean **takedown**, **frozen royalties**, a **majority publishing share**, or the track's entire income.

## Why it matters

You sample — so this is the shortest distance between a knowledge gap and a real cost. The routes that avoid the problem: licensed libraries, royalty-free packs, interpolation, replay-and-rewrite, or full clearance through a service.

## Drill

Take one sample-based idea you've made. Which of the five routes applies, and what would you actually do to release it?
