# ValhallaDelay

![[Images/ValhallaDelay.jpg]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Valhalla DSP

## What it is

Valhalla's flagship delay plugin, covering a huge range of delay algorithms (digital, tape, lo-fi, and more) in one plugin.

## What it's used for

General-purpose creative delay duty across nearly any genre or delay style.

## FX chain notes

Used on a send/return or inserted for a dedicated delay effect.

## Techniques

- **Kept as a producer's original vocal-delay send, praised for ease of use, paired with an [[H-Delay]] and a ~1.5 kHz EQ cut on the harsh part of the return.** "So with this delay, this is also from Mark, H-Delay, Vintage Valhalla. This Valhalla is so easy to use." Part of a two-bus vocal send setup the mixer chose to preserve rather than rebuild — see [[Mix Handoff]]. → [[H-Delay]], [[Delay]] (effect), [[Dua Lipa Vocal Chain]] — Mix with the Masters (Josh Gudwin), [[Mixing Dua Lipa's vocals with Josh Gudwin]]
- **A mid-chain sound-design stage inside a dense vocal "backgrounds" reverse-reverb texture, feeding a very long (~19-second) closing reverb.** Illangelo's Weeknd backgrounds chain (the reverse-reverb swell that opens the record): an Audio Effect Rack with an unidentified "whirl"-type device → **this delay** → the ~19-second reverb → a final [[Pro-Q 4]] top-end roll-off. No exact time/feedback given — logged as a chain-position sighting rather than a settings recipe; full chain at [[The Weeknd Vocal Chain]]. → [[The Weeknd Vocal Chain]], [[Reverb]] (effect) — Illangelo (Mix with the Masters), [[Producing The Weeknd's vocals with Illangelo]]
- **Time kept very short with feedback pushed really high, specifically so the repeats read "almost like a chorus effect" rather than a distinct echo — the closing stage of a Rhodes chain.** On a Rhodes-led soulful sample (AAS Lounge Lizard electric piano): short mono spring reverb → [[Ghz Vulf Compressor 3]] (tight) → EQ (cut lows, boost highs) → this delay last, mix kept short, Wow/Flutter left on for a little detune character. Same short-time/high-feedback-as-chorus-substitute pattern already documented elsewhere on this page (dark synth trap counter melody) — a repeatable move, not a one-off. → [[Ghz Vulf Compressor 3]], [[EQ]], [[Reverb]], [[Soulful Sample Stacking Chain]], [[Sound Selection Guide]] — loner, [use this easy technique to take your samples to the next level!? (making a soulful sample)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vN0fgGDxp6E)
- **Closing stage of a 5-device piano chain: EQ → valve compressor → vintage verb → this delay, added last purely for extra space.** On a chill/ambient Frank Ocean-style beat, a Postcard Piano lead gets EQ'd to separate it from the Rhodes bed (low + high cut), tightened with a valve compressor ([[UAD Tube-Tech CL 1B mkII]] best-match), given a [[ValhallaVintageVerb]] pass, and finished with this delay as the last word. Also used, pushed "pretty heavy," on a bell one-shot from an unnamed one-shot kit, paired with a medium-sized spring reverb. → [[UAD Tube-Tech CL 1B mkII]], [[ValhallaVintageVerb]], [[EQ]], [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[Frank Ocean Chill Ambient Chain]] — loner, [how to make chill ambient beats for frank ocean (sad/melodic chord technique)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=whI75NJcom8)
- **Heavy delay used purely to fill out space, run as the last stage of a vocal-chop chain in a beat with only two melodic elements total.** On a Tory Lanez/Chris Brown-style underwater R&B beat, a Splice vocal chop runs bandpass EQ → an automated half-time effect → medium reverb → **a "pretty heavy" ValhallaDelay** — reached for specifically "because obviously there's only the synth and the vocal chop," i.e. the delay's repeats are doing arrangement-level work (filling perceived empty space), not just adding echo character. *(general starting point — no exact time/feedback given)* → [[Delay]], [[Reverb]], [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[Tory Lanez Underwater Synth Chain]] — loner, [how to make melodic r&b beats for tory lanez & chris brown (underwater synth/vocal sample)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JCup6FVW5o)
- **"Reverse pitch" 4-bar reverse-loop preset on a vocal chop, mix pulled well down since the repeats land off-time — the off-time-ness IS the texture, not a flaw to fix.** "I did the reverse pitch, four bar reverse loop preset from Valhalla Delay — make sure you bring the mix quite far down, because it's a bit off time or whatever, but it just adds this nice little texture at the end." Used as the finishing stage on one vocal-loop layer, after a short-but-heavy delay and before an EQ pass. → [[Delay]], [[EQ]], [[Vocal Stacks and Ad-libs]], [[Future-Style Vintage Sample Chain]] — loner, [this is how frank dukes & cubeatz make vintage samples in 2022!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nak-TAIPQ-c)
- **Delay time set to triplet, specifically to match a triplet-quantized plucky-violin bounce line elsewhere in the same chord/pad layer's chain.** On a diminished-chord-ended strings-and-pad progression (Augmented Strings), ValhallaDelay is placed after an EQ + reverb stage and switched to triplet timing so its repeats lock to the same third-note grid the melodic bounce line was quantized to (see [[Rhythm and Groove]]) rather than a straight 8th/16th default. → [[Rhythm and Groove]], [[EQ]], [[Reverb]] — loner, [is this the best new vst for adding vocals to your melodies!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uQyDT0NEfTE)
- **A plain texture stage on a pad-in-progress, mid-chain before a bounce-and-rechop pass.** On a chord layer being deliberately manipulated from "keys" into "pad" (AAS Lounge Lizard "broken" preset): reverb (a "clean chorus" preset, mix ~50%) → EQ cut low → chorus for width → **ValhallaDelay for texture**, then the whole thing gets bounced to audio and chopped up before a Kickstart pulse and a second reverb finish the pad. *(general starting point — no exact time/feedback given)* → [[Chorus-Ensemble]], [[Kickstart 2]], [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[Afro Sample Chain]] — loner, [how i made the sample that got a 1 million stream placement (it's easier than you think!?)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=atjGOpxBzag)
- **A single very long/dragged-out delay to turn a too-quiet, too-thin synth texture layer into a present element, ahead of a light saturation stage.** On a Kendrick Lamar-inspired soul sample, a dry "chiclets"-preset synth counter-layer (Arturia Analog Lab, see [[Plugin Wishlist]]) reads as "too quiet and not present enough" until this delay is set to a long time and dragged way out — feeding into [[Decapitator]] (Drive up, Mix pulled back, light low cut) right after. Auto-captioned as "vagina delay" on this video, a near-certain homophone garble for Valhalla Delay. *(general starting point — no exact time/feedback figures given)* → [[Decapitator]], [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[Kendrick Lamar Soul Sample Chain]] — loner, [how to make the most realistic vintage soul samples for kendrick lamar!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oMbeATqXOww)
- **Two instances of the same delay at different note subdivisions (16th vs. 8th notes), layered on one lead, for rhythmic thickness rather than a single clean repeat.** On an Analog Lab dirty-lead sample layer, one ValhallaDelay instance is set to 16th notes and a second to 8th notes, run together after a [[Cassette]] crush + EQ stage — the layered subdivisions give the lead a busier, dirtier repeat pattern than one delay alone. → [[Cassette]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]] — loner, [instantly make your melodies sound like vintage samples with this secret trick!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=amvSl5ACFeo)
- **Pitch mode on a reverse guitar swell for extra high-end texture** — copying a Weeknd-style ("Escape From LA") intro move: record a reverse guitar part playing the same chords as the main synth part, then run it through ValhallaDelay with **Pitch mode selected** specifically (not a standard tape/digital mode) to add shimmering high-end texture to the reversed rise before the beat drops. → [[1 Electric Guitar DI Track|Electric Guitar DI Track]], [[Depth and Space]] — loner, [the production secrets behind the weeknd's evolving sound!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GMeZtNImaT0)
- **Freeze a chord into a texture/accent one-shot by running it through a very long, non-synced delay time, then bounce and save it.** Chop just the first chord off a progression, feed it into ValhallaDelay with a very long delay time so the repeats smear the chord into a drone-like texture, then record/bounce that smear as a new standalone one-shot (pitched up an octave to sit as a topline accent over the original chord). Built a personal "accents and textures" library this way, filed by key + tempo — save any loved element to a folder the moment it happens rather than losing it. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]] — loner, [these one shot secrets will transform your melodies!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNLIX3Z4Ohk)
- **Go-to for experimental/pitchy delay effects** (vs. a subtler tape-style ping-pong delay for a more restrained sound) — reach for this specifically when you want the delay itself to sound crazy/pitched rather than just repeat cleanly; reversed/pitch-shifted feedback settings can produce genuinely unusual textures worth building a whole moment around — Disclosure, [First Twitch Stream Ever (Production Tutorial)](https://youtu.be/IObzp3OugtY)

- **Time set in milliseconds (not a synced subdivision) and kept short, with heavy feedback, specifically so it reads "almost like a chorus" instead of a distinct rhythmic echo.** On a dark-synth-trap counter melody (Kontakt "Tape Synths," Fono Loop), this delay sits ahead of a heavy vintage reverb and a "Reels" tape-detune stage — the short unsynced ms delay thickens the layer without adding an audible slapback. → [[Kontakt 7]], [[Reverb]], [[Southside Dark Synth Trap Chain]] — loner, [how southside makes dark synth trap beats for future from scratch (dark melody music theory)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVjyuF5-xUo)
