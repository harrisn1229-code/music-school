# Disclosure - 'Deeper' with Leon Thomas Production Breakdown

Guy breaks down "Deeper" (feat. Leon Thomas) — a heavily **sample-based**, evocatively "underwater" R&B/house tune written largely on a plane out of sped-up sample packs. The richest material: the **speed-up sampling** method and a long, genuinely useful passage on **where to actually get samples**; a **double-sidechain** bass (whole-volume + lows-only); the **MS-20→303 acid trick** (drive the resonant peak into distortion); Leon's **varispeed ad-libs** (artistic) vs a **slowed-not-pitched** lead (precision); and the current **clip + 100% Inflator** master. Whisper transcript (no captions) — high confidence.

## Techniques
- **Speed-up sampling** — the "bubbly" chorus synth and most of the track are slow R&B/license-free samples **sped up hard** (an 87 BPM part running at 136 BPM); the flutter/character comes *from* the speed-up. Everything is **pitched to match the key** with a key-match sampler (ADSR Sample / Splice Bridge). → new [[Sampling and Sound Selection]] note; [[TEXTURE Slot]] / [[BED Slot]].
- **Evocative, not literal, sound selection** — "underwater" is built from *sounds* (ancient flute, reverse vocal, plucks, a distorted reverse-reverb pad) with **no water SFX at all**; pick sounds for the feeling, not the label. Hours spent auditioning "what sits with the bubbly vibe without covering it." → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Sound Palette]].
- **Where to get samples (community > trawling):** trade packs with people on Discord (more interesting than YouTube wormholes); sample **movies via PlayPhrase.me** (type a phrase → clips of people saying it); keep your ears open in real life ("When a Fire Starts to Burn" was found by accident); get a **great singer to improvise oohs/ahs over your chords** and sample that (Lianne La Havas → "Simulation"); use **top-loops not full loops** and **play your own drums in** — drums are the most revealing of a stock loop. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]].
- **Double-sidechain bass** — the sawtooth [[Repro-1]] bass gets **two** sidechains off the kick: one grabbing the **whole volume gently (~3–4 dB)** and a second, aggressive **[[Pro-MB]] grabbing only the lows (~12 dB)** so the sub ducks hard while the mid detail stays. → [[Sidechaining]], [[Multiband Compression]], [[BASS Slot]] / [[SUB Slot]], [[3 Bass Bus|Bass Bus]].
- **OTT to tighten a wide bass's mono center** — the synth bass has lots of default chorus/sides, so a touch of [[OTT]] (upward — "keeps the quiet parts loud, a push not a squash") makes the **mono** low end punchier. → [[Multiband Compression]], [[Stereo Field]].
- **MS-20 → 303 acid trick** *(with settings)* — to fake a 303 out of the Korg MS-20: an **aggressive resonant filter (~+20 dB) sweeping**, fed into a distortion unit ([[Decapitator]]) driven **hard so the resonant peak drives the distortion**, not the whole sound. Reverb/delay/pan + a little [[Soothe2]] to tame. Works on any synth with a hot resonant filter. → [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[LEAD Slot]] / [[MIDS Slot]]. Reese bass from [[Serum 2]] (Splice preset) alongside.
- **Whole-kit-through-reverb drum bus** — API → a bit of [[Saturn 2]] → **H-Verb across the whole kit** (a signature move) → [[Oxford Inflator]] driven a touch into the red → EQ (small bass + treble boost, tame harsh bits — "EQ is more transparent than Soothe here and lets you mess with the transients") → a couple dB of limiting. → [[3 Drums Bus|Drums Bus]], [[TRANSIENT Slot]].
- **Layered percussion** — subby quiet kick + rims + claps + hats + **four tambourines** + battery 909/808 + a flanger on the ride. Two-step groove. → [[TRANSIENT Slot]].
- **Varispeed ad-libs (artistic) vs slowed-not-pitched lead (precision)** — Leon's ad-libs were tracked slowed then sped up so his **pitch rises** (underwater/"different person" tone); the **lead** was recorded slowed but **not pitch-shifted** — slower delivery for precision while keeping his natural voice. → [[Pitch and Formant]], [[LEAD Slot]].
- **Leon vocal chain, minimal** — Sony C100 → Neve preamp → Tube-Tech (very bright, "tame the presence after"); per-track just **compressor + EQ (kill a couple resonances) + de-esser**; singer-requested **CLA Vocals** comp on the pre/chorus. → [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]], [[LEAD Slot]].
- **Vocal bus** — a touch of [[Soothe2]] → **[[Gem Dopamine]]** (de-ess + high boost, "doing a lot" — shiny top) → more de-essing → **[[OTT]]** (tiny, upward) → **[[H-Delay]] slapback** for a room feel (Leon's taste — "very John Lennon/Beatles") → a **short "toilet" reverb** to put him *in a space* rather than "flood a cathedral," with a longer delay/reverb automated in only for a V2 rise. All delays are H-Delay (quarter + eighth). → [[Reverb]], [[Delay]], [[Delay Send Slap]], [[AIR Slot]].
- **Throws instead of doubles** — muted delay throws fill the stop-start verse gaps (Leon's request) rather than doubling the line. → [[Delay]], [[Automation]].
- **Chorus stacking + stereo repair** — 4 vocals, split-pan; because some ad-lib takes were mono and some stereo, stereo automation balances them so the image doesn't jump mid-phrase. → [[Stereo Field]].
- **Drop-down (de-hyped) chorus wins** — a big "mega" hype B-section was tried and cut; the simple "deeper with you" hook was better. Don't overthink the hook. Keep an **"unused ideas" folder** at the bottom of the project.
- **Breakdown = palette cleanser** — strip the bass/drums, go spacey/airy, take the ear somewhere else so the returning chorus hits fresh.
- **Mix-as-you-go for electronic** — "the mix *is* the writing" (effects/sends/delays are the arrangement); beat 70–80% done before vocals, then a fresh-ears final pass to make space once the vocal's on.
- **Master = clip + 100% [[Oxford Inflator]]** — V-Clip ~3–4 dB then Inflator at 100% (Pro-L bypassed, sent plain to the mastering engineer). **Loudness:** no target, −9 to −6 LUFS, never past −6 — streaming normalizes, so "if you can hear the clip/limit you've overdone it." Gain staging is the real key. → [[Limiting]], [[Master Chain]], [[Clipping and Loudness]], [[Gain Staging]].
