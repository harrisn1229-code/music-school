# Nexus — Produced EP

**Instrument:** [[Nexus]] · **Slot:** [[BED Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Electric Piano Track|Electric Piano Track]]

## What it's for
Instant electric-piano patches, fully produced — reach for it when the part needs to be done in 10 seconds, same brief as [[Nexus — Stacked Pop Piano]] but for the EP role.

## Settings
- Browse the `Keys`/`EP` category, audition ≤5, commit — patches arrive already EQ'd/compressed/often effected
- Bypass most of the chain below this patch; re-processing an already-produced Nexus patch tends to fight its internal mix
- Use the 4 macro knobs only for brightness/level, not reshaping
- Confirm no tremolo/chorus is already baked in before adding [[PanMan]] or a chain chorus downstream — check the FX tab

## In context
- The speed option against [[Keyscape — Rhodes LA Custom E & Mark I]] / [[Keyscape — Wurlitzer 140B & 200A]] / [[Operator — DX7 FM E-Piano]] when there's no time budget to build or audition a full patch.
