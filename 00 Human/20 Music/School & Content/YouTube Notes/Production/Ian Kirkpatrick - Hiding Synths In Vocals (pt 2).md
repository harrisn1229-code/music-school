# Ian Kirkpatrick — Hiding Synths In Vocals (pt 2)

## Synopsis

Part 2 of the hidden-synth idea, with the actual method: **build the synth patch out of the vocal word itself.** He drags the word "move" into **PadShop** (the granular synth) and makes a patch so the pad sounds like the "ooh" of *move*; then does the same for "me." The synth is literally the vowel of the word, so it fuses invisibly under the vocal.

## Techniques

- **Make a PadShop patch from the vocal word** — drag the sung word ("move," "me") into PadShop's granular engine, build a pad from it, and play the note under the word. Because the patch *is* the vowel, it accentuates the word without sounding like a separate synth. → [[PadShop]], [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]], [[Pitch and Formant]]
- **A fun way to accentuate specific words** — pick the one or two words you want to lift and hide a matched-timbre synth under each. → [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]], [[Ian Kirkpatrick - Hiding Synths In Vocals]]
