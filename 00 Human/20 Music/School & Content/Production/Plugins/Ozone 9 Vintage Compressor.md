# Ozone 9 Vintage Compressor

![[Images/Ozone 9 Vintage Compressor.png]]

**Category:** Compressor/Limiter
**Vendor:** iZotope

## What it is

Ozone 9's vintage-modeled bus compressor, emulating classic analog compressor character for mastering-bus glue.

## What it's used for

Adding vintage-style glue compression across a master bus.

## FX chain notes

Used mid-chain in the Ozone mastering rack, after EQ and before the maximizer.
