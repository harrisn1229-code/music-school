# Tonelib-BassDrive

![[Images/Tonelib-BassDrive.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Tonelib

## What it is

Tonelib's bass amp and cabinet simulator plugin for shaping DI bass recordings.

## What it's used for

Amp-sim tone shaping on DI-recorded bass guitar.

## FX chain notes

Used as the amp-sim stage on a bass track, typically before EQ and compression.
