# Ian Kirkpatrick — Fever (Dua Lipa) Breakdown Stream

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mc3foPZpyaw)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mc3foPZpyaw)

## Synopsis

The full 2-hour Fever source stream (companion to [[Ian Kirkpatrick - Fever Synth (Dua Lipa)]] and [[Ian Kirkpatrick - Vocal Production for Dua Lipa (Fever)]]). Adds the story (written 2018 with Julia Michaels & J Cash, Angèle + **Tristan Salvati** added the second-verse production later) and several distinctive craft moves: Salvati's gap-filling muffled four-on-the-floor kick and high-frequency air, the "reverb-100%-then-compress-in-mono" synth trick, a Kontakt session-strings-to-timestretch technique, and his full tracking front-end (BAE 1084 + B2 Bomber).

## Techniques — additive to the focused Fever pages

- **Add a *muffled* four-on-the-floor kick to fill the gaps** — Tristan Salvati dropped a muffled kick on the four that "sits in the gaps — four-on-the-floor without paying full price frequency-wise." A great half-house move. → [[Kick Design]], [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Squeeze high-frequency air/white noise into a dull verse** — Salvati heard "no high end in the verse" and added super-high white noise + a sizzly ride, opening the section up. → [[EQ]], [[AIR Slot]], [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]]
- **"Reverb mix 100%, then compress hard, in mono" for a nostalgic synth** — the Cowboy-Bebop-ish lead runs its reverb at 100% wet, then gets compressed so the (low) verb pops out, summed to mono — a retro, "weird," nostalgic tone. → [[Reverb]], [[Compression]], [[Stereo Field]], [[Nexus]]
- **Kontakt session-strings → timestretch mode, then speed up** — inside Kontakt he converts all ~1700 samples to time-stretch mode so he can speed the strings up for a plucky staccato. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Pitch and Formant]]
- **Split kick: beef layer + a very quiet transient (−28 dB)** — later in the song, raise *only* the transient so the kick stays present in a dense mix. → [[Kick Design]], [[Layering and Stacking]], [[TRANSIENT Slot]]
- **Manual sidechain on 808s via volume fades** — mute/fade the 808 for the kick hit by hand instead of a compressor. → [[Sidechaining]], [[Automation]], [[Kick Design]]
- **Spiff transient designer (Delta mode)** — Delta shows only what it's affecting; great on loops/sampled loops for wild transient reshaping. → [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[TRANSIENT Slot]]
- **NI Velocity for its reverb + noise gate** — one particular reverb setting + a noise gate he loves "for cutting off reverbs all weird." → [[Reverb]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Three go-to house organs:** Nexus, a Serum "fishbird" pack bass/organ, and one in NI **Hybrid Keys** — sound recommendations for that classic house-organ vibe. → [[Nexus]], [[Serum 2 FX]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Full tracking front-end: SM7 → BAE 1084 → (rarely a Distressor) → B2 Bomber converter/clock** — the B2 Bomber as the converter/clock "helps quality a ton"; he usually won't compress on the way in. → [[Dua Lipa Vocal Chain]], [[Shure SM57]], [[Latency-Free Tracking]]
- **Master: Max-Martin stereo-low-end mono trick** — mono the sub on the master (learned looking over Max Martin's shoulder in Vegas). → [[Width and Stereo]], [[Master Chain|8 Master]]

## Philosophy — writer's block (Q&A)

- **Don't force it — and *check whether you're actually listening to music*** — when blocked he realizes he's only been hearing his own work; a "brain-stretch" record (he cites **Makaya McCraven** — wild time signatures) re-inspires. → [[Referencing and Translation]]
- **A/B your sound against what's currently out** — "get off your high horse and be subjective." → [[Referencing and Translation]]
- **Satisfy the A&R and artist — that's what a great producer does** — reframe criticism as the job, not an attack on your taste. → [[Ian Kirkpatrick - On Loops, Artist Ego and Originality]]
