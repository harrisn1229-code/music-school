# Lophiile on Help Me Devvon — Sound Design, Resampling & Career

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-v7NEBcX830)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-v7NEBcX830)

## Synopsis

Long-form podcast interview ("This Is Why Artists Keep Picking Lophiile's Beats"). Career arc: Sonic Foundry Acid → Reason → 7 years of Cubase → Ableton (~2015-16, sold by saved audio-effect-rack chains as reusable "sounds"), the Zedd remix-contest win at Guitar Center that got him signed to Zedd's UMPG imprint, the her "Avenue" and Coldtrain sessions, and why he stepped back from artist life ("successful artists don't give a f* what people think — that fire wasn't in me"). The production meat: his humanize-the-synth philosophy (MVibrato on everything), Serum as his from-scratch synth, the definitive statement of his resample trick, "all pitch shifters are colors — mix them," and how he builds soul: two chords, arrangement as a conversation between bass and keys, real instruments, then "make it 3008" with pitching/resampling.

## Techniques

- **Humanize synthesis — the core philosophy.** Real instruments are imperfect; synthesis is perfect by default, so "if you want it to breathe, you need to add that imperfection." His #1 tool: **MVibrato (Melda, free)** — on *everything*, sometimes only on the reverb or delay *trail*, now drawing custom LFO waveforms in it. He's tired of the default tape-emu move ("RC-20/J37 = lo-fi beats to study to"); MVibrato gives the tape vibe but more random and human. → [[Modulation]], [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **Serum for from-scratch design** — misses Massive's workflow but "Serum sounds better, so I try to just do it in Serum." → [[Serum 2]]
- **Save FX-rack chains as reusable sounds** — the thing that converted him to Ableton: build an audio-effect-rack chain, save it, and that *chain* is now an instrument in your palette you can recall forever. → [[EffectRack]], [[FX Chain System]]
- **The resample trick, definitive version:** "Once you write your part, you double the speed and throw it up an octave, bounce that fast octave-up to audio, then stretch that audio back down" (Ableton: **Re-Pitch** mode) — original key/tempo, but with artifacts that read as *sampled*, especially with tape on it. For pianos that need to feel like a sample. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Resample the whole idea into a new project** — bounce stems, open a fresh project, pitch/flip from there; "a big thing I do."
- **All pitch shifters are colors — collect and mix them:** UAD **Eventide H910** — breaks down past a semitone, but *at* a semitone gives beautiful stuttery artifacts; Pro Tools **elastic audio (X-Form)** — shifts formant with the pitch, its own flavor; Waves **SoundShifter** — the OVO sound ("I can tell the OVO guys love it"), with a tell: on drums it occasionally makes a snare super-wide (listen to the third snare on Drake's "Ratchet Happy Birthday"). "If you mix those together you start to create a pretty dope sonic landscape." Owned colors to mix: [[Little AlterBoy]], [[kHs Pitch Shifter]], [[MicroShift]], Ableton warp modes, [[PitchDrop]]. → [[Pitch and Formant]]
- **Soul production formula:** the *idea* stays simple — Leon Thomas "Mutt" is **two chords**; the interest is arrangement: "what story is the bass telling, what story are the keys telling, and do they talk to each other?" Three instruments going. Then the *sonics*: real instruments (he bought a piano, learned guitar properly, chops Splice horns) mixed with synths — and once arranged, "you go in and make it 3008 with the pitching and the resampling." → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[Bassline Writing]]
- **Kontakt + factory strings still work** — most-used Komplete instrument is [[Kontakt 7|Kontakt]]; factory-library strings are fine as parts, **8Dio** for nicer/slidy violins (Spitfire-competitor tier). → [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Artist-reference palette fluency** — artist says "I've been listening to Tears for Fears" → he's instantly at "sweet chorus, guitar, LinnDrum." Knowing era palettes cold is the session skill. → [[LinnDrum]], [[Genres Index]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Sample your own YouTube (the Devvon story)** — Devvon couldn't find the one-shot behind the "Avenue" patch, so he ripped lophiile's own breakdown video to MP3, looped 3 seconds of it, and built the record; lophiile: "there's no bigger compliment — that's the type of shit I'd be doing." (Clear it, obviously.) → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Fast records are real, but so are fights** — "Avenue" and the Coldtrain record happened in minutes ("checking ideas out from the universe — you can't deny that, that's magic"), but his own "You Changed" took 18 months; he explicitly rejects "if it's not there in 5 minutes it ain't it."
- **Magic Dice (free, Devvon's pick)** — dice-roll random reverb/delay/octave FX, no recall; Devvon snapshots the channel state to keep a roll. *(Devvon's tip, not lophiile's.)* → [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **Career notes (non-technique):** DAW dogma is fake — "use the DAW you make the best music in"; LA is a "necessary evil" for session pull-up culture (the Alina song was written and OUT within a month); London = creatively enriching, commercially slow; publisher/imprint relationships drove the her and Mick Jenkins placements; the Mick Jenkins "Carefree" beat came from making a lo-fi beat *angrily* ("wow, I can make lo-fi beats too") and it ending up genuinely good.
