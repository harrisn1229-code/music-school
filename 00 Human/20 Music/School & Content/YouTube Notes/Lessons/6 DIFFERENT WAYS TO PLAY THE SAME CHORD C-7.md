## What to watch for

- **Where each voicing sits on the neck and which string set it uses.** Six Cm7 grips will span drop-2, drop-3, rootless and shell shapes — sort them by string group as you collect them. [[Chord Voicings]].
- **Which are rootless.** A rootless Cm7 is really an E♭maj7 shape doing double duty — that is the idea behind [[Multifunctional Voicings]] and it is worth spotting live.
- **The top note of each.** Six voicings of one chord are six different melody notes; that is the whole basis of chord-melody. [[Chord Melody Arranging]].
- **Run it as a drill.** Cycle all six on Cm7, then move the set to Fm7 and B♭m7 around the cycle of fourths. See [[Chord Quality Cycle Exercise]].
