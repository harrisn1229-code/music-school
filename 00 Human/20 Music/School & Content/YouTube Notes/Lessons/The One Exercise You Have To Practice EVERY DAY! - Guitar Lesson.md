## Synopsis

Same core hammer-on/pull-off daily workout as [[The BEST Daily Finger Exercise! - Guitar Lesson]] (same channel, same week — 14 notes per string in a 7/8 measure), with the emphasis shifted toward equal-dynamics articulation and a simpler single-note starting drill before the full pattern.

## What's taught

- **Starting drill: one fretted note, all four fingers, hammer-ons and pull-offs to a metronome.** Simpler entry point than the full 14-note pattern — isolates the equal-dynamics/timing goal on a single note before adding the fretboard-spanning main exercise.
- **Named failure mode: uneven dynamics, not wrong notes.** The most common mistake isn't missing the hammer-on/pull-off technique itself but producing wildly inconsistent volume/articulation across notes — demonstrated directly (a hard picking attack followed by progressively quieter hammered notes). Fix: aim for identical dynamics and articulation across every note, picked or legato, so a listener can't tell which is which.
- **Hand-position fundamentals restated for this specific exercise:** thumb positioned behind the middle finger (not out of position for the higher-fret extension the pattern requires); cupped-hand angle with fingers curled, not flat, to avoid blocking adjacent strings.
- **Main workout: 14 notes per string, notated as 16th notes inside a 7/8 measure** — same odd-meter/note-spacing rationale as its sibling video: the irregular grouping trains rhythm/spacing alongside the hammer-on/pull-off mechanics themselves, not as a separate skill.
- Recommended dose: 5-10 minutes daily.
