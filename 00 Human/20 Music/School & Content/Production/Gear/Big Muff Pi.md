# Big Muff Pi

**Category:** Pedal (fuzz/distortion)
**Vendor:** Electro-Harmonix
**Ballpark value (used, 2026):** $60–90

## What it is

EHX's legendary four-stage fuzz — huge sustain, scooped mids, violin-like saturation. Three knobs: Volume, Tone, Sustain.

## What it's used for

Wall-of-fuzz rhythm and singing lead sustain; the shoegaze/psych/grunge food group ([[Psych-Pop (Tame Impala)]] adjacency).

## Signal chain notes

After [[Wah Pedal]], before [[Line 6 DL4]]; the mid-scoop can vanish in a band mix — stack the [[Boss DS-2]] or push amp mids to compensate.

🎬 *From video:* a second source's board runs a fuzz **before** the overdrive rather than after — "oftentimes I'll stack it just in front of my overdrive sound" (a Yard Box fuzz into a King of Tone overdrive) — the same fuzz+dirt stacking family as this pedal stacked with [[Boss DS-2]], just reversed order. Worth A/B'ing fuzz-first vs. fuzz-second rather than assuming one fixed sequence. — Jack Ruch, [My Home Recording Setup for Guitar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KYTGFneEJ2A)
