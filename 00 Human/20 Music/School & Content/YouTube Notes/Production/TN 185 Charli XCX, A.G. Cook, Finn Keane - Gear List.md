## What's shown

**Guitar pedals:**
- Boss SD-1 Super OverDrive — asymmetrical-clipping overdrive, warm tube-like tone, focused midrange/defined low end.
- Lovepedal Englishman — "Vox-in-a-box" overdrive replicating a cranked vintage Vox AC30, Volume/Gain + 3-way "Fail" switch.
- Boss PH-3 Phase Shifter — multi-stage phasing plus "Rise"/"Fall" unidirectional effects, tap-tempo sync, expression input.
- Gamechanger Audio Plasma Coil (× Third Man Records) — high-voltage distortion circuit + multi-mode octave/boost + Guitar World fuzz.
- Pro Co RAT 2 — industry-standard analog distortion, 3-knob layout, true-bypass.

**Vocal / mix plugins:**
- Waves Butch Vig Vocals — signature vocal channel strip (filters, EQ, compression, de-essing, parallel harmonic saturation).
- Soundtoys Little AlterBoy — pitch/formant vocal manipulation, hard-tune, vocoder-style MIDI effects. *(Owned: [[Little AlterBoy]].)*
- Kilohearts Multipass — modular multiband effects host, up to 5 frequency-band lanes for stacked Snapins.
- Chase Bliss CXM 1978 (× Meris) — studio reverb pedal emulating a Lexicon 224, motorized faders, 3 algorithms.
- Cableguys ShaperBox — multi-effects "Shapers" host for sound design/rhythmic motion, drawable LFOs/envelopes/triggers. *(Already tracked, curated section: [[Plugin Wishlist]].)*

**Synths / sound-design tools:**
- Sonic Charge Bitspeek — real-time LPC vocoder effect, robotic "Speak & Spell"/vintage vocoder textures. *(Owned: [[Bitspeek]].)*
- Waldorf PPG Synth (PPG Wave 3.V) — software reincarnation of the 1980s PPG Wave hybrid, wavetable synthesis + analog-style filters.
- Roland TR-606 Drumatix — 1981 analog drum machine, punchy kick/snappy snare/sizzling hi-hats, TB-303's rhythmic companion. *(Owned: [[Roland TR-606]].)*
- Moog Minimoog (Model D) — 1970 monophonic analog synth, warm/punchy bass, first synth without patch cables. *(Owned: [[Minimoog Model D]].)*
- Korg MS-20 — 1978 patchable semi-modular monosynth, aggressive HP/LP filter, External Signal Processor.
- Sequential Synths (Prophet series, incl. Prophet-5) — flagship analog/hybrid polysynth line, warm/punchy/organic sound. *(Owned equivalent: [[Sequential Prophet-5]].)*
- DataMind Audio Concatenator — AI-powered audio mosaicing/concatenative synthesis, reconstructs input from the user's own sample libraries.
- Kilohearts Phase Plant — semi-modular softsynth, drag-and-drop generators (Analog/Wavetable/Sampler/Noise/Granular) + unlimited modulators/effects.
- Vermona DRM1 MKIV — 8-voice analogue drum synth, 73 real-time-tweak knobs, no preset storage/step sequencing.
