# SCL-C01 — Natural minor

**Q.** Name the notes of A natural minor. What's the parallel-view formula?

> [!question]- Answer
> **A B C D E F G** — the white notes from A. Parallel view: **major with ♭3, ♭6 and ♭7**.

## Why it matters

Two ways in: derivative (same notes as C major, different tonic) and parallel (alter the major from the same root). The parallel formula is the one that lets you spell it from *any* root.

## Drill

Spell natural minor from A, D, E and C — using the ♭3/♭6/♭7 formula, not the relative-major shortcut.
