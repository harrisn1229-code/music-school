# HAR-C04 — Deceptive cadence

**Q.** In a deceptive cadence, V resolves to what instead of I — and why use it?

> [!question]- Answer
> **vi.** The dominant sets up a resolution to I and lands on the submediant instead — so the phrase stays open when the ear expected closure.

## Why it matters

It's the cheapest way to extend a phrase that was about to end, and vi shares two notes with I, which is exactly why the swerve is believable rather than jarring.

## Drill

Take a progression ending V–I and change it to V–vi. Then continue the phrase from there.
