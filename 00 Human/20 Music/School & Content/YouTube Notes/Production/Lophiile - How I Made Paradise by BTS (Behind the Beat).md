# Lophiile — How I Made "Paradise" by BTS (Behind the Beat)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L968H6x2sBY)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L968H6x2sBY)

## Synopsis

Full track-by-track breakdown of BTS's "Paradise": started on a plane using the Suggester iOS app to build a chord progression (inspired by Ariana Grande's "True Love") and export MIDI, sped up + half-step up on MNEK's advice, topline written by MNEK, picked up by BTS a year later. The production lesson is layering: a custom Serum pluck ("Dusty" — character comes from the noise osc) doubled by sustained Keyscape chords, a two-layer bass (clicky "How About Now" tone + sub), Omnisphere organ with an opening filter, and a chorus built from deliberately weird Massive noise patches. Also a candid "old habits" segment on how his process changed.

## Techniques

- **Sketch chords on your phone, export MIDI** — the whole beat started in the **Suggester** app (chord-progression builder) on a flight; exported MIDI, opened it in Ableton on landing. Capture the harmonic idea wherever you are. → [[Song Seeds Index|Song Seeds]], [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Speed it up + half step up for energy** — the demo was much slower ("True Love" tempo); MNEK's note was "speed it up" — a lot, plus a half-step key lift. When a track feels precious but sleepy, try the drastic tempo/key jump. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], /transpose-song
- **Character from the noise oscillator** — his [[Serum 2|Serum]] pluck patch "Dusty" is a simple patch whose entire identity is the noise layer; name-your-patch-after-the-noise energy. → [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[LEAD Slot]]
- **Pluck + sustain layering** — the twinkly pluck carries attack, the same chords *sustained* underneath (M-Audio Venom first, then [[Keyscape]]) carry body — two patches, one part. → [[BED Slot]], [[Masking and Carving]]
- **Two-layer bass: click + sub** — a "classy clicking bass" patch (his "How About Now" Drake reference tone) layered with a dedicated sub; the click reads on small speakers, the sub owns the low end. → [[BASS Slot]], [[SUB Slot]], [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]]
- **Fake pitch-bend by clicking in grace notes** — no pitch wheel on the plane, so he drew short approach notes before targets to imitate bends; kept it because it worked. → [[Bassline Writing]]
- **Omnisphere organ with opening filter + widening** for the chorus lift moment. → [[Omnisphere]], [[Stereo Field]]
- **Chorus sound design = deliberate weirdness that works in context:** [[Massive]] "machine plucks" with harsh noise, TWO reverbs stacked on one layer, Omnisphere "Baritone Boardwalk" ("Old MacDonald chords"), a one-envelope simple Serum patch, and a [[Nexus]] "tippy top" sparkle layer. "For a while I was like, what am I doing — but it ended up working."
- **Print endangered plugins** — his Nexus broke, so he had to figure out how to print the part; bounce irreplaceable sounds to audio while they still load. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Pitch automation on chopped guitar = slide guitar** — drew pitch automation on the audio of chopped guitar riffs so it reads like a lap steel; "very beachy." → [[Pitch and Formant]], [[2 Guitar Group|Guitars]]
- **Massive macro patch "RnB Twinkles"** — one macro opens a filter AND adds reverb; automate that single macro over a simple line for the bridge and it "can sound pretty." Build combo-macros so one lane does the expressive work. → [[Massive]], [[Automation]]
- **Old habits he's dropped:** no more hi-hats-to-reverb-send (uses a **sample delay** instead of pushing hats off-grid with audio nudges), and hats are MIDI now instead of audio. Interesting inversion of the usual advice — his sends count went way down over the years. → [[Depth and Space]]
