# Overdrive

![[Images/Overdrive.jpg]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Ableton

## What it is

Ableton's stock overdrive/distortion effect with a resonant filter and dry/wet control, geared toward classic tube/pedal-style overdrive tones.

## What it's used for

Guitar and synth overdrive, adding edge to basses and leads, or general lo-fi character on drum/instrument busses.

## FX chain notes

Placed early in an instrument's effects chain (before EQ) so the tonal shaping downstream can tame the added harmonics.

## Techniques

- **A very light touch (~5% dry/wet) for subtle warmth, not obvious distortion** — a much stronger setting was tried first and rejected as too much for a sensitive/delicate part; pulled back to roughly 5% wet it adds just a touch of crunch/warmth without reading as a distortion effect. → [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]] — You Suck at Producing, [Warmth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yv3v9SsoDMw)
- **Built-in notch/resonant filter adds crunch on top of plain drive** — Overdrive's Filter Band section is called out specifically for making a sound (an 808 in this case) "nice and crunchy" beyond what the Drive knob alone gives you — dial in the filter band's frequency/width to target where that extra crunch bites. — You Suck at Producing, [How to use Distortion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EolO6-evB2M)
