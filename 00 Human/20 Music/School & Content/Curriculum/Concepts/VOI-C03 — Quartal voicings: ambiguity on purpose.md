# VOI-C03 — Quartal voicings: ambiguity on purpose

**Q.** What is a quartal voicing, and why does stacking 4ths sound ambiguous where stacking 3rds does not?

> [!question]- Answer
> A quartal voicing stacks **4ths instead of 3rds** — 1-4-7 off any scale degree rather than 1-3-5. A stack of 3rds names a **quality** (major, minor, dominant) and therefore implies a function; a stack of 4ths states a set of notes **without committing to a quality**, so nothing resolves anywhere. That is the point, not a side effect.

## Why it matters

It is the clearest case of a voicing carrying harmonic meaning on its own. One fixed quartal grip reads as minor 11, ♭maj7♯11, sus4, 6/9 or major 13 depending purely on which note the ear takes as the root — the fingers never move. That is why quartal voicings are the natural vocabulary for modal playing, where re-implying a key centre is the failure mode.

## Drill

Build 3-note quartal shapes up one scale on strings 2-3-4, hold a low D drone, and play them over it. Then move the drone to another note and play the identical shapes again.
