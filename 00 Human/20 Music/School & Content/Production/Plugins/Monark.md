# Monark

**Category:** Instrument (monophonic synth)
**Vendor:** Native Instruments (Reaktor)

## What it is

Native Instruments' Minimoog Model D emulation — a monophonic three-oscillator analog-modeled synth known for its fat, characterful low end and non-linear "mangle" when pushed.

## What it's used for

Disclosure's go-to bass and lead-mono synth — Guy's stated **favorite Moog emulation**, and (with the [[Poly6]]) one of the two most-used plugins on the *ENERGY* album ("the blueprint of ENERGY"). Reach for it when a bass needs analog weight, glide, and living movement.

## Techniques
- **One glued synth that *glides*, not chopped audio** — run an entire bassline as a single Monark instance start-to-finish and automate cutoff + resonance + 2–3 oscillators coming in/out for texture, so it rises/falls and glides between mangled moments rather than sounding like multiple presets. → [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]] — Disclosure, [Who Knew](https://youtu.be/e9d7KYEG3VM)
- **Detuned-oscillator drone for movement** — two oscillators pulled slightly sharp/flat, bouncing off each other, make a constantly-moving bed under a track. → [[BED Slot]] / [[TEXTURE Slot]] — Disclosure, [Never Enough](https://youtu.be/lGopQwQo9Ag)
- **Perform the filter live** — map cutoff/resonance to MIDI knobs and ride them by hand (then neaten) so the soft synth breathes like a performed analog. → [[Automation]], [[Modulation]] — Disclosure, [Who Knew](https://youtu.be/e9d7KYEG3VM)

## FX chain notes

Used as the bass/lead source; typically followed by light glue compression, saturation ([[Decapitator]]/bit-crush), and a **sub-only multiband sidechain** to the kick ([[Sidechaining]]). Keep the sub mono ([[Width and Stereo]]).

## Key Parameters

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Oscillators (×3) | Minimoog-model oscillators | Detune/stack for the fat Moog bass/lead |
| ② | Filter | The ladder filter (Moog character) | Resonance + drive for warmth/squelch |
| ③ | Envelopes | Amp/filter contours | Shape the punch and movement |
| ④ | Glide | Portamento between notes | On for classic Moog bass slides |
