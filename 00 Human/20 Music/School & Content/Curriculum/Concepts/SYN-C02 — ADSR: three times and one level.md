# SYN-C02 — ADSR: three times and one level

**Q.** In ADSR, which parameters are *times* and which are *levels*?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Sustain is the only level. Attack, decay and release are all times.**

## Why it matters

Filing decay as a level is the classic ADSR misread, and the reason decay knobs never behave the way people expect. Decay is a time because it describes a journey: how long to fall *from* the attack peak *down to* the sustain level. Sustain is a destination, not a duration, and it lasts exactly as long as you hold the key.

## Drill

Say out loud: rise over A, fall over D until you reach S, hold, then fall to zero over R. Then set sustain to 100% and explain why decay now does nothing.
