# LFO Tool

![[Images/LFO Tool.png]]

**Category:** Volume/Filter LFO Shaper
**Vendor:** Xfer Records

## What it is

Xfer's LFO-driven volume/filter/pan shaper — draw a custom curve and it modulates the signal in sync with the tempo. The standard tool for **rhythmic sidechain ducking** without a compressor, and Ian Kirkpatrick's self-described favorite plugin. (Ableton equivalents exist via a shaped envelope / the stock LFO on a Utility, but LFO Tool's drawn curves are the workflow.)

## What it's used for

Pumping/ducking, rhythmic gating, and creative volume shaping — from a subtle groove duck to chopping a vocal into slivers.

## Techniques

- **Shape a supporting key layer's filter into a rising/falling movement** — after a supporting key layer was added purely to fill out low-mid frequencies, an LFO Tool pass gave it a rising-and-falling cutoff-style motion rather than leaving it static — a quick way to add movement to a held/pad-like part without automating a filter cutoff by hand. → [[BASS Slot]], [[BED Slot]] — loner, [do you need pedals to make industry level melodies!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H-3SmPmGJOg)
- **His favorite plugin — rhythmic ducking + volume shaping** — used everywhere: duck the hats hard, pump a synth, or make it "more about the hit" by softening a transient. → [[Sidechaining]] — Ian Kirkpatrick, [Pretty Please (24 Tips)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j5aA59B_T58) / [Interesting Drum Hits](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/MNWckYiVVhY)
- **Automate the duck ON at the drop** — off in the build, switched on exactly when the chorus hits so a chopped vocal only pumps in the hook. → [[Automation]] — Ian Kirkpatrick, [HIT Vocal Chain](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LMjkfMIhXdA)
- **Near-inaudible fast duck to clear the kick** — a very short, tiny curve so the bass gets out of the way for the smallest second ("almost inaudible sidechain"). → [[Kick Design]] — Ian Kirkpatrick, [Making Music](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHwc5Xrxm04)
- **MIDI-triggered gating** — trigger LFO Tool from MIDI notes to let only slivers of a vocal (or a whole synth group) through on a rhythm — "put it on the master and gate everything from your keyboard." → [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]] — Ian Kirkpatrick, [FFF (Zara Larsson)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ge9PAxBdOg8)
- **Turn a crowd sample into a riser** — shape a crowd/noise sound into an "up"/riser; the only difference between Pretty Please's verse 1 and 2. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]] — Ian Kirkpatrick, [Pretty Please (24 Tips)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j5aA59B_T58)

## Key Parameters

| Parameter | What it does | Artist / record example |
|---|---|---|
| Curve / points | The drawn LFO shape per bar | The duck shape; chop-to-slivers |
| Rate | Sync division (e.g. 1/2 bar) | Half-bar duck — Ian Kirkpatrick, Look At Her Now |
| Depth / mix | How much modulation | ~65% on New Rules' chorus synth |
| MIDI trigger | Gate from MIDI notes | Rhythmic vocal gating — Ian Kirkpatrick, FFF |
