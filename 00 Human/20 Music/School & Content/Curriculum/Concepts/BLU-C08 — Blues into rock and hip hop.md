# BLU-C08 — Blues into rock and hip hop

**Q.** Name a way blues harmony or form shows up in rock or hip hop, and trace how it got there.

> [!question]- Answer
> **Rock** took the 12-bar form and the pentatonic vocabulary almost wholesale — early Beatles and Stones material is blues form with different production. **Hip hop inherited it through sampling**: soul and funk records built on blues harmony got sampled, so the ♭3/♭7 vocabulary and call-and-response phrasing survive in the loops even where the 12-bar form doesn't.

## Why it matters

It's the question that explains why this strand matters to the music you actually make. The dominant-7th-as-home-sound and the blue-note melodic language are still present in genres that essentially never play a 12-bar.

## Drill

Find the blues vocabulary in one hip hop track and one Beatles track you know.
