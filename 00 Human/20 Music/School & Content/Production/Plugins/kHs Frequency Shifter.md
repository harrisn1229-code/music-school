# kHs Frequency Shifter

![[Images/kHs Frequency Shifter.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A frequency-shifting (as opposed to pitch-shifting) snap-in producing inharmonic, robotic tonal shifts.

## What it's used for

Metallic, robotic, or dissonant texture effects distinct from standard pitch-shifting.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain for sound-design effects.
