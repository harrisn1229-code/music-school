# Course 21 — Hanon III

The opening of Hanon's Part II, the transcendent series — exercises 21 to 31, where both hands trade the same figure across the bar and the writing leaves the single-page format.

**What it assumes**

- [[Course 20 — Hanon II]] — Hanon is cumulative and these exercises assume the ones before them are still in the warm-up.
- A piano or keyboard, [[The Virtuoso Pianist - Hanon.pdf]] open on a stand, and a metronome.
- No theory. This course teaches no ideas and tests none — it is the place you play the exercise.

**What this unlocks**

- [[Course 22 — Hanon IV]] — the next block of exercises, which assumes these are in the hand.
- Both hands equally fluent on the same figure — the mirroring is what the transcendent series is for, and it is the last thing before the book stops being about fingers alone.
- The evenness the thumb-under work in exercises 32–38 needs before scales are worth attempting.

## How to run it

These are Hanon's own instructions, printed in the book, and they are the method rather than preamble. They apply to every lesson below, so each lesson states only what is particular to its exercise.

- **The metronome mark is a range, not a target.** Start at the low number, get it clean and even, then move up. Arriving at the top number with an uneven hand is not a pass.
- ***"Lift the fingers high and with precision, playing each note very distinctly."***
- **Both hands carry the same difficulty throughout** — the figure the left hand plays ascending is copied by the same fingers of the right descending. That refusal to let the weak hand off is the whole design of the book.
- **Hanon is cumulative.** *"As soon as Ex. 1 is mastered"* means move on, not stop playing it. Finished exercises stay in the warm-up.
- Page numbers below are the PDF's own count, 1–116, matching the `pages` parameter — not the printed book-page numbers visible in the scan.

### Lesson 1 — Exercise 21: The Transcendent Series Opens: 3-4-5

**The material**

- **What it trains** — the 3-4-5 pattern that opens Hanon's transcendent series — the left hand's figure on beat 1 is mirrored by the right hand on beat 3. Part II, pp. 21–22 of [[The Virtuoso Pianist - Hanon.pdf]].
- **Tempo** — ♩=60, worked gradually up to ♩=108. Part II restates Part I's block mark at its head and prints nothing new until exercise 32, so this one carries the block figure.

## Course final

A **performance check**, not a test page. There are no `type: school-test` pages in this course and nothing here enters the spaced-repetition drip — finger facility is a motor skill, and the drip is for facts.

The check is a rep: play exercises 21 through 31 back to back, in order, at the tempo each one is marked for, fingers high and precise, both hands equal. Pass is your own honest read of whether it was clean and even. Nothing in the system can hear the piano, so nothing here pretends to grade it.
