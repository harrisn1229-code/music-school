# 5 Habits That RADICALLY Improved My Guitar Playing!

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_dAlQzHh8ww)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_dAlQzHh8ww)

## Synopsis

A mindset/methodology video, not a technique lesson — BERNTH lists five non-exercise habits he credits for his own progress: practice-first scheduling, recording and sharing videos, taking jobs outside his comfort zone, cutting alcohol, and teaching. No tabs, licks or theory content.

## What's taught

- **Make practice the first daily task, before anything else (except breakfast).** Reasons given: most energy/focus available early (after coffee), and it avoids the degraded focus of practicing after 8-10 hours of work. His own structure: a 1-hour practice routine split into four 15-minute blocks, with topics/content rotated regularly to stay interesting. Explicitly separates this from his earlier approach of "just sitting down and playing randomly for a couple hours," which he says never produced the same progress curve — the fix was **separating free playing from goal-directed exercise practice**, not doing more of either.
- **Record and share guitar videos regularly, deliberately made challenging.** Every video/lesson is built to challenge his own technique or theory knowledge, not just demonstrate what he already has down. Three stated benefits: (1) camera pressure raises the bar versus private practice, (2) accomplishment/community feedback from finishing and sharing a take, (3) a running video record to compare against later — explicitly recommends revisiting old footage to see real progress when motivation is low.
- **Take jobs/gigs outside your comfort zone.** Cites accepting acoustic-guitar sideman work (not his preferred setting) and slide/bottleneck studio work despite feeling like a non-expert — both cited as genuinely improving his playing and technique. Framed as a corrective for over-confidence: an in-ear-monitored acoustic gig on stage exposes small technical details a comfortable setting hides.
- **Cut alcohol.** Personal habit change (one year alcohol-free at time of filming), motivated by lost productive days after touring/shows/sessions — framed as a practical productivity cost, not a moral stance.
- **Teach/answer student questions.** Working through other players' problems (via his Patreon community — analyzing footage, answering questions, reviewing recordings) is credited with revealing his own blind spots and surfacing the most common, highest-value exercises/fixes across many different students — feeding back into his own practice and his ability to help others more precisely.
