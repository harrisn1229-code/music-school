# Devil-Loc

![[Images/Devil-Loc.png]]

**Category:** Compressor/Limiter
**Vendor:** Soundtoys

## What it is

Soundtoys' aggressive compressor modeled on an extreme vintage compression circuit, known for its crushed, 'stuck-down' character.

## What it's used for

Extreme, characterful compression for drum room mics, bus crushing, and aggressive parallel compression.

## FX chain notes

Often used in parallel (blended with a dry signal) on drum busses for maximum character without losing all dynamics.

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Crush | Drives the extreme gain-reduction/limiting circuit — how hard the signal gets flattened. | This is the whole plugin. Push it until the source stops breathing, then decide whether you want that; there's no threshold to hide behind. |
| ② | Crunch | Amount of added saturation/distortion character on top of the crush. | Crush sets *how squashed*, Crunch sets *how dirty*. On a room mic, a lot of Crush with modest Crunch gives you weight without fizz. |

> **Only two knobs.** This is the original Devil-Loc — it has just Crush and Crunch. The **Tone (Dark)** and **Mix** controls, plus Release and Darkness, are on [[Devil-Loc Deluxe]]. If you need to blend the destroyed signal under the clean one, either use the Deluxe or put this on a parallel send.
