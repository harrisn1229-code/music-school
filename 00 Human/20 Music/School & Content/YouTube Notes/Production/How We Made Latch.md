# How We Made Latch

Guy breaks down "Latch" (feat. Sam Smith) — their breakout — and the through-line is *you don't need fancy gear*: the entire record is **Logic stock plugins** (Logic delay, reverb, compressors, both synths). Most valuable here: the **synthesized kick**, the **Burial-style field-recording texture** that sits under the whole song, the minimal Sam Smith vocal chain (great mic → Neve → barely any plugins), the **whisper-for-air** trick, and the F-minor-that-avoids-its-root chords. Whisper transcript (no captions) — high confidence, spoken.

## Techniques
- **Whole record = Logic stock plugins** — Logic delay/reverb/compressors + both synths (ESP + FM). "It is definitely just Logic; you don't need fancy gear to make a hit." Mixing philosophy over gear GAS.
- **Synthesized kick** — the kick is a *synth note*, not a sample: pitch it to the song's root and add a short downward pitch-envelope for the click. → [[TRANSIENT Slot]] body into [[SUB Slot]]; see the new [[Kick Design]] note and [[1 Drums Kick Track|Drums Kick Track]]. *(Settings starting point: sine/tri body tuned to F1, pitch env +12→0 st over ~15–30 ms, amp decay ~200–400 ms to taste.)*
- **Two claps layered for depth** — one nearly dry, one drenched in reverb, blended → a close+far clap. → [[TRANSIENT Slot]] + [[Stereo Field]]; Roland-drum-machine sounds + a pitched-down weird percussion hit "to be different" rather than straight 808/909.
- **Sub-drops via the Ultrabeat envelope** — automate a pitch/amp envelope diving down for riser subs → [[SUB Slot]]; keeps the chorus "going into liftoff" after a sparse pre.
- **One drum MIDI region, low→high** — kick at the bottom, hats up top, so the whole kit reads in one clean lane. Groove detail: **snare–clap–snare–clap** alternation, not four identical hits.
- **Gymnasium (Logic) reverb as the bus verb** — the go-to Settle-era bus reverb: load it, **take some lows out**, ~2 s decay. → [[Reverb Send Room]] / [[Reverb Send Long]]; high-pass the return so it doesn't muddy [[BASS Slot]]; sits the kit in a space (feels dead without it, "almost adds distortion").
- **Field-recording texture under the whole song** — the sound of a road runs under all of Latch (the Burial influence: ambient soundscapes + vinyl crackle) for a nighttime/headphones feel. → [[TEXTURE Slot]]; see the new [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]] note. Monotron for noise buildups.
- **Early drum bus = just a limiter** — no bus saturation or comp back then; per-drum EQ/comp/reverb/delay instead, then a Logic limiter (contrast the current [[3 Drums Bus|Drums Bus]] which stacks saturation). Tremolo for stereo beep panning → [[Stereo Field]].
- **F-minor that avoids its root** — bass notes dodge the F, D♭s and "naive voicings" make it dark; the kick hits F every bar to establish the key while nothing else lands "home." → [[LEAD Slot]] chord writing; see [[Intervals]] / [[Chord Naming by Context]].
- **Stacked subs into impact** — three subs building to the kick/vocal hit → [[SUB Slot]]; [[Decimort 2]] on the fast sub for sampler grunge.
- **Sam Smith vocal, minimal by design** — recorded through a great mic (Telefunken/Neumann) into a Neve channel, so it's "good on the way in": just a **tiny Logic DS high-boost + Logic comp ~5:1**, a **tape-delay send**, and a **100% feedback delay loop muted at the drop**. → [[LEAD Slot]], [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]], [[Delay Send Slap]] / [[Delay Send Other]].
- **Whisper-for-air** — record the singer whispering the line, bury it under the lead: adds air/sheen without harsh S's (the Mariah / Mary J. Blige R&B trick). → [[AIR Slot]] + [[De-essing]].
- **Verse = 1 vocal, chorus = 5–6 stacked** for width and size; no auto-tune ("just Sam singing excellently") → [[LEAD Slot]] + [[Stereo Field]] + [[2 Main Vocal Group|Main Vocal Group]].
- **ESP synth (Juno-106 copy) chords + Ring Shifter + ~3.2 ms glide** — the glide is inaudible until the octave-jump ending, where it smears musically. → [[BED Slot]] + [[1 Synth Bed Track|Synth Bed Track]].
