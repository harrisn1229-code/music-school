# Disclosure - 'Talk' with Khalid Twitch Breakdown

Guy and Howard break down "Talk" (feat. Khalid). Best material: the **mono→wide drop trick** (make a chorus bigger without turning it up), the guitar-bus chain built around a **Surfer EQ** and a **DBX 160**, Khalid's mostly-pre-processed vocal getting just Inflator + Gem Dopamine + Vocal Rider, and the top-note-first chord-writing method. Whisper transcript (no captions) — high confidence.

## Techniques
- **Mono→wide on the drop** — make a chorus feel bigger *without volume*: keep the verse mono, then on the drop mute the reverb and **fade in a chorus + rotary (Roto Cab)** so it opens to full width. Fastest tool: the **[[RC-20 Retro Color]] "Whip"/space knob** mono→wide; or just switch on a flanger/ chorus/phaser for the drop. → [[Stereo Field]] + [[Width and Stereo]] + [[Width Send]] + [[Modulation]].
- **Main synth = a free plugin ("Sinister")** + **[[Monark]]** bass — "our most-streamed song, on a free synth; you don't need expensive plugins." → [[BED Slot]] / [[BASS Slot]].
- **Fade-in *and* fade-out** (Motown, anti-radio) — starts in silence, ends fading. Heavy compression on the intro synth, cranked, to boost level up out of the fade. → arrangement; [[Compression]].
- **Placeholder MIDI horns left in** — one Logic horn per trumpet (separate instances for stereo) meant as a scratch for real brass they forgot to replace; "sounded fine, what's the point." → [[MIDS Slot]].
- **Guitar (Telecaster, 5 notes)** clean into a **Neve (a bit of drive) → DBX 160** ("best comp for clicky guitar; I'd track through one") → guitar bus: **overdrive → Surfer EQ → more EQ to boost the click**, plus [[Decapitator]]. → [[2 Guitar Group|Guitars]] chains; [[MIDS Slot]] / [[LEAD Slot]].
  - **Surfer EQ (note-following):** instead of ducking a fixed band, it *tracks the note* and moves the EQ band with it — one moving notch does the work of five fixed ones. Guy used it a lot pre-Pro-Q-3. → also on the **[[Monark]] bass** ("take out the next harmonic up, not the sub"). On the wishlist: [[Plugin Wishlist]].
- **Khalid's vocal comes pre-processed** by his engineer (Neve/Tube-Tech-ish, de-essed in Pro Tools) → Guy adds almost nothing: **[[Oxford Inflator]]** first → EQ (control low-mids, **dynamically boost the high-mids**, +1.3 dB air, cut lows so it doesn't fight the synths) → **[[Gem Dopamine]]** for treble/ air without loud S's → **Vocal Rider** (subtle, instead of hand-automating the falsetto↔full-voice jumps). No de-esser needed. → [[LEAD Slot]] + [[AIR Slot]] + [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]]; see [[Automation]] for the "rider instead of manual volume automation" call.
- **Reverb: cut the lows** (too boomy) and **sidechain it to the kick** so the tail ducks under the beat → [[Reverb Send Long]] + [[Sidechaining]].
- **Top-note-first chord writing** — Howard hears the top *melodic* note of each chord and voices the rest underneath it, so progressions carry a melody (F maj9 → Em → Dm, ending F maj7/G). → [[LEAD Slot]] writing; [[Chord Naming by Context]].
- **Kick design:** 808/battery jack kit → **Softube saturation + overdrive** to beef it up + EQ (tame super-highs) + comp for punch → [[Kick Design]] + [[1 Drums Kick Track|Drums Kick Track]] + [[Saturation and Distortion]].
- **Sneaky sidechained percussion** — heavily distorted perc buried in the mix that only pokes out when the sidechain pump releases (brings up the hats) → [[Sidechaining]] + [[TEXTURE Slot]]. Clicks (dry + a wet reverb copy, sidechained).
- **Sample-delay widening** — a mono camera/dial-turn one-shot made wide with a short sample delay L/R → [[Stereo Field]] + [[Modulation]]. Reversed sweeps for transitions.
- **Rainforest noise in the chorus** as a subtle texture bed → [[TEXTURE Slot]] + [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]].
- **Tension via effects on/off, not more instruments** — mute/unmute reverb+delay, stick to two synths; "the most Disclosure thing about it." → [[Automation]].
- **Fourths/fifths harmonies** ("medieval," guitar-like thickening) — Khalid's signature interval choice.
