## Techniques

### The delayed sidechain (the big one)
- **Three trigger lanes: kick / straight sidechain / DELAYED sidechain** — UAD plugins run outside the computer, and Soothe/Inflator are latent, so a straight sidechain lands out of sync in Logic; he keeps a **delay-compensated duplicate of the sidechain trigger** and feeds latent chains from that. If the pump feels late, the trigger — not the compressor — is the fix. → [[Sidechaining]], [[Latency-Free Tracking]]

### The Prince edit (rescuing a YouTube rip)
- **Rip-rescue chain: EQ out the digital hiss → [[Soothe2]] → [[Oxford Inflator]] → [[Gem Dopamine]]** (test the highs first — "I don't think any highs really exist in the sample"). → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[RX Voice De-noise]] lane
- **Warp in Ableton, produce in Logic** — "I just prefer Ableton's algorithms in terms of how it warps"; warp/chop the sample in Live, then bounce into the Logic session. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Moog-filter sweep to de-loop a sample** — "getting a bit bored of the sample looping round — let's try the Moog filter" with resonance; movement instead of new material. → [[Auto Filter]] lane, [[Automation]]
- **"It's in the key of Prince"** — the rip "has all the chords, it's not even in a key really"; find the pocket by ear and stop worrying. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Send masters with headroom** — −10 to −8 dB peaks, "6 is okay but I wouldn't send anything above 6 — give them room to work." (Matches the Ultimatum rule.) → [[Master Chain]]

### Percussion & width
- **"Always overdrive your shakers — ALWAYS, big time"** — the distortion "reminds me of how a shaker sounds on a 7-inch record — it just breaks up"; he's permanently chasing that breakup. → [[2 Drums Hats Group|Drums Hats Group]], [[Saturation and Distortion]]
- **Straight hat under a swung hat, sidechained rhythmically** — a quiet straight hi-hat layered under the swung one adds "a bit of flaming" and fills gaps without sounding busy; works because the afrobeat loop underneath is straight while everything else swings. → [[2 Drums Hats Group|Drums Hats Group]] — Sol State, [In the Studio: Disclosure "Talk VIP" (11 Best Tips from Twitch)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XR38TQ685-A)
- **Pans drums from the drummer's POV, not the listener's** — hi-hat goes left (not the usual right) because "that's where it is for me when I'm playing"; toms follow the same logic. Panned elements also get turned down since panning already makes them cut through. → [[Width and Stereo]] — Sol State, [In the Studio: Disclosure "Talk VIP" (11 Best Tips from Twitch)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XR38TQ685-A)
- **Haas-delay panning for aux perc** — cabasa "super left and right" by **delaying one channel ~680 samples**; small amounts = separation, large = the sound splits in two. → [[Width and Stereo]], [[Utility]]
- **[[Roland TR-8]]-style step sequencing + polyrhythms, then pull it off the grid.** → [[2 Drums Group|Drums Group]]

### Talk VIP + synth layering
- **VIP = re-tempo the vocals** — Talk sits at ~68 half-time; the VIP warps the same vocals (slowed, Ableton-warped) into a 129 BPM track. A remix lane for own material. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Minimoog emu carries the bass (volume automation only) + OBXD layered on top** — "the Prince machine" (free plugin!) adds the squelchy top the Moog doesn't have. **Mix multiple basses by giving each its own frequency band.** → [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]], [[Masking and Carving]], [[Plugin Wishlist]] (OBXD, free)
- **Bass guitar as a third layer (max 2-3 basses)** — on top of the Minimoog+OBXD stack, a real bass guitar track is cut at 100 Hz (sub) and cut on top (kills finger noise), leaving just the octave above the sub to thicken it; "I wouldn't usually layer my basses more than two or three." → [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]], [[Masking and Carving]] — Sol State, [In the Studio: Disclosure "Talk VIP" (11 Best Tips from Twitch)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XR38TQ685-A)
- **CLA-compressor on vocals as a volume rider** (quiet verses vs big moments). → [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]]

### Listening-sesh feedback one-liners
- **The deadmau5 clap** — "so ahead of the kick, so clean, cuts through everything, and the mix of everything else is down there."
- **"Cut that one rogue kick"** — one sticking-out hit ruins an otherwise-good loop; **"too clean — it needs dirt"** on a vocoder vocal; **"I like everything except…"** — feedback is always one specific element, not vibes. → [[Referencing and Translation]]
