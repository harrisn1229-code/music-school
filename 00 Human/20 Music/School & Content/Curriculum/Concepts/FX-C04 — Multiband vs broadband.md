# FX-C04 — Multiband vs broadband

**Q.** When would you reach for multiband compression instead of broadband?

> [!question]- Answer
> **When only part of the spectrum has the dynamic problem.** Broadband sees one band's peaks and pulls the *whole* signal down with them — a vocal whose low-mids bloom on certain notes gets ducked entirely on those notes. Multiband compresses just that range.

## Why it matters

Knowing the trigger is the easy half; the failure mode is the useful one. The 'whole signal ducks' part is what makes the choice obvious in practice.

## Drill

Find a source where one band misbehaves. Fix it broadband, then multiband. Listen to what broadband costs.
