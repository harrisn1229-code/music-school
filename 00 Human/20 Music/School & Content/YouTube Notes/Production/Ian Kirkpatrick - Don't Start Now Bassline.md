# Ian Kirkpatrick — "Don't Start Now" Bassline

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iJSqHfQZLSo)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iJSqHfQZLSo)

## Synopsis

How the "Don't Start Now" bass — the song's disco engine — is actually built from **three layers**: a simple printed **NI Scarbee** bass patch (main note, but light on low end), a **sine sub** mixed under it for weight, and a separate **"slap" layer** from **Trilian's Atmosphere patch**. In the pre-chorus he swaps in a "big and clicky" **Trilian** sound to pull *away* from the pure-disco feel for a moment.

## Techniques

- **Main bass = NI Scarbee (printed), but it lacks low end** — the finger-bass character/note source, committed to audio; on its own it's thin. → [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[1 Electric Bass DI Track]]
- **Add a sine sub underneath for the low end** — a plain sine layer tuned to the bass note fills [[SUB Slot]] that Scarbee doesn't cover. → [[SUB Slot]], [[Bassline Writing]], [[Layering and Stacking]]
- **The "slaps" are a separate patch — Trilian "Atmosphere"** — the percussive slap articulation comes from a different Trilian patch layered in, not the main bass. → [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[TRANSIENT Slot]]
- **Pre-chorus: a "big and clicky" Trilian sound to leave the disco** — deliberately swap timbre in the pre to "go somewhere else" before the disco chorus returns. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
