# Ghz Midside Matrix 3

![[Images/Ghz Midside Matrix 3.png]]

**Category:** Pan/Imaging
**Vendor:** Goodhertz

## What it is

Goodhertz's Mid/Side Matrix, offering flexible M/S encoding, decoding, and matrix routing options beyond a basic M/S split.

## What it's used for

Advanced mid/side routing and width manipulation for mix and mastering work.

## FX chain notes

Used around mid/side-specific processing chains on a bus or master.
