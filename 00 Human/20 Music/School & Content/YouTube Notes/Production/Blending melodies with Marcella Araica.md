# Blending melodies with Marcella Araica

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OqdukOXzF7s)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OqdukOXzF7s)

## Synopsis

A short, screen-recorded Pro Tools clip of Marcella Araica (Mix with the Masters) working two separate melodic elements of the same record: a doubled/harmonized string pair (a lower and a higher string line) and an 808 that Danja has placed twice in the same verse. On the strings, she demonstrates a sub-mix-first workflow — route the harmony pair through a shared, labeled Aux, EQ each part individually to carve them apart from each other and from the (not-yet-processed) bass, then glue the pair with a UA 33609 stereo compressor on the Aux itself. On the 808, the clip is a monitoring-and-color pass: she mixes 808 low end primarily on NS-10s (notoriously bad at translating sub content, which forces careful decisions) before confirming on the mains, adds distortion purely for grit and cut-through, and reaches for the Pultec EQP-1A as her go-to low-end EQ for its emulated-hardware "sense of bottom." The clip closes mid-thought, checking whether Danja's second 808 hit in the verse is a genuine duplicate of the first or a distinct sound. Light on hard numbers for the 808 stage (qualitative — "add distortion," "I love this EQ") but the string EQ carve gives three concrete frequency values, and the sub-mix-first routing habit and NS-10-primary monitoring choice are both concrete, transferable workflow moves.

## Techniques

- **Route a harmonized/doubled melodic pair (here, a lower string line + a higher string line singing the same phrase) through one shared, labeled Aux before any bus processing — sub-mix first, individual EQ per track, glue after.** "When I get sounds like this where I'm basically getting a harmony of another... I will just create a little sub-mix for it and just route everything through that, and process everything through there. I'll do some individual EQ'ing on each particular track as well, so everything is going to be now coming out of this Aux." Labeling the Aux is called out explicitly as its own habit: "it's important to always label, to keep things organized." → [[Session Templates]], [[Masking and Carving]] — Mix with the Masters (Marcella Araica)
- **Complementary EQ carve between the two string parts so their boosts never overlap in the same band — high-pass the low part to leave room for the (not-yet-mixed) bass, then trade a body boost for a body cut between the pair.** On the low string: high-pass **60–90 Hz** specifically because "I know my bass, which I haven't done anything with yet, is going to be somewhere in that world, and I don't want it to get interference" — clearing the [[BASS Slot]] pre-emptively for an instrument that hasn't even been touched yet, not reacting to a collision already heard. Body is then restored with a boost around **300–400 Hz** ("bringing back the body of the string"). On the high string, roughly the same **~300 Hz** area is instead CUT — "with the high strings, I'm actually taking out somewhere around 300 just because I'm boosting [elsewhere]... there's no need for me to have any type of overlapping boosts in the frequency where I'm actually trying to take away." A worked example of never letting two related parts both boost the same [[BED Slot]] band — one part owns the boost, its pair gets a cut there instead. → [[Masking and Carving]], [[BASS Slot]], [[BED Slot]], [[EQ]] — Mix with the Masters (Marcella Araica)
- **A UA 33609 stereo bus compressor as an automatic, near-default reach for gluing a strings sub-mix — "that stereo compressor always is wonderful for strings."** Applied on the Aux *after* the individual per-track EQ carve above, not per-track. "There's a number of different things that you could use but that's kind of automatic, I'm like, okay, I'm going to go for this." No ratio/threshold/GR figures given — a stated go-to choice by instrument type (strings) rather than a worked setting. Owned nearest-lane stand-in: [[API-2500]] (already the general owned bus-glue reach in this vault); unowned unit already tracked on [[Plugin Wishlist]] from a separate Marcella Araica video (drum/music-bus use — this is a new, strings-specific data point for the same unit). → [[Compression Staging]], [[API-2500]], [[Plugin Wishlist]] — Mix with the Masters (Marcella Araica)
- **Mix 808 low-end decisions primarily on NS-10s — a monitor famously stingy on sub — before ever checking the mains.** "They're usually really difficult to hear on NS-10s, but... I'm trying to do everything on the NS-10, and then if I have to, I'll go up on the mains just to kind of get the feel of it." The stated logic (implicit): if a low-end decision reads right on a monitor that flatters nothing down there, it'll translate everywhere else too — mains are a confirmation pass, not the primary judgment call. → [[Referencing and Translation]], [[SUB Slot]] — Mix with the Masters (Marcella Araica)
- **Add distortion to the 808 purely for grit and cut-through, not tone-matching or harmonic reinforcement.** "Another thing that I love to do is add distortion to the 808s. It just brings it a nice grit. Little tricks that I use to kind of cut things through." No plugin named, no drive/mix figures given — a qualitative endorsement of distortion-as-audibility-tool on sub content, consistent with this vault's own 808-chain thesis (driving the *heard* mids rather than the *felt* sub is what makes an 808 cut through small speakers). → [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[Bass Design]], [[SUB Slot]] — Mix with the Masters (Marcella Araica)
- **The Pultec EQP-1A named directly as a go-to low-end EQ, praised specifically for how closely the plugin emulates the hardware's "sense of bottom."** "I love this Pultec EQP-1A EQ, which is another one of my go-to EQs when I'm doing anything in the low end. I feel like it just brings a sense of bottom that is emulated pretty much like the exact hardware." Used here on the 808 stage, after the distortion pass — a plain endorsement (no exact Low Boost/Atten frequency or amount given) rather than a worked setting, but a direct confirmation of the classic Pultec low-boost trick's low-end character from a working mix engineer. → [[UAD Pultec EQP-1A]], [[SUB Slot]] — Mix with the Masters (Marcella Araica)
- **A producer (Danja) doubling an 808 hit within the same verse — flagged by the mixer as worth ear-checking rather than assumed identical.** "Danja has put another 808 in this verse. Well, it's actually not another one because the original 808 comes out during the verse so he has this particular 808 here hitting twice... I'm going to hear to see if they're almost the same." Song-specific arrangement detail (no general technique stated beyond "check by ear before assuming") — noted here as the master record; not broad enough on its own to warrant a technique-note entry. *(clip ends mid-thought)*
