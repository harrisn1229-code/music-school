# MEL-C05 — Register and intensity

**Q.** What's the relationship between a melody's register/range and a section's emotional intensity?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Higher reads as more intense** — partly convention, partly physiology, since a singer audibly works harder up there. So **verses sit lower and choruses lift**, and the lift is much of what makes a chorus feel like one. **Widening the range** does similar work: bigger leaps and a wider span feel more dramatic than a melody circling three notes.

## Why it matters

The practical payoff: if a chorus isn't landing, check where it sits *relative to the verse* before rewriting it. Moving it up a third often does more than new notes.

## Drill

Take a chorus that isn't landing. Move it up a third without changing anything else.
