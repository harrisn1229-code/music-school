# H-Delay

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** "Waves"

## What it is

A hybrid analog/digital-style delay plugin known for its tape-style character and modulation.

## What it's used for

Vocal-send delay work — slap/echo and rhythmic ping-pong repeats, with tape-style modulation available for character.

## FX chain notes

Used on a send/return, after EQ/compression/leveling on the dry vocal, alongside a reverb send for the vocal's "imaging."

## Techniques

- **Kept a producer's original vocal-delay send as-is rather than rebuilding it, paired with a Valhalla delay and a ~1.5 kHz EQ cut on the return's harsh midrange.** "So with this delay, this is also from Mark, H-Delay, Vintage Valhalla. This Valhalla is so easy to use. And then some EQ'ing on the harsh stuff, whatever midrange, what is that? 1.5 kHz?" Part of a "build off a good producer mix rather than replace it" philosophy running through the whole session. → [[ValhallaDelay]], [[Delay]] (effect), [[Dua Lipa Vocal Chain]] — Mix with the Masters (Josh Gudwin), [[Mixing Dua Lipa's vocals with Josh Gudwin]]
- **Quarter-note ping-pong, used as a fixed always-the-same delay send on every vocal.** "I always use the H-Delay, quarter note ping-pong" — same setting reached for on every session rather than tailored per song, paired with a fixed R-Verb medium-hall reverb send for the vocal's overall imaging/space.
