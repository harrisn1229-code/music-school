## Synopsis

A beat-battle-day R&B cook-up whose highlight is Brass Tracks (Ivan) dropping in remotely and playing/recording/comping a full horn section in ~20 minutes. Mostly music and stream banter — thin on spoken technique, but three real takeaways survive: real players over sample packs, the Deco pedal used as deliberate "lag" on the Rhodes, and levels revealing themselves relative to each new element. **Watch-mostly** — the vibe/arrangement lives in the audio, not the transcript.

## Techniques

- **Real players beat Splice — cultivate musician collaborators** — "sick of Splice samples? Sorry, you have to personally be friends with Brass Tracks": a player friend delivering played/recorded/comped parts in 20 minutes is the actual cheat code ("that's called cheating and I love doing it"). Build the collaborator list like a sample library. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Deco pedal "for some lag" on the Rhodes** — deliberately monitoring/printing the Rhodes through a Strymon Deco for tape-machine lag/slew — the behind-the-beat feel as a *hardware artifact*, not quantize. Owned lane: [[Cassette]] or [[EchoBoy]] tape mode with a touch of single-repeat delay, or a small negative track delay. → [[1 Electric Piano Track|Electric Piano Track]]
- **Levels reveal themselves one element at a time** — "sometimes I don't realize where my levels are at till I add one thing and then — why is my hi-hat louder than the sample?" Every new element is a fresh referee on the balance; re-check the mix each add. → [[Gain Staging]]
