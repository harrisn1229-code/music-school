# VOX-C05 — The treatment hierarchy

**Q.** How does treatment change from a lead vocal to a double to a harmony to an ad-lib?

> [!question]- Answer
> **The further a part is from delivering the lyric, the more it can become texture.** **Lead** — centre, most present, least processed. **Doubles** — wide, well under the lead, not heard as separate. **Harmonies** — lower still, more processing, colour not content. **Ad-libs** — widest, furthest back, heaviest effects.

## Why it matters

0.5/5 — you knew there were different chains and couldn't state the principle. You have **22 vocal chains** that already implement this, so it's purely a naming gap. The rule works because the lead needs every word parseable and an ad-lib doesn't.

## Drill

Set level, pan and processing for a lead/double/harmony/ad-lib from the hierarchy alone. Then compare to what instinct would have done.
