# You Suck at Producing: Creating Drums Pt II

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5xnTt2wnPMo)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5xnTt2wnPMo)

## Synopsis
One of the channel's earliest videos (2016) — a quick follow-up on a basic drum loop, using the innuendo-heavy comedic style of the channel's early run. Underneath the framing, this is the simplest possible version of drum-loop humanization: lowering the tempo, alternating hi-hat velocity by ear (no plugin, no randomization range — just manually varying strength note-to-note), and adding one accented open hi-hat as a second cheap variation layer. Useful mainly as the "simplest starting point" bookend to the more developed velocity-randomization and Drum-Bus-glue techniques covered in the channel's later drum videos.

## Techniques
- **Lower the tempo for a looser, less mechanical feel** — dropping BPM (120 → ~90 in the video) is presented as a quick way to take the edge off a stiff-feeling loop before doing any other humanization work.
- **Manually alternate hi-hat velocity, hit-to-hit, with no plugin or randomization range** — going through each hi-hat note by hand and alternating its velocity (referred to in the video as adjusting the "temperature," hot vs. cold) is the simplest version of velocity-based humanization: no device, no bounded-range randomization, just deliberately uneven strength note to note.
- **Add one accented open hi-hat as a second, cheap variation layer** — dragging in an open hi-hat sample, boosting its volume by a couple dB, and placing it on a chosen beat (here, a quieter second kick) gives a second point of contrast without needing a new instrument or plugin.
