# SMP-C01 — The two rights

**Q.** In clearance terms, what's the difference between sampling a record and interpolating it?

> [!question]- Answer
> A recording is **two** properties. **Sampling** uses the actual audio, so it needs the **master** (label) *and* the **composition/publishing** (songwriters). **Interpolation** — replaying the part yourself — uses only the **composition**, so it needs publishing only.

## Why it matters

The musical difference is the obvious one; the clearance difference is the one that costs money. This distinction is why interpolation is the standard move on a recognizable hook — it removes the label from the conversation entirely and halves the negotiation.

## Drill

For a sample you've used: who owns the master, who owns the composition, and would interpolating it be workable?
