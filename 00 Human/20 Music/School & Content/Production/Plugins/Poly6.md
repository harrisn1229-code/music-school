# Poly6

**Category:** Instrument (polyphonic analog-modeled synth)
**Vendor:** Korg Polysix emulation (e.g. Cherry Audio Polysix / KORG Collection)

## What it is

An emulation of the Korg Polysix — a warm 6-voice analog polysynth with a characterful filter, built-in chorus/ensemble, and detune. Not free, but inexpensive; Guy "swears by it."

## What it's used for

Disclosure's main pad/chord synth — with [[Monark]] (bass), one of the two most-used plugins on *ENERGY* ("the blueprint of ENERGY"). The go-to for washy, evolving chord beds that carry a two-step/garage tune.

## Techniques
- **Unison detune + spread + ensemble = the reason it beats a Juno** — it detunes the *oscillators inside the synth* (a chorus/flange done in the synth, not as an effect) and spreads the voices out; that's what turns "sounds like a Juno" into its own richer beast. → [[Width and Stereo]] — Disclosure, [Ultimatum](https://youtu.be/uWe3UvI4KlU)
- **Perform cutoff + resonance live off mapped MIDI knobs** — ride the filter by hand as the chords are played one-take, then neaten; the human motion (+ a low reverb/delay on the wild sweeps) makes it read as a real analog synth, not a preset. Let the filter moves drive the arrangement. → [[Automation]], [[Modulation]] — Disclosure, [Who Knew](https://youtu.be/e9d7KYEG3VM)
- **Washy pad chain:** EQ (cut lows/rumble, tame resonances when notes cluster, lift the airy top) → Valhalla Delay (light automated ping-pong) → ValhallaVintageVerb "concert hall" ~3 s → light comp to tame builds → Logic Chorus + Roto Cab for width. → [[Reverb]], [[BED Slot]] — Disclosure, [Who Knew](https://youtu.be/e9d7KYEG3VM)
- **Garage-string counter-melody** — a subtle high Poly6 line you only notice at the end; "take it out and the mix feels empty." — Disclosure, [Birthday](https://youtu.be/b-6x_4XRwOQ)

## FX chain notes

Instrument source for the [[BED Slot]] / [[MIDS Slot]]; give up its low-mids to the vocal/bass when it's the dominant synth ([[Masking and Carving]]). Its built-in chorus/detune is part of the sound.
