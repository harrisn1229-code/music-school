# Dijon Vocal Chain

**Reference/vibe:** cracked, desperate, *performed* — a voice breaking up at the edges with the whole room bleeding in. The character is mostly capture, not plugins: omni mic in an untreated room, monitors blasting, take-one energy kept. What sounds like "processing" on Absolutely is largely **bleed** — the entire song coming back through the vocal mic ("It's literally just his voice… you're hearing the entire song coming through" — Tommy King).
**Signal source:** a raw, committed take. Comp *lightly* — imperfections are the genre.

## Step 1 — Record (this chain lives or dies here)
- **Mic:** [[Neumann TLM 102]] in **omni** if available — otherwise back off the mic 1–3 ft in an untreated room. His album chain was an AKG 414 (omni) → Neve 1073 — yours: **UAD Neve 1073 (Unison)**, driven harder than a clean pop vocal (the 1073 pushed = the first distortion stage).
- **Record with the speakers ON** (moderate, or one small speaker) — the bleed is the glue. Or the [[Shure SM57]] handheld with monitors up for the aggressive lane.
- Sing it like a take, not a comp — punch full phrases, keep cracks, breaths, mic handling. No **UAD 1176LN** needed on the way in unless peaks are wild (2 dB max); **UAD Pultec EQP-1A** bypass.

## Chain overview
Level → drive → band-shape → break up → real-room space. Everything pushes toward "performance in a room," away from "vocal on a track."

## Step 2 — Process (Ableton)

### 1. [[LALA]] — Leveling
- **3–6 dB GR**, pushed harder than tasteful — the constant opto squeeze keeps the quiet cracked phrases as loud as the shouts (that suffocating closeness).

### 2. [[Decapitator]] — The break-up
- Style N or E · Drive 4–6 · **Punish on for the peaks moments** · Mix 50–70% — this is a *lead* effect, not seasoning
- **Why:** The signature — the voice audibly distorting when he pushes. Automate Drive/Mix up on the emotional peaks so the breakup follows the performance.

### 3. [[Pro-Q 4]] — Rough shape
- Gentle HPF ~80 Hz (keep chest — don't clean it) · tame 2.5–4 kHz only if the distortion gets spiky · **no air shelf** — polish is the enemy.

### 4. [[RC-20 Retro Color]] — Texture
- Tape/Lo-Fi · wobble 10–20% · noise 10% · Mix ~30% — the take sounds *found*, not produced.

### 5. [[Soothe2]] — Only if needed
- Light, 2–5 kHz — rescue listenability without sanding the grit.

## Step 3 — Effects (sends)
- [[Reverb Send Room]] pushed **hot** (this mix's "space" = one shared room, everything in it) · [[Delay Send Slap]] wobbly · occasional one-word throws ([[Delay Send Other]]) that distort with the voice.
- Stacks: loose, unaligned, different distances from the mic — pile takes like a crowd, not a choir (opposite of the pop rules).

## Vocal arrangement

| Section | Lead | Behind it |
|---|---|---|
| All of it | **one take, start to finish** | the room, and the rest of the song bleeding through the mic |

**The move — there is no vocal arrangement.** The performance is the arrangement. Sections differ by how hard he pushes, where the voice cracks, and how much the monitors bleed back in. Stacking would destroy the thing that makes it work.

**What to actually plan:** which section he sings *hardest*, and whether the bleed gets louder with it. That's the only structural decision on the table — and it's made while tracking, not after.

**Stack count: 1. Always.**

## Notes / variations
- **The arrangement is the effect:** vocal LOUD and center (louder than a "correct" mix), band sparse and bleeding — if the vocal feels uncomfortably present, it's right.
- **Screamed lane ("Big Mike's"):** [[Shure SM57]] → [[UAD Neve 1073]] slammed → [[Decapitator]] Punish, no cleanup.
- **Soft lane ("Skin"):** drop Decapitator to mix ~20%, keep RC-20 + room — intimate but still textured.
