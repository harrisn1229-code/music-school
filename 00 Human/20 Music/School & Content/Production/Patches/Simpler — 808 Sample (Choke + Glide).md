# Simpler — 808 Sample (Choke + Glide)

**Instrument:** [[Simpler]] · **Slot:** [[SUB Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]]

## What it's for
The industry-standard SpinZ/Zay-type sampled 808 workflow — a real 808 sample (from a pack) played through Simpler configured so overlapping notes never phase-cancel or double up, with glide for slides between pitches.

## Settings
- Playback mode: Classic (pitch-tracked across the keyboard, not One-Shot/Slice)
- Voices tab: Polyphony = 1, Mono — every new note cuts the previous one off instead of letting them ring together (the "cut itself" behavior the chain note calls for)
- Retrig: on, so each repeated note re-triggers cleanly rather than legato-sliding into itself
- Glide: on, 60–100 ms — for slides between notes when the part calls for a bend rather than a cut
- Sample start trimmed to the transient — avoid extra silence eating into the groove
- Filter/envelope left mostly flat — the sample already has its tone; shape length with the amp envelope release only if the pack's tail is too long for the tempo

## In context
- Same [[SUB Slot]] role as [[Ramzoid 808 Cooker — Trap 808]] and [[Operator — Clean FM Sub]] — pick this one specifically when the part needs a recognizable, already-mixed 808 sample sound rather than a synthesized one.
- Mono voice count is the load-bearing setting here — without it, overlapping 808 hits are the single most common source of SUB-slot mud in a trap mix.
