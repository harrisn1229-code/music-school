# Talking Bass | "Dream Weaver" by Gary Wright

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qfMxdjELR68)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qfMxdjELR68)

## Synopsis

A short, focused recreation of the "Dream Weaver" bass — a simple two-oscillator unison patch, but the real lesson is timing the filter envelope's decay to the track's tempo/meter rather than to a fixed feel, so a funky bass line cuts off exactly in the pocket instead of ringing through the bar.

## Techniques

- **Two oscillators in UNISON (both sawtooth, no octave offset), both cranked full in the mixer** — a simpler, thicker-not-brighter alternative to the octave-apart layering recipes used elsewhere on this channel; no keyboard tracking on the filter, deliberately, so the tone "just keeps thumping" evenly across the range rather than brightening on higher notes.
- **Time the filter envelope's DECAY explicitly to the track's tempo/meter, not to a fixed "feel" — the decay should finish before the bar does, so the note visibly/audibly cuts off rather than ringing through.** Demonstrated directly: with more decay time, the note sustains audibly into the next beat (wrong for this funky bass part); with decay timed correctly, the note is unmistakably "off" before the next hit lands. The same timing discipline applies to the VCA's loudness envelope in parallel, so neither stage rings past the beat.
- **Resonance does double duty: it "enhances" the audible decay character AND thins out the low end at the same time**, because raising resonance narrows the effective pass band around the cutoff frequency — less overall low-frequency energy passes, even though the resonant peak itself gets louder. Useful to know when resonance is being reached for purely for character — it will also change the perceived bass weight, not just the "talk."
- **Automate/ride the VCA's sustain level UP during a specific performance moment (a walking bassline fill) to get longer note lengths only where the arrangement calls for it**, rather than committing to one sustain setting for the whole part — the same patch plays short/clipped for the main groove and longer/held for the walk-up fill.
- **Explicitly stated: the recipe (two oscillators, tempo-timed decay, resonance-as-thinner) transfers to any synth** — "you don't need a Minimoog to make it... you just need two oscillators."
