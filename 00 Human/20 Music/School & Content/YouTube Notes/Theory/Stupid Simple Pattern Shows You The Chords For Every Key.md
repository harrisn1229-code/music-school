# Stupid Simple Pattern Shows You The Chords For Every Key

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HGhNHfmOb44)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HGhNHfmOb44)

## Synopsis

A short, high-density lesson: a single movable fretboard shape, anchored on the low E string's root note, produces all 7 diatonic chords of any key (and their 7th-chord extensions) without needing to count scale degrees. Worked through three keys (C, E, A♭) to prove it transposes cleanly.

## Techniques

- **Root position → I, IV, V (major).** Find the key's root on the low E string, play the fixed shape, and the pattern lands on the three major chords of the key.
- **3 frets below root → ii, iii, vi (minor).** Same shape, moved down 3 frets from the root, gives the three minor chords.
- **4 frets below root (1 fret below the minor position) → vii° (diminished).**
- **Same three anchor points extended to 7th chords:** root position → Imaj7, IVmaj7, V7 (the V-position chord is always dominant 7, never major 7 — a fixed rule regardless of key); 3 frets down → ii7, iii7, vi7; 4 frets down → viiø7 (half-diminished).
- Explicitly framed as homework to extend to more keys, reinforcing the pattern through repetition rather than memorizing chord-by-chord.
