# BAS-C08 — Tune the 808 up high, then drop it back

**Q.** How do you tune an 808, and how do you get slides on an audio 808?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Pitch it up an octave or more to hear the note centre, tune it there, then return it to the sub register.** Never guess at pitch down at 40 Hz. **Slides on audio 808s:** pitch to the note with warp **off** (a clean stretch), then set warp mode to **Tones** — best for bass — and draw the slides with the clip's transposition envelope. Easier than MIDI slides. Grace notes clicked in just before a target read as bends when no wheel is handy.

## Why it matters

Sub-register pitch is genuinely inaudible as pitch on most systems, so the ear can't do this job in place. It's also the same principle as tuning the kick — a low end on the wrong note fights the song.

## Drill

Detune an 808 deliberately, then tune it by ear at the sub octave. Fail. Then do it an octave up and drop it back. Compare with a tuner.
