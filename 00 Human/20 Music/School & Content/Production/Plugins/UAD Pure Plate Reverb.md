# UAD Pure Plate Reverb

![[Images/UAD Pure Plate Reverb.png]]

**Category:** Reverb (Plate)
**Vendor:** Universal Audio — owned (confirmed 2026-07-17).

## What it is

UAD studio plate reverb modeled on a classic mechanical plate — smooth, dense, bright decay with no early-reflection room signature.

## What it's used for

Vocal and snare sheen; the go-to plate for lush, forward tails that sit up in a mix. Pair short predelay for intimacy, longer for space.

## Techniques

- **A vocal-reverb send strong enough to make a mixer buy the plugin after mixing on the demo/trial — kept over rebuilding the producer's original send from scratch.** "When it comes to vocals, these guys already had some sends from Mark. I kept those on because the reverb actually sounded pretty cool and I ended up having to buy it, this Pure Plate... this Pure Plate kind of bit me in the ass because I demoed it and of course, I kept it, and then I had to buy it." Ran as one of Dua Lipa's two lead-vocal reverb buses (paired with a second, unidentified reverb, plus separate sends to a Bricasti hardware unit and a hall reverb). → [[Reverb]], [[Dua Lipa Vocal Chain]] — Mix with the Masters (Josh Gudwin), [[Mixing Dua Lipa's vocals with Josh Gudwin]]
- **Closing-clean plate stage on a flute chop, called out explicitly as "something super clean."** On a Ventus Ethnic Winds flute layer, a plate reverb runs between a Cassette "Crushed" preset and a clean chorus — a deliberately transparent, non-colored plate rather than a character reverb, contrasting with the crunchier stage before it. Best-match owned equivalent for the video's plain "plate reverb" mention. → [[Cassette]], [[Chorus-Ensemble]], [[Sound Selection Guide]] — loner, [making a soulful afrobeats beat for rema & wizkid from scratch (use voice notes to start ideas!?)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e-KvnLXHAy4)
