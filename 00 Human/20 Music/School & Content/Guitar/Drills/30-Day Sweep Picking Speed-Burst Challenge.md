# 30-Day Sweep Picking Speed-Burst Challenge

A 30-day daily sweep-picking ladder on just two shapes — E minor and E major — alternated, with an escalating fast speed-burst added on top of each repetition as tempo climbs.

## How to run it

- Play E minor shape → E major shape → alternate between them, tempo climbing session to session.
- Add a fast burst of sweep picking on each repetition — meant to push past the comfort zone, not to be played clean immediately. Failing to complete the full workout on day one is expected.
- Log the tempo you got stuck at and try to beat it next session.

## Why `reinforces: []` is correct here

Two fixed shapes, pure speed/burst mechanics — no scale/mode/chord-tone decision-making involved.
