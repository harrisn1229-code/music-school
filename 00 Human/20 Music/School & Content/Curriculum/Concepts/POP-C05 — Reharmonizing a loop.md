# POP-C05 — Reharmonizing a loop

**Q.** Name three *harmonic* ways to create movement in a stale 8-bar loop — not arrangement or mixing moves.

> [!question]- Answer
> **1) Reharmonize the last bar** so the loop resolves differently each pass. **2) Insert a secondary dominant** in front of a chord to pull harder into it (`Am` → `E7 Am`). **3) Move the bass** under static harmony with inversions or a pedal. Also: swap a chord for its parallel, or open voicings into sus/add9.

## Why it matters

The question deliberately excluded arrangement and mixing — the fixes you'd reach for by instinct. These three are the harmonic toolkit, and the first one is the highest-leverage single change.

## Drill

Take one loop and make three versions, one per technique. Keep the arrangement identical.
