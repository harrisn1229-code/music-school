# Disclosure - Looking For Love (Production Breakdown)

An in-person breakdown of the "Alchemy" opener at RAK Studios, in front of a live Discord audience. The richest material is on **gain staging** (matching output through every plugin, target bus levels, auto-gain plugins), **frequency-conscious sidechain to make space** (feeding Soothe's sidechain with the vocal so instruments duck exactly where the vocal sits), and Howard's **varispeed vocal character** (record at +9% speed, pitch back down) with **formant automation tied to the lyric's meaning**. Also a long, honest passage on the minimal mastering handoff and continuous-album logistics. High caption confidence.

## Techniques
- **Gain-stage honestly through every plugin.** Most plugins have input/output gain — match the level before/after so you judge the processing, not the volume jump. Too much makeup gain "sounds awesome" but level-matched it can be peaking higher on transients while sounding quieter.
- **Love auto-gain plugins** that turn themselves down as you drive them (Decapitator's auto, Saturn) so you hear what they *do*, not just "louder/bigger." Pro-L's 1-for-1 mode lets you hear the limiting. A wet/dry knob helps A/B.
- **Target bus levels for headroom:** drum bus ~−8, bass ~−10, everything else to taste — go into the master with lots of headroom (a drum bus hitting 0 is already trouble when things sum). A big lead vocal must stay clean or the instruments pump distortion into it.
- **Sidechain instead of automating levels:** two comps end the synth bus — one keyed off the *vocal* pulls the whole [[BED Slot]] down ~2 dB when he sings, so the [[LEAD Slot]] vocal always has room; fills gaps, stays consistent and thick (like added body). *(Starting point: 2–4:1, fast attack, release to the vocal's phrasing, 2–3 dB GR, key = lead vocal.)* → [[Sidechaining]], [[3 Inst-Synth Bus|Inst-Synth Bus]].
- **Frequency-conscious "make space":** feed **[[Soothe2]]**'s sidechain with the vocal on the synth bus so the [[BED Slot]] synths give up *exactly* the frequencies the [[LEAD Slot]] vocal occupies — like Trackspacer but sharper (the sharpness knob = a broad EQ bubble or a surgical notch). Space, not level. *(Starting point: Soothe in sidechain/ducking mode, depth to taste, sharpness high for surgical, focus 300 Hz–5 kHz where the vocal lives.)* → [[Multiband Compression]], [[Masking and Carving]].
- **Bass processing:** DI'd bass, not re-recorded; bass-amp sim to make it "real"; wanted **no high end at all** so it sits purely in the [[BASS Slot]]/[[SUB Slot]] under the busy stabby synths, vocal, and skippy drums, then compressed for consistency. **Pultec** boosts the lows; **Surfer EQ** (note-following) changes the bass *tone* by tracking pitch; two **[[Decimort 2]]** bit-crushers for the 2000s-house buzz. *(HPF only ~30–40 Hz; roll the highs off above ~2–4 kHz; heavier leveling than any melodic slot — see [[BASS Slot]].)* → [[1 Electric Bass DI Track|Electric Bass DI Track]], [[3 Bass Bus|Bass Bus]], [[EQ]].
- **Varispeed vocal character:** change *pitch* not speed — vary project speed +9% (tempo +9 BPM), record, then set it back; you sing in your natural range but the tone shifts (tape-varispeed; the Beatles slowed the whole "Strawberry Fields" tape in mastering). Melancholic "different frame."
- **Sing in falsetto then pitch down** to deliver a melody the way you mean it.
- **Formant automation follows the lyric:** format down on sad/"going down" lines, up on hopeful ones — production tracks the emotional path.
- **Whispers layer** backs up a mono center vocal — fills the [[Stereo Field]] gap and adds shiny top/[[AIR Slot]] on S's and T's when the main vocal is dead. *(Record a soft whisper of the line, pan it wide or dual-mono, sit it low under the lead; see the whisper trick in [[De-essing]].)*
- **Vocoder (iZotope VocalSynth) for harmony** instead of sung harmonies (a lot of the album is vocoder).
- **Synths:** Logic ESP (Juno copy) + Logic FM synth (the "Windows 98"/Latch sound); **Vital** (free wavetable) endorsed; **Arturia DX7** for stereo wishy-washy background layers; Poly Six on one note to exaggerate an ARP's end note. **Stereo detune / two short delays = chorus.** A **Logic Bit Crusher** LFO'd 1–2% keeps a static synth "alive/broken" (a bit of drift + moving bit-crush).
- **Layer a single note through a second synth** to exaggerate it in the mix.
- **Drums:** Big Kick + Kick 2 layered ([[Kick Design]]) for clarity over a "dead" demo beat → [[TRANSIENT Slot]]; drum bus = comp → [[Soothe2]] → EQ cutting low-mids (~200–400 Hz) → a little limiting, hitting ~−8. A simple turn-a-knob Waves saturator. *(Bus GR 2–3 dB; limiter as a safety net only.)* → [[3 Drums Bus|Drums Bus]], [[Gain Staging]].
- **Even vs odd harmonics** — a Waves saturator split even (smoother, tube) vs odd (aggressive); he swapped off Saturn for variety (and warns: a "new plugin sounds better" is often just novelty).
- **Minimal mastering handoff:** cut the master chain right down — just [[Oxford Inflator]] + a touch of glue, no tape, no EQ. Do EQ in plugins as a *guide*, bypass, screenshot for the engineer. (He wanted −0.5 dB bass; the engineer vetoed it.) Mastering houses have the vintage gear (a Fairchild).
- **Continuous-album logistics:** each song a standalone project + a master project with all pre-masters and interludes in order; give the mastering engineer songs and interludes *separately* (they need gap/silence timestamps and treat interludes differently).
- **Writing by ear:** move MIDI notes on screen (no piano-hand habits) until it "sounds how I feel"; keep every chord moving (a new inversion/lead note even on the same bass note).
