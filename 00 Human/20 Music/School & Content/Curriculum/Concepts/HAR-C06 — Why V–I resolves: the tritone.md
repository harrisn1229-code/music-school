# HAR-C06 — Why V–I resolves: the tritone

**Q.** Explain why V–I feels resolved, in terms of the tritone.

> [!question]- Answer
> **V7 contains a tritone** — in G7, between the 3rd (B) and the ♭7 (F). That interval is maximally unstable, and each note has a **half-step target** in the tonic triad: **B rises to C, F falls to E.** Both voices resolve by semitone into consonance, so the resolution feels inevitable.

## Why it matters

It's the mechanism under every cadence. It also explains tritone substitution — D♭7 works in place of G7 because it contains the *same* tritone.

## Drill

Play G7 and find the B and the F. Resolve just those two notes to C and E. That's the cadence, in two voices.
