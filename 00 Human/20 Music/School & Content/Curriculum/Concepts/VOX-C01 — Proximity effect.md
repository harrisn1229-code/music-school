# VOX-C01 — Proximity effect

**Q.** What is proximity effect, and how do you use it?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Bass response rises as a directional mic gets closer to the source.** Use it as a tone control: move in (2–6 in) for weight and intimacy; **back off to stop a vocal getting boomy** — the same fix as a low-end EQ cut, applied at the source. Only applies to **directional** mics; omni has essentially none.

## Why it matters

The most useful mic fact there is, because it turns distance into a knob. Directly relevant to the TLM 102 and SM57. It's also why a pop filter helps twice — it blocks plosives and sets a minimum distance, which keeps tone consistent.

## Drill

Record the same line at 3, 8 and 14 inches. Name the tonal difference without looking at a meter.
