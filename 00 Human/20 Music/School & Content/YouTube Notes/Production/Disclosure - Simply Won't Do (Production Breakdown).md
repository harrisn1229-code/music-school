# Disclosure - Simply Won't Do (Production Breakdown)

A lighter, Q&A-heavy Twitch session on the sample-chop tune "Simply Won't Do" — less plugin detail, more workflow and philosophy. The reusable takeaways are the **Serato Sample chop-and-stem-separate** approach, the **album-assembly** wisdom (one big project at the end; judge the silences between songs), a strong **"move with intention"** mixing principle, and a **noise-floor/texture** habit (never let a track hit absolute silence). Includes a genuinely good passage on pain, creativity, and knowing when a song is done. High caption confidence, but light on settings.

## Techniques
- **Serato Sample chop workflow:** drag a WAV/MP3 in, tap the pads to reveal/hide the sample; newer versions do AI stem separation. Let it play to do its thing; keep a long note as "press play," then fall back into the chop. Later, separate the vocal out and keep the music chop. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]].
- **Album assembly:** at the end, put every track in *one* project and listen as a whole to judge the silences/gaps between songs — you can't just cut one song into the next, it needs some "nothing." Skipping around averages out the balance and reveals if a vocal/guitar is too loud. Do interludes in folders (each with a beginning + tail); bounce pre-masters into the album project so per-song automation stays clean.
- **Move with intention:** don't throw an effect on just to have one — ask if it benefits the song's meaning; don't straighten an automation line just because it looks nicer.
- **Noise floor / texture:** never let a track hit absolute silence — a baseline of hiss / vinyl crackle / white noise fills the [[TEXTURE Slot]] so it "feels like play is pressed" (RingShift on vinyl crackle for texture). → [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]].
- **"Fake out" the drop:** come in on beat 2 instead of beat 1, or add an extra bar, to surprise a club. → [[Automation]].
- **Compress a summed vocal** (no stem separation) more, to bring out the breaths in the [[LEAD Slot]]; a natural flanger for movement. *(Slower attack to let breaths through, 3–6 dB GR; see [[Compression]].)*
- **Kick-shaper for its click library** — used more for the hundreds of click top-ends it ships than the shaping itself.
- **God Particle (Cradle)** all-in-one finalizer on the master (multiband comp + EQ + saturation) — Max put four on a demo master; "makes everything sound better level-wise" used sparingly.
- **Logic workflow gripes:** XO / Plug Search / sample-in-time-browsing are Logic workarounds Ableton already covers (in-time sample preview, plugin search).
- **On knowing when it's done / creativity:** pain strengthens the creative connection; genre is just a tool (a brush); walk/shower to hear what's stuck in your head. Mostly songwriting philosophy.
