# Ian Kirkpatrick — Making Music (17 Apr 2021 Stream)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHwc5Xrxm04)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHwc5Xrxm04)

## Synopsis

A build-from-scratch stream centered on **found-sound sampling** (Awesome Tapes From Africa) fitted to key with **Zynaptiq Pitch Map**, plus a hands-on **Reaktor bass-building** demo (harmonic control, sub + white noise) and his glitch/texture toolkit. Also his approach to sessions ("I go in ill-prepared, play random chords, adapt") and the origin of a couple of his signature drum sounds (tapping a desk).

## Techniques

- **Sample vintage found sound — Awesome Tapes From Africa (awesometapes.com)** — a blog of digitized African tapes with the hiss/nostalgia intact; rip a "goes-off" clip and build with it. A source for genuinely mysterious, un-clearable-sounding texture. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]], [[TEXTURE Slot]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Zynaptiq Pitch Map to fit a found sample to your chords** — Pitch Map does Melodyne-DNA-style **polyphonic re-pitching driven by MIDI**: load a found sample, send it your chord MIDI, and it re-pitches the sample's closest notes to match your key in real time. **Transpose the sample *before* it hits Pitch Map** to make it find new/weird notes. → [[Pitch and Formant]], [[Melodyne]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **Get chords from Scaler** — pulled the stream's chord progression out of **Scaler**. → [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Build a bass in Reaktor for total harmonic control** — Serum patch ("Seven Skies" bank) → **mono** → add **sub from Reaktor** (control the harmonics exactly) → add **white noise** for grit/air; "for iPhone listeners you won't hear the sub, but the noise carries it." → [[Bassline Writing]], [[Layering and Stacking]], [[SUB Slot]], [[Serum 2 FX]]
- **Near-inaudible LFO Tool duck to clear the kick** — a **very fast, tiny** LFO Tool duck so the bass "gets out of the way of the kick for the smallest of seconds — almost an inaudible sidechain." → [[LFO Tool]], [[Sidechaining]], [[Kick Design]]
- **Resample a guitar into Ableton's Sampler on slice mode to make a bass** — drop a chopped guitar into the **Sampler**, use slice mode, and it plays back like a bass — instrument-swapping by resampling. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Bassline Writing]]
- **Texture toolkit:** **Saturn** + **Spiff** on guitars; **Valhalla Supermassive** for huge reverb; **SynPlant** (Sonic Charge) for atmospheric pads; **Lossy** for creepy degraded texture; **Q3** for filtering. → [[Saturn 2]], [[Reverb]], [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]], [[Pro-Q 4]]
- **EchoBoy automated so smoothly "you don't notice it moving"** — slow, hidden delay automation as motion. → [[EchoBoy Jr]], [[Automation]]
- **Some signature drums are just a tapped desk** — "the drums at the start of Fresh Eyes? That's me tapping a desk." Found percussion again. → [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]], [[TRANSIENT Slot]]

## Philosophy

- **Go into sessions ill-prepared and adapt** — he'll have one or two ideas at most, play random chords, and if the artist doesn't bite, pivot completely ("that's how sessions go"). Rejected chords get reused later (this stream's chords were Tate McRae/Julia Michaels "forgotten chords"). → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[Ian Kirkpatrick - On Loops, Artist Ego and Originality]]
- **"A roller"** — his term for a cyclical, chugging, locomotive groove he loves to build. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **"I'm a producer who streams, not a streaming producer."** — the work comes first.
