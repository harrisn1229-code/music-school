# Pedal

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Ableton

## What it is

Ableton's stock guitar-stompbox emulation — a distortion/overdrive/fuzz pedal model with a Gain knob, three-band tone shaping (bass/mid/treble), and switchable pedal types including a Fuzz mode.

## What it's used for

Guitar-pedal-style drive and fuzz on any source, not just guitar — a favorite go-to distortion option for adding aggressive, band-shapeable grit to basses, 808s, and drum busses.

## FX chain notes

Placed early in a track's chain (before EQ/compression) like any other drive device, so downstream processing can tame or shape the added harmonics; its per-band gain controls mean it can also do some of that shaping itself before anything else in the chain sees the signal.

## Techniques

- **Gain + per-band drive + Fuzz mode for heavier, bass-emphasized distortion on an 808** — turning up Gain increases distortion the same way any drive device does, but Pedal's separate bass/mid/treble band controls let the low, mid, and high content get driven by different amounts instead of one broadband push; switching on **Fuzz** mode adds a heavier, more bass-emphasized character on top ("more base").
