## Techniques

- **Why this specific exercise is effective (3 stated reasons)**: every picking stroke syncs to a different finger (full hand-synchronization coverage); all six strings are used (maximizes string-transition practice, "the hardest aspect of playing fast and clean"); and the 7-note (odd) grouping per string continuously flips which stroke direction starts each string, so the exercise never lets a fixed down/up pattern become the crutch.
- **Video-comparison self-diagnosis, applied concretely.** Filming the same exercise months apart and freeze-framing side by side surfaced problems invisible in the moment: pinky bending away vs. staying curled and controlled; flat fretting-hand-finger angle blocking the string above (messing up transitions); and — the most consequential finding — the picking hand's fingers forming an open "hole" shape and physically scraping against the strings at speed, which had left scar tissue. Fixing the picking-hand shape (closed grip) eliminated a dependence on a fretwrap/string dampener that the old technique had required to sound clean.
