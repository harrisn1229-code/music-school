# Can You Shred WITHOUT A Pick?? - Fast Fingerpicking Lesson

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SCW6WLS-aKE)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SCW6WLS-aKE)

## Synopsis

A fast-fingerpicking shred technique built from combining pull-offs to an open string with a thumb/index plucking pattern — no pick involved. The lesson builds the motion from a slow two-note foundation up to a fluid triplet-based lick, moved through a scale in different positions.

## Techniques

- **Hand assignment:** thumb plucks the lowest string in the pattern, index finger plucks the highest — get the feel first with a metronome on straight eighth/quarter notes before adding the trick.
- **The core trick:** after the index finger plays its note, pull off to the open high E string, then pluck the lower note with the thumb — a group of three notes per cycle, naturally suited to triplet phrasing (index → pull-off → thumb).
- **Right-hand economy:** the whole lick only takes two actual plucking strokes per group (index + thumb) since the middle note is a free pull-off — that's what makes it fast without needing pick speed.
- **Starter shape:** worked with three notes — D (7th fret, G string), A (5th fret, high E), and the open high E string — then the same pattern is moved through different scale positions.
- **Framed as a shred-context technique**, not a chord-arrangement/Travis-picking application — the goal is fast, impressive lead lines played with fingers instead of a pick.
