# EAR-C14 — The four skills a daily routine has to touch

**Q.** What four recognition skills does a complete ear-training routine cover, and what's the daily shape?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Melodic scale-degree** · **bass-note** · **chord-quality** · **chord-number/position**. Daily shape: drone practice on sustained degrees · a melody-transcription rep on something simple · improvisation over a drone with conscious degree-tracking · the five-step chord/progression drill (bass alone → add chords → quality swap → position swap → transpose).

## Why it matters

Three minutes a day beats an hour a month here — this is a perceptual skill, and it's the reason EAR runs as a standing daily drill rather than a course you finish.

## Drill

Run one rep of each of the four, timed. If it takes more than five minutes you're doing too much per day.
