# Legato Finger Strength Workout

Fretting-hand strength and evenness. Hammer-ons and pull-offs carry the notes; the pick starts a phrase and then gets out of the way.

- 9 pages, standard tuning
- **Tempo ladder in 10 BPM steps from 40 to 130** — starts deliberately slow, because the point is even volume between picked and unpicked notes, which only shows up when it's slow enough to hear.
- Play through [[Legato Finger Strength Workout.pdf]]

## How to run it

- The measure of success is **whether a hammered note is as loud as a picked one**. Speed is a by-product. Record a phrase and listen back rather than trusting the hand.
- Four-finger patterns across positions (`7-9-10-11` shapes and their reverses) — the 3rd-to-4th finger transition is where it falls apart first, and that's the rung to sit on.
- Complements [[Downstroke Workout]]: one loads the picking hand, this one loads the fretting hand. Running both in a session covers each hand.
