# FND-C05 — Interval inversion

**Q.** What happens to an interval when you invert it? Invert a major 3rd.

> [!question]- Answer
> **The numbers sum to 9 and the quality flips.** Major↔minor, augmented↔diminished, perfect stays perfect. A major 3rd inverts to a **minor 6th** (3 + 6 = 9).

## Why it matters

This converts a recall question into arithmetic you can do under pressure. It's also why a shell voicing works — inverting guide tones keeps the quality readable.

## Drill

Invert: M3 · m6 · P5 · M2 · m7 · P4 · A4.
