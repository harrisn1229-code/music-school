# Synthet - Use BABY Notes

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vsL82SeL3po)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vsL82SeL3po)

## Synopsis

A ghost-notes primer: very short, low-velocity extra notes ("baby notes") added between the main hits of a melody, percussion part, or bassline. Synthet distinguishes two uses — audible-but-tiny grace notes that spice up a line, and notes shortened almost to inaudibility that still function percussively, shaping the groove even though you can barely hear the pitch. Demonstrated on a chord stab (shortened until it barely reads as a chord) and directly on a bassline.

## Techniques

- **"Baby notes" = ghost notes** — small, quiet, often very short notes added around the main notes of a part; the general term for what shows up on drums as ghost notes and on melodic/bass parts as grace-note-like ornaments.
- **Uneven velocity reads as more natural than uniform velocity** — demoed on a run of same-length, same-volume notes vs. the same run with velocity tapered down on the added notes — the varied version reads as more human/less robotic. → [[Rhythm and Groove]]
- **Shorten a note until it's almost inaudible and it still works percussively** — take a chord stab and shorten its length drastically (to the point it barely sounds like a full chord anymore) and it still contributes to the groove as a rhythmic tick rather than a harmonic statement. Same idea applies directly to bass notes.
- **Works on basically anything** — melody, percussion, chords, bass — the same short/quiet-note device, just tuned differently per part. → [[Bassline Writing]]
