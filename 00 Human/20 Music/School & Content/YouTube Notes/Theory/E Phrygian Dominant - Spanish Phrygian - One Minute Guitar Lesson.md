## What's taught

- **Two equivalent derivations of Phrygian dominant:** (1) Phrygian scale with the minor 3rd raised to a major 3rd, hence the alternate name "Phrygian major"; (2) the 5th mode of harmonic minor — E Phrygian dominant contains the identical note set as A harmonic minor, just centered on a different root.
- **Characteristic notes:** the ♭2 (inherited from Phrygian) and the augmented 2nd gap between the 2nd and 3rd scale degrees (the same harmonic-minor-derived tension already documented in [[Harmonic Minor Modes]]).
