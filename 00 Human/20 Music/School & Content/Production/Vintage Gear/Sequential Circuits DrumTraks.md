# Sequential Circuits DrumTraks

## What it is
Sequential's (the Prophet-5 company's) entry in the sampled-drum wars — a LinnDrum-class machine with a killer differentiator: **programmable tuning and level per step**. The synth company made the drum machine you could *sequence like a synth*.

## What makes the sound
- **8-bit companded EPROM samples** in the Linn/Oberheim mold — a tight, bright kit sitting tonally between the [[LinnDrum]]'s gloss and the [[Oberheim DMX]]'s slab.
- **Per-event tune + velocity programming**: pitch changes *inside the pattern* (tom melodies, detuned snare accents, pitched claps) — a trick no Linn or DMX could do, and its calling card on record.
- Swappable EPROMs like the [[Oberheim DX]] made custom kits common.
- Full MIDI from day one (Sequential invented MIDI's spec alongside Roland) — the drum machine that slotted straight into 1984's new MIDI studios.

## Digital versions
- **Owned:** [[Drum Rack]] with DrumTraks samples + per-step pitch automation in the Ableton piano roll recreates the signature move exactly; [[kHs Bitcrush]] for the 8-bit print.
- **Paid:** UVI Beat Box Anthology 2; vintage archives.
- **Free:** DrumTraks kits circulate in EPROM-dump collections.
