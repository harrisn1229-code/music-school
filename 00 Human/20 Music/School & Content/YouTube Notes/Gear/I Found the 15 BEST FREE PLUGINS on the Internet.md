# I found the 15 BEST FREE PLUGINS on the internet (and they are soooo good)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0BTcW2PNrnA)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0BTcW2PNrnA)

## Synopsis

A rundown of free plugins across every category — synths, width/chorus, dynamics, clipping/limiting, metering, EQ, and reverb — picked for actually being usable rather than just "free." Several of these (Vital, Spitfire LABS, TAL-Chorus-LX, Valhalla Supermassive, kHs Tape Stop, Youlean Loudness Meter) are already owned or already on the wishlist from other sources; the new finds worth logging are a free synth (TAL Tyrell N6), a free spectrum analyzer (Voxengo SPAN), a free clipper, a free limiter, and a free Baxandall-style mastering EQ.

## Techniques

- **Boost a Pultec-style EQ's lows aggressively to hear what it's actually doing, then back off to taste** — demoed pushing a Pultec-clone's low boost extreme purely to illustrate the character, before settling on a small boost for the actual mix. Directly applicable to the owned [[UAD Pultec EQP-1A]]. → [[UAD Pultec EQP-1A]]
- **Automate a reverb's Delay and Warp knobs as a buildup effect** — used on a lead vocal with Valhalla Supermassive, automating both the pre-delay ("Delay") and the pitch/modulation ("Warp") parameters together over a buildup section rather than leaving the reverb static. Directly applicable to the owned [[ValhallaSupermassive]]. → [[ValhallaSupermassive]]
- **A Juno-60-style chorus (TAL-Chorus-LX) widens synths more musically than Ableton's stock Chorus** — reached for specifically as an upgrade over Ableton's own chorus for width on synth sounds; owned equivalent: [[TAL-Chorus-LX]]. → [[TAL-Chorus-LX]]
- **Loudness meter setup for a useful -14 LUFS reading: pull the color threshold to -14 and the scale range to -8** — on a free loudness meter (Youlean), these two settings make the blue/white bars land right at -14 LUFS-I (a solid streaming-era loudness floor) with much higher visual resolution across the 0 to -8 range than the defaults give. Directly reusable on any LUFS meter with the same threshold/scale controls. → [[Clipping and Loudness]]
- **Identify a problem frequency by eye on a spectrum analyzer before fixing it by ear** — a spectrum analyzer (Voxengo SPAN in this case) caught an "annoying hat" that wasn't obviously a problem on the meters otherwise — a visual pass as a second check alongside listening, not a replacement for it. → [[Referencing and Translation]]

## Plugin notes (free finds, new to the wishlist)

- **TAL Tyrell N6 (free)** — a virtual-analog-style synth singled out for having a full, non-thin, non-"plasticky" low end compared to most free synths — demoed layering a low patch under a high patch for a big classic-synth sound.
- **Voxengo SPAN (free)** — spectrum analyzer; used here to visually catch a masking/resonance problem a mix's meters didn't flag.
- **A free clipper with oversampling, soft/hard clip modes, and a clip visualizer** (captions render the name unclearly) — demoed knocking ~6 dB of peaks off a drum bus for a "more together" sound. Functionally the free-tier equivalent of what [[ClipGain]]/[[Gold Clip]] already do in the owned chain.
- **A free limiter, compared directly against FabFilter Pro-L** (captions render the name unclearly) — doesn't get quite as loud as Pro-L under heavy pushing, but holds up well short of a full loudness-war push. Owned equivalent already covers this ground: [[Limiter]], [[XMLimiter]].
- **A free Baxandall-style mastering EQ clone** (captions render the vendor name unclearly) — praised for boosting high end with less harshness than most plugin EQs; the classic passive Baxandall tone-control circuit shows up in a lot of mastering-chain EQs.
