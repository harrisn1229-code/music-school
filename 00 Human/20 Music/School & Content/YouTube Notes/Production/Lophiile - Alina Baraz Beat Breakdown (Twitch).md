# Lophiile — Alina Baraz Beat Breakdown (Twitch)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mdfdbKBmY_A)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mdfdbKBmY_A)

## Synopsis

Breakdown of an Alina Baraz record he produced with Spencer Stewart. The song opens on TWO samples of his own past work — a track from his free vaporwave EP (alt SoundCloud) and an unreleased song written with Talay Riley years earlier — which is the headline lesson: your own dead catalog is a sample library. The rest is his signature moves: acoustic guitar through Little AlterBoy pitched down an octave ("the No Bus sound"), nylon guitar with auto-wah + Brigade chorus, OB-Xa-style synths through Cassette/Reels tape, designed delay tails with automated reverb, and the "Mariposa" Diva lead he jams on in every beat. Ends with the philosophy: the beat is simple (~40 tracks, low for him) because he added everything, then removed all of it.

## Techniques

- **Sample your own unreleased/past material.** Both opening samples are his own: a vaporwave EP cut and a 5-year-old unreleased London session. "Think of stuff that maybe an artist would f* with that you've been involved in that didn't go anywhere — you can use it (and clear it)." Dead projects are a private, pre-cleared sample library. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Song Seeds Index|Song Seeds]]
- **The "No Bus" sound: acoustic guitar → [[Little AlterBoy]] pitched down an octave (+ distortion).** His recognizable bass-adjacent texture — an acoustic performance recast as a woozy low instrument. Spencer's reaction: "yeah, that sounds like lophiile." → [[Pitch and Formant]], [[BASS Slot]], [[1 Acoustic Guitar Track|Acoustic Guitar Track]]
- **Nylon guitar + auto-filter wah + chorus** — the chorus-section hook guitar runs an auto-wah (Ableton Auto Filter doing "the W") plus UAD **Brigade Chorus**; owned equivalents [[TAL-Chorus-LX]]/[[Chorus-Ensemble]]. → [[Modulation]], [[2 Guitar Group|Guitars]]
- **Design your delays — don't use them stock.** "I really like to design my delays so they're not just whatever — they have some modulation to them"; he automated **reverb wet on the delay tail** so the echo blooms as it decays. → [[Delay]], [[Automation]], [[EchoBoy]]
- **OB-Xa pad through tape** — Spencer's pre-chorus synths: OB-Xa-style patch (Analog Lab) through **Cassette** and/or **Reels**, possibly both layered. Nate owns [[Cassette]]. → [[Oberheim OB-Xa]], [[TEXTURE Slot]]
- **Keep a jam-lead patch forever:** his "Mariposa" lead = **"Tuc Funk Smacks" in Diva** — "every single beat I've ever made on stream, I bring this out to jam; sometimes it stays in." Have one known-great lead you always audition melodies with. → [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[LEAD Slot]]
- **Ear-candy grunts** — "blap kits" vocal grunts dropped as one-shot ear candy in the groove. → [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **Fake guitar is fine in the mix** — a Kontakt jazz archtop run through UAD flanger + Brigade chorus + auto filter "sounds kind of fake by itself, but in the mix I barely notice it." Judge layers in context, never solo. → [[Masking and Carving]]
- **Tape stop as section transition** (into verse 2). → [[kHs Tape Stop]], [[TapeStop 2.0]], [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Add the world, then delete it.** The beat was "insanely difficult to finish" because the initial idea was so good — he kept adding, then muting: "adding all the right stuff but not the wrong stuff." ~40 tracks is LOW for him; effective sounds mean you don't want a lot. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Sample anything — even a Discord call.** He sampled a friend's keyboard patch *through the Discord call*, made "Buck's Patch" from it, and built an entire YG-type beat where "literally every sound is from Buck" — the call compression makes "really cool artifacts." Lo-fi capture paths are a texture source (same spirit: Mount Dwel records rooms on iPhone mics for the roomy sound). → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[TEXTURE Slot]], [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]]
- **Mix note:** the final mixers added a lot more low end to the drums — "the snare is way more thick" — leave headroom for the mix stage to fatten drums. → [[1 Drums Snare-Clap Track|Drums Snare-Clap Track]]
