# TOP 15 London music history locations

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=42atjihGryQ)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=42atjihGryQ)

## Synopsis

A travel-guide-format tour of 15 musically significant London locations — album cover sites, historic studios, and performance venues — narrative/trivia rather than instructional.

## What's taught

A location catalog: Abbey Road Studios (opened by Elgar for classical recordings before The Beatles made it iconic, hence the studio's eventual renaming after the album); Battersea Power Station (Pink Floyd's *Animals* cover, including the famous runaway pig-balloon incident); Berwick Street, Soho (Oasis's *(What's the Story) Morning Glory?* cover); Trident Studios, 17 St Anne's Court (1968–1981; recorded "Hey Jude," Elton John's "Your Song," Bowie's *Ziggy Stardust*, Queen's early albums, Lou Reed's *Transformer* — notable for its bright-sounding Bechstein piano, already documented in the vault's own Trident Studios coverage); Heddon Street (*Ziggy Stardust* cover location, a few minutes from 3 Savile Row); 3 Savile Row (Apple Corps HQ, site of the Beatles' final live rooftop performance, 30 January 1969, also seen throughout the *Get Back* documentary).
