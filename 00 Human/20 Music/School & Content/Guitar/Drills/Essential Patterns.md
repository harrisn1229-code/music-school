# Essential Patterns

A single-page pattern sheet at ♩=40 — the shortest thing in the drill library and the easiest to actually do on a bad day.

- 1 page, written slow on purpose
- Play through [[Essential Patterns.pdf]]

## How to run it

- **Five minutes, no ladder.** This one isn't built around climbing tempo; it's built around being short enough that there's no excuse to skip it.
- Best used as the thing you do when the answer to "am I practising today" is otherwise no. A five-minute page beats a missed session, and the guitar drills only work on frequency.
