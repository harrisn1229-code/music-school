## Techniques

- **Tempo-track slowdown before the hook (Julia Michaels' idea)** — draw the tempo track to match the singer's natural rall. into the chorus. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[Automation]]
- **Nate Smith hi-hat feel** — chase that "almost-triplet, pushed-back-but-not-swung" hi-hat placement (faster here than the source). → [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[1 Drums Hat Track]]
- **Layered drums: "beef" layer for the snare + a clock-tick ambience sample** — snare gets a dedicated low "beef" layer; a ticking clock runs as texture. → [[Layering and Stacking]], [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]], [[TRANSIENT Slot]]
- **Oliver sample + LFO Tool** — a printed Oliver loop with LFO Tool "let go" for a pumping groove element (same sample family as "Break My Heart"). → [[LFO Tool]], [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Section-by-section drum swaps** — a **less-bassy kick in the verse**; hold back the **real snare until halfway through verse 2**; **ride cymbals cut off** so the little silence after reads as an accent. → [[Kick Design]], [[Arrangement and Song Form]], [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]]
- **Bass = P-bass with dull strings, direct into a Kemper (stock preset "Can't Burn"), nothing else** — the plain finger-bass tone is just the DI through the Kemper's first simple bass amp. → [[1 Electric Bass DI Track]], [[Bassline Writing]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **2nd-hook bass layer: play the bassline on GUITAR and drop it an octave** — a guitar-played bassline pitched down an octave, edited tightly to the live bass, comes in only on the second hook; plus a **sub-bass under all the hooks**. → [[Layering and Stacking]], [[Bassline Writing]], [[SUB Slot]], [[Pitch and Formant]]
- **"Less to more" chord scheduling** — the song withholds chords: verse is just bass/drums/vocal, the **first real chords land in the 2nd pre-chorus**; go from less to more as a rule. → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **First-verse vocal FX chain** — RC-20 → EchoBoy Jr → iZotope Trash → FabFilter → L2 → C2 → Imager (mono, automated) → 1176 → OTT. → [[EchoBoy Jr]], [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[UAD UA 1176LN]], [[OTT]]
- **Skrillex sample-envelope trick for a percussive hit** — boost the **front** of a sample and drop the **back by ~90%** so it becomes a percussive transient (and "messes with the compressor"). Same move behind the "woo." → [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]], [[Compression]], [[TRANSIENT Slot]]
- **Contact fill from a specific one-shot ("908_LNG.wav"), envelope-tweaked** — pulled a Kontakt one-shot, tweaked its envelope and shaved the first transient for the fill. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Kick Design]]

## The "bridge from scraps" (Don't Start Now)

- **Build a bridge from spare parts when there's no session for it** — the song had no bridge and no one available (Dua was "out being a superstar"), so he made one from leftover pieces: **Kontakt "mouth" horns**, a **stretched jazz-vocal sample**, a **Bobby Brown vocal** ("the Charlie Puth moment"), **"sweet relief" through a vocoder**, and a **re-pitched New Rules "I tell them to myself" made in Melodyne**. "The levels of desperation when you create a bridge from thin air." → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[Melodyne]], [[Ear Candy and Hiding Synths]], [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Make a phrase out of the minutiae** — "a gajillion little pieces" combine into one part you can hum walking around the kitchen; the ear latches onto the phrase, not the details. → [[Layering and Stacking]], [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
