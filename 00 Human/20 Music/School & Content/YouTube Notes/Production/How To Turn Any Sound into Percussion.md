## Synopsis

A short, focused percussion sound-design lesson that extends a technique already documented in the vault (Beats-mode warp + the preserve-transients slider to turn any audio into rhythmic percussion) with a genuinely useful addition: manually double-clicking to INSERT new warp markers wherever a hit is wanted, rather than only shortening existing transients. Demoed on the creator's own voice and on a melodic loop from his sample pack, both finished with Drum Buss (Transients pushed) + OTT for punch.

## Techniques

- **Insert new warp markers by hand to place percussive hits anywhere, not just at existing transients** — double-click directly on the waveform to drop a brand-new warp marker at any point; each one becomes its own hit, letting the rhythm be built wherever it's needed rather than only where the source audio happened to have a natural transient. Works identically on a voice recording or a melodic loop repurposed as a percussion layer. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Finish a percussified sound with Drum Buss (Transients up) + OTT** for extra punch and glue once the rhythm is dialed in. → [[Drum Bus]], [[OTT]]
