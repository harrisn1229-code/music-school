# Computer Guitarist VS Real Guitarist (Real Guitar VS MIDI Guitar)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jbkzDAKYiWU)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jbkzDAKYiWU)

## Synopsis

A lighthearted "human vs. MIDI" challenge (three shred licks played both live and emulated with an unhumanized MIDI guitar track) that surfaces a genuine practice technique: deliberately playing with flattened dynamics to expose timing and note-spacing problems that accented playing can hide.

## Techniques

- **Setup:** three fast alternate-picking/sweep-picking/tapping licks recorded both as real guitar takes and as intentionally "undynamic," unhumanized MIDI guitar takes (no articulation or accent programming) for comparison.
- **The practice-relevant insight:** deliberately mimicking the MIDI track's flat, unaccented sound on real guitar — no picking-stroke accents on the "natural" beats — is a genuinely useful exercise, not just a novelty. Heavy accents and picking inconsistencies are a common source of hidden timing and note-spacing problems; playing a fast alternate-picking lick with deliberately suppressed dynamics forces those problems to the surface.
- **Long-term goal stated plainly:** not to literally sound like a machine, but to reach a fluid, clean, consistent feel — flattening dynamics on purpose is a diagnostic/training step toward that consistency, not the end goal itself.
