# Dynamic Tube

![[Images/Dynamic Tube.jpg]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Ableton

## What it is

Ableton's tube-emulation waveshaper that reacts to input level dynamically, modeling the response of a real tube circuit under varying signal strength.

## What it's used for

Adding responsive, level-dependent tube warmth to vocals, bass, and mix buses — subtler and more dynamic-aware than static saturation.

## FX chain notes

Often used post-compression (so the tube stage reacts to already-controlled dynamics) and before a final EQ pass.

| ⑤ | Envelope | How much input level modulates the bias | Positive to open the tube on louder hits |
| ⑥ | Attack / Release | Timing of the envelope-driven bias | Fast for per-transient grit; slow for a swelling tube warmth |
| ⑦ | Output | Makeup level | Match bypass for a fair A/B |
| ⑧ | Dry/Wet | Parallel blend | Blend to keep transients under the saturation |
