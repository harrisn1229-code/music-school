# Crystallizer

![[Images/Crystallizer.png]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Soundtoys

## What it is

Soundtoys' pitch-shifting granular delay/reverb effect, creating shimmering, crystalline pitched echo textures.

## What it's used for

Shimmer-reverb-style pitched delay textures on vocals, pads, and guitars.

## FX chain notes

Used on a send/return for a dedicated shimmer texture layered under the dry signal.

## Used in my chains

- **[[1 Drums FX Track|Drums FX Track]]** — *granular smear texture* (alongside [[Echobode]]): Splice small, Pitch +12, Recycle high for a shimmer cascade over a drum hit or reverse swell. This whole track is the FX lane — go wild, then gain-stage it back down. Not a fixed slot; printed per moment.
