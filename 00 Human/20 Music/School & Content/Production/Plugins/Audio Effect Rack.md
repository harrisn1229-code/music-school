# Audio Effect Rack

**Category:** Utility
**Vendor:** Ableton

## What it is

Ableton's container for audio effects — wraps any number of devices into one unit with up to eight **Macro** knobs, each mappable to one or many parameters at once, and optional parallel **chains** with their own zone/velocity splits.

## What it's used for

Two jobs. **Macro control:** collapse a multi-device move onto one knob — the standard example is mapping Delay Time *and* Beat Division to the same macro so a single turn changes the delay's character rather than just its length. **Parallel processing:** split a signal into chains and process each differently, which is how most rack-based texture and glitch effects are built.

Chain-relevant: the racks in `Production/Chains/7 Effects/` are Audio Effect Racks, and the [[Glitch Delay Rack]] is the worked example of macro-mapped delay built this way.
