# E-mu SP-1200

## What it is
E-mu's SP-1200 sampling drum machine is **the sound of golden-era hip hop** — 10 seconds of total sample time, 12-bit resolution, and a workflow so limited it forced an aesthetic. Producers chopped records into it for a decade after better specs existed, because "better" didn't sound like *that*.

## What makes the sound
- **12-bit / 26.04 kHz sampling** — immediate loss of top end and added quantization grit; the "dusty" sound is literal information loss.
- **SSM2044 analog low-pass filters on the outputs** — after the crunchy digital conversion, everything passes through warm analog filtering. Digital dirt + analog rounding is the signature combo → same principle as running [[kHs Bitcrush]] into a dark filter.
- **The 33/45 trick**: 10 seconds was nothing, so producers sampled vinyl at 45 rpm (or pitched up) and played it back pitched **down**, stretching sample time — and the repitch artifacts (darkened, grainy, slightly warbly loops) became the boom-bap texture → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]].
- Coarse pitch steps and chunky pad-bank workflow made chops percussive and confident — the machine pushes you toward loops-as-drums.

## Digital versions
- **Owned:** [[Decimort 2]] (D16's vintage-sampler emulation — has SP-1200 mode: bit depth, sample rate, and filter character in one plugin; put it on any [[Drum Rack]] or [[Sampler]] chop chain), [[RC-20 Retro Color]] for the vinyl/dust layer, [[kHs Bitcrush]] for a quick version.
- **Paid:** Inphonik RX950/RX1200 (dedicated SP-1200 plugin), Rossum Electro-Music SP-1200 reissue (hardware, by the original designer).
- **Free:** the Decimort/bitcrush recipe above covers it; SP-1200-processed drum packs are common.

## Used on
- Public Enemy — Bomb Squad productions (*It Takes a Nation of Millions...*): SP-1200 collage chaos.
- Pete Rock, Large Professor, EPMD — the boom-bap canon; the SP-1200 (often paired with an Akai S950 for longer samples) *is* that era's texture.
- Daft Punk — "Da Funk" (1995): that beat is an SP-1200, documented by the band — the crunch works outside hip hop too.
- Genre/era: golden-era/boom-bap hip hop 1987–1995 ([[Rap Subgenres]] boom bap card), early French house.
