# Disclosure - 'Never Enough' Twitch Breakdown

Guy tears down "Never Enough" (the EP lead track), a **hardware-heavy UK-garage** cut written with Andy (aka luxury) and Howard. Three master-classes here: (1) a **break-driven drum arrangement** where *nothing lands on beat 1* — every fill and takeaway engineered to feel "never enough," matching the lyric; (2) a **robot/Todd-Edwards vocal bus** built on Logic Pitch Correction + doubled tremolos + a mono-vocal widener, backed by a *non-tuned gospel choir*; and (3) his signature **"do it live with a mouse, then print it"** philosophy — automate everything DJ-style, bounce unstable FX (Decapitator, granular) to audio. Ends on the full mix bus + an SSL Fusion hardware print. Captions transcript — high confidence.

## Techniques

### Drums
- **Build the whole track on one break, chopped + bounced to a single region.** The breakbeat was the first thing in the session; everything grew around it. Processing chain on the break: **Overdrive → Transient Master (pull *sustain*) → 2nd Transient Master (snappier) → EQ (boost mids + super-highs, scoop low-mids, notch the "tinnitus" resonance) → Limiter.** → [[2 Drums Group|Drums]] chains, [[Parallel Processing]] / [[Compression]].
- **Automate Transient-Master *sustain* to build energy** — ride sustain from ~−100 up to ~+32 through a section: it brings the room/space in the sample back as the build rises, then cut it out at the drop so all "the crap" drops away. His go-to energy-builder. → [[Automation]], [[Compression]].
- **Match transients across a loop** — the first snare had a bad transient/pop, so he chopped just that one note so it matched the rest. → [[2 Drums Group|Drums]].
- **Kick = short + punchy to leave room for sub** — big kick only ~120 ms with release automation (~40 ms then it opens up); *not* a subby EDM kick, just a pop, because "there's so much sub in this tune." A second **acoustic/clicky kick** copy-pasted (matched MIDI) sits on top for click detail. → [[Kick Design]], [[1 Drums Kick Track|Drums Kick Track]], [[SUB Slot]] / [[BASS Slot]].
- **909 hats with zero loops** — via Battery: every note a *different length*, velocity = volume, played in *and* drawn in, notes overlapping across the bar so it sounds alive/jammed. Triplets scattered. "Not a single loop in this tune." → [[2 Drums Hats Group|Drums Hats Group]], [[House - EDM]].
- **909 clap through Logic ChromaVerb, EQ *inside* the reverb** — draw filter/damping-EQ automation on the reverb tail with the mouse (ChromaVerb's internal EQ is more detailed than Valhalla or vintage verbs; praises its visualizer + freeze). A moving filtered-verb under the beat you barely consciously hear. → [[Reverb]] (best-match owned verb; ChromaVerb is Logic → [[Plugin Wishlist]]).
- **Layer many small risers instead of one Splice riser** — Ring Shifter (Logic freq-mod) build-up on the hi-hat + [[Timeless 3|FabFilter Timeless 2]] delay build + filtered-clap-reverb + two tambourines drifting in early, all cresting together to *equal* a riser. → [[Modulation]], [[Depth and Space]].
- **Human touch in the drums** — Splice hip-hop acapella **vocal shouts** + a Splice white-noise sweep as an impact tool. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[TEXTURE Slot]].
- **Tambourines recorded himself at different speeds (varispeed trick)** — same tambourine tracked slow/fast so they lock up when pitched together; tight, swung, Transient-Master'd. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]].
- **Drum bus → FX bus, worked "DJ-style"** — drum bus = compression → tape saturation → EQ → limiter, then sent to a second bus used purely as an effects mixer: EQ-as-filter + reverb + a pre-delay that *speeds up* into the build. "I'm just using my DJ brain — build the reverb, take out the bass — and put it in the track." → [[Automation]], [[3 FX Bus|Sends & FX]].
- **Never drop on beat 1 (arrangement as meaning).** Started 4-to-floor, then deconstructed into the most fills/takeaways possible so nothing arrives where you expect — engineering the lyric "it's never enough, I always leave wanting more." Every takeaway different. → [[House - EDM]] arrangement.

### Vocals (the "magic" bus)
- **Robot/Todd-Edwards lead via Logic Pitch Correction** — scale limited to black notes + F (the F# "skip" makes it more robotic); **automate attack/release** to let *vibrato* through on sustained notes (otherwise it sounds terrible). Howard wanted the choppy-sample vibe. → [[Pitch and Formant]], [[Automation]].
- **Sing through tremolo, then double it** — Howard tracked *through a tremolo on his master out* for the Todd-Edwards chop; Guy re-created it with **two tremolos battling**: Logic tremolo (8ths) + **SoundToys Tremolator** (swung 16ths — it has *swing*, the reason to reach for it). ⚠️ The SoundToys unit **distorts like a real tremolo when pushed** — keep it out of the red or it adds nasty distortion. → [[Modulation]].
- **Widen a *mono* vocal with Ozone Imager** — the stereo pitch-correction adds side info; push the sides so each vocal sits in a "weird bathroom box." Harmonies panned ~1 dB L/R. Per-vocal chain: overdrive → EQ (notch resonance) → **[[Gem Dopamine]]** (highs) → Imager. → [[Stereo Field]], [[Width and Stereo]].
- **Non-tuned gospel choir under the robot Howard** — natural + bright choir does *each* harmony beneath the perfectly-chopped auto-tuned lead; the contrast is the whole trick. 4–5 part Howard harmony; the low-D bass vocal note "adds so much weight when the vocal kicks in." → [[LEAD Slot]] / [[BED Slot]].
- **Vocal bus (all automated, no sends):** Blue-Stripe **1176** comp → EQ (roll low-mids, boost highs) → **[[Soothe2]]** (resonance) → **[[Oxford Inflator]]** (loudness + widening + air; frequency slider to the bright side) → **Ring Shifter** automated *only on the long "moooores"* (momentary flanger/phase — the Tame Impala / RHCP "Dani California" whole-mix-flanger move) → tremolos → EQ → filter-EQ sweeps → **H-Delay + reverb** → gain. → [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]], [[2 Main Vocal Group|Vocals]] chains, [[AIR Slot]].
- **Use effects violently on whole buses / the mix** — keep FX plugins on the master out to mess with like a DJ set, hit record for happy accidents (Valhalla ÜberMod too). → [[Automation]], [[Modulation]].

### Synths, beeps & bass (mostly hardware)
- **"Insane beeps" = a real Juno-106, made to sound *even more* analog with a mouse.** EQ (boost high-mids) → **[[Decapitator]] Punish** (subtle — instantly analog) → bit-crush → a 2nd Decapitator on full. **Printed to audio** because Decapitator "behaves differently every playback." → [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[Roland Juno-106]].
- **Decapitator drop trick** — on the drop, *switch the distortion style/type* with feedback set just over 100 (self-oscillating): it fades/morphs into the next setting for mental transition moments ("E" is the most aggressive style). → [[Decapitator]], [[Saturation and Distortion]].
- **[[Monark]] drone throughout** — two oscillators detuned sharp/flat, bouncing off each other for constant movement under the track. → [[BED Slot]] / [[TEXTURE Slot]].
- **Sawtooth stab chords through RC-20 "wobble," blended via the mix knob** — parallel phase/flange character rather than full-wet. → [[RC-20 Retro Color]], [[Modulation]].
- **Granular gospel-choir texture via Quanta** — dragged a bit of the choir sample through Quanta (granular), recorded himself performing it to a bus; subtle bed, most audible in the last drop, complemented by a one-time hi-hat fill. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[TEXTURE Slot]].
- **Cut the Prince-style guitar** (Connor Albert) — set the key but didn't fit once the big bass went in; kept the session moving, then dropped. → arrangement discipline.
- **Substantial bass = Juno-106** (Andy on keys): overdrive; **no chorus** (wanted it mono/centred — his Juno's chorus is broken anyway); EQ = roll <30 Hz, +50 Hz, cut low-mids, **boost the transient "click" at the start of each note** (Juno/Moog have a better note *entry point* than a TAL soft-synth's digital click); tiny full-drive Decapitator; **RC-20 "flux" bit-crush** = a *different* crush per note (vs a static bit-crusher); delay + reverb; a bit of Logic chorus + Roto Cab for slight width. → [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]] / [[2 Bass Group|Bass]] chains, [[Roland Juno-106]].
- **Multiband sidechain the *sub only*** — duck ~100 Hz-and-down with the kick, leave the highs/harmonics unducked (they don't clash with the kick, so keep the movement). → [[Sidechaining]], [[Multiband Compression]], [[SUB Slot]].
- **RC-20 as an effects box, not a lo-fi crusher** — Guy uses it for sprinkles/flanger/character, "not to make a lo-fi piano like everyone else." → [[RC-20 Retro Color]].

### Mix bus + print
- **Mix bus:** gain (does nothing) → **SSL G-Bus comp** ([[Glue Compressor]]) → tape → **The Drama** (Softube multiband) → **[[Saturn 2|Saturn]]** clean-tube setting (top-end sparkle) → **[[Oxford Inflator]]** → **[[Soothe2]]** low setting on the master (wet/dry, to catch mid-range jumps from the loud rim/vocals) → EQ. → [[Master Chain|Master]] chains.
- **Print through hardware, use the software EQ as a *visual guide*.** Set a software EQ how he wanted (e.g. +dB @ 50, cut treble, high-band comp), then dial his **SSL Fusion** to match it, send output 3/4 through the SSL desk → Fusion → record ("SSL return"). → [[Master Chain|Master]] chains, [[Plugin Wishlist]] (SSL Fusion hardware).
- **Never bounce through a limiter before mastering** — limiter is only on the master for the demo bounce; leave it off for the mastering hand-off. → [[Referencing and Translation]] / [[Clipping and Loudness]].
