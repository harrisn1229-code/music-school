# MIX-C01 — No limiter on the 2-bus

**Q.** Should you leave a limiter on the master bus when sending a mix to be mastered?

> [!question]- Answer
> **No — take it off.** Deliver peaks at roughly **−6 to −3 dBFS**, reached with a gain move rather than a limiter. If you want them to hear your loudness intent, send **both** versions clearly labelled.

## Why it matters

A brick-wall limiter that pulled 8 dB off your peaks isn't headroom you saved — it's 8 dB of information deleted before the engineer ever sees the file, and there's no undoing it. This is the most consequential mistake in the whole MIX strand, and it is live because you actually hand off.

## Drill

Open your most recent bounce. Is anything on the 2-bus? What are the peaks? Fix both.
