# Synthet - Do This When You're Stuck

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ntrcrE25Zhc)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ntrcrE25Zhc)

## Synopsis

An "unstuck" workflow trick for a song you've been staring at too long: separate the stems, pitch everything *except* the drums down, and add a light chorus on the instrument bus — the pitch/tone shift is often enough to make the song sound fresh to tired ears again, doubling as a quick way to test whether the song would actually sit better in a different key. Comes with a fair warning that pitch-shifting introduces artifacts and changes the original sound, which the video treats as an acceptable tradeoff for breaking a rut.

## Techniques

- **Pitch everything but the drums down to refresh your ears on a stuck song** — separate stems, drop the pitch of the instrument/vocal bus (drums excluded, to avoid ruining transients), and listen back — the changed pitch/tone is often enough to make an overfamiliar song sound new again and reveal whether it's actually working. → [[Referencing and Translation]]
- **Layer a light chorus on the instrument bus** after pitching down, for extra freshness on top of the pitch change.
- **Doubles as a real key-selection test** — beyond just refreshing tired ears, pitching the song down is a legitimate way to audition whether the song would sit better in a different key entirely, not just a psychological trick.
- **Accept the tradeoff:** pitch-shifting introduces artifacts and changes the original character somewhat — treated here as an acceptable cost for getting unstuck, not a mix-ready technique to leave in the final version.
