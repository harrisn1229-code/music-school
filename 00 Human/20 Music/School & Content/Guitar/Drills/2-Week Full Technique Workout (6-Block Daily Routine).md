# 2-Week Full Technique Workout (6-Block Daily Routine)

A six-block daily workout meant to be run in one 10-minute sitting for two weeks, touching every major technique family in sequence.

## The six blocks

1. Alternate-picking ascending arpeggios in 5/8 (Bb major) — warmup, tempo climbs each rep.
2. Legato/stretching — hammer-on/pull-off with the fretting thumb positioned behind the index finger for reach; a hybrid-picked note before the last note of each sequence.
3. Sweep/economy picking through common arpeggio shapes, following a specific picking pattern.
4. Alternate-picking string transitions across mixed 6/8–7/8 measures — both up- and down-escape motions needed.
5. Tapping lick with multi-finger tapping and tapping slides — pick held between index and thumb throughout.
6. Hybrid picking — pick plus middle/ring fingers on D/G/B strings for string-skipping lines.

## Why `reinforces: []` is correct here

A mixed-technique physical routine — no scale/mode/chord-tone knowledge being taught or tested, just running each hand-technique family through its motions daily.
