# Downstroke Workout

Right-hand stamina. **All downstrokes, no upstrokes** — the picking hand never recovers on the way back up, which is what makes it a conditioning drill rather than a speed drill.

- 18 pages, standard tuning
- A **tempo ladder in 10 BPM steps from 60 to 210** — the whole structure of the drill is the ladder. Each rung is the same figure at the next tempo.
- Play through [[Downstroke Workout.pdf]]

## How to run it

- Start at `tempo_current`, not at 60 every time. Move up a rung only when the current one is clean for the full page.
- **The failure mode is tension, not speed.** If the forearm locks up, drop two rungs rather than pushing — downstroke stamina is built by staying relaxed under load, and practising tense just trains tension.
- Short and frequent beats long and occasional. Five minutes daily moves the ladder faster than half an hour on Sunday.

## Progress

Track by tempo. Update `tempo_current` when a rung is genuinely clean; don't rate it, and don't run it through spaced repetition — this wants daily reps at a rising tempo, which is the opposite of a widening SR interval.
