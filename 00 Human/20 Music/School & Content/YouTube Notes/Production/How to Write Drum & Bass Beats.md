# How to Write Drum & Bass Beats + Announcing my 3rd Remix Contest

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7eP5r1VgNso)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7eP5r1VgNso)

## Synopsis

A concise, step-by-step DnB drum-programming lesson (paired with a remix-contest announcement for the creator's song "Asterism"): building a foundational kick/snare pattern from scratch, filling it out with velocity-reduced ghost snares and hi-hats, prepping a sample-pack loop for double-time with a genuinely useful warp-mode chain (÷2 BPM, Re-Pitch mode, clip Quantize), and — the most valuable technique in the video — physically editing a sampled drum break's audio until its kick pattern matches the programmed MIDI kicks exactly, rather than just layering the two and hoping they gel.

## Techniques

- **Foundational DnB kick/snare pattern**: snare on beats 2 and 4, kick on beat 1 and halfway between beats 3–4 (breakbeat placement, not four-on-floor); genre tempo typically 174–187 BPM. → [[EDM Subgenres]]
- **Ghost-snare fills + velocity reduction for dynamics, then 8th-note hi-hats with one small variation** — sprinkle a secondary snare into the kick/snare gaps (varied per bar), turn its velocity down so it reads as a ghost hit, then fill hi-hats on an 8th-note grid with a single skip/variation so the pattern doesn't loop identically. → [[Rhythm and Groove]]
- **Prep a sample loop for double-time: ÷2 BPM button → Warp mode to Re-Pitch → Quantize the clip** — halve the loop's BPM reference (rather than just cranking the stretch) to cleanly double its playback speed, switch Warp mode from Complex to Re-Pitch so the pitch rises naturally instead of fighting to stay level, then Quantize the audio clip itself to snap it hard onto the grid. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]], [[EDM Subgenres]]
- **Match a layered drum break's kick pattern to the programmed MIDI kicks by physically editing the audio** — compare the break's and the MIDI's kick placement by ear; delete break kicks that fall where the MIDI has none (patch the gap with a copied neighboring hi-hat hit), and copy one of the break's own kicks into any beat the MIDI kicks but the break doesn't — target rhythmic identity, not just similarity. Transpose the break up a few semitones first if it feels loose/floppy. → [[Layering and Stacking]], [[EDM Subgenres]]
- **EQ out the break's own kick low end so it doesn't compete with the programmed MIDI kick** — a straightforward carving move once the two kick patterns are rhythmically aligned. → [[Masking and Carving]]
