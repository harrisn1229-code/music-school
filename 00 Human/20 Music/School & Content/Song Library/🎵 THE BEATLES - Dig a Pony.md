## Pollack's analysis

*Extracted from [[AWP — Dig A Pony]] — Alan W. Pollack, "Notes On…" #168.*

**Form.** Far more detailed writing than the deliberately rough performance suggests, and one of very few Beatles tracks with a **genuine ternary backbeat** (company: "Baby's In Black," "I Me Mine"). Verse/refrain like a folk ballad, but with doubled verses and a half-time instrumental break borrowed from pop. Intro **11 bars, AAB** — it would be 12 except the last bar overlaps the start of verse 1, and its first two phrases preview the refrain instrumentally. Verse an odd **13 bars, ABB′**, all three phrases different lengths. Refrain **AA′, roughly 10 bars**, uncountable exactly because a dramatic pause stretches A′. Outro is the intro again as a bookend — and he argues **Spector's cut of one phrase from both bookends damages the design**, since they are what make the song a struggle between John's two personae rather than a tidy alternation of moods.

**Harmony.** `Intro: ♭VII – IV – I (×2) · ♭VII – viiø7 – I` · `Verse: I held 4 – vi (6) · ii – ♭VII (3) · ii – ♭VII – V (4)` · `Refrain: ♭VII – IV – I (×2), plus the pause`. Six of the seven diatonic chords appear — **only iii is missing** — and ♭VII gets an enormous share of the airtime, most of it inside the **double plagal ♭VII – IV – I** ([[POP-C09 — Mixolydian ♭VII]], [[HAR-C03 — Plagal cadence]]). Because all three intro phrases approach home from ♭VII rather than V, **the tonic arrives without authority**, which is exactly the unsettled footing the song wants.

**Notable.** The verse tune strains to stay pentatonic and cannot, because ♭VII carries both degree 4 and ♭7 ([[SCL-C03 — Pentatonic scales]]). Contour is the engine ([[MEL-C01 — Melodic contour]]): the verse leaves its second upswing hanging, the refrain takes over from that exact pitch and immediately posts a higher ceiling. The riff's third iteration is broken on purpose, and the drums reset the tempo *before* the verse downbeat rather than on it.

**Against this page.** Agrees on A major and 3/4. He hears no bridge — verse and refrain only — where the `structure:` field reads I-V-C-V-C-B-C.
