# Akai MPC3000

## What it is
Roger Linn's final Akai design and the [[Akai MPC60]]'s grown-up successor — 16-bit stereo sampling, more memory, filters per voice, the same pads and swing. It's the machine of 90s hip hop's second golden age, and one specific unit sits in the Smithsonian: **J Dilla's**.

## What makes the sound
- **16-bit / 44.1 kHz stereo sampling** — clean on paper, but Akai's converters and per-voice **resonant filters** keep a rounded, punchy print; "warm CD-quality" rather than sterile.
- **The MPC60 swing engine carried forward** — same Linn quantize/swing pocket, same note-repeat, same played-not-drawn feel → [[Group Humanizer]], Ableton MPC grooves.
- **Dilla's revolution was *turning the quantize off*** (or nudging events against it): drunk, dragging hats and rushed kicks programmed by hand on this machine became an entire rhythmic language — the [[Neo-Soul - Lo-fi]] playbook's "Dilla time" traces to this box.
- Per-pad tune/filter/envelope made the 3000 a real sound-design sampler, not just a chopper — basslines and keys got sampled and *played* from the pads.

## Digital versions
- **Owned:** [[Drum Rack]] + [[Simpler]]/[[Sampler]] with MPC-style grooves (Ableton ships MPC groove files); [[Group Humanizer]] for the pocket; [[Decimort 2]] dialed gently for the converter rounding.
- **Paid:** Akai MPC Software / current MPC hardware (direct lineage), MPC3000-sampled kits.
- **Free:** Akai MPC Beats; Dilla-style groove templates circulate widely.

## Used on
- J Dilla — the canonical MPC3000 artist (Slum Village, *Donuts*-era beats; his unit is in the Smithsonian's NMAAHC collection).
- Young Guru (Jay-Z's longtime engineer/producer) — still MPC-rooted today: "I love my MPC 3000," workflow now carried everywhere via the modern MPC Studio hybrid unit; stated reason is tool-fluency (grab the tool you know fastest during creative time) over chasing new plugins — [Starting a new production with Young Guru](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QDvQ8x0QyFI)
- Genre/era: mid-90s→2000s hip hop and the neo-soul lane it fed (Soulquarians orbit), boom bap's second wave → [[Rap Subgenres]], [[Neo-Soul - Lo-fi]].
