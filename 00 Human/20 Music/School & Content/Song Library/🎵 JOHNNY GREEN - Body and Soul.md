# Body and Soul — Johnny Green

> Dense jazz ballad, 32-bar AABA in D♭ major, full of 2-5-1s and a bridge key change — a study piece for tritone substitution, chromatic bass motion, and default scale choices on altered dominants.

## Chords (as taught for guitar)
Key: D♭ major. Frequent 2-5-1s, chromatic descending bass under slash chords, a bridge key change into D major, and a **backdoor dominant** moment (D natural minor, read as "a 2-5 in the key of D"). The 2nd chord ("5 of 2") is treated as coming from harmonic minor with an implied ♭9/♭13 by default, even where the lead sheet only states a plain dominant symbol. → [[Chord Substitution]] · [[Altered Chords]]

## Steal this
- **Insert a dominant a half-step above any chord, freely, as decoration** — the tritone-sub logic applied as a comping/reharmonization habit rather than a fixed arranger's choice. → [[Jazz Comping]]
- **Default any dominant-to-minor resolution to harmonic minor with an implied ♭9/♭13**, regardless of the written symbol — one reliable choice instead of agonizing over "the" correct reading.
- **Voice a chromatic descending bass line explicitly, even solo** — the composed slash-chord bass motion is part of the tune's character, not incidental.
