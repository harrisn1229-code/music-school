# Pitch Hack

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Ableton (Creative Extensions pack, w/ Amazing Noises)

## What it is

A free Max for Live device from Ableton's Creative Extensions pack (bundled with Live Suite) — a single delay line with pitch-transposition control (semitones + cents), reversal, and random-transposition options, plus a **Recycle** control that feeds the already-transposed output back into the delay's own cycle.

## What it's used for

A pitch-shifting delay/reverse effect distinct from a plain echo — self-compounding pitch drift and reversed-repeat textures for sound design and transitions.

## FX chain notes

Insert on a texture/transition layer; the Recycle-feedback behavior means small transposition amounts compound quickly, so dial in the base pitch shift before pushing Recycle up.

## Techniques

- **Recycle feeds the already-transposed signal back through the delay for compounding pitch drift; duplicate + pitch back down for a self-reversing "recycle reception"** — the device reverses the incoming sound while pitch-shifting it (with a random-pitch option); the **Recycle** knob sends that *already-transposed* output back into the delay's own cycle, so e.g. a +5-semitone signal runs back through the delay again already shifted, compounding further each pass. Abuse case: duplicate the device and pitch the copy back down, so the chain reverses itself while a self-sustaining "recycle reception" loop keeps building on top. → [[Delay]] — dnksaus, [Ableton Delay Sound Design](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DCIEayyv1bk)
