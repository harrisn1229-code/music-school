## Synopsis

A short satirical bit about the temptation to shortcut original production entirely with sample packs (escalating to the joke extreme of just re-exporting a full finished track as "your song"). Underneath the joke is one genuinely useful, simple workflow habit: build a personal curated folder of proven samples as you find them, rather than re-digging through the same packs from scratch every session.

## Techniques

- **Build a personal "keepers" folder while combing through sample packs** — most individual samples in any given pack won't be usable; when one actually is, right-click and move it into a dedicated personal folder rather than leaving it buried in the original pack, so future sessions pull from an already-vetted pile instead of re-auditioning hours of unused material each time. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
