# Kenny Beats — Beat Mix & Creation Process

![](https://youtu.be/FC__hbvnETY)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/FC__hbvnETY)

## Synopsis

Two halves. First, how he actually mixes while producing: an iterative "that sucks" loop — volume moves first, and only when something fails at every level does a plugin come out (and that plugin is usually Soothe2). Plus the select-all gain-staging move: push the whole beat up to just under the red and the real balance problems reveal themselves. Second, the melody-collage composition method: three unremarkable airy melodies in one key stacked into a drone/texture, then a hard bassline that implies the chord changes — reharmonization without theory chops (the Young Chop Chicago-drill blueprint).

## Techniques

### Mixing
- **Volume before plugins — the "that sucks" loop** — louder-sucks/quieter-sucks, 5–20 passes; only when a sound fails at *every* volume does he reach for EQ / [[Soothe2]] / compression / limiting. Level balance is the mix; plugins are for what level can't fix. → [[Gain Staging]], [[Masking and Carving]]
- **[[Soothe2]] is the first plugin off the bench** — "that's really what I'm using a lot" when a sound sucks at every level: the resonance is the problem, not the volume. → [[Soothe2]]
- **Select-all, push to just under the red** — Cmd-A the whole beat, raise all faders together until the master almost clips, then back off a touch. At full level the problems announce themselves ("oh, my snare's too loud, my hat's louder than I thought") — quiet mixes hide bad balance. Then re-balance element by element. → [[Gain Staging]], [[Clipping and Loudness]]
- **Know your loudest beat as the reference point** — if the speakers are cranked and the beat still isn't loud, the mix is quiet, not the speakers; play your hardest-hitting beat back-to-back to calibrate what "loud on this system" feels like. → [[Referencing and Translation]]
- **Mixing is sculpture, producing is a hammer** — chip-chip-chip vs two good hits; accept the iterative loop instead of hunting one big move. → mindset

### Composition (the melody collage)
- **Three unremarkable melodies > one perfect one** — stack ~3 airy melodies in the same key/chord (high octave + low octave Rhodes + a bell flutter), none a strong hook on its own; filter/texture/reverse them into a drone. "It doesn't matter if any of them is a really strong melody once they all sit there." → [[BED Slot]], [[TEXTURE Slot]]
- **The bassline does the harmony — reharm without theory** — over the static collage, a hard bassline that walks different roots *implies* the chord changes (reharmonization); the 808/bass melody dictates where the song goes (his Young Chop / Chicago-drill influence: Nexus melody stacks + the 808 line IS the song). Drawn in one note at a time — no keys skills needed. → [[Hip Hop - Trap]], [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]]
- **When the notes are meh, make the RHYTHM interesting** — anticipation/swung placement on the first-expected beat; "even if I hate what I play, if the bassline is hard and the drums slap, something forms." → arrangement craft
