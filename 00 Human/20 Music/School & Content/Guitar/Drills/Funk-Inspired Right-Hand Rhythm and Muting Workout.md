# Funk-Inspired Right-Hand Rhythm and Muting Workout

Three chord-strumming rhythm exercises on a single E7♯9 ("Hendrix chord") voicing, aimed at picking-hand wrist looseness and timing rather than fretting-hand technique.

## The chord

Root E (A string, 7th fret), ♯3 G♯, ♭7 D, ♭3 G — low E string blocked with the fretting thumb.

## The three exercises

1. Alternating open chord stabs and fully-muted dead notes (percussive picking-hand mute at the bridge, plus the fretting block) — big tonal contrast between the two, no blur.
2. Same vocabulary with a syncopated group-of-3 rhythm, 32nd notes (not 16th-note triplets — a deliberately different feel).
3. Triplet feel with a downstroke placed on every strong-beat accent (two downstrokes in a row where needed), rather than continuous alternation.

## Why `reinforces: []` is correct here

Right-hand rhythm/muting/wrist looseness on one fixed chord shape — no new scale, mode or chord-tone knowledge, just physical timing and mute control.
