# RX De-plosive

![[Images/RX De-plosive.png]]

**Category:** Pitch/Vocal
**Vendor:** iZotope

## What it is

An RX module that detects and reduces plosive ('p'/'b') bursts in vocal recordings. Owned in both the RX 10 and RX 11 suites.

## What it's used for

Cleaning up plosive pops in vocal takes recorded without adequate pop-filtering.

## FX chain notes

Used early in a vocal cleanup pass.
