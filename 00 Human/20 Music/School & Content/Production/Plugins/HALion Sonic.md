# HALion Sonic

![[Images/HALion Sonic.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Steinberg

## What it is

Steinberg's workstation-style sample/synth player, bundling a large factory library of instruments, pads, and sounds in one plugin.

## What it's used for

Fast access to a broad general-purpose instrument library (keys, strings, synths, drums) for scratch tracks and full productions.

## FX chain notes

Used as the instrument source, followed by standard mix-bus processing.
