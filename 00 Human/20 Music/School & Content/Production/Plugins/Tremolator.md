# Tremolator

![[Images/Tremolator.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Soundtoys

## What it is

Soundtoys' tremolo effect with extensive rhythmic pattern control beyond a simple sine-wave tremolo.

## What it's used for

Rhythmic, patterned amplitude-tremolo effects on guitars, keys, and synths.

## FX chain notes

Used as an insert effect, post-EQ, for rhythmic movement.
