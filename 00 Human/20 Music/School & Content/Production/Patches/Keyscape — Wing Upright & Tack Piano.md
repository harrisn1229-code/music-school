# Keyscape — Wing Upright & Tack Piano

**Instrument:** [[Keyscape]] · **Slot:** [[BED Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Acoustic Piano Track|Acoustic Piano Track]]

## What it's for
Character/vintage piano — Wing Upright for an intimate, boxy, band-in-a-room sound; Wing Tack for the muted, percussive "tack piano" color (thumbtacks-on-hammers timbre). Reach for either when the C7 flagship grand ([[Keyscape — LA Custom C7 Grand]]) is too polished for the part.

## Settings
- ⑥ Patch: Wing Upright (warmer, boxier) or Wing Tack (brighter, more percussive/muted) — the two live in the same browse list, audition both
- ⑧ Internal FX: reverb OFF always
- ④ Mechanical/pedal noise: keep it audible on the Upright — the room/mechanism noise is part of the vintage character, don't gate it out
- ① Velocity curve: standard per-controller default
- ⑤ Drive/EQ knob: light touch on the Upright for extra grit if the part is exposed; leave the Tack patch untouched — its character is already baked into the sample

## In context
- Tack piano sits further toward MIDS in practice (percussive, less sustain) even though it's filed as a BED-slot piano — check against comping keys/plucks in the same section.
- Swappable with a Kontakt felt-piano library for an even more intimate/lo-fi version of the same idea (library-dependent — no single settings recipe, browse and commit).
