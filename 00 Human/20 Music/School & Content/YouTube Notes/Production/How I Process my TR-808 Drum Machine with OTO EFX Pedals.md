# How I Process my TR-808 Drum Machine with OTO EFX Pedals

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kE7JSi70TKU)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kE7JSi70TKU)

## Synopsis

Anthony demos a fixed four-pedal effects chain (all OTO Machines pedals) he runs his Roland TR-808 through so he can process the drum machine "live," DJ-style, by hand rather than drawing automation after the fact — a deliberate preference for real-time hands-on manipulation over post-hoc editing.

## Techniques

- **Fixed live-processing chain order and why it's ordered this way**: bit-crusher+filter → distortion/compression → tap-tempo delay → reverb. Each stage is chosen and ordered to keep the signal usable and jam-able in real time rather than requiring recall of exact settings.
- **OTO Biscuit (bit-crusher + tri-mode filter)**: bit-depth reduction ranges from a light fuzz/buzz to a fully crushed, ridiculous character; the filter section is color-coded by mode (green = low-pass, yellow = band-pass, orange = high-pass) with resonance. **Dry/wet blend as its own sound-design control**: filtering down while keeping some dry signal preserves the transient attack under the filtered tail; filtering down with zero dry signal removes the sound entirely — an audible A/B of wet-only vs. blended-with-dry filtering.
- **OTO Boum (distortion/compression/low-pass combo, described as a "warmer")**: heavy distortion plus compression plus a low-pass filter in one unit; drive amount and filter cutoff are adjusted together live for tonal shaping mid-performance.
- **OTO Bim (digital delay)**: tempo is tapped in real time rather than set numerically, with live switching between subdivisions (quarter, eighth, triplet, whole notes) while the drum machine plays; can also route distortion into the repeats for a different color than clean delay.
- **OTO Bam (reverb)**: room-size control, tap-tempo pre-delay for rhythmically "measured" pre-delay, and a performance move — stopping the drum machine and letting the reverb tail ring out on its own as an intentional ending/transition gesture.
