# MIX-C06 — Level-matching a reference

**Q.** What is a reference track for, and what's the one step that makes it work?

> [!question]- Answer
> A finished commercial record in the same lane, used as an external anchor for tone and balance. **Level-match it.** Anything louder sounds better, so an unmatched reference only tells you it's louder.

## Why it matters

The target-sound framing is the well-known half; level matching is the one that gets skipped. Without it you chase a loudness difference with EQ and compression, which is how references make mixes worse. Use it for specific questions — low end, vocal position, brightness — not as something to clone.

## Drill

Pick a reference for a current track. Match its loudness to yours, then answer three specific questions about the difference.
