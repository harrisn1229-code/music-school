# RHY-C07 — Boom-bap vs trap

**Q.** Rhythmically, what distinguishes a boom-bap drum pattern from a trap one?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Boom-bap:** ~85–95 BPM, backbeat on **2 and 4**, straight 8th hats with an offbeat open hat, swung and laid back, sampled drums. **Trap:** written ~130–145 but **felt half-time**, snare on **beat 3**, hats in 16ths with **32nd and triplet rolls**, tight kit and loose hats, and the **808 is the kick and the bass at once**. The giveaways are hi-hat activity and the half-time backbeat.

## Why it matters

'Triplets' is the usual shorthand for trap hats, and it misses the structure: the bed is 16ths, with 32nds and triplets layered as *rolls on top*. Three parameters explain nearly every genre distinction: tempo, backbeat placement, hat subdivision.

## Drill

Program 8 bars of boom-bap and 8 of trap from an empty clip using the same kit. Change only tempo, backbeat and hat subdivision.
