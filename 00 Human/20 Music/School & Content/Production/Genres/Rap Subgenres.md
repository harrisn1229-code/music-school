# Rap Subgenres

Companion to [[Hip Hop - Trap]] — the quick-identity card for each rap lane: tempo, the defining element, the technique that gets you there, and **examples** to reference. Core rules (808/kick lanes, hats-as-melody, dry upfront vocal) carry across; each subgenre bends them.

## Boom Bap — 85–95 BPM
- **Identity:** chopped soul/jazz samples over dusty swung drums — the MPC feel; rapping is rhythmic, vocals barely effected.
- **Techniques:** sample flip ([[Serato Sample]] chop, pitch ±2–4) · drums layered from vinyl breaks, [[Decimort 2]]/[[RC-20 Retro Color]] 12-bit crunch · swing 54–62% · bass = simple sub following the sample's root · vocal: [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]] minus tune, compressed hard, mono room only.
- **Examples:** Nas — *N.Y. State of Mind* · Wu-Tang — *C.R.E.A.M.* · Joey Bada$$ — *Paper Trail$* · Griselda (modern revival) — Westside Gunn *327*.

## Trap (core/modern) — 130–160 BPM halftime
- **Identity:** the [[Hip Hop - Trap]] playbook itself — 808 melody, rolled hats, snare on 3, hard-tune.
- **Examples:** Future — *Mask Off* · Migos — *Bad and Boujee* · Metro Boomin — *Superhero* · Young Thug — *Hot*.

## Drill (Chicago → UK → NY) — 140–145 BPM
- **Identity:** the **sliding 808** doing melodic glissandos (the UK signature), sparse dark keys/bells, syncopated hats with triplet ghosts, menace over bounce.
- **Techniques:** 808 with long glide, written as a *countermelody* with slides between phrase notes ([[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]] glide) · minor 2nds/dark bell loops · percs on the "wrong" 16ths (the UK shuffle) · vocals dry, ad-libs sparse and low.
- **Dark chord voicing trick: drop the minor 3rd down a half-step to the major 2nd.** In a C minor chord, swap Eb (the 3rd) for D (the 2nd) rather than leaving the "obvious" chord tone in — a small wrong-note substitution that's the actual mechanism behind "minor 2nds/dark bell loops" above. Paired with a hi-hat-led drum build (808 → hi-hat, sound-selection-critical → rim → snare-roll/perc texture → kick last, deliberately spaced) and a manually-auditioned 808-slide workflow (stretch the 808 across the beat, listen back, chop + isolate wherever a slide feels right) — full build order on [[Genre Drum Patterns]]. → [[Genre Drum Patterns]], [[Bassline Writing]], [[Melody Writing]] — loner, [how to make hard drill beats with synths from scratch (headie one, drake etc.)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rcJ-j6X9bAU)
- **Examples:** Pop Smoke — *Dior* (NY/UK hybrid) · Chief Keef — *Love Sosa* (Chicago origin) · Headie One — *Know Better* (UK) · Fivio Foreign — *Big Drip*.

## Rage — 130–150 BPM
- **Identity:** distorted sawtooth synth stabs as the whole beat — EDM energy in trap structure; vocals blown out and chant-like.
- **Techniques:** [[Serum 2]] detuned saw lead through [[Decapitator]] (the beat IS one riff) · 808 distorted to match · vocal: [[Travis Scott Vocal Chain]] UTOPIA/rage variation (parallel Punish, tune locked) · empty arrangements — riff, 808, hats, voice, nothing else.
- **Examples:** Playboi Carti — *Stop Breathing*, *New Tank* · Trippie Redd — *Miss the Rage* · Yeat — *Gët Busy* · Ken Carson — *Fighting My Demons*.

## Plugg / PluggnB — 130–150 BPM
- **Identity:** soft, toy-like bell/pluck melodies and airy pads over bouncy 808s — trap's gentle inner child; nB variant adds R&B chords and sung tune.
- **Techniques:** music-box plucks ([[Collision]]!, [[Operator]] FM bells), lush 7th/9th pads ([[1 Synth Bed Track|Synth Bed Track]]) · 808 rounder, less distorted · hats simpler than trap · vocals melodic, tuned, drenched in short plate.
- **Examples:** Playboi Carti — *Location* (plugg roots) · MexikoDro — *Won't Believe* · Lil Tecca — *500lbs* (nB-leaning) · Summrs — *Bandman*.

## Cloud Rap — 60–75 (or halftime 120–150) BPM
- **Identity:** everything underwater — washed-out ambient samples, reverb-drowned vocals, blurred edges; the *feel* over articulation.
- **Techniques:** pads/samples LPF'd + [[Reverb Send Long]] on the *instrumental* (breaking the "no reverb on beats" rule on purpose) · vocal tuned but tucked and wet ([[Tame Impala Vocal Chain]] placement logic applied to rap) · [[RC-20 Retro Color]] haze everywhere.
- **Examples:** A$AP Rocky — *LVL* / *Purple Swag* (Clams Casino era) · Lil B — *I'm God* · Yung Lean — *Kyoto* · Bones — *Dirt*.

## Memphis / Phonk — 130–150 BPM (drift phonk ~125)
- **Identity:** lo-fi horrorcore — detuned eerie melodies, cowbell as a lead instrument (drift phonk), distorted 808s, cassette grit, chopped 90s Memphis vocal samples.
- **Techniques:** everything through [[RC-20 Retro Color]]/[[Decimort 2]] (tape hiss + bit reduction are the aesthetic) · cowbell riffs pitched around the scale · vocal samples pitched down, formant off ([[Little AlterBoy]]) · master intentionally crunchy ([[Kramer Tape]] slammed).
- **Examples:** Three 6 Mafia — *Mystic Stylez* era (the source) · DJ Smokey — *Evil Wayz* · Kordhell — *Murder in My Mind* · Kaito Shoma — *Scary Garry*.

## West Coast / G-Funk — 90–100 BPM
- **Identity:** the whiny portamento sine/saw lead (the "G-funk whistle"), live-feel funk bass, laid-back swing, talkbox/vocoder colors — sunshine menace.
- **Techniques:** mono lead with heavy glide + pitch LFO ([[Drift]]/[[Analog]]) · bass played behind the beat ([[1 Electric Bass DI Track|Electric Bass DI Track]], Hofner thumb works) · claps + live kit layered · [[Bitspeek]]/[[Echobode]] for talkbox-ish hooks.
- **The modern "Mustard bounce" (the LA-bounce descendant already named above): two bouncy major-scale pianos layered** for the melodic chord identity, drums built in a specific order — snap + cowbell + rim first, a tom added purely as a section-transition device, then hi-hat + clap layered in only when the hook needs more energy, spaced-out kick, deep synth bass or 808 to finish. Full build (plus a darker/uptempo variant that doubles the hat rate at the same ~100 BPM, and a stripped-down pop-hit variant for outside artists) on [[Genre Drum Patterns]]. → [[1 Electric Piano Track|Electric Piano Track]], [[Melody Writing]] — loner, [the secrets behind DJ Mustard's ICONIC beats!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bID1-whdYB0)
- **Examples:** Dr. Dre — *Nuthin' but a 'G' Thang*, *Still D.R.E.* · Snoop — *Gin and Juice* · Warren G — *Regulate* · Kendrick/Mustard — *Not Like Us* (modern LA bounce descendant).

## Jazz / Conscious Rap — 85–100 BPM
- **Identity:** live-band or live-feeling harmony (Rhodes, upright-ish bass, horns), musical drums, lyric-forward mixes — the To Pimp a Butterfly / Roots lane.
- **Techniques:** [[1 Electric Piano Track|Electric Piano Track]] extensions as the bed · real-feel drums ([[2 Drums Group|Drums Group]] Beatles/live variation, less quantize) · vocal dry-ish and dynamic (light comp — intelligibility is king) · [[Neo-Soul - Lo-fi]] pocket rules apply wholesale.
- **Examples:** Kendrick — *Alright* · A Tribe Called Quest — *Electric Relaxation* · J Dilla — *So Far to Go* · Mac Miller — *What's the Use?*

## Melodic Rap / Emo Rap — 70–90 (or 130–160 halftime) BPM
- **Identity:** sung-rapped hooks over guitar or piano loops — pop songwriting in trap clothes; feelings out front.
- **Techniques:** the loop is a *recorded* guitar ([[1 Electric Guitar DI Track|Electric Guitar DI Track]] clean + [[RC-20 Retro Color]]) or music-box keys · vocal: hard-tune but *emotional* delivery (retune 10–20, Humanize 30) + stacked hook harmonies · 808 follows the loop's chords strictly.
- **Examples:** Juice WRLD — *Lucid Dreams* · Lil Peep — *Star Shopping* · Rod Wave — *Heart on Ice* · Post Malone — *Circles* (the pop endpoint).

## Crunk / Snap → Modern Club — 78–85 BPM (or doubled)
- **Identity:** chant vocals, minimal snap/clap percussion, huge group energy — the party-command lane; resurging via Jersey/Baltimore club-rap hybrids (135–140 BPM, kick-pattern-as-hook).
- **Techniques:** gang vox stacks ([[1 Backup Vocal Track|Backup Vocal Track]] crowd variation) · snaps/claps dry and upfront · Jersey club: the 5-kick bounce pattern + bed-squeak samples · arrangement = call-and-response.
- **Examples:** Lil Jon — *Get Low* · Dem Franchize Boyz — *Lean wit It* · Lil Uzi Vert — *Just Wanna Rock* (Jersey hybrid) · Cash Cobain — *Fisherrr* (sexy drill/club).

## Alternative / Abstract (the Kanye–Tyler–JPEG lane) — anywhere
- **Identity:** production-as-auteur — beat switches mid-song, blown-out samples, choir/gospel moments, genre theft on purpose. Less a subgenre than permission.
- **Techniques:** commit to stunts: full beat switch at 2/3 mark, one section through [[Gorillaz Vocal Chain]]-style degradation, gospel stacks ([[1 Harmony Vocal Track|Harmony Vocal Track]] block fours), sample flips of *yourself* (resample your own hook, pitch it, chop it — the [[Psych-Pop (Tame Impala)]] flip workflow).
- **Examples:** Kanye — *Runaway*, *Father Stretch My Hands* · Tyler, the Creator — *EARFQUAKE*, *NEW MAGIC WAND* · JPEGMAFIA — *1539 N. Calvert* · Denzel Curry — *Ultimate*.

## Arrangement — the signature per lane

The [[Hip Hop - Trap]] arrangement block is the parent: write the hook, subtract to make the verse, and let drum muting carry the structure. Each lane bends *how* it subtracts.

| Lane | The arrangement engine | The move | Density |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Boom Bap** | the loop runs unbroken; verses are long | drop the drums under the last four bars of a verse | 4 |
| **Trap** | drum muting every 8 bars | 808 out for 4 bars before the hook | 3–5 |
| **Drill** | the **slide pattern** changes, nothing else does | 808 glide rewritten between sections while the loop holds | 3–4 |
| **Rage** | almost none — riff, 808, hats, voice | total cut, then the riff back at full distortion | 3 |
| **Plugg / PluggnB** | pad in / pad out | bells alone for 4 bars, then the 808 lands | 4 |
| **Cloud** | no sections — one wash, edges blurred | a reverb tail is the transition | 3 |
| **Memphis / Phonk** | loop + tape stop | pitch the whole loop down for a section | 3 |
| **G-Funk / Mustard** | live-band form: intro, verse, hook, bridge | the tom is a *transition device*, added for that job alone | 5–6 |
| **Jazz / Conscious** | song form with real changes | instrumentation swap, band-style | 5–6 |
| **Melodic / Emo** | pop structure — verse, pre, hook | stack hook harmonies; guitar loop never stops | 4–5 |
| **Crunk / Club** | call-and-response | the crowd answers; kick pattern is the hook | 3–4 |
| **Alt / Abstract** | **beat switch at the 2/3 mark** | replace the loop and the kit entirely | anywhere |

**The one that transfers everywhere:** drill proves you can build a whole section by rewriting *one line* — the 808's slides — while every other element loops untouched. Cheapest section contrast in rap.

**Density rule across all lanes: 3–6.** Rap arrangements are subtractive. The count that changes between lanes is the *floor* (rage sits at 3, G-funk at 5), not the ceiling.

## Picking a lane per track
Drums + 808 behavior + vocal-tune amount = the subgenre. The [[Hip Hop - Trap]] chains cover all of them — swap the drum sound source, the 808 treatment (clean/slide/distorted), and the vocal chain variation, and keep the [[Master Chain]] trap targets (−7 to −9 LUFS) except the jazz/conscious lane (−10 to −11, dynamics kept).
