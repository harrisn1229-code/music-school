# HAR-C03 — Plagal cadence

**Q.** What chord movement makes a plagal cadence, and how does it differ from an authentic one?

> [!question]- Answer
> **IV → I** — the "amen" cadence. Softer than V–I because **there's no leading tone**, so there's no semitone pull into the tonic.

## Why it matters

The missing-leading-tone point is the whole reason it sounds gentler and more modal — and it's why IV–I is everywhere in rock and gospel where V–I would sound too formal.

## Drill

Play IV–I and V–I back to back in the same key. Name the difference in your own words.
