# Little Radiator

![[Images/Little Radiator.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Soundtoys

## What it is

A simplified version of Radiator focused on quick analog-tube-amp-style saturation.

## What it's used for

Fast analog saturation/coloration on any track.

## FX chain notes

A coloration stage, typically before EQ.

## Key Parameters

```visual-key
image: Images/Little Radiator.png
coords: Images/Little Radiator.coords.json
```
*Tap a numbered circle for what that control does, the technique, and any artist example.*

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Heat | Drive into the modelled **1566A tube preamp**, −15 to +15. | The main control — this is a preamp emulation, so Heat is both gain and character. Push it for tube thickening rather than reaching for a separate saturator. | — |
| ② | Mix | Dry/wet blend. | Parallel saturation: drive Heat hard and blend back for weight without losing the source's clarity. | — |
| ③ | Noise | Adds the preamp's noise floor. | Off for clean work; on when you want the recording to feel like it went through real hardware. | — |
| ④ | Bias | Shifts the tube's bias point, changing the distortion character. | The subtle one — nudge it for a different flavour of breakup at the same Heat setting. | — |
