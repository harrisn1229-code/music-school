# Operator — DX7 FM E-Piano

**Instrument:** [[Operator]] · **Slot:** [[MIDS Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Electric Piano Track|Electric Piano Track]]

## What it's for
DX7-style FM e-piano — the 80s ballad glass tone. Modern trap loves it pitched dark, so this is the chain-specific build on top of Operator's own [[Operator#Patch Recipes|FM e-piano]] recipe.

## Settings
- ① Algorithm: 2-pair FM (osc B → osc A, osc D → osc C), outputs mixed
- ③ Modulator (B/D) coarse ratio ~14 — the classic DX-style bell/e-piano ratio
- ④ Modulator envelope: fast attack, medium decay, low sustain; carrier envelope: fast attack, long decay/release
- ⑤ Feedback: 0–low — keep it clean for the glassy e-piano character
- ⑥ Filter: gentle low-pass, cutoff open, mostly out of the way
- **Trap-dark variant:** transpose the patch down 1–2 octaves and pitch-bend/tune it slightly flat (a few cents) for the pitched-dark trap e-piano sound — the FM ratio stays the same, only register and tuning change
- FX: short plate/room reverb send, light chorus for width

## In context
- Same MIDS-slot comping role as [[Keyscape — Clavinet C]]; pick Operator over Keyscape when the part specifically wants the FM/DX7 glass tone rather than a sampled acoustic-EP character.
