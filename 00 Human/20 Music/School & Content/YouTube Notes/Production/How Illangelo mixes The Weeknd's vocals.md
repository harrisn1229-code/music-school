# How Illangelo mixes The Weeknd's vocals

![](https://youtube.com/shorts/fG2s168La_E?is=dN4ixQppvlexuZOz)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/shorts/fG2s168La_E?is=dN4ixQppvlexuZOz)

## Synopsis

A short Mix with the Masters clip of Illangelo (Weeknd producer/mixer) soloing the lead vocal on an *After Hours*-era song and demonstrating a single plugin move: **iZotope Vocal Doubler** inserted directly on the lead. He calls it "one of the most pleasant vocal doublers I've ever come across" and credits it as a big part of the "very 3D sound" across the whole *After Hours* album. He solos the lead with the doubler bypassed so the dry take is audible, then shows the automation: the plugin sits at 50% through the first half of the song, and on the second half he rides it up to 90%, then for the demo pushes it all the way to 100% so the effect is unmistakable. The whole clip is one plugin, one control (wet %), automated to build over the course of a song rather than left static.

## Techniques

- **A doubler as a lead-vocal insert, not just a double-track fallback** — Illangelo runs iZotope Vocal Doubler directly on the *lead* vocal track (not on a separate double take) to generate the widened, layered "3D" texture heard across *After Hours*. This is a different use-case than the usual "fake the missing double" application — here the doubler is a permanent color/width stage on the main vocal itself.
- **Automate the wet % across the song for a widening arc, not a static setting** — starts the doubler at 50% wet for the first half of the song, then rides it up to 90% for the second half so the vocal noticeably opens up/thickens as the song progresses; pushed to 100% only to demonstrate the extreme end on camera. The takeaway is the *automation move* (low → high wet over the arrangement), not a single fixed number.
- **A/B with the effect fully bypassed** to confirm how much of the "sound" is actually coming from the doubler before committing to the automation curve — same audition discipline as any parallel/synth layer.
