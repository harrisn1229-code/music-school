# 10 K-pop songs based on Classical music

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cx8CaKWynHI)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cx8CaKWynHI)

## Synopsis

A K-pop-specific catalog of the same classical-borrowing phenomenon already covered in depth from Western pop/rock ([[28 Songs That Are Based On Classical Music]], [[14 Songs That 'Rip Off' Classical Music]]) — same public-domain legal framing, same borrowing mechanisms, applied to a different genre. One genuinely new borrowing variant is worth extracting; the rest is catalog redundant with existing coverage and not repeated in full here.

## Techniques

- **A borrowing variant not yet in the vault's classical-to-pop toolkit: reharmonize a borrowed classical MELODY under a completely new, different chord progression each time it recurs**, rather than borrowing the classical piece's chord progression (the far more common pattern, e.g. Pachelbel's Canon chord progression showing up under new melodies in dozens of pop songs). "Last Goodbye" borrows Pachelbel's Canon's actual melodic line, but sets it against a different, changing chord progression on each repetition — the inverse borrowing direction from the usual Canon-progression pop pattern. → [[Chord Progressions]]
- **Catalog, not repeated in full detail** (same public-domain framing and borrowing mechanisms as the vault's existing classical-borrowing coverage): Blackpink's "Shut Down" lifts a loop from Paganini's Violin Concerto No. 2 ("La Campanella"); VIXX speed up and transpose Beethoven's "Moonlight Sonata" to A minor; "Top Dog" (Top Dog) and an earlier H.O.T. track both draw on Mozart's Symphony No. 25 (the later song possibly directly inspired by the earlier one); TVXQ's "Triangle" uses the opening theme of Mozart's Symphony No. 40; Cherry Bullet and Ivy ("Sonata of Temptation") both sample Beethoven's "Für Elise," the latter using its melody and chord progression as a full chorus foundation; a Tchaikovsky "Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairy" reference closes the catalog.
