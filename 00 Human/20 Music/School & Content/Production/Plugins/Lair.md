# Lair

![[Images/Lair.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Aberrant DSP

## What it is

A freeware analog-character/saturation plugin by Aberrant DSP designed to add subtle harmonic coloration and warmth reminiscent of analog signal paths.

## What it's used for

Gentle analog-style warming on individual tracks or busses where a lighter touch than a dedicated tape/tube emulation is wanted.

## FX chain notes

Typically an early-chain coloration stage, before EQ and compression finalize the tone.

## Techniques

- **Named directly as a favorite reverb for extending a heavily-processed bridge vocal, layered alongside other reverbs and delays.** Building a dense bridge vocal stack ("I really love this plugin called Lair, it's like a Reverb"), Lair is added on top of an already-distorted, delayed vocal (see [[Saturn 2]]) specifically "so now you have like an extended feeling" — everything except the vocal is then cut away so the processed result still reads as vocal, not wash. *(qualitative only — no Sparsity/Pre-Delay/Mix values given)* → [[1 Main Vocal Track]], [[Reverb]] — Mix with the Masters (Benny Blanco & Selena Gomez), [Behind the Track｜'Younger And Hotter Than Me' by Selena Gomez & benny blanco](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rkJ-LaoArUY)
