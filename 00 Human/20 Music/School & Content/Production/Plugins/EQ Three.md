# EQ Three

**Category:** EQ
**Vendor:** "Ableton (stock)"

## What it is

Ableton's stock 3-band EQ (Low/Mid/High with per-band kill switches), used for quick tonal shaping and band-kill moves.

## What it's used for

## Techniques

- **Standing role as the go-to "surgical EQ" in a deliberately small, memorized plugin toolkit — reached for on problem-solving cuts specifically because it's known well, not because it's the most powerful option.** Andrew Scheps: "I use a really small sampling of plugins, and I actually do work this way... if I need a surgical EQ, the EQ Three, I'm done; if I need a musical EQ, usually [a character-style channel EQ]... I'm done." → [[EQ]], [[Session Templates]] — Andrew Scheps (Mix with the Masters), [[Mixing Guitars & Bass - Andrew Scheps]]
- **Sweep EQ Three's Freq Lo / Freq Hi split points across a kick without touching any gain knob — it still audibly changes the transient.** Discovered by accident (reaching for EQ Eight, grabbing EQ Three instead): with all three band gains left flat, just sliding the Freq Lo and Freq Hi crossover-point knobs across a kick changes how its transient reads, because moving the crossover split itself reshapes the signal even with zero boost/cut applied. A fast way to reshape a kick's attack character without committing to an EQ cut. → [[Kick Design]] — Habstrakt (Sol State), [How Habstrakt makes his SIGNATURE KICK](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mlNUCjTmfTA)
