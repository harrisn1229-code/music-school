## Pollack's analysis

*Extracted from [[AWP — If I Needed Someone]] — Alan W. Pollack, "Notes On…" #88.*

**Form.** The verse has **almost no harmonic motion at all** — the key is asserted by a drone, not by a progression — which makes this an early, plain instance of the sustained-pedal writing that becomes George's default in his Indian period. His useful side-observation is that the device may descend as much from John's habit of sitting on the I chord for four-plus bars ("Ticket To Ride," "Day Tripper") as from Indian classical drone. Two-bridge model with an odd twist: **three consecutive verses between the bridges**, the middle one instrumental. Intro 4 bars, a 2-bar solo-guitar ostinato played twice, previewing the tune's pitch content, range and off-beat syncopation without giving the melody away. Verse 8 bars but parsed **2 + 3 + 3**, very unusual. Outro is 2 bars of I-chord vamp using the **exact original ostinato** rather than a variation, then a final chord voiced as bare open fifths.

**Harmony.** `Verse: I (4 bars) · ♭VII/I sus (2) · I (2)` — the ♭VII superimposed over a held I ([[POP-C03 — Pedal point]], [[POP-C09 — Mixolydian ♭VII]]). `Bridge (b minor): iv – V – (V) – i · iv – V – i(=ii of A) – V of A`, a textbook pivot modulation ([[HAR-C10 — Tonicization vs modulation]]) whose return trip is the instructive half: the B minor in bar 7 is heard as i until the E major arrives and the ear retroactively rewrites it as ii. The home key only gets a proper V–I at the very end of the bridge — and that E major chord is **the single place in the whole song where G♯ appears**, everywhere else it is G natural ([[SCL-C05 — Characteristic notes]]).

**Notable.** The verse tune is straight Mixolydian in a very constricted range, using exactly five pitches; the bridge tune is straight minor. Entrances are staggered **across the intro and the first two verses**, not just the intro, and inside the harmony phrases the voicing moves from parallel 5/3 chords to a **6/3 inversion at the melodic apex**.

**Against this page.** Pollack reads it in **A major** — which matches this page's Chords section ("A major, Mixolydian") but not the `key:` field, which says G major. Nothing above has been changed.
