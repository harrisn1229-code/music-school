# HAR-C08 — Borrowed chords and modal interchange

**Q.** What is modal interchange? Name the four workhorse borrowed chords in C major.

> [!question]- Answer
> **Keep the tonic, raid the parallel mode.** In C major you borrow from C minor: **iv (Fm), ♭VI (A♭), ♭VII (B♭), ♭III (E♭)**. The key centre never moves, so it reads as colour rather than a key change. The classic move is **IV → iv → I**.

## Why it matters

Both blank. Contrast with secondary dominants (HAR-C07): **secondary dominants add pull, borrowed chords add colour.** One creates motion, the other creates mood. IV→iv→I works because the A♭ in Fm falls a half step to G as the tonic returns.

## Drill

In a C major loop, swap one F for an Fm. Then try A♭ and B♭. Say what each one does to the mood.
