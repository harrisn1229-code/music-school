# SCL-C06 — Spelling a mode from its root

**Q.** Spell D Dorian and E Phrygian — from the root, using the parallel method.

> [!question]- Answer
> **D Dorian: D E F G A B C** (D natural minor with a raised 6th — B♮ not B♭). **E Phrygian: E F G A B C D** (E natural minor with a flattened 2nd — F♮ not F♯).

## Why it matters

Both blank, and this is the strand's core skill. Reciting the mode list is recall; spelling from a root is construction, and only the second one is usable while playing.

## Drill

Spell all seven modes from D. Then all seven from A. Parallel method only — no rotating a parent scale.
