# Kenny Beats — 808 & Kick Together + Subtractive EQ

![](https://youtu.be/4iAKvWWlCvY)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/4iAKvWWlCvY)

## Synopsis

The anti-sidechain-band-aid manifesto plus a hands-on subtractive-EQ masterclass. Core claim: a kick and an 808 CAN both hit at once — when they don't, the fix is sample choice, subtractive EQ, leveling, and panning, not automatic ducking. Then he demos the whole workflow in Pro-Q: low-cut the sub-100 Hz junk out of everything that isn't the kick/808, hunt resonances with the freeze-and-click trick, even them out instead of deleting them, and watch the entire beat get louder for free. The definitive companion to [[Masking and Carving]].

## Techniques

- **Sidechain is a production technique, not a band-aid** — duck the 808 to the kick if you *like the sound/feel* it creates; using it because "kick and 808 can't hit together" means the real problem is sample choice, timing offset, or masking you haven't cut. Fix those first. → [[Sidechaining]], [[1 Drums Kick Track|Drums Kick Track]]
- **Maybe you don't need a kick at all** — modern trap 808s (Cardi/Lil Baby/Drake-lane) often carry enough attack alone; if you're sidechaining to make the 808 punch, you might just need a *better 808 sample with more attack*, no kick. "A lot of the best shit right now is no kick." → [[Hip Hop - Trap]], [[Kick Design]], [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]]
- **The kick owns sub-100 Hz — evict everyone else** — his kick's energy lives below ~100 Hz (rough number, re-find it per song); guitars/keys/synths all carry inaudible low junk there. Hard low-cut them at ~100 Hz: the instrument sounds identical, but now "the only thing in that frequency band is my kick" — the low end gets clean and solid without touching the kick itself. Even the hi-hat can have random low end. → [[Masking and Carving]], [[BASS Slot]] / [[SUB Slot]]
- **Resonance hunting in Pro-Q: play → watch the spikes → hover to FREEZE → click the point** — the analyzer holds the peaks under your cursor and hands you the exact resonance band (his guitar demo: the pokey 1–2 kHz zone). Then **bring the spikes even with each other, don't drag them to the floor** — you're leveling the pokes, not removing the character. Same loudness, dramatically easier to listen to. → [[Pro-Q 4]], [[Masking and Carving]]
- **Subtractive EQ = free loudness** — "the worst feeling: nothing feels loud but you're already in the red — this is the fix." Every resonance you even out and every junk band you cut is headroom; after the cuts you can push the kick, 808, everything louder. Nothing to do with ideas/melodies/tempo — it's the professional-vs-amateur line. → [[Gain Staging]], [[Clipping and Loudness]]
- **Pan the layers, keep the low center** — duplicate/octave-layer guitars and pan them L/R; keep kick/808/lead center. Clears both the frequency fight *and* the stereo-field fight ("everything in their beat is in the center"). → [[Width and Stereo]]
- **EQ is different every time** — "your EQ is not the same every time you make a song, it's like picking a new kick sample." Presets and yesterday's curves are starting points only. → [[Masking and Carving]]
