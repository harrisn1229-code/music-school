# Ghz Wow Control 3

![[Images/Ghz Wow Control 3.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Goodhertz

## What it is

Goodhertz's Wow Control, simulating tape-style pitch wow/flutter instability.

## What it's used for

Adding vintage tape pitch-wobble character to a track or mix for a nostalgic, lo-fi feel.

## FX chain notes

A creative coloration effect, typically applied late in a chain for final character.

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Speed | Modulation rate, in **Hz** or **BPM-synced** to a note value (grid below), with a Multiplier. | This is the "how fast does it warble" control. Slow speeds read as tape wow; fast reads as flutter — there's no separate flutter control, it's the same modulator. | — |
| ② | Amount | Depth of the pitch modulation. | Small amounts (10–20%) on sustained keys/pads sell "tape" without anyone noticing. Past ~40% it's an effect, not a texture. | — |
| ③ | Shape | Waveform of the modulation. | Smooth shapes for tape drift; squarer shapes for a stepped, broken-machine feel. | — |
| ④ | Dry / Wet | Blend. | — | — |
| ⑤ | Color | Analog coloration amount. | The wow is only half the illusion — Color is what makes it sound like tape rather than a pitch LFO. | — |
| ⑥ | Saturate | Saturation added alongside the modulation. | — | — |
| ⑦ | Mode | The modelled machine — e.g. **15 IPS** tape, **33.3 RPM Vinyl**. | Pick the machine first; it sets the character that Speed/Amount then shape. Vinyl modes have a different, more periodic wobble than tape. | — |
