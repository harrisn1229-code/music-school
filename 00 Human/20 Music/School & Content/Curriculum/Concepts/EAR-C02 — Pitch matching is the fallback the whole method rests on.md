# EAR-C02 — Pitch matching is the fallback the whole method rests on

**Q.** What three pitch-matching abilities are the prerequisite for scale-degree work, and why do they matter more than they look?

> [!question]- Answer
> **(1)** Sing back a note you just heard, in any octave. **(2)** Find a heard pitch on your instrument. **(3)** Sing a random pitch, then find *that* on the instrument. They matter because every later skill — degrees, chords, transcription — falls back on "sing it or find it on the instrument" the moment recognition fails. Without the fallback you're simply stuck.

## Why it matters

This is the diagnosis step, not a warm-up. If any of the three is shaky, that's your actual starting point and no amount of drone drilling will substitute for it.

## Drill

Play three random notes. Sing each back. Then sing a note cold and find it on the guitar within five seconds.
