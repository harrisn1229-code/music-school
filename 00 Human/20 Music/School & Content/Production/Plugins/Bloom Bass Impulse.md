# Bloom Bass Impulse

![[Images/Bloom Bass Impulse.png]]

**Category:** Instrument
**Vendor:** Excite Audio

## What it is

A bass-focused rompler/instrument plugin from Excite Audio (UJAM's sound-design imprint) built around sampled and impulse-driven bass tones.

## What it's used for

Fast access to production-ready bass sounds for modern pop, hip hop, and electronic tracks without deep sound design.

## FX chain notes

Used as the bass instrument source, typically followed by EQ and compression to sit it against the kick.

## Used in my chains

A **bass instrument/rompler** (Excite Audio — instrument source, not FX slot) — sampled + impulse-driven bass tones with fast macro shaping:

- A **bass source** option on [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]] / [[1 Synth Bed Track|Synth Bed]] when you want a designed, ready-made bass timbre fast (vs. building one in [[Serum 2]]/[[Operator]]).
