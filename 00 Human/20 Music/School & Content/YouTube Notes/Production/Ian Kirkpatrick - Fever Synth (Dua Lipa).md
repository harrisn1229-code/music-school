# Ian Kirkpatrick — "Fever" Synth (Dua Lipa)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CP8Qw9RYENg)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CP8Qw9RYENg)

## Synopsis

Ian opens the main synth from Dua Lipa & Angèle's "Fever" and walks the whole insert chain. The synth itself is a **Nexus preset ("Pikes One")** from an older Nexus version — but the *character* is all in the stacked processing, aimed at a **crackly, digital-clipped, "messed-up" tone**. Includes his half-joking "secret tip": a multiband doing nothing.

## Techniques

- **Source: reFX Nexus, preset "Pikes One"** — an old-version Nexus preset; the raw sound is unremarkable, the plugins make it. → [[Nexus]], [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **The insert stack (in order, roughly):** **Sausage Fattener** (just louder) → **Saturn** (adds "crackle") → **OTT** (more crackle) → **sidechain** (ducks under the vocal) → **LFO Tool** (~10%, subtle) → **Simplon** (highpass/lowpass — pick up a little highs/lows, run **post-fader**) → **Brickwall limiter at −13** (a ceiling so it doesn't exceed) → **multiband comp doing nothing**. → [[Saturn 2]], [[OTT]], [[Simplon]], [[Sidechaining]], [[LFO Tool]], [[Limiting]]
- **The whole point is crackle / digital clipping** — Saturn + OTT + Sausage Fattener are stacked to add a "messed-up," digitally-clipped grit, not to clean the sound up. → [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[Clipping and Loudness]]
- **"Secret tip" (tongue-in-cheek): put on a multiband and don't do anything with it** — a jab at habit-processing; he admits some of these inserts aren't doing much. Lesson: audit your chain, don't stack out of ritual. → [[Ian Kirkpatrick - New Rules (25 Big Tips in 13 Min)|rate of information]]
- **Simplon post-fader for filtering** — Kilohearts Simplon (2-band filter) placed post-fader to shave a little top/bottom off the saturated result. → [[Simplon]], [[EQ]]
