# FX-C06 — Chain order: EQ, compression, de-ess

**Q.** A vocal is dull, inconsistent in level and sibilant. In what order do EQ, compression and de-essing go — and why?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Subtractive EQ → compression → de-ess → tone-shaping EQ.** Clean up first so the compressor isn't reacting to rumble. **De-ess after compression**, because pulling the body down *raises* the esses. Boosts last, so a presence lift doesn't get immediately squashed.

## Why it matters

1.5/5 — you put de-ess after compression, which is right and is the part most people get wrong. What's missing is corrective EQ *first* and tone-shaping *last*. This is the applied form of the 'compress first, EQ after' rule already in [[Compression]].

## Drill

Build the chain in that order on a real take. Then move the de-esser before the compressor and hear the sibilance return.
