# RHY-C02 — Syncopation

**Q.** What is syncopation?

> [!question]- Answer
> **Accenting a weak beat or an offbeat** — putting emphasis where the meter doesn't expect it. Three forms: accent a weak position, place a note on a weak position and **tie it over** the strong one, or **rest** on the strong beat.

## Why it matters

The single most important rhythmic device in every genre you make. The key insight: the meter has to stay in the listener's head for syncopation to mean anything — remove the steady pulse and there's nothing to syncopate against.

## Drill

Take a straight 16th hat pattern. Make it syncopated three ways using velocity only, no note moves.
