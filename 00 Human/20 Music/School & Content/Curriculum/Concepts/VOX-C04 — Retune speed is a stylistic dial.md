# VOX-C04 — Retune speed is a stylistic dial

**Q.** What does retune speed control, and how is it a stylistic choice?

> [!question]- Answer
> **How fast correction pulls a note to pitch.** **Slow** preserves scoops, slides and vibrato — inaudible, transparent tuning. **Fast/zero** snaps instantly, killing scoops and stepping the vibrato — the hard Auto-Tune sound. No correct setting: a genre at each end.

## Why it matters

The reference points are widely known. The addition worth keeping: **fast retune destroys vibrato**, and the real mistake isn't picking either end — it's reaching for fast retune to fix a pitch problem and getting the effect by accident.

## Drill

Take one phrase. Tune it at slow, medium and zero retune speed. Name where the vibrato disappears.
