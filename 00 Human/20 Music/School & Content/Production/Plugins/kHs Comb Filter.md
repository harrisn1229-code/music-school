# kHs Comb Filter

![[Images/kHs Comb Filter.png]]

**Category:** EQ
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A comb-filtering snap-in creating notched, metallic resonant tones via short delay feedback.

## What it's used for

Metallic, phaser-adjacent resonant textures and creative sound design.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain for creative tonal effects.
