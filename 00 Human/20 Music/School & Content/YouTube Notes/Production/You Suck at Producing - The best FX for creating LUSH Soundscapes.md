# The best FX for creating LUSH Soundscapes

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wysKsyJf7dg)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wysKsyJf7dg)

## Synopsis
A fast-paced tour of the effects chain Underbelly uses to turn a boring, dry source (demoed on his own voice) into a lush ambient soundscape, pulled from his and Hawk's song "Vitreous." Covers four stages in sequence — reverb (ValhallaShimmer or its free sibling Supermassive), granular texture (Grain Delay plus two dedicated granulizer plugins), tape-style movement (a paid tape emulation plugin's dropout/wobble features, with a stock Chorus-Ensemble + Auto Pan combo as the free alternative), and finally Pitch Map, a note-level re-pitching plugin used three different ways (manual note-dragging, feeding it a vocoder's noise output for instant harmonies, and MIDI-routing it directly for full melodic control). Genuinely useful as a stackable signal-chain recipe rather than one single trick.

## Techniques
- **ValhallaShimmer (or free Supermassive) as the reverb foundation for a lush soundscape** — for turning a dry source into an ambient wash, ValhallaShimmer is the first reach; Valhalla's free Supermassive gets close as a zero-cost alternative, especially starting from one of its own reverb presets. → [[ValhallaShimmer]]
- **Grain Delay cranked to a full octave up as a fast ambient-wash recipe** — pitch slider all the way up, then turn Frequency down and Spray up, dialing Dry/Wet and Feedback to taste — a quick way to layer granular shimmer on top of the reverb rather than building the full parameter set from scratch. → [[Grain Delay]]
- **Dedicated granulizer plugins for a denser wash than Grain Delay alone** — a granulizer plugin (heard in captions as "BLEASS Granulizer") whose "Multi-Bus Dream" preset pitches grains both up AND down by octaves simultaneously; "Fragments" is named as another strong option in the same family. → [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **A tape-emulation plugin's Dropouts feature for randomized movement** — cranking Depth, Width, and Intensity together on a tape-emulation plugin's "dropouts" control creates a randomized panning effect that adds fragility and motion to a sound, alongside a classic pitch-wobble control. → [[Plugin Wishlist]]
- **Two stacked Chorus-Ensemble instances on Vibrato mode as a free tape-wobble alternative** — one instance subtle and fast, a second slow and more pronounced, run in tandem; turn up Warmth on both for extra saturation/analog color on top. → [[Chorus-Ensemble]]
- **Auto Pan cranked + Sample & Hold + full Width as a free dropouts alternative** — Amount up, LFO shape switched to Sample & Hold, Width maxed — reproduces a similar randomized panning/dropout movement to the paid tape plugin's dedicated feature, entirely from stock. → [[Auto Pan]]
- **Pitch Map: drag individual melody notes up/down to rewrite what a source is singing/playing** — its display shows the incoming melody left-to-right on the bottom and re-pitch targets top-to-bottom on the right; dragging a note-point changes what pitch that note gets remapped to, and Shift-clicking a point flips it into a relative up/down offset arrow instead of an absolute target — used here to shift a whole vocal or guitar part down an octave for a glassy character.
- **Feed Pitch Map a Vocoder's noise output for instant "super lush" harmonies** — put a Vocoder ahead of Pitch Map so the vocoder reduces the source to noise; Pitch Map then finds and re-pitches notes inside that noise, generating harmony content that wouldn't exist from the dry source alone.
- **Route external MIDI directly into Pitch Map for exact pitch control instead of relying on its own detection** — via Ableton's In/Out track routing, feed a MIDI track's notes straight into Pitch Map sitting on a different (audio) track, so the plugin re-pitches to exactly the notes played rather than whatever it detects; used to turn a simple bass loop into the foundation of a full drop.
