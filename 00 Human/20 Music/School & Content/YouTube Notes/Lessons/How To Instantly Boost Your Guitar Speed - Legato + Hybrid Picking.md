# How To Instantly Boost Your Guitar Speed: Legato + Hybrid Picking

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adr2tL8hSsM)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adr2tL8hSsM)

## Synopsis

Combines legato and hybrid picking as an alternate-picking bypass for a fast repeating-note-per-string B minor sequence — a lick that normally fights alternate picking because of constant string-transitions.

## Techniques

- **The lick's shape:** a classic 3-note-per-string B minor position, but instead of just running the scale up and down, the pattern plays 4 notes up, backs up to the 2nd note, plays another 4 up, backs up again, and so on — a jump-back sequencing pattern that's notoriously hard to speed up with alternate picking because of the constant string-switching it forces.
- **The fix — hybrid picking + legato (hammer-ons) instead of alternate picking:** rather than picking every note, use hammer-ons for the ascending run within each group and a hybrid-picking (pick + middle finger) approach at the string transitions — removes most of the alternate-picking string-transition problem entirely.
- **Fingering note:** on the low E/A strings, index-middle-ring fingering is used; moving to the D/G strings requires a small stretch — best kept as the *same* fingering shape across the transition rather than switching to ring/pinky, even though that would avoid the stretch, because a consistent shape reduces errors more than avoiding the stretch does.
- **Building block before the full lick:** practice the hammer-on run in isolation first (one picking stroke per string, rest of the notes hammered on), making sure every hammered note is fully audible and evenly articulated — not just a blur leading to the picked note.
- **Demoed at two different tempos** to show the technique scaling from a controllable practice speed up to full performance speed.
