# MJ's "Man in the Mirror" Minimoog bass

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VrN4GeQSYtI)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VrN4GeQSYtI)

## Synopsis

A Minimoog recreation of the "Man in the Mirror" bass, framed as a variation on a familiar classic-Minimoog-bass formula (square + sawtooth an octave apart) — the real content is in the customization knobs: how decay time relates to tempo, and a fast live trick for getting a second bass voice out of one patch.

## Techniques

- **Square wave on the bottom oscillator, sawtooth an octave above**, both cranked to full mixer level for extra saturation potential — the square gives a focused, "basier" fundamental; putting the sawtooth on bottom instead reads thinner/more "tenor" by comparison (demonstrated both ways).
- **Filter decay time set to a deliberate note-value relative to the track's tempo** (a quarter-note decay vs. a half-note vs. an eighth-note, demonstrated back to back on the identical patch) — decay isn't dialed "until it sounds right" in isolation, it's dialed to match the song's rhythmic feel.
- **Filter attack time (normally zero) raised slightly for a "talking"/funkier onset** — a small, often-overlooked knob change that shifts the patch's whole character without touching anything else.
- **A small amount of portamento/glide for "slipperiness"**, pushed further for an obviously sliding bass — a dial-in-by-ear amount rather than a fixed setting.
- **LFO vibrato from a dedicated oscillator (oscillator 3) routed to modulation, with tracking on that vibrato oscillator turned OFF** so vibrato rate stays constant across the keyboard rather than speeding up on higher notes.
- **Live-toggle trick: flip keyboard filter tracking ON mid-performance for an instantly brighter alternate bass voice from the same patch**, then flip it back OFF to restore the original — a fast way to get a usable second bass sound for a different section without building a separate patch.
