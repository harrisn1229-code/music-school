# Ozone 9 Low End Focus

![[Images/Ozone 9 Low End Focus.png]]

**Category:** EQ
**Vendor:** iZotope

## What it is

Ozone 9's low-end processing module using spectral shaping (rather than simple EQ) to tighten and clarify bass without muddiness.

## What it's used for

Cleaning up muddy or undefined low end on a master without simply cutting bass energy.

## FX chain notes

Used on the master bus, often early in the Ozone chain to clean up bass before broader EQ/dynamics.
