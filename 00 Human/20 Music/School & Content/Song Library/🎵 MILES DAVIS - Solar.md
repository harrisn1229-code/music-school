# Solar — Miles Davis

> Jazz standard, 12 bars, moving through five key centers in that span — a compact, fast-changing form that makes it a go-to for practicing scale/key-change navigation.

## Chords

*Chord-scale map, as taught for guitar improvisation.*
Five zones, mapped as close together as possible in a single fretboard position (four of the five sit in the very same position; only the D♭ zone needs a small shift):

| Measures | Scale / key center |
|---|---|
| 2 | C melodic minor |
| 4 | F major |
| 3 | E♭ major |
| 2 | D♭ major |
| 1 | C harmonic minor |

12 measures total, then the form repeats. Some zones have more than one workable scale option — the choices above are one workable path, not the only one.

## Steal this
- **A 12-bar form that changes key center five times** — an efficient way to drill fast key-change navigation without a long tune to sit through. → [[Modulation (Key Change)]]
- **Melodic minor and harmonic minor both appear as the "home" scale for the C-minor zones** (not just natural minor) — worth having both minor-scale colors ready over a minor tonic, not just one.
