# Lophiile — Maria Isabel Beat Breakdown (Cookups Stream)

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RXrF27xqogg)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RXrF27xqogg)

## Synopsis

Stream segment breaking down a Maria Isabel EP cut plus an older "Birthday" beat and an Elijah Fox sample session. The Maria Isabel lesson is drum-arrangement as listener-retention: a manually-performed "wavy" filtered drum version for verse 2, then a full kit swap (trap kit + 808) for another section, one bassline fill "in the perfect place," and subtle genre spice ("a little Spanish sauce, not overboard"). Plus his fake-sidechain audio edit, the old two-808 wide/mono layering trick, and phone-mic room drums.

## Techniques

- **Fake sidechain by editing audio: cut + fade.** Instead of a compressor, he cuts the sustained element where the hit lands and fades it back in — "just cutting it and fading it makes it [duck]." Total control, no pumping artifacts. → [[Sidechaining]], [[Automation]]
- **Verse-2 drum switch-up #1: the wavy version.** Print the drums through **filter + phaser + auto-pan**, with the filter moves performed *manually and recorded* — "I don't like to pick a FilterFreak preset pattern, I like to just do it myself... record what you're moving." A whole alternate drum texture for one section. → [[Automation]], [[FilterFreak1]], [[PhaseMistress]], [[2 Drums Group|Drums Group]]
- **Verse-2 drum switch-up #2: swap the entire kit.** Later section jumps to a completely new trap kit + 808 — "just a little switch to keep the listener interested." → [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **One fill, perfect place** — the bassline is dead simple with a single fill placed exactly where it matters. → [[Bassline Writing]], [[Arrangement and Song Form]]
- **Subtle genre spice** — small Spanish-flavored percussion touches ("subtle sauce"), deliberately NOT going overboard with the genre signifiers. → [[Sound Selection Guide]], [[Genres Index]]
- **Sound sources:** the fill is from a "black kids" pack; jingle bells + sticks percussion from **Fred's pack** ("I use it over and over"); weird echo ear-candy bits. → [[Sound Selection Guide]]
- **The old two-808 layer trick (SoundCloud era):** one 808 track with **no bass** — chorus + widening (wide top), one with **all the bass** + distortion, kept **mono**. Wide-sounding 808 with mono-safe low end. He'd "maybe not do it anymore" but it's a legit width recipe — modern owned version: one 808 through [[kHs Stereo]]/[[Doubler4]] highs-only + mono sub lane. → [[Width and Stereo]], [[SUB Slot]], [[Stereo Field]]
- **Phone-mic room drums** — for an Elijah Fox sample he played kit "vibe stuff," captured with a phone in the room; the trashy room capture IS the character. → [[Ambient Texture and Field Recording]], [[TEXTURE Slot]]
- **Make your samples obvious-vibe** — on the Elijah Fox sample: "the vibe is so obvious — you feel me"; a good original sample telegraphs its use instantly. → [[Sampling and Sound Selection]]
- **Talkbox as a topline stand-in** — keeps a talkbox rigged to jam hook ideas. *(gear note)*
