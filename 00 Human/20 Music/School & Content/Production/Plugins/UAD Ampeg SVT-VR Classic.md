# UAD Ampeg SVT-VR Classic

![[Images/UAD Ampeg SVT-VR Classic.png]]

**Category:** Bass Amp
**Vendor:** Universal Audio — owned (confirmed 2026-07-17).

## What it is

Emulation of the Ampeg SVT Vintage Reissue — the classic all-tube 300W bass head + 8x10 cabinet, the definitive rock/soul bass rig.

## What it's used for

Amp'd bass tone: grind and body on DI bass, from clean tube warmth to driven rock growl. Reamp a clean DI through it in the box.
