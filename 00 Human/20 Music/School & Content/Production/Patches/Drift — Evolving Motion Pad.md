# Drift — Evolving Motion Pad

**Instrument:** [[Drift]] · **Slot:** [[BED Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Synth Bed Track|Synth Bed Track]]

## What it's for
The Drift half of the evolving/motion pad pair — same brief as [[Wavetable — Evolving Motion Pad]] (slow internal movement, no automation needed), with Drift's analog modeling adding a touch of circuit imperfection instead of Wavetable's cleaner digital motion.

## Settings
- From [[Drift#Patch Recipes|Drift — Synth bed / pad]]: Voice Mode Poly
- Osc 1: Saw or Saturated, ① Shape ~40%, ② slow LFO → Shape for gentle movement
- Osc 2: same waveform, ⑦ detuned a few cents for width
- ③ Filter: 12 dB DFM-1, cutoff open ~70%
- ④ Drift: light — analog glue, not audible pitch wobble
- Attack 0.5–2 s, release 1–3 s (Step 1a universal pad rule)
- FX: chorus/ensemble send for width, moderate reverb send
- Velocity → nothing; no sub octave — the chain HPFs the track

## In context
- Pick Drift over Wavetable when the section wants a slightly less pristine, more "analog" pad; otherwise interchangeable.
