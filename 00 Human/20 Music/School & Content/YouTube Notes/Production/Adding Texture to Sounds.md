## Synopsis

A short, simple sound-design lesson demonstrating a 4-stage device chain for taking a clean, sterile synth pad and giving it grit and perceived history: Erosion (Wide Noise mode) for a noise texture layer, Redux for light bit-crushing, a touch of Overdrive for character, and Vinyl Distortion's Crackle for an aged finish. Each device contributes a distinct flavor of "wear" rather than one plugin doing all the work.

## Techniques

- **4-stage aging texture stack: Erosion (Wide Noise) → Redux (light bit-crush) → Overdrive (light) → Vinyl Distortion (Crackle)** — applied in sequence to a clean pad, each stage adds a different kind of grit/wear rather than relying on one device — Erosion for noise texture, Redux for lo-fi crunch, Overdrive for a pushed/driven character, and Vinyl Distortion's Crackle for a worn, aged top layer. → [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[Erosion]], [[Redux]], [[Overdrive]], [[Vinyl Distortion]]
