# New 8 Step Method for GRAMMY-LEVEL Bass Lines (from Grammy Winner Lophiile)

## Synopsis

A short Sol State-edited clip of Lophiile writing a bassline live over a guitar loop, packaged as an "8 step method." Heavy overlap warning: this is essentially the same teaching material already captured in [[Lophiile - All About Basslines (Twitch Stream)]] and synthesized into [[Bassline Writing]]'s numbered "The method" (steps 1–10) — the groove-first approach, finding root notes by ear, octaves as the default fill vocabulary, mirroring the guitar/melody's rhythm for fills, recording busy then muting down to the best parts (never repeating the same fill twice), not getting in the way of vocals (same Chris Brown "Fine China"/Thundercat example, nearly verbatim), and the "imagine a bassist in the band playing it" reality check are all restated here almost word-for-word. The on-screen "8 step" framing itself isn't recoverable from the caption transcript (likely Sol State's overlaid text/graphics, not spoken). The only genuinely new material is the explicit bass-*sound*-type audition step at the top of the process and a brief punch/clarity tightening pass after the mute-down — both folded into the two bullets below; everything else is intentionally NOT re-documented since it's already on the existing pages.

## Techniques

- **Audition bass sound *type* first, by feel, with no fixed formula** — before committing to a patch, Lophiile explicitly tries across categories rather than one instrument: an 808/bassline-style hit, a short punchy bass, a long subby synth bass, or a "real" bass, saying he "often doesn't get the sound exactly right" and has to try a few before it's right — a step ahead of (not a replacement for) [[Bassline Writing]]'s existing sound-ladder (step 10, synth → different synth → real/VST bass → DI+amp sim), which assumes you already know the tone is wrong; this is the earlier "which category am I even in" decision. → [[Bass Design]], [[Sound Selection Guide]] — Lophiile (Sol State), [New 8 Step Method for GRAMMY-LEVEL Bass Lines (from Grammy Winner Lophiile)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oYZecKE8a4U)
- **A distinct punch/clarity tightening pass after the mute-down, with room to simplify further** — after recording busy and muting down to the keepers (the existing "record busy, mute down" step), Lophiile runs one more pass specifically for punch and clarity ("let's make these really punchy and clear sounding so I can hear that") and stays open to cutting even more than the first pass removed ("I'm actually feeling like this is too busy still... we could even simplify even more") — a second, separate editing pass rather than a one-shot mute-down. → [[Bassline Writing]] — Lophiile (Sol State), [New 8 Step Method for GRAMMY-LEVEL Bass Lines (from Grammy Winner Lophiile)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oYZecKE8a4U)
