# Secret Songwriters Behind Classic Songs

## Synopsis

A songwriting-credits history survey — behind-the-scenes writers responsible for major hits performed by other, more famous artists. Primarily historical/attribution trivia rather than transferable technique, so honestly kept as a page-only reference rather than forced into a technique note.

## What's taught

- **Rod Temperton** (ex-Heatwave keyboardist) wrote several of Michael Jackson's biggest hits — "Thriller," "Off the Wall," "Rock with You" — via producer Quincy Jones, plus George Benson's "Give Me the Night."
- **Jim Steinman**, known for a dramatic/theatrical style, wrote most of Meat Loaf's signature material, plus Bonnie Tyler's "Total Eclipse of the Heart" and "Holding Out for a Hero," and Celine Dion's "It's All Coming Back to Me Now."
- **Stock Aitken Waterman** (Mike Stock, Matt Aitken, Pete Waterman) wrote Rick Astley's "Never Gonna Give You Up" among many UK 80s hits.
- **Billy Steinberg and Tom Kelly** wrote "True Colors," "Alone" (I-Ten, later covered by Heart), "Eternal Flame" (The Bangles), "I'll Stand by You" (co-written with Chrissie Hynde), and "Like a Virgin."
- **Married-couple songwriting duos**: Boudleaux and Felice Bryant wrote most of the Everly Brothers' catalog ("Bye Bye Love," "Wake Up Little Susie," "Love Hurts," "All I Have to Do Is Dream"); Nickolas Ashford and Valerie Simpson wrote several Motown hits ("Ain't No Mountain High Enough," "Reach Out and Touch").
