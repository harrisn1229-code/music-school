# ValhallaSupermassive

![[Images/ValhallaSupermassive.png]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Valhalla DSP

## What it is

Valhalla's free massive-scale delay/reverb hybrid plugin, capable of enormous, evolving ambient textures and huge reverb-like delay clouds.

## What it's used for

Huge ambient pad textures, massive reverb-like tails, and evolving delay clouds for cinematic/ambient production.

## FX chain notes

Used on a dedicated send/return for large-scale ambient texture design.

## Techniques

- **Automate Delay and Warp together over a buildup for a growing lead-vocal reverb effect** — rather than a static send level, ride the pre-delay ("Delay") and pitch/modulation ("Warp") controls together across a buildup section so the reverb itself grows and shifts character as the section develops. → [[Automation]] — Sol State, [I found the 15 BEST FREE PLUGINS on the internet (and they are soooo good)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0BTcW2PNrnA)

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Mode | Delay/reverb algorithm (named after constellations — Gemini, etc.) | Huge ambient delays → massive reverbs |
| ② | Delay / Warp | Base delay time + Warp (smears the taps toward reverb) | Long delay + high Warp for a cavernous, blooming space |
| ③ | Feedback | Regeneration/tail length | High for near-infinite ambient washes |
| ④ | Density | Tap density — denser packs the taps toward a smooth reverb | Higher for a smooth wash; lower for distinct echoes |
| ⑤ | Mod (Rate / Depth) | Modulation of the tail | Add for movement/chorusing on the wash |
| ⑥ | EQ (High / Low) | Band-limits the tail | High-cut to darken the wash; low-cut to keep it off the sub |
| ⑦ | Mix / Width | Dry/wet blend + stereo width | High Mix on a send; full Width for a huge stereo space |
