## What's shown

**Plugins:**
- Soundtoys EchoBoy — analog-delay emulation plugin. *(Owned: [[EchoBoy]] — no bullet added, no technique given.)*
- Goodhertz Lossy — lo-fi/digital-artifact emulation plugin (compressed-MP3, bad-stream, glitchy-digital character). *(Owned: [[Ghz Lossy 3]] — no bullet added, no technique given.)*
- TC Helicon — vocal-processor/effects brand (studio-quality vocal processing hardware/plugins for singers, podcasters, streamers). No specific unit/model named.

**Hardware / instrument:**
- Korg M1 — 1988 sample-based workstation synth, 16-bit PCM drum/percussion sounds, up to four separate drum kits.
- Roland TR-808 Rhythm Composer — analog drum machine, commercial flop turned foundational hip-hop/R&B/EDM drum sound. *(Owned: [[Roland TR-808]] — no bullet added, no technique given.)*
- Hammond Organ — electromechanical tonewheel organ (Laurens Hammond, 1934), additive synthesis via spinning metal tonewheels rather than acoustic pipes.
- E-mu Systems Sampler — generic "E-mu sampler" mention, no specific model named. *(Nate owns three E-mu units — [[E-mu SP-1200]], [[E-mu SP-12]], [[E-mu Drumulator]] — but the gear-list page doesn't identify which one, if any, matches; too ambiguous to bullet onto a specific owned note.)*
- Korg VC-10 Vocoder — 1978 20-band analog vocoder, "robot voice"/"talking synthesizer" effect.
- Roland Space Echo (RE-201 implied) — tape-based delay/reverb unit, analog tape saturation, classic spring reverb.
- Ovation Guitars — aerospace-engineered "bowl back" acoustic-electric guitars.
- Moog Minimoog Model D — 1970 monophonic analog synth, first portable patch-free synth. *(Owned: [[Minimoog Model D]] — no bullet added, no technique given.)*
