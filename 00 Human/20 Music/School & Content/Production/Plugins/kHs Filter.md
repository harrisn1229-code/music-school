# kHs Filter

![[Images/kHs Filter.png]]

**Category:** Modulation
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A general-purpose multimode filter snap-in (low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, notch).

## What it's used for

Core filtering duty and filter-sweep automation within a modular chain.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain, often combined with distortion for filtered-distortion effects.
