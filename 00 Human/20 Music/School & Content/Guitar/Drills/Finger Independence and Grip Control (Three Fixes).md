# Finger Independence and Grip Control (Three Fixes)

Three short fretting-hand fixes targeting index-finger over-anchoring and excess grip tension.

## The three exercises

1. **Open-string finger workout** — incorporate open strings into a chromatic pattern so every finger must lift off between notes; run it with no wrist assistance, isolating the motion to the fingers alone.
2. **Non-anchored lick** — play a lick where the index finger deliberately can't stay planted on one string, forcing the other fingers to work without its support.
3. **Decreasing-grip chromatic workout** — the open-string pattern from exercise 1, this time deliberately reducing grip pressure rep by rep until notes start to die, to find the real minimum-pressure sweet spot by feel.
