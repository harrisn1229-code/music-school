## What it is

Two minutes and twenty-two seconds of *Somewhere Over the Rainbow* with a visualizer showing **the scale degree of every note as you hear it**. No commentary. You watch the numbers and listen.

His analogy for why it works is the good part: **it is subtitles in a foreign language.** You are not translating — you are receiving sound and correct label simultaneously, over and over, until the pairing stops needing conscious effort. That is the same **"information soup"** mechanism he describes at length in [[From Ear Training Apps to Real Music]], reduced to its smallest possible form.

Max's stated prerequisite: if scale degrees are new, watch the opening of his Sonofield User Guide first. His production note is worth honouring — **watch in 4K**, since YouTube's compression can smear the numbers.

## How to use it

- **Watch, don't test yourself.** There is nothing to answer. Stay conscious of the number on screen as its note sounds; that is the whole exercise.
- **Repeat it.** At 2:22 it costs nothing to run several times, and the melody is deliberately one of the most familiar in existence — the ear brings no confusion of its own, so all the attention lands on the degree labels.
- **It is the gentlest thing in the ear-training lane** — the hour-long drone drills ask you to identify from feeling alone; this hands you the answer in advance and simply asks you to notice it.
