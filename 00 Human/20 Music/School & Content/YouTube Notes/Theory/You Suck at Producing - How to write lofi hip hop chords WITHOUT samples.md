# How to write lofi hip hop chords WITHOUT samples

## Synopsis
A dense, ground-up build of a lofi hip-hop chord progression from scratch instead of sampling one: build a major scale, convert it to minor, save that scale as a reusable clip, stack triads and then 7th chords on every degree, tell major-7 apart from dominant-7 by ear/half-step count, assemble a jazz-borrowed ii–V–I progression, and then dress it up with 9ths, voicing balance, an applied dominant on the ii chord, root-octave doubling, held common tones, and a raised 5th for extra color — using the Melodics app afterward to actually practice playing the result. Most valuable as a full worked example of taking a progression from bare scale to a finished, genre-specific voicing, one deliberate decision at a time.

## Techniques
- **Build a major scale from the W W H W W W H formula, then convert it to minor by flattening 3, 6, 7** — demoed in E major → E minor (same method as [[Diatonic Modes|Aeolian's parallel-formula build]]). → [[Scales]]
- **Save a built scale as a reusable clip you can drag into any future project** — once a scale clip is built (with its notes pushed left of the loop bracket and Fold enabled so anything dropped in snaps to it), name it and drag it into the clip-browser sidebar; from then on dragging that saved scale into a new project instantly re-establishes the same key/scale with no rebuilding. → [[Scale]]
- **Extend a triad into a 7th chord by continuing the leapfrog one more note** — after building a triad (root, skip, add, skip, add), skip and add one more scale note on top to get a 4-note 7th chord; repeat on every scale degree for the full diatonic 7th-chord set. → [[Chords by Key]], [[Seventh Chords]]
- **Tell major-7 and dominant-7 apart by counting the half-steps from root to top note** — a maj7 chord's root-to-top interval is 11 half-steps (an octave minus a half-step); lowering that top note one more half-step (to 10 half-steps root-to-top) turns the exact same major-triad-plus-7th shape into a dominant 7th — described as sounding like a "barbershop quartet," much more tense than the maj7. → [[Seventh Chords]]
- **Build a ii–V–I progression** — a jazz-borrowed staple that also carries straight over into lofi hip-hop; slow the tempo (halve it) rather than keeping it fast, since a rushed ii–V–I reads as trying too hard for the genre. → [[Chord Progressions]]
- **Extend 7th chords into 9ths by leapfrogging one note further** — skip and add once more past the 7th to add the 9th on top. → [[Extended Chords]]
- **Balance voicing density across a progression by trimming, not just adding** — if one chord in a progression has more notes than its neighbors (e.g. a 9th chord next to a plain triad), remove a less-important inner note from the denser chord so the voicings feel more even across the progression. → [[Extended Chords]]
- **Turn the ii chord into a dominant 7th for extra tension (an applied V7/V)** — the diatonic ii chord is naturally a minor 7th; raising its 3rd a half-step to make it a dominant 7th borrows the "barbershop" tension quality onto a chord that's normally soft — this is the textbook V7/V secondary dominant in practical use. → [[Secondary Dominants]]
- **Drop root notes down an octave for extra "beefiness"** — after the harmony is set, take just the root note of each chord and move it down an octave to reinforce the low end without touching the voicing above it. → [[Chords]]
- **Hold a common tone across adjacent chords for smoothness** — when the same pitch (e.g. the 5th of one chord) is also present in the next chord, keep that note sustained across the chord change instead of retriggering it, gluing the two chords together. → [[Chord Inversions]]
- **Raise the 5th of a chord a half-step (♯5) for extra tension/color** — an altered-dominant move: sharpening the 5th of an otherwise-plain 7th chord adds noticeable extra bite. → [[Altered Chords]]
- **Melodics (third-party app, not a DAW plugin)** — used afterward to actually practice playing the finished progression on a MIDI keyboard; not routed further since it isn't a production plugin/effect.
