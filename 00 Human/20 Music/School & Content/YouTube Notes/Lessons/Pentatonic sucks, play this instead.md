## Synopsis

Short A/B pitch for chord-tone soloing over pentatonic-only soloing, with a minimal beginner-friendly practice method: restrict to one fretboard area, solo only chord tones there, then move to a new area.

## Techniques

- **A/B comparison over one progression:** three solos targeting chord tones, one pure pentatonic — the point being that chord-tone targeting sounds more deliberate.
- **Area-restricted chord-tone drill:** pick a single fretboard area, play the progression's chords there, solo using only the chords' own notes without leaving that area, then repeat the whole process in a new fretboard area once confident.
