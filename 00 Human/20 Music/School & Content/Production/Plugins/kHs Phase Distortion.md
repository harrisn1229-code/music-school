# kHs Phase Distortion

![[Images/kHs Phase Distortion.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A phase-distortion synthesis-style waveshaping snap-in (Casio CZ-style tonal character).

## What it's used for

Classic phase-distortion synth tones and harmonic coloration.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain for creative harmonic effects.
