# Splice Sounds Listener

![[Images/Splice Sounds Listener.png]]

**Category:** Utility
**Vendor:** Splice

## What it is

A background listener component of the Splice Sounds plugin that keeps the sample browser in sync with the current session.

## What it's used for

Supporting the Splice Sounds browsing workflow in the background.

## FX chain notes

A background utility process, not an audio-processing device.

## Key Parameters

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Background Listener | Keeps the Splice plugin in sync with the app | Support component — no audio processing |
