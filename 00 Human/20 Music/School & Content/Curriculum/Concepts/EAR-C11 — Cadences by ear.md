# EAR-C11 — Cadences by ear

**Q.** Name the four cadences by the *feeling* of their landing, not by their Roman numerals.

> [!question]- Answer
> **Authentic (V–I)** — full arrival, the door closing. **Plagal (IV–I)** — arrival without the pull; softer, already-home rather than coming-home. **Half (…–V)** — suspended, a question left open, the sentence stopping mid-breath. **Deceptive (V–vi)** — the pull of an authentic cadence resolving somewhere that isn't home; the specific feeling is *surprise plus incompleteness*.

## Why it matters

EAR-18 gave you four chords and asked for the cadence in the last two. Cadence recognition is where degree-feeling and progression-feeling meet, and it's the cheapest way to start hearing form rather than chords.

## Drill

Play all four in C, then in G, then have them shuffled. Call each one before the last chord finishes ringing.
