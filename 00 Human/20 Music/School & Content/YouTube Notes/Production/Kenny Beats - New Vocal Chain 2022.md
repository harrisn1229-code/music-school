# Kenny Beats — New Vocal Chain 2022

![](https://youtu.be/-SvBNOBWJ_8)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/-SvBNOBWJ_8)

## Synopsis

The 2022 update of Kenny's vocal template: dual-track recording (tracking + ghost latency track), a lean tracking chain (EQ → Pro-DS → R-Compressor → PuigTec-style EQ), **all five delay sends consolidated onto EchoBoy** using its internal high/low cut instead of separate EQs, three reverb tiers (Pro-R short / Valhalla long / super-long), the doubler dropped entirely, and the ad-lib group architecture (pan L / pan R / phone-filter center) plus an "effects track" for spotlight delay throws. Maps almost one-to-one onto Nate's vocal chain family.

## Techniques

- **Record on two tracks at once, always** — tracking track (monitored, FX, → master) + latency track (nothing on it, monitor off, out to a spare hardware pair, not the master); keeper takes come off the ghost track into the group. Even with buffer + compensation fixed, "I want no room for it showing up on screen." → [[Latency-Free Tracking]], [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]]
- **Tracking chain 2022: EQ → [[Pro-DS]] (Male Wide Band preset, threshold to taste) → R-Compressor → PuigTec-style EQ** — de-esser BEFORE the compressor on the way in, light "fake Pultec" sweetening last. Owned equivalents: [[Pro-Q 4]] → [[Pro-DS]] → [[Pro-C 2]]/[[LALA]] → [[UAD Pultec EQP-1A]]. → [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]]
- **The chain lives on the GROUP, tracks stay empty** — verse/hook tracks carry zero plugins; one chain on the group keeps every punch-in identical (Nate's group convention, confirmed). Auto-Tune is the one exception — it goes on individual tracks when takes need different keys/settings. → [[2 Main Vocal Group|Main Vocal Group]], [[Auto-Tune Pro]]
- **All delays = [[EchoBoy]], EQ'd internally** — 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, slap, 1/2 sends share one EchoBoy setting varied only by subdivision, and he stopped putting EQ after the delays: **EchoBoy's built-in high/low cut does the band-limiting**. One fewer plugin per return, identical tone family across every delay. → [[Delay Send Slap]], [[Delay Send Other]], [[EchoBoy]]
- **Reverb tiers 2022: short = [[Pro-R 2]] "Vocal Backings Bright" preset (tweaked) — **he used the original Pro-R in this 2022 video** · long/super-long = [[ValhallaVintageVerb]]** — VVV "just sounds right on vocals"; the doubler he used for years is **gone** from the chain. → [[Reverb Send Room]], [[Reverb Send Long]]
- **Ad-lib group = pan-L track + pan-R track + phone-filter track (center)** — two takes alternate L/R (or one full take per side); the **phone-filter track makes an ad-lib cut through between the mains** — band-limited beats louder. → [[1 Backup Vocal Track|Backup Vocal Track]], [[Masking and Carving]]
- **Stack harmonies by copy-pairs at stepped pans** — duplicate the L/R pair at 50/50, again at 30/30; one harmony per slot, two per side = big panned stack with no plugin widener. (In Live's pan terms: pairs at 25L/25R and 15L/15R.) → [[1 Double Vocal Track|Double Vocal Track]], [[Width and Stereo]]
- **Backgrounds/ad-libs get MORE tune than mains** — a touch more retune speed on stacks makes singers "push for it"; or invert it (mains soft-EFX, ad-libs Legato) for smoother beds — decide per part, never one setting for all lanes. → [[Auto-Tune Pro]], [[1 Backup Vocal Track|Backup Vocal Track]]
- **The "effects track" for spotlight throws** — drag the last word of a line onto a dedicated track: big loud [[EchoBoy]] **insert** (not send) with the high end cut, change the subdivision per throw, plus long-reverb send ducked under the ad-libs. The delay-throw as an arrangement event, not a constant. → [[Delay Send Other]], [[Automation]]
- **Playback psychology** — the group chain is slightly nicer than the tracking chain (a bit more reverb/EQ love); when the artist hears the drag-down playback it sounds *better* than their headphones and they push harder. "Psychological warfare, but healthy."
