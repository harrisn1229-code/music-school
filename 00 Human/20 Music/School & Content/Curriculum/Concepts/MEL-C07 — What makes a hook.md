# MEL-C07 — What makes a hook

**Q.** What makes a hook memorable? Name at least three factors.

> [!question]- Answer
> **1) Repetition** — it has to come back, and be short enough to hold after one pass. This outranks everything. **2) Rhythmic distinctiveness** — identifiable from its rhythm alone, with the pitches stripped out. **3) Singable range and clear contour.** Then: **contrast** against what surrounds it. **Simplicity usually beats complexity.**

## Why it matters

The test for factor 2 is concrete — tap the hook with no pitches. If it's still recognisable, it's rhythmically distinct. A hook has to survive being remembered badly.

## Drill

Tap three hooks you know, no pitches. Which are still identifiable?
