# ARR-C01 — A boring loop needs a B section, not a new idea

**Q.** You made a loop, loved it, and an hour later it feels worthless. What's the actual diagnosis?

> [!question]- Answer
> **The idea was never bad — it was alone.** A loop that bores you isn't asking for a new idea, it's asking for a **B section**. Kenny Beats: "a lot of ideas you're throwing out are really good ideas missing one other little idea to point out what it is you're doing." There is also a hard ceiling here: no amount of subtractive variation on one loop sustains a full song — a genuinely second loop is eventually unavoidable, and most attempts at it will be discardable before one lands.

## Why it matters

Loop-itis is the single biggest reason projects die in the folder, and it's a misdiagnosis rather than a skill gap. Expecting the mediocre second-loop attempts is part of the technique, not a sign something's wrong.

## Drill

Open three abandoned projects. For each, write one sentence describing what a complementary B section would do. Don't build them yet.
