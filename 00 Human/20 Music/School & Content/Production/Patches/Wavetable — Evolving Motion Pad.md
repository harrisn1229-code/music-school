# Wavetable — Evolving Motion Pad

**Instrument:** [[Wavetable]] · **Slot:** [[BED Slot]] · **Used by:** [[1 Synth Bed Track|Synth Bed Track]]

## What it's for
An evolving/motion pad built on Wavetable's position-LFO — slow internal movement that OTT loves downstream. The go-to when the bed needs to breathe on its own without automation.

## Settings
- From [[Wavetable#Patch Recipes|Wavetable — Bed]]: ② Osc 1 + Osc 2 — a warm analog-style or "Pad" category wavetable each
- ①⑧ Slow LFO or long Env → Wave Position on Osc 2 for internal movement, not melody
- ⑥ Filter routing: Parallel
- ⑤ Filter circuit: Clean or MS2, cutoff moderate, low resonance
- ⑨ Unison: Position Spread mode, 3–4 voices, moderate amount — width without just detuning
- ⑧ Amp envelope: attack 0.8–1.5 s, high sustain (Step 1a universal pad rule)
- HPF ~120–150 Hz on the way out — the chain also HPFs the track; this trims mud at the source
- Velocity → nothing; no sub octave

## In context
- Swappable with an [[Analog]] or [[Drift — Evolving Motion Pad]] bed — same slot, same motion-first brief.
