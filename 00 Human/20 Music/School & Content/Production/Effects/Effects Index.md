# Effects Index

General, tool-agnostic best practice organized by *kind* of processing — the reusable lessons pulled from production breakdowns, independent of any one plugin. Specific settings live on the plugin notes in [[Plugin Index]]; the *why* and the general technique live here. Fed by `/music-enrich`.

- [[Compression]] — matching makeup gain, fake-comp transient nudges, bus glue, OTT-as-glue
- [[Limiting]] — the clipper+Inflator master chain, the −10 to −6 handoff, limiters on drums only
- [[Multiband Compression]] — frequency-specific sidechain (duck only the sub), dip one band, OTT glue
- [[Saturation and Distortion]] — Decapitator Punish, stacked gentle stages, saturation as loudness
- [[EQ]] — subtract-don't-add, carve air vs hi-hats, rhythmic EQ automation, small-speaker warmth
- [[De-essing]] — air vs de-essing, manual de-essing, the whisper trick, George Michael's inverted send
- [[Reverb]] — high-pass the send, reverse-reverb, slapback for presence, muting tails
- [[Delay]] — throw sends, gap automation, two-short-delays chorus, tape-delay endings
- [[Modulation]] — chorus width, stereo detune, same-take formant doubling, rotary on vocals
- [[Pitch and Formant]] — auto-tune as texture, varispeed character, formant-follows-lyric, vocoder harmony

> Processing a sound is this folder. **Making** one is the [[Synthesis Index]] — same model, pages per concept rather than per plugin.
