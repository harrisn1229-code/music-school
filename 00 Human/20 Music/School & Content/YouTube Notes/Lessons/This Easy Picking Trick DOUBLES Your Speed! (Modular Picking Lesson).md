## Synopsis

Introduces "modular picking" — a named technique for playing full chords fast by sweeping through symmetrical repeating modules, distinct from plain strumming (played tight to a metronome) and from a normal arpeggio sweep (chord grips, not single notes). Two worked exercises included.

Summary: symmetrical mirrored "modules" of picking motion, chord shapes chosen to consciously overlap notes/intervals rather than separate them, playable for both rhythm and lead work, clean or distorted tone.
