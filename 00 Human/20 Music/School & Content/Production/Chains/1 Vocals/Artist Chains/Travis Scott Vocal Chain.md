# Travis Scott Vocal Chain

**Reference/vibe:** hard-tuned voice as a *synthesizer*, drowned in space — the tune locked to zero, the voice dark and hooded, huge hall/plate behind it, delay throws and pitched ad-lib layers everywhere. Psychedelic rap: the effects are the arrangement.
**Signal source:** U87-class condenser in his sessions — [[Neumann TLM 102]]. Delivery: melodic mumble, ad-libs performed with full commitment (they're half the record).

## Step 1 — Record (UAD Console)
1. **UAD Neve 1073 (Unison)** — a little drive; his vocal reads warm-dark, not crisp
2. **UAD 1176LN** — 4:1 fast, 3–4 dB (rap phrasing spikes hard)
3. **UAD Pultec EQP-1A** — low ATTEN @ 100 Hz, no air boost (dark on purpose)

## Chain overview
Hard tune → dark EQ → heavy comp → saturate → the space. The intelligibility rules get inverted: the *blur* is the aesthetic; the dry/wet balance and the throws carry the drama.

## Step 2 — Process (Ableton)

### 1. [[Auto-Tune Pro]] — THE effect
- **Retune 0–10, Humanize 0**, key set carefully (a wrong key note = full robot warble) · Classic mode algorithm for the harder snap
- Flip Formant/Throat on ad-lib layers for character spread.

### 2. [[Pro-Q 4]] — Dark shape
- HPF 90–110 Hz · **shelf DOWN the top slightly (−1–2 dB above 8–10 kHz)** — the hooded quality; presence lives 2–4 kHz, not in air · cut 400–600 boxiness.

### 3. [[Pro-C 2]] — Flatten
- 4:1–6:1 · **4–6 dB GR** — modern rap density; dynamics come from arrangement, not the voice.

### 4. [[Saturn 2]] or [[Decapitator]] — Density
- Tube, medium drive, mix ~30–40% — thickens the tuned tone into that synthy sustain.

### 5. [[Pro-DS]] — De-ess firm.

## Step 3 — Effects (sends — this is the Travis part)
- **The big verb:** [[ValhallaVintageVerb]] or [[ValhallaPlate]] — **decay 2.5–4.5 s, pre-delay 20–40 ms**, send/return (never insert), HPF'd return, **ducked hard from the dry vocal** — huge behind him, clear when he speaks.
- **Throws:** [[Delay Send Other]] 1/4 (and 1/4T) ping-pong, automated onto line-end words, feedback 30–50% — throws answer *in the reverb*, so they smear into the hall.
- **Ad-lib layers:** [[Little AlterBoy]] pitch/formant shifted (±3–5 semis, or octave), panned wide, drenched wetter than the lead — the crowd of hims ("it's lit!" energy).
- [[Width Send]] on hook stacks · [[Reverb Send Room]] barely used — this aesthetic skips "natural."

## Vocal arrangement

| Section | Lead | Behind it |
|---|---|---|
| Verse | hard-tuned, dark, hooded | ad-libs answering **every** line, panned wide |
| Hook | tuned lead + pitched doubles (octave down, octave up) | ad-lib bed running underneath continuously |
| Throw | a line thrown into delay, dry vocal drops out | the throw *is* the transition |

**The move — the effects are the arrangement.** Stated in the vibe line above and worth taking literally: sections change because the space changes. A hall opens, a delay throw carries a line across a bar-line, a pitched layer arrives. Almost nothing changes in the parts.

**Ad-libs are structural, not decoration.** They run continuously and they're the second most important vocal lane — panned hard, pitched, degraded. Budget real time for them.

**Stack count: 2 → 6+, plus a permanent ad-lib bed.**

## Notes / variations
- **Verse vs hook:** verses = tune + slap + light verb (closer); hooks = everything opens (long verb send up, throws, octave stacks). The wet/dry automation IS the songwriting dynamic.
- **MY EYES / ambient lane:** slower retune (10–20), more formant layers, verb decay toward 5 s, tempo-synced throbbing delay.
- **Rage lane (UTOPIA):** distortion forward (Decapitator Punish on a parallel), verb shorter but darker, tune unchanged.
- 🎬 *From video (God's Country, UTOPIA):* clone the vocal into TWO differently-processed layers rather than one chain doing two jobs. Layer 1 = this chain's default (Auto-Tune → reverb → EQ). Layer 2 = a guitar amp ([[Morgan Amps Suite]]) + EQ, run specifically to cut through the mix — an amp-sim used as a presence stage on a voice, not a guitar tone.

## From videos

- **Clone-into-two-layers with distinct JOBS, not just doubled processing.** God's Country's vocal is recorded once, cloned, and each clone gets a *different* chain: the "any vocal" default (Auto-Tune → reverb → EQ) on Layer 1, and a guitar amp + EQ specifically for cut-through on Layer 2 — two layers solving two different problems (tone vs. presence) rather than a stack of the same processing twice. → [[Auto-Tune Pro]], [[Morgan Amps Suite]], [[Saturation and Distortion]], [[Vocal Stacks and Ad-libs]] — loner, [the secrets behind Travis Scott's 'UTOPIA' production!?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vHZnAAegfw)
