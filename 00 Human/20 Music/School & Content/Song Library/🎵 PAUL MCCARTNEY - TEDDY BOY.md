# TEDDY BOY — Paul McCartney

> Lighthearted story song about a boy caring for his widowed mother. Started during the Get Back sessions; Lennon famously improvised mockery over it during rehearsals. Released on McCartney (1970).

## Chords
**Key:** D major · ~83 BPM · 4/4

**Verse:** D – A – Am – Em – G – A
→ **I – V – v – ii – IV – V** (the A**m** is a borrowed Mixolydian minor-v — the signature color of the verse)

**Chorus:** F♯ – B – E – A – D – A
→ **III – VI – II – V – I** (a descending circle-of-fifths chain of secondary dominants resolving home to D)

**Bridge** (lifts toward F♯): F♯ – C♯ – C♯m – G♯m – B – C♯
→ tonicizes F♯ before dropping back to the verse.

## Production & Arrangement
From **McCartney (1970)** — his first solo album, recorded mostly **at home on a Studer 4-track** (plus some Abbey Road time) with **McCartney playing every instrument himself**. A deliberate reaction to Beatles polish and the breakup: loose, intimate, lo-fi, unfinished-on-purpose.

- Folky, narrative — originally worked up during the Get Back/Let It Be sessions, then finished solo at home.
- Acoustic-driven, loose, storytelling delivery.

## Steal this
- **Folk-narrative arrangement:** let the story lead; keep the backing simple and acoustic.
- **Rescue a band idea solo:** a song that didn't fit the group can become an intimate solo cut.
- **Loose feel:** don't grid-tighten a storytelling folk take.
