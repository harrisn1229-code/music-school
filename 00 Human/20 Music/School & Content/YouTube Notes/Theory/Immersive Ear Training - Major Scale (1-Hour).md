# Immersive Ear Training - Major Scale (1-Hour)

> [!info] Listen-along page — this one is *used*, not read
> Sixty-eight minutes of ear training you put on and listen to. There is no transcript and there should not be one: the answers are spoken aloud two seconds after each tone, so a transcript would be an hour of *"…that was 3… that was 5…"* — an answer key for an exercise that already gives you the answer. **The value is in playing it.** Everything below is grounded in Max's own description of the exercise.

## What it is

A continuous drill on the seven degrees of the **major scale**, heard **against a drone**. The loop is always the same three moves: **a tone is played → the correct scale degree is spoken → the same tone is played again**, so you get to feel the degree a second time now knowing what it was. That second hearing is the part that does the work — it is where the *feeling* and the *number* get welded together. **The key changes every few minutes**, which is what stops you memorising pitches instead of learning degrees.

It is a direct recording of Max's app **Sonofield** running in "Pocket Mode" — built for hands-free training while walking, commuting, or doing nothing in particular.

Start here. The major scale is the reference every other collection is heard against, and its degrees are the ones already wired into you by a lifetime of listening — so it is the set where the feeling→number association forms fastest.

## How to use it

- **Do not sit down and concentrate on this.** It is designed for the background — a walk, a commute, the washing up. An hour of relaxed exposure beats ten minutes of straining.
- **Focus on the *feeling state* of each tone, not the pitch.** Max's core claim, and the one the vault has already adopted: a degree's feeling is the same in every octave and every key. That is the thing being learned — see [[EAR-C05 — Close in pitch is not close in feeling]].
- **If you are starting out, do not try to guess.** His explicit instruction: just listen and relax. Guessing wrong repeatedly trains anxiety, not recognition.
- **When the tone repeats after the answer, that is the rep.** Connect the number you just heard to the feeling you are having. Everything else is setup.
- The key change every few minutes is a feature — if a degree only feels right in C, it has not been learned yet.
