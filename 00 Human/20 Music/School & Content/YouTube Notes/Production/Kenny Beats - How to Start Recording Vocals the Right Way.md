# Kenny Beats — How to Start Recording Vocals the Right Way

![](https://youtu.be/3Nw1d37nluo)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/3Nw1d37nluo)

## Synopsis

The must-dos before and during a vocal session: lock tempo + key before pressing record, get latency to near-zero, gate the room noise at a specific threshold, and — the most underrated one — **chop out every second of audio that isn't vocal** so downstream compression doesn't amplify room noise into mud. Plus the Kaotica Eyeball as a $175 portable booth. Short, dense, and everything maps straight onto [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]] recording setup.

## Techniques

- **Know the tempo AND key before recording** — wrong tempo = no loopable stems, no clean copy/paste, session reset mid-take; unknown key = no Auto-Tune when someone asks for it. If nobody knows the key, find it at a piano: the note that fits best is usually the root. → [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]], [[Auto-Tune Pro]]
- **Latency to near-zero before anyone sings** — buffer 128 (64 if the machine allows, never 1024+), then Driver Error Compensation trimmed until overall latency ≈ 0 ms; use the simplest good-sounding plugins while tracking (CPU-heavy chains add latency). Performers sing differently when their headphones are late. → [[Latency-Free Tracking]]
- **Gate at −40 to −43 dB for room noise** — stock Ableton [[Gate]]; that threshold clears AC/fan/rustle while letting every vocal through; smooth the attack/release so word edges don't clip off. Demo: threshold on chimes = only the loud hits pass. → [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]], [[Gate]]
- **Chop out ALL dead space between phrases (the big one)** — everything that isn't vocal gets cut: don't clip word fronts/ends, keep natural breaths, but delete the silence/rustle between lines, especially across 5 stacked ad-lib tracks. **Why:** every compressor downstream raises the floor of whatever you leave — uncut room noise is why "my vocal feels muddy on top of the beat." Editing IS gain-staging. → [[1 Main Vocal Track|Main Vocal Track]] Step 1.5, [[Compression Staging]]
- **Kaotica Eyeball (~$175) as a micro-booth** — foam tunnel around the mic: blocks room reflections + doubles as pop filter for S/P/T harshness; standard for car/hotel makeshift sessions and "extremely effective." Candidate purchase if untreated-room vocals keep fighting [[RX Voice De-noise]]. → gear note on page only
