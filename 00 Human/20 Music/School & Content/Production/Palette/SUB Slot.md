# SUB Slot — 20–60 Hz

Physical weight; felt more than heard. The mix's anchor — everything else is balanced against it.

## Swap list (interchangeable occupants)

- **808** — [[Serum 2#Patch Recipes|Serum 2 808 recipe]]; tuned to song key, glide for slides
- **Sub sine** — [[Omnisphere]] sub recipe · [[Analog]] quick sine
- **Bass guitar fundamental** — when the bass gtr is deep enough, it *is* the sub; don't add another
- **Kick tail** — in some genres (house/techno) the kick's tail owns this slot

## Mix rules

- **Mono. Always.** No reverb, no chorus, no widener below 100 Hz — ever.
- **One occupant, ever** — never stack two subs; the second one only makes mud and phase.
- Level: set it against the kick first, the vocal second; check on small speakers that the *harmonics* (BASS slot) carry the note.
- Standard processing: see [[FX Chain System]] bass chains; saturation adds audible harmonics, not level.

## When it's the hook
808-driven trap: the 808 pattern *is* the hook — push it, HPF everything else at 100–120 Hz, and let slides/glide carry melody. Vocal dips slightly for the 808's attack.

## When it competes
- **vs Kick** ([[TRANSIENT Slot]]): sidechain the sub to the kick, offset the 808 to avoid stacking hits, or make the 808 the kick.
- **vs everything else**: nothing else belongs down here — HPF all BED/MIDS/AIR sources ≥100 Hz ([[Masking and Carving]]).

## Drum lanes in this slot (from books)

- **The kit's high-pass ladder starts here.** Every drum gets filtered out of the sub except the kick: kick low cut **40–60 Hz** (remove rumble) — and even the kick only keeps this slot when no 808 is present; in a trap/808 context the kick HPFs at 40–60 Hz and the [[Bass Design|808]] owns the sub outright. Toms **60–200 Hz**, snare **100–200 Hz**, hats **200–300 Hz** — nothing else in the kit reaches down here at all. → [[EQ]], [[Kick Design]], [[Masking and Carving]] — Whipped Cream Sounds, [[Drum Mixing EQ Guide - Enrichment|Drum Mixing EQ Guide]]
