# kHs Distortion

![[Images/kHs Distortion.png]]

**Category:** Drive/Color
**Vendor:** Kilohearts

## What it is

A general-purpose distortion/waveshaping snap-in with multiple distortion algorithms.

## What it's used for

Harmonic saturation to full-on distortion character on any track.

## FX chain notes

Stacked in a Snap Heap chain, typically before EQ shaping.

## Key Parameters

```visual-key
image: Images/kHs Distortion.png

```
*Tap a numbered circle for what that control does, the technique, and any artist example.*

| # | Parameter | What it is / does | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | Mode | The distortion algorithm/curve (the dropdown, shown on the graph above) | Audition modes per source — soft-clip for warmth, harder curves for grit |
| ② | Drive | Input gain into the waveshaper — more = more distortion | Push for saturation → full distortion; back off for harmonic warmth |
| ③ | Dynamics | Restores transient dynamics lost to clipping | Turn up to keep punch under heavy drive |
| ④ | Bias | DC offset — shifts even/odd harmonic balance | Add bias for asymmetric, more even-harmonic character |
| ⑤ | Spread | Stereo spread of the distortion | Widen for a bigger image; keep low to stay centered |
| ⑥ | DC Filter | Removes DC offset from the output (toggle) | Leave on to keep the output centered after biasing |
| ⑦ | Mix | Dry/wet blend | Parallel for controlled grit that keeps the dry punch |
