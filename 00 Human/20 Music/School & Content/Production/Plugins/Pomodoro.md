# Pomodoro

![[Images/Pomodoro.jpg]]

**Category:** Utility
**Vendor:** Max for Live

## What it is

A Max for Live productivity timer device implementing the Pomodoro focus-session technique inside the Live window.

## What it's used for

Session time management while producing — not an audio device at all, a workflow utility.

## FX chain notes

Runs inline in the signal/MIDI chain at the point it's inserted; combines with whatever device follows.
