# VOI-C15 — Renumber off the chord, not the key

**Q.** In chord melody you play the tune on top and the harmony underneath. What do you read each melody note as, and how should the voicings you reach for be indexed?

> [!question]- Answer
> Read every melody note as **a scale degree of whichever chord is sounding right now**, not of the parent key — the same pitch is "5 of C" under a C chord and "4 of D" a beat later under Dm. Index your go-to voicings by **which chord tone the melody note is, and which string it lands on** — that is the lookup you actually need mid-arrangement.

## Why it matters

Thinking in the parent key hides the relationship that tells you which shape fits; renumbering off the current chord reveals it, and the shape then transfers to any chord of that quality by sliding to the new root. The other half is physical: **do not harmonise every note.** Playing single melody notes between chord grabs is easier and sounds better — space reads as phrasing.

## Drill

15 min: take eight bars of a tune you know. Say each melody note's degree relative to the chord under it, out loud, as you play. Harmonise only the notes that start a phrase.
