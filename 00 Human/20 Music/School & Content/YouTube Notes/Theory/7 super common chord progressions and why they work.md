# 7 super common chord progressions and why they work

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vyc8lezaa9g)
📺 [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vyc8lezaa9g)

## Synopsis

A broad survey of seven progressions: the Axis Progression and its minor-flavored rotation, two closely related Spanish/modal vamps that differ only by whether the final dominant chord is present, a common mislabeling of the doo-wop progression as "Blue Moon," a simple major-scale loop, and a three-chord Mixolydian vamp.

## Techniques

- **Axis Progression — I–V–vi–IV** — moves maximally away from home first (I→V), then vi and IV each give partial (not full) resolution, which is what keeps the loop wanting to continue rather than settle. Full writeup: [[The Axis Progression]].
- **vi–IV–I–V** — the same four chords started on vi instead of I, a minor-flavored variant; the Axis of Awesome comedy sketch (the progression's namesake) used this relationship to chain two "different" progressions into one longer medley.
- **Andalusian cadence — i–♭VII–♭VI–V** (V made major/dominant) — flamenco-associated descending progression; the major V pulls hard back to i, closing the loop tightly.
- **"Aeolian vamp" — i–♭VII–♭VI** — the same opening three chords as the Andalusian cadence, but without the closing V; removing the dominant pull leaves the loop floatier and less directional, staying in pure Aeolian color instead of resolving.
- **Doo-wop / "50s" progression — I–vi–IV–V** — often mislabeled the "Blue Moon" progression; "Blue Moon" itself actually uses I–vi–ii–V. The confusion is understandable (ii and IV are both subdominant-function chords, differing by only one note) but the two are not identical.
- **I–V–IV–V** — all-major, major-scale-only loop; simple, upbeat, functional bed usable across many styles.
- **"Mixolydian vamp" — I–♭VII–IV–I** (F–E♭–B♭–F) — three chords establish Mixolydian color via the borrowed ♭VII; also reads as circle-of-fifths root motion (each chord a 4th/5th from the last), giving it an "epic, open" quality beyond just the modal color.
