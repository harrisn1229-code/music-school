# California Dreamin' — The Mamas and the Papas

> Written by John & Michelle Phillips; signature 1965 folk-rock hit.

## Chords
**Key:** C♯ minor as recorded · **Capo 4**, played in **A minor** shapes · ~112 BPM

**Verse:** Am – G – F – G – E7sus4 – E7
→ **i – ♭VII – ♭VI – ♭VII – V7** (the descending i–♭VII–♭VI is the song's hook; the E7 is the harmonic-minor dominant pulling back to Am)

**"I'd be safe and warm…" / Chorus:** F – C – E7 – Am
→ **♭VI – ♭III – V7 – i**

All intervals are relative to the capo'd A-minor shape; the sounding key is C♯ minor.

## Production & Arrangement
1965 — lush folk-rock with **dense four-part harmony**, a minor-key verse, Wrecking Crew backing, and the famous **alto flute solo** (Bud Shank). The vocal-arrangement is the star.
