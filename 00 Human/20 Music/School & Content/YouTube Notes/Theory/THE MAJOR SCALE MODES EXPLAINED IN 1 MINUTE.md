## Synopsis

A deliberately compressed run through the seven modes of the major scale. The description states the goal — break the modes down as quickly as possible — and names what they are for: **composing and soloing**. A one-minute orientation, not a substitute for [[Diatonic Modes]].

## What to watch for

- **The order he presents them in.** Ionian through Locrian in parent-scale order is the standard framing; presenting them as *parallel* (all rooted on one note) teaches a different thing. Which one this is changes how you should practise it.
- **The characteristic note of each mode** — the single degree that distinguishes it from the major or minor scale it most resembles. That note is the entire practical content of modal playing.
- **Any use case he names.** The description flags composing and soloing; a one-minute video that also gives a chord to play each mode over is worth more than one that gives seven scale spellings.
