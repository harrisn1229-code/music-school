# PrimalTap

![[Images/PrimalTap.png]]

**Category:** Reverb/Delay
**Vendor:** Soundtoys

## What it is

Soundtoys' lo-fi delay plugin modeling vintage, imperfect analog delay units with wow/flutter and degradation.

## What it's used for

Gritty, characterful lo-fi delay effects distinct from EchoBoy's cleaner delay models.

## FX chain notes

Used as a send/insert delay effect for lo-fi character.
