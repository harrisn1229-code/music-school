# BLU-C04 — Turnarounds

**Q.** What is a turnaround, where does it happen, and give one in A.

> [!question]- Answer
> **The last two bars** (11–12) — a figure that leads back to the top of the form. Its job is to **break the resolution** and usually **land on V**, so the ear expects bar 1 again. In A: **A7 – F♯7 – B7 – E7**, or a chromatic line descending over an A pedal.

## Why it matters

Both blank. Without a turnaround the form sounds finished every twelve bars — ending on V is what makes it circular. Any short sequence that creates motion and lands on V will do the job.

## Drill

Play the A7–F♯7–B7–E7 turnaround, then loop back to bar 1. Then find one in E.
