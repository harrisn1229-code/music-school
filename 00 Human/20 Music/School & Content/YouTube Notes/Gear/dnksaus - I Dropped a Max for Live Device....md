## Synopsis

A quick demo of dnksaus's own new Max for Live release, **Wave LFO** — an LFO that generates its modulation shape directly from a dropped-in audio sample's waveform rather than a standard sine/saw/square shape, with Scale, a scroll position, Zoom (to isolate/loop just one portion of the waveform), Offset, Smooth, and Phase controls, plus the usual LFO sync behavior. The pitch: it's bipolar (peaks and troughs around center) where the closest owned tool, Envelope Follower, is unipolar (only rises from the bottom), and it can be scrubbed/auditioned silently without needing a live audio track feeding it. Sold on his Gumroad.

## Techniques

- **Draw an LFO's shape from a sample's own waveform, not a stock shape** — drop any audio file into Wave LFO and it generates a modulation curve straight from that file's amplitude contour; **Scale** sets the wave's amplitude range, a scroll knob moves through the sample, **Zoom** isolates and loops just the specific stretch of waveform worth using, **Offset** repositions it, and **Smooth** tames overly spiky results. **Phase** behaves like a normal LFO's phase control — set the modulation's timing relative to the transport/drum beat, starting subtle. → [[Envelope Follower]], [[LFO]]
- **Bipolar vs. unipolar as the real differentiator from Envelope Follower** — Envelope Follower only rises from a floor (unipolar); Wave LFO's output has real peaks and troughs oscillating around the center (bipolar) — a genuinely different shape of movement for the same "shape comes from a sample" idea. → [[Envelope Follower]]
- **Scrub a sample's shape silently, without a live audio track** — unlike Envelope Follower (which needs to actually hear a signal play back to track it), Wave LFO reads the sample file directly, so you can scroll/zoom to a usable section without ever routing it through an audio track or hearing it triggered live.
