# Roland TR-808

## What it is
Roland's TR-808 Rhythm Composer is the most influential drum machine ever made — a fully analog, 16-step programmable box that flopped commercially (~12,000 units, discontinued after three years) because it sounded nothing like real drums, then conquered the world precisely *because* it sounded nothing like real drums. It is the sonic foundation of hip hop, trap, electro, Miami bass, and half of modern pop's low end.

## What makes the sound
- **Every voice is an analog circuit, not a sample** — bridged-T oscillators, filtered noise, and VCAs, which is why 808 sounds are so tweakable and so clean at the bottom.
- **The kick is a self-oscillating bridged-T resonant circuit**: a trigger pings the filter, which rings down as a decaying sine. Long decay + high resonance = a *pitched sub bass note*, not a drum hit — the entire "808 bass" concept (the [[SUB Slot]] as an instrument) comes from this one circuit. Tune it and it's your bassline → [[Kick Design]].
- **Snare** = two bridged-T oscillators (~180/330 Hz "snappy" body) + filtered white noise; **claves/cowbell** = square-wave oscillators through band-passes (the cowbell's two detuned squares are why it's so iconic); **hats/cymbal** = six detuned square oscillators mixed and band-passed — metallic but obviously synthetic.
- **Character comes from imperfection**: Roland used out-of-spec, noisy 2SC828 transistors for the sizzle; when supplies ran out, the 808 couldn't be built the same way again.
- Accent + per-voice decay/tone knobs made it *performable* — long-decay kick + snappy snare + tick hats is a mix that arrives pre-carved.

## Digital versions
- **Owned:** [[Kick 3]] (synthesize/tune 808-style kicks per key), [[Serum 2]] (808 recipe — see [[Sound Palette]]), [[Operator]] (sine + pitch-envelope 808 from scratch), [[Analog]], [[Microtonic]] (analog-style drum synthesis), [[Drum Rack]]/[[Impulse]] for the thousand free 808 sample packs, [[Ramzoid 808 Cooker]] for saturating/tuning 808 samples into modern trap weight.
- **Paid:** Roland Cloud TR-808 (official ACB model), D16 Nepheton 2, Softube Heartbeat, 808 Studio / 808 Studio 2 (deep parametric 808-synthesis plugin — many independent envelope parameters per voice, favored specifically over the "find a sample, pitch it" workflow, see [[Plugin Wishlist]]); Behringer RD-8 in hardware.
- **Free:** sample packs everywhere (the 808 is the most-sampled instrument on earth); AudioThing things aside, honestly samples + [[Ramzoid 808 Cooker]] cover the modern use case.

## Used on
- Travis Scott — *UTOPIA* (2023): sub 808 paired with a "Wondagurl-style" kick on the trap-switch beat, plus a second, differently-patterned 808 closing out the HYENA/SIRENS switch-up — see [[the secrets behind Travis Scott's 'UTOPIA' production!?|the UTOPIA production breakdown]].
- Marvin Gaye — "Sexual Healing" (1982): the 808 as a *groove*, first mega-hit built on it.
- Afrika Bambaataa & the Soulsonic Force — "Planet Rock" (1982): birthed electro; the 808 becomes hip hop's drum kit.
- Beastie Boys — *Licensed to Ill*; Miami bass (2 Live Crew) built a genre on the boomed kick.
- Kanye West — *808s & Heartbreak* (2008): the 808 as melodic/emotional centerpiece, named in the title.
- Trap wholesale — Lex Luger, Metro Boomin, Southside: the tuned, distorted 808 *is* the bass of modern [[Hip Hop - Trap]].
- Genre/era: electro, Miami bass, Detroit techno, freestyle, modern pop low end.
- Finneas — an unnamed song's 808 built entirely inside **808 Studio**, envelopes hand-tweaked rather than sampled and pitched, valued for the plugin's deep per-voice parameter set: [[Creative drum sampling with Finneas]].
