## New / extra in this cut
- **Space Designer "sax reverb" on vocals** — a deliberately *dull/dead* short reverb preset he likes because "it doesn't sound too diggy"; a good pick when you want space without obvious verb tail. → [[Reverb]].
- **Bass = a sampled note + a "rim" layered on top for attack** — the rim gives the pitched sub-sample a transient. → [[1 Digital Bass Track|Digital Bass Track]] (same "sample the one good note" idea as the canonical).
- **Chords:** B♭7sus4 → Fm(add11), then E♭11; a middle-eight harmony (Howard's) that works over *both* chord sets despite being harmonically rich. Strings = Logic factory "orchestral violins, legato" ("great as a stringy synth, not for a real violin").
- **Extended spin-back-delay DJ outro** — added for this version to mix out (drops back to the chords); not in the original release.
- **Logic workflow tips (for the chat):** **Bounce-in-Place** with the *"bypass effect plugins"* toggle — leave it off to *print* the plugin (e.g. commit a compressor), turn it on to **duplicate the plugins onto the new track**; and **Freeze** tracks to save CPU while staying non-committal.
- Origin: written at **Red Bull Studios** (free for teenagers who'd hold a Red Bull for photos) with London Grammar (Hannah + Dot Major, Bruno Major's brother) and Jimmy Napes; engineer Brendan.
