# Clariphonic DSP MKII

![[Images/Clariphonic DSP MKII.png]]

**Category:** EQ
**Vendor:** Kush Audio

## What it is

Kush Audio's parallel-EQ plugin, modeled on the classic passive/parallel EQ technique for adding 'air' and presence without harshness.

## What it's used for

Adding smooth top-end lift and vocal/instrument presence using a parallel-EQ approach rather than standard subtractive/additive EQ.

## FX chain notes

Used in parallel (blended in) after the main EQ pass, for final polish on vocals and mix busses.

## Techniques

- **Run LAST in a vocal chain, explicitly as a restoration move rather than a first-pass brightening decision.** "After that I'm doing a bit of Clariphonic to then make up for overall Shimmer where I might have lost it along the way" — placed after a de-ess → drive → EQ → Soothe → proximity-fix → parallel-comp → de-ess → cross-vocalist sidechain-duck stack, specifically to top up whatever top-end got dulled by everything upstream. A useful placement rule generally: treat this plugin as the corrective final stage of a busy chain, not the tone-shaping starting point. → [[EQ]], [[AIR Slot]], [[James Blake Vocal Chain]] — Mix with the Masters, [[MSM Mixing James Blake's Vocals]]
